Its [research overview](master/research-overview/OVERVIEW.md) connects the proposed proof to the specialized investigations and the earlier geometric studies. The [working papers for the four parallel investigations](master/README.md#working-papers-for-the-parallel-investigations) give the intermediate arguments and their outstanding hypotheses.

The initial investigations did not directly study the smooth Poincaré conjecture. They retain their own materials and optional continuation plans; their inclusion does not activate further work. Each investigation or nested task family owns one research-progress/REGISTER.md, with stable task IDs retained across folder renumbering. Parents link child registers without copying their live rows.

The [second master](master-2/README.md) and its [second series](investigations-2/README.md) were set up on 27 November 2026 at the author's request, by Claude (`claude-fable-5-2`). They study the conjecture through closed self-dual two-forms with a pole and carry their own working rules. Nothing in them is activated, and the first master and first series are unchanged.

The [historical overview](research-overview/PROJECT_SUMMARY_2026-11-20.md) records the earlier twistor program and is not a current status register.
