The scientific tasks belong to the six registers of the [second series](../../investigations-2/README.md); this plan holds only the reading of the manuscript, its integration, the gates and the request to the formal side.

**Routing.** B tasks are conceptual and go to the lead; E tasks have fixed inputs, method and completion test and go to bounded helpers, as in the [model workflow](../../../GPT_RESEARCH_MODEL_WORKFLOW.md). In a Claude session Fable leads and takes B tasks and Opus takes E packets, in the roles that workflow gives Astra and Sol; a GPT lead uses Astra/ultra and Sol 6.2/ultra. The lead selected by the author takes precedence, and the model and effort actually used are recorded in every return.

**Evidence and certification.** The [working rules of the series](../../investigations-2/README.md#working-rules) apply. Every statement in a return is labelled *import* (a published theorem, read in the primary source, with its exact hypotheses), *project theorem* (proved here), *certificate* (a finite computation whose data can be checked independently) or *heuristic*. A project theorem counts as *working* when its proof is written, as *replayed* when a reader who did not produce it has checked it, and as *established* only when Lean proves it from Mathlib, the pinned library and the ledger of imports. An E task that meets a change of mechanism, target or evidence standard stops and returns to the B task named in its row, or to the lead. A B note records the alternatives considered and the reason for the choice. A failed estimate is recorded for the class actually examined and is followed by the next step named in its row.
