## Invariant comparisons and marked diagram receipts

**Imports at the candidate level.** Reutter Corollary B(2) gives equality of the closed oriented scalars for an orientation-preserving homotopy equivalence of simply connected manifolds, for a TFT over an algebraically closed field in which both the local $Z(S^3)$ and fusion $Z(S^2\times S^1)$ algebras are semisimple. It applies to all three open candidate cases in the table. At eleven and seventeen the opposite original framings are Gluck twists of standard $S^4$ along the null-homologous original cap belts. The rational classical $\mathfrak{gl}_2$ Gluck theorem preserves the grading in this case, giving $\mathbb Q$ in tridegree $(0,0,0)$ alone. On the same coordinate ball puncture chosen in the common exterior and for every framed oriented boundary link $L$, the relative module is the usual framing-sensitive $\operatorname{KhR}_2(L;\mathbb Q)$. The unique rational empty-link Lee generator has filtration zero by oriented negative-projective-plane standardization and Lee additivity. The ordinary boundary Rasmussen consequences use the two signed Gluck stabilizations and Ren’s $S^3$ inequality over every field, with the Bar–Natan version in characteristic two and each cobordism component meeting the outgoing link. They give $s_{\mathbb F}(L)=1-m$ for a nonempty strongly slice $m$-component link and $\lvert s_{\mathbb F}(K)\rvert\le 2g$ for a knot bounding a connected genus-$g$ surface. For an oriented surface $G$ in the puncture with boundary $L$, $c$ components and no closed component, the two-sided comparison is $1-2c+\chi(G)\le s_{\mathbb F}(L)\le1-\chi(G)$. The integral prism module remains undetermined here.
