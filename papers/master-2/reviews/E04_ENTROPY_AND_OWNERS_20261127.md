## Exact recognition threshold

Fix a closed smooth homotopy four-sphere \(M\). For a smooth embedding \(F:M\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^5\), let \(\Sigma=F(M)\) and use the Euclidean metric and multiplicity-one four-area. No orientation, chirality or intrinsic conformal class enters the entropy.

\[
\lambda(\Sigma)=\sup_{a\in\mathbb R^5,\ t>0}(4\pi t)^{-2}
\int_\Sigma e^{-|x-a|^2/(4t)}\,d\mathcal H^4(x),
\qquad I_{\rm ent}(M)=\inf_F\lambda(F(M)).
\]

- **Import, checked passage.** Chodosh–Mantoulidis–Schulze, arXiv:2309.03856v2, Corollary 1.5(a,b), gives smooth isotopy of each smooth closed connected embedded four-dimensional hypersurface in Euclidean five-space to a round sphere for \(\lambda\le\lambda(S^3)\), or to a standard handlebody boundary for \(\lambda\le\lambda(S^2)=4/e\). A suitable initial smooth perturbation is allowed in the flow construction. Corollaries 1.5(b) and 1.22(b) print the solid dimensions one too low. In dimension five the solids are \(B^5\) and boundary sums of \(B^4\times S^1\), as corroborated by the discarded hypersurface types in Daniels–Holgate, Theorem 5.9, and the ambient-solid models in Haslhofer–Kleiner, Corollaries 1.25–1.26. The original-image isotopy is the CMS conclusion. The raw surgery slices can change topology. [CMS v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.03856v2) · [Daniels–Holgate, published text](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/170485/1/WRAP-Approximation-of-mean-curvature-flow-with-generic-singularities-by-smooth-flows-with-surgery-Daniels-Holgate-2022.pdf) · [Haslhofer–Kleiner, author text](https://www.math.toronto.edu/roberth/papers/HaslhoferKleiner_MCFsurgery.pdf)
- **Import, checked passage.** CMS Corollary 1.19 and Definition 1.12 supply the separate \(\lambda\le2\) conclusion. For each smooth closed embedded initial hypersurface in that band there are arbitrarily small smooth graphs such that every flow in the specified class from the selected graph has only multiplicity-one generic spherical or cylindrical singularities. The class consists of unit-regular cyclic integral Brakke flows obtained as weak limits of such flows from initial hypersurfaces disjoint from and converging smoothly to the graph. In dimension four the allowed models are \(S^4\), \(S^3\times\mathbb R\), \(S^2\times\mathbb R^2\) and \(S^1\times\mathbb R^3\), with shrinking radius \(\sqrt{2j}\) for the \(S^j\) factor at normalized time \(-1\). The four models describe possible tangents. Corollary 1.5 is the recognition citation. [CMS v2, Definitions 1.6, 1.11–1.12 and Corollaries 1.5, 1.19 and 1.22](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.03856v2) · [E01 source receipt](../../investigations-2/04_entropy_of_embeddings/research-progress/handoffs/E01_FLOW_RETURN.md)
- **Project theorem, replayed.** The embedding set is nonempty. Kervaire's Theorem 3 and its smooth four-dimensional proof supply a compact contractible smooth five-filling \(V\). Rounding \(V\times I\) gives a compact contractible six-manifold with simply connected boundary, so Smale's Theorem (1.1) identifies it with \(D^6\). The double seam then embeds in \(S^5\) and hence in \(\mathbb R^5\) after removing a complementary point. For each smooth embedding, the closure of the bounded side is an unmarked smooth five-ball exactly when \(M\cong_{\rm diff}S^4\), by boundary restriction and the separate uses of Smale's Corollary (1.3) at ambient dimension five. [Kervaire 1969, p. 70](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/kervsmoo.pdf#page=5) · [Smale 1961, p. 498](https://www.maths.gla.ac.uk/~mpowell/Smale-diff-comb.pdf#page=2) · [E07 replay](../../investigations-2/04_entropy_of_embeddings/reviews/E07_EMBEDDING_REPLAY.md)
- **Project theorem, replayed.** A handlebody boundary with \(k\) one-handles has \(H_1(-;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^k\). Thus a homotopy sphere with an individual image in the inclusive band has standard type. The round comparison is \(\lambda(S^j)=|S^j|(j/(2\pi e))^{j/2}\), from evaluating the normalized compact shrinker at its full Gaussian maximum. For \(j=2,4\) this gives \(4/e\) and \(32/(3e^2)\), respectively, with \(32/(3e^2)<4/e\). Bernstein–Wang Theorem 1.1 supplies the sharp lower bound over closed smooth hypersurfaces for \(2\le j\le6\), with equality precisely for translates and positive dilates of the round sphere. Thus a round image attains \(I_{\rm ent}(S^4)=32/(3e^2)\). [Colding–Minicozzi, Lemma 7.10 and proof](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v175-n2-p07-p.pdf#page=31) · [Bernstein–Wang, arXiv:1406.2966v2, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.2966) · [E05 replay](../../investigations-2/04_entropy_of_embeddings/reviews/E05_SPINE_REPLAY.md)

The infimum property gives an individual strict witness whenever \(I_{\rm ent}(M)<4/e\). CMS and the first homology calculation carry that witness to standardness. In the reverse direction standardness supplies the round embedding. The resulting equivalence is

\[
M\cong_{\rm diff}S^4\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\,F:M\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^5\;\;\lambda(F(M))\le4/e
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad I_{\rm ent}(M)<4/e.
\]

If \(I_{\rm ent}(M)=4/e\), every embedding has entropy strictly above \(4/e\). A sequence of images with entropy decreasing to the ceiling from above gives only the weak infimum bound. The endpoint argument in CMS §6.1 gains slack from the selected perturbation of a given qualifying image. A quantitative entropy decrease sufficient to cross the ceiling for a sequence above it remains unavailable. In [proposal §4](../../master/brainstorm/notes/claude/ALTERNATIVE_FRAMEWORKS_AND_INVESTIGATIONS_2_20261127.md#4-framework-m-entropy-of-an-embedding), the weak infimum implication is therefore unsupported, and the solid ball in the one-handle-free case has boundary \(S^4\). The current manuscript's reference to an independent criterion does not specify the inequality. Universal strict entropy entry is equivalent to the conjecture. [CMS §6.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.03856v2#S6.SS1) · [ENT master return](../../investigations-2/04_entropy_of_embeddings/research-progress/handoffs/TO_MASTER2_ENTROPY_20261127.md)
