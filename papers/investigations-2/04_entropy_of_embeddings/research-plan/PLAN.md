**Evidence and certification.** The [working rules of the series](../../README.md#working-rules) apply. Every statement in a return is labelled *import* (a published theorem, read in the primary source, with its exact hypotheses), *project theorem* (proved here), *certificate* (a finite computation whose data can be checked independently) or *heuristic*. A project theorem counts as *working* when its proof is written, as *replayed* when a reader who did not produce it has checked it, and as *established* only when Lean proves it from Mathlib, the pinned library and the ledger of imports. An E task that meets a change of mechanism, target or evidence standard stops and returns to the B task named in its row, or to the lead. A B note records the alternatives considered and the reason for the choice. A failed estimate is recorded for the class actually examined and is followed by the next step named in its row.
