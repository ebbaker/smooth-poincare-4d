## Finding for the lead

The overview’s categorical statement that surgery does not exist for the two higher cylinders should be corrected. Sun–Wang–Xue give a local surgery description for both cylinders under a full-rank nondegeneracy hypothesis. Székelyhidi constructs a global flow with this kind of surgery for smooth compact embedded mean-convex initial hypersurfaces. Bamler–Lai give canonical neighborhoods for both multiplicity-one cylinders even when the full-rank hypothesis and global mean convexity are absent. The bounded search identified no enlargement of the Chodosh–Mantoulidis–Schulze recognition theorem for general low-entropy closed connected embedded four-dimensional hypersurfaces in $\mathbb R^5$. Applying Székelyhidi’s theorem requires global mean convexity of the initial hypersurface. Deducing an ambient isotopy from a track involving the higher surgeries would require a separate argument. [Sun–Wang–Xue](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.16678v1) · [Székelyhidi](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.00964v1) · [Bamler–Lai](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24524v2) · [Chodosh–Mantoulidis–Schulze](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.03856v2)

## Tangent flows and topology change

**Published source baseline.** In the pinned arXiv v2 of Chodosh–Mantoulidis–Schulze, Theorem 1.3 and its precise Corollary 1.19 give arbitrarily small smooth graphical perturbations for closed embedded $M^4\subset\mathbb R^5$ with $\lambda(M)\le 2$ such that all flows in the paper’s admissible class from the perturbed data have only multiplicity-one spherical and cylindrical tangent models. The higher cylinders in that list are $C_{4,k}=S^{4-k}(\sqrt{2(4-k)})\times\mathbb R^k$ for $k=2,3$, at time $-1$ of the tangent flow. Corollaries 1.5 and 1.22 supply the recognition comparator. The discussion after Corollary 1.5 explicitly distinguishes the difficulty of extending isotopy from the existence of a surgery construction. The exact version and endpoint of the recognition statement belong to ENT-E01. [Primary text, §§1 and 1.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.03856v2)

**Theorem in preprint.** Sun–Wang–Xue, *Passing through nondegenerate singularities in mean curvature flows*, arXiv v1, Introduction, Definition 2.6 and Theorem 1.1(i), (iv)–(viii). The setting is a unit-regular cyclic mod-2 Brakke flow in $\mathbb R^{n+1}$ on a time interval containing the singular time, with a nondegenerate cylindrical singularity at the point. In their normal form the leading quadratic Hermite term in the rescaled graphical profile has rank $k$ on the $\mathbb R^k$ factor. For sufficiently small regular times before and after the point, the singularity is isolated in a spacetime neighborhood, the boundary intersection evolves smoothly and the local slice changes by the following surgery. Here $D^j$ is the closed $j$-disk and the trace index is for increasing flow time on a five-dimensional cobordism. [Primary text, Theorem 1.1 and subsequent discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.16678v1)

| Four-dimensional tangent model in $\mathbb R^5$ | Local slice before | Local slice after | Surgery dimension | Forward trace index |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $S^2(2)\times\mathbb R^2$ | $S^2\times D^2$ | $D^3\times S^1$ | 2 | 3 |
| $S^1(\sqrt 2)\times\mathbb R^3$ | $S^1\times D^3$ | $D^2\times S^2$ | 1 | 2 |

The graphical descriptions on either side of the singularity specify the surgery in the actual flow. Corollary 1.3 gives a Morse description of the spacetime track if the flow has only spherical and nondegenerate cylindrical singularities before extinction. Corollaries 1.5–1.6 give handle descriptions conditional on that same singularity premise, with the compact-domain version also using convexity conditions. Conjecture 1.4 proposes the premise for generic flow from closed embedded initial data. A backward cylindrical tangent model by itself supplies neither the full-rank condition nor the ambient isotopy needed for recognition. The paper distinguishes the Morse model of the topology change from the actual time function, which fails to be $C^2$ there. [Primary text, Corollaries 1.3, 1.5–1.6 and Conjecture 1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.16678v1)
