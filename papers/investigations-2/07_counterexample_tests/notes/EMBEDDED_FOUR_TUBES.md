# Embedded upper bound with four prescribed paths

27 November 2026, America/New_York. For Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance. WIT-B05, selected lead, serving variant and effort unavailable. Checkout e171829, [native seams and chart](../01_examples/notes/E02_PUNCTURE_INPUT.md), [collar comparison](ONE_BELT_TRANSFER.md), [based genus bound](BASED_EXTERIOR.md) and [ambient path calculation](IMMERSED_EXTERIOR.md). Working geometric proof in the original smooth category.

Fix two distinct full sections of each cap (for example n = ±e₃), one of each sign, and an oriented signed chart knot K. Fix four rooted path classes, one to t=0 on each seam circle, in the order r,r⁻¹,s,s⁻¹. The least genus of a connected *embedded* exterior connector realizing those data is bounded by

    cl(W) ≤ g_emb ≤ g₄(K) + 2.

Here the basings are part of the surface data: disjoint rooted cuts with the given classes and ordering must exist. Boundary collars on all five circles use the original framing. The lower bound is the surface relation. We construct the upper bound.

Put a least-genus surface S for K in a thin collar of the marked coordinate sphere, with a product collar at K. For this step an ordinary ball surface in the standard ball can be made disjoint from a smaller central ball by moving a small ball in its complement there by a boundary-fixed isotopy; then rescale that shell in the native collar. Independently, in the outside collar of each original cap build an annulus Aᵢ joining its two seam circles. At the seam circle t ranges over S¹. In the other coordinates use an embedded arc from (0,n₋) to (0,n₊) in [0,η) × S² with interior at positive collar depth, running first and last along the collar coordinate. For example join two radial end segments by a simple spherical arc at depth η/2 and round the two bends. Take the product with S¹ in the native tube coordinates. Orient the annulus to give its specified opposite boundary signs. These three surfaces are pairwise disjoint and have disjoint embedded product ends.

Remove four small interior disks from S (the four source feet), all near a single boundary basepoint, and two small interior disks from each Aᵢ (the four destination feet). Each destination can be chosen close to its own t=0 rim point, away from the actual collar joins, with a short disjoint access arc to that rim. On S choose disjoint spokes to its four feet, rooted at the basepoint and ordered in its interior half-disk to give the specified boundary word. Starting with the three unpunctured surfaces, choose four embedded core arcs connecting the paired feet in the interior of the four-dimensional exterior. Each core may be chosen in any prescribed relative path class with the chosen endpoints. Indeed, lift the required projected path class with the fixed endpoints by the π₁ isomorphism, subtracting the projected spoke and end arc by path concatenation. Prescribe short core germs normal to the respective surfaces. Relative general position gives disjoint embedded arcs missing the three surfaces except at their designated endpoints: the relevant dimensions are 1+1<4 and 1+2<4. Work in a trimmed exterior to protect all product ends.

Along each core choose a thin solid D² × I with end disks on the feet, axis the core and interior missing all three original surfaces. In straight endpoint charts the disks are tangent to the original surfaces and the transverse core exits normally. Transport the disk planes down the normal three-bundle of the core. Its oriented two-frames form a connected space; either prescribed oriented endpoint frame can be reached by a path, with signs chosen to make the side cylinder join the punctured oriented surfaces. Tubular coordinates and compactness permit a common small radius, with disjoint supports away from the ends, and ordinary product-corner rounding at each rim. Feet and access arcs can be chosen at this final radius. Replace the eight disks by the four side cylinders. The class of each cut that follows its spoke, a longitude of its own tube, and its short access arc equals the assigned one: the longitude is homotopic to the core with the tiny end offsets. The four cuts avoid one another except at the root. A regular neighborhood in the resulting surface of the five boundary circles and this tree of cuts is planar with one additional boundary, so the root order gives the prescribed surface relation independently of the remaining handles.

The four tubes connect the three components. They subtract eight from the Euler characteristic while preserving all five exterior boundary circles. Thus χ = 1−2g₄(K)−8, giving genus g₄(K)+2. Reattaching the four original cap sections uses their product collars; each cap has exactly its two specified transverse belt points and the signed outer knot stays fixed.

In particular, once cl(W)=2 is certified for a specified path tuple, the embedded minimum for that tuple and **any standard slice K** is exactly two. The marked latitude unknot gives a concrete comparison. With an unmarked choice of paths this same unknot already bounds the embedded two-cap disk from WEX-E07. This result measures the cost of the prescribed exterior paths; it furnishes no strict saving in the unpenalized genus of a native knot.

## When one cap has the two trivial paths

For the family of projected paths (1,xᵐ,1,yⁿ), a cap with both paths trivial needs only one tube from S to its annulus. Place a single destination foot midway between its boundary circles, inside the rectangular strip with t near zero. Two disjoint arcs from the foot rim to the two t=0 seam points run through this strip. Its projection lies in the contractible central disk for sufficiently small |t|, by cᵢ(0)=0 and tube clearance. Bring two neighboring source spokes around the same foot and run them along parallel longitudes on the single tube. Choose their common core class to give the trivial based path. Choose end offsets and the angle of the tube frame so these two cuts reach their respective annular access arcs in order. Equivalently, in the strip put the two access endpoints at opposite points on the foot circle and rotate the chosen opposite source offsets to match them respecting the cylinder parametrization. The two spokes occupy neighboring positions at the root. All changes take place in the marked exterior. One such omission of a tube saves one genus, since it saves two disk removals and the three original surface components are still connected.

Thus for a standard slice K the embedded minimum at (0,0) is zero. At (m,0) with m nonzero or (0,n) with n nonzero it is exactly one: the single nontrivial commutator gives the lower bound, and the three-tube construction realizes it. The four-tube upper bound realizes two at (1,1) by the separate word certificate. These statements use existential cut systems with the given ordered projected classes; K and the four signed full sections are fixed throughout.
