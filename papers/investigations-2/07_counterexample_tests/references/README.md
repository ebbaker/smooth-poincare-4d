## Prior work on the marked genus test

27 November 2026, New York. Primary passages checked for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance. Culler, *Using surfaces to solve equations in free groups*, Topology 20 (1981), 133–145, §1.1 and Theorem 2.1, identifies the least genus of an orientable one-boundary surface mapping with a specified boundary word and computes it in a free group by orientable inverse-letter pairings. Remark 2.3(2) credits the reduced genus-one word form to Wicks (1962). The inverse-pairing enumeration in our word replay is an application of Culler's method. The project-specific step matches four signed physical sections and their ordered paths to that word, then realizes the lower bounds by embedded connectors in the given four-dimensional exterior. The search located no published calculation of the same three marked embedded minima. Priority for this particular application remains undetermined.
