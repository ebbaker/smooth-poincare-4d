**Fourfold test.** Golla–Pinzón-Caicedo, Theorem 1.2, gives nonzero class for \(Y=\Sigma_4(C)\) in smooth rational homology cobordism. Their statement is independent of Piccirillo's ordinary lower bound. Reversing the orientation of a model would negate the cobordism class and preserve nonvanishing. Reversing the knot orientation changes the meridian character by a unit of \(\mathbb Z/4\) and leaves the cyclic cover unchanged up to isomorphism. Thus the nonvanishing applies to the specific signed diagram fixed above, even if two authors use opposite ambient conventions. [Golla–Pinzón-Caicedo, Thm. 1.2 and §4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22378)

Here is the bridge to the actual filling. Suppose \(D\) is a smooth proper disk in a compact oriented smooth integral homology ball \(H\) with marked boundary \(S^3\), bounding \(C\). A product tube exists since \(D\) is contractible. Mayer–Vietoris on this tube and the closed exterior, with intersection \(D\times S^1\), gives \(H_1(\mathrm{ext}\,D;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z\) generated by the meridian. Reduce its character mod 4, take the connected cyclic cover, and complete it across the normal disks by \(z\mapsto z^4\). The cyclic cover is smooth with boundary \(Y\) under the marking. Factor it into two double branched covers, each along the same disk or its ramification disk.

For either double cover \(q:B'\to B\) with fixed disk \(A\), use an adapted cell structure, with branch cells fixed and other open cells lifting in pairs. Over \(\mathbb F_2\) there is a short exact sequence
\[
0\longrightarrow C_*(B,A)\xrightarrow{\mathrm{sum\ of\ lifts}}
C_*(B')\xrightarrow{q_*} C_*(B)\longrightarrow0.
\]
The paired boundaries cancel on fixed cells, and the two lifts of each nonbranch cell sum to the kernel generator there. If \(B\) has mod-two homology of a point then \(C_*(B,A)\) is acyclic, as \(A\) is a disk. The same conclusion follows for \(B'\). Induction gives a mod-two homology ball upstairs in the fourfold cover, hence a rational homology ball by finite generation. Puncturing it would give a rational homology cobordism of \(Y\) to \(S^3\), contradicting the imported theorem. Nonvanishing of the correction term and the computation in the cited preprint are imports.

Consequently \(g_H(C)=1\) for **every** such \(H\). The upper bound in general follows by capping the unknot end of the genus-one crossing cobordism by an unknot disk pushed into the collar. In the chosen \(W\) one can use the actual WEX disk, with one transverse point of each sign on each belt. \(W\) has the required integral homology by excision of the coordinate ball. An embedded connector with the four sections and domain genus zero is excluded for every choice of paths. No budget for a search over Whitney disks affects this conclusion. For a different prospective disk the fourfold branched cover bounding a rational ball is a necessary filter; passing it would leave the embedding question open.
