The norm J integrates out both the position of the error and its value in the fibre. The projected derivative in DIR-E12 already identifies one norm of that jet with Weyl energy. Two other operations are cheap once the marked data exist: characteristic insertion on the space of solutions of one fixed metric, and a commutator of two positive-spin transports. The projective parameter for a quaternionic two-dimensional solution space has real dimension four, exactly the degree of a point insertion. Choose the quotient by constant right unit quaternions, with its specified action on fields. A dynamical gauge quotient would be a different experiment. For transport compare first the closed smooth metric, then the singular scale selected by a finite-cost spinor. A topological holonomy obstruction from the closed bundle could disappear after blowing up the zero.

Test finite-type uniqueness of this perturbed twistor equation before inferring local invertibility of the parameter map. Test minimization with L²-orthonormal generators as well as a fixed pencil.
