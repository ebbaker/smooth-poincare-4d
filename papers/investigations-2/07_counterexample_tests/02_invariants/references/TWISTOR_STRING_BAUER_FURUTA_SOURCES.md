| V. P. Nair and J. Schiff, *Topological Gauge Theory and Twistors*, Phys. Lett. B **233** (1989), 343–348. Author preprint CU-TP-439, July 1989. `NairSchiff1989`. [Author PDF](https://u.math.biu.ac.il/~schiff/Papers/pap1.pdf) | Equations (11), (17)–(18) give a chiral superspace action for topological Yang–Mills and identify the point insertion with the Donaldson–Witten scalar observable. The final paragraph gives the conformally flat extension and states the limitation concerning arbitrary metric variations. This is a field-theory construction predating Witten’s twistor string. |
| E. Witten, *Perturbative Gauge Theory as a String Theory in Twistor Space*, hep-th/0312171v2, 6 October 2004. `Witten2004TwistorString`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0312171v2) | §4, especially (4.18)–(4.19) and the discussion of D-instantons. The open B-model on complex supertwistor space has holomorphic Chern–Simons fields. The curve expansion is proposed to recover perturbative spacetime amplitudes. |
| N. Berkovits and E. Witten, *Conformal Supergravity in Twistor-String Theory*, hep-th/0406051v2, 26 July 2004. `BerkovitsWitten2004`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0406051v2) | Abstract and §1, especially preprint p. 2. The conformal-supergravity states of the original models interact at the Yang–Mills coupling and contribute beyond the pure-gauge tree amplitudes. |
| A. D. Popov and M. Wolf, *Topological B-Model on Weighted Projective Spaces and Self-Dual Models in Four Dimensions*, hep-th/0406224v2, 15 September 2004. `PopovWolf2004`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0406224v2) | §4, equations (4.16)–(4.17) and paragraph immediately following. The weighted model with fermionic weights (4,0) yields a classical self-dual truncation of topological Yang–Mills in Euclidean four-space. The fields include an odd one-form and an odd anti-self-dual two-form in the authors’ convention. |
| L. J. Mason, *Twistor Actions for Non-Self-Dual Fields; a New Foundation for Twistor-String Theory*, hep-th/0507269v2, 2 September 2005. `Mason2005TwistorActions`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0507269v2) | Abstract and opening of §4. The conformal-gravity action uses a variable almost-complex structure on a six-manifold modeled there on R⁴ × S². This is a precedent for allowing nonintegrability off shell. The derivation of scattering generating functions has tree-level scope. |
| R. Boels, L. Mason and D. Skinner, *Supersymmetric Gauge Theories in Twistor Space*, hep-th/0604040v1, 6 April 2006. `BoelsMasonSkinner2006`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0604040v1) | Abstract and §3, equations (3.2), (3.4)–(3.5). Holomorphic Chern–Simons plus an integral of a log determinant along Euclidean supertwistor lines gives a gauge-theory action perturbatively equivalent to spacetime N=4 SYM. The independent gauge action omits the conformal-supergravity sector of the string. |
| N. Seiberg and E. Witten, *Monopoles, Duality and Chiral Symmetry Breaking in N=2 Supersymmetric QCD*, hep-th/9408099v1, 17 August 1994. `SeibergWitten1994Matter`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9408099v1) | Introduction and §16, equation (16.24). In the SU(2) theory a mass for the adjoint hypermultiplet breaks N=4 to N=2. The authors give the curve for the low-energy mass-deformed theory. This is a four-dimensional gauge-theory operation. A corresponding global twistor-string model on a smooth sphere would require a separate derivation. |
| E. Witten, *Monopoles and Four-Manifolds*, hep-th/9411102v1, 15 November 1994. `Witten1994Monopoles`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9411102v1) | §§1–2. The twisted N=2 theory uses an infrared description near a monopole singularity by an abelian gauge field and monopole matter. The resulting equations couple a Dirac spinor to self-dual curvature. |
| C. Vafa and E. Witten, *A Strong Coupling Test of S-Duality*, hep-th/9408074v2, 1 September 1994. `VafaWitten1994`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9408074v2) | Abstract and §2. A different spacetime twist starts with N=4 SYM and studies its partition function. The simplification to an Euler characteristic has additional vanishing hypotheses, discussed for Kähler metrics of nonnegative scalar curvature with gauge group locally a product of SU(2)s. |
| S. Bauer and M. Furuta, *A Stable Cohomotopy Refinement of Seiberg–Witten Invariants: I*, math/0204340v1, 29 April 2002. `BauerFuruta2002I`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204340v1) | Theorem 1.1 for closed oriented smooth spinᶜ four-manifolds gives the metric-independent circle-equivariant stable class. The passage to the integer SW invariant in that theorem requires a homology orientation and b₂⁺ > b₁ + 1. §2, Theorem 2.6 and its proof, and §3, Proposition 3.1, supply finite-dimensional approximation, homotopy over an interval and bounded inverse images in the specified Sobolev spaces. |
| S. Bauer, *A Stable Cohomotopy Refinement of Seiberg–Witten Invariants: II*, math/0204267v1, 22 April 2002. `Bauer2002II`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204267v1) | Theorem 1.1 gives the connected-sum smash product. Proposition 2.3, preprint p. 6, gives the identity for every spinᶜ structure on a connected closed oriented smooth four-manifold with b₁ = b₂ = 0. The fixed-point map is the linear isomorphism on the one-form summand. The computation concerns the ordinary single-manifold circle-equivariant class. |
| C. Gerig, *No Homotopy 4-Sphere Invariants Using ECH = SWF*, arXiv:1905.10938v3, 9 November 2020. `Gerig2021NoHomotopySphereInvariants`. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.10938v3) | Main Results 1–2 and Theorem 6.2. On the asymptotically standard punctured smooth homotopy sphere, carry the standing assumption of **no twisted zero-circles** in the curve count. The ECH class paired in the indicated grading gives the common relative SW value 1 modulo 2. Example 1.7 exhibits a single twisted circle in an asymptotically standard model on standard R⁴. The [existing 07 receipt](../../references/README.md) uses the distinct existence theorem 1.6. |
