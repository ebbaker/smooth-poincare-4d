André Guerra, Xavier Lamy and Konstantinos Zemas, *Sharp quantitative stability of the Möbius group among sphere-valued maps in arbitrary dimension*, arXiv:2305.19886v1, 31 May 2023. Checked in the [original author text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.19886). Theorem 1.1, equations (1.4)–(1.5), Remark 1.3, and the centering step in Lemma 2.5. Specialize n=5. Round source and round target S⁴, Sobolev W¹,⁴ map of degree +1 (degree −1 by target reflection), probability surface measure, real HS tangential derivative in the common ambient ℝ⁵. The fourth power of the derivative distance to an appropriately oriented Möbius map is controlled by a dimensional constant times the round quartic deficit. The theorem gives no numerical value of that constant in this application. Its centering by conformal reparametrization of the domain does not implement the WIN constraint against a prescribed compact metric volume.

E23 tests converting the round deficit to the actual distortion and exhibits a round Möbius control with zero round deficit but positive metric deficit. For the effective lower bound B08 derives the weaker L² derivative estimate it needs directly with explicit constants and a measured mean.
