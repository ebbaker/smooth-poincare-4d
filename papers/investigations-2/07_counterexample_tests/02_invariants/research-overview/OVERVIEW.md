## The punctured genus profile

Fix a closed connected oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere Σ. Remove the interior of a small parametrized coordinate ball and use the outward-normal-first boundary of the complement. Fix a reflection in the model identification if required to compare this boundary with the oriented standard S³. Transport the same convention to every chart, and track mirroring and knot orientation. Write \(g_\Sigma(K)\) for the proposed minimum of the genus of a connected orientable compact smooth proper surface with the single specified boundary K over these punctures and surfaces. At threshold h, the set of knots with \(g_\Sigma(K)\le h\) records which knots they span within the genus budget. On homotopy spheres the relative second homology of the puncture vanishes. Extending the comparison to a manifold with second homology requires specifying the surface class.

The standard calibration is \(g_{S^4}(K)=g_4(K)\). Audit the coordinate-ball restriction, boundary identifications and transport under diffeomorphism before using the proposed function. For a fixed probe K, a verified surface yields \(g_\Sigma(K)\le g\), and an applicable standard-ball theorem yields \(g_4(K)\ge L\). A strict gap \(L>g\) then distinguishes Σ from S⁴. Rasmussen's rational invariant supplies the candidate bound \(L=|s(K)|/2\). A lower bound valid in *every* integral homology ball constrains the same surface in the candidate. A failure on one knot leaves the rest of the profile open. Manolescu–Marengon–Piccirillo's Corollary 1.4 distinguishes two specified homeomorphic indefinite four-manifolds by null-homologous sliceness of the trefoil. Cochran–Harvey's Definition 3.8 allows all smooth manifolds homeomorphic to B⁴ in its minimization. ## Two ways of retaining pole data

For a smooth Riemannian conformal class c and pole p, use A1's rank-three residue space \(U=\Lambda^-_p\) with its conformal inner product, and the normalized closed self-dual form \(\omega_{p,u}\) on \(\Sigma\setminus\{p\}\). The inverted end has the manifold orientation. The leading form is nonzero for \(u\ne0\). A2 identifies a sphere carrying a zero-free pole form with the standard sphere. These statements enter at the evidence levels of investigation 01. [A1](../../../01_chiral_transfer_and_pole_forms/notes/E01_A1.md) · [A2](../../../01_chiral_transfer_and_pole_forms/notes/E02_A2.md)

* Vary \((c,p,[u])\) over all triples with transverse pole form and minimize the number of connected components of its zero locus. This counts a compact one-manifold, possibly empty. A zero-free form is vacuously transverse. Leave the count undefined on a sphere where the admissible domain is empty. The first decision is whether Perutz's relative mergers and E04's end realization reduce every positive finite count to one across allowed markings. Check that the operation fixes a sufficiently far end, retains closedness and transverse positive first jets, and yields a smooth conformal structure on the original compactification with the stated normalized residue. Even a successful collapse on the nonempty domain would give no new procedure for bounding the count below across all choices. * Fix \((\Sigma,c,p)\) and set \(D=\{[u]\in\mathbb P(U):\omega_{p,u}(q)=0\ \text{for some }q\ne p\}\). Branch 02 obtains D as a compact projection including directions contributed by lower-rank fibres. Measure it with the rotation-invariant projective probability measure from the conformal inner product. The set itself and its normalized area are candidates for comparison up to conformal equivalence of marked objects. Check naturality under change of residue frame and marked pullback. The round class gives D empty. A certified subset of D or its complement with positive area supplies a one-sided bound even when the actual area is unknown. A signed trace estimate for a single component on a cap supplies neither inclusion. Restriction to a chosen metric can retain information erased by minimization. To descend such a statistic to smooth type, specify an intrinsic family of markings, establish its existence and naturality, and control the statistic throughout that family or prove a choice-independence theorem. If the admissible family is all classes and poles, A2 would force D to be the whole plane on an exotic sphere, while the round class makes its area zero on S⁴. This suggests a recognition reformulation for the unrestricted infimum. No uniform exclusion or new descent method is supplied here.
