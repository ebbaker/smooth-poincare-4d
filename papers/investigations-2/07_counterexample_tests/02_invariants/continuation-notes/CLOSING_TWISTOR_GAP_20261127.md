Two smooth positive spinors that generate the fibre define a map f from the four-manifold to HP¹: at x take the line of coefficients k for which the combined field s_k vanishes. Let E be pointwise evaluation and k unit on that line. The number
\[
 R=\int_M |(P s_k)(x)|^4/\|E_x\|^4\,dV_g
\]
uses the derivative with k held constant. The normalized angular average of the ordinary fourth-power defect diverges as 12R log(1/ε)+O(1). The Chern point count gives one on every smooth type, whereas R retains a first derivative at each of the counted points. [Angular derivation](../notes/E14_ANGULAR_RESIDUE.md) · [Point and evaluation bundle](../reviews/E09_INSERTION.md).

Center the kernel map by ∫ f dV=0 in the unit sphere S⁴⊂ℝ⁵, and let b(g) be the infimum of √R over all such L²-orthonormal generating pairs. There are such pairs on every smooth type. **At fixed g, b(g) vanishes exactly for the conformally round class, and then zero is attained.** Nonzero Weyl curvature at one point certifies b(g)>0. An explicit lower bound for L=λ₁(g)√Vol(g) is (L²−16Vol(S⁴))₊/(408L). Both the positivity from the one-point test and the explicit spectral lower bound hold for every permitted pencil on that representative. [Compactness proof and replay](../reviews/E21_FIXED_METRIC_GAP.md) · [Spectral proof and replay](../reviews/E17_BALANCED_SPECTRUM.md).

The controls locate the limitation for smooth type. Without centering, orthonormal generating pairs already have infimum residue zero at **each fixed metric**. With centering, the gap is uniform under conformal changes whose normalized volumes remain uniformly absolutely continuous. Allow all representatives of any one class and the infimum is zero again. Allow all metrics on one smooth type and the supremum is infinite, even at unit volume. DIR-E33 on standard S⁴ has positive fixed-metric gap and the round representative has zero. No quantified estimate for the positive E33 gap follows from the compactness proof alone.
