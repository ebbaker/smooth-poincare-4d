# A positive centered twistor gap on every fixed non-conformally-round metric

27 November 2026 (America/New_York). For Edward B. Baker III, with OpenAI Codex assistance. WIN-B07, lead proof revision 1. Selected GPT-6.2 Astra/ultra, serving settings unavailable. Baseline 86b24d9f9c2d and the frozen [determinant estimate](BALANCED_SPECTRAL_BOUND.md), [volume comparisons](CENTERED_EXTREMA.md) and [two primary-source requests](../research-progress/handoffs/REQUEST_FIXED_METRIC_GAP_R1.md). Here b(g) is the infimum of √R over volume-centered L²-orthonormal generating pairs, with HP¹ identified with the unit round S⁴.

**Fixed-metric conclusion.** For every smooth Riemannian metric on any closed connected oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere,
\[
 b(g)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (M,[g])\ \text{is conformally diffeomorphic to the round sphere}.
\]
In the zero case a smooth centered pair attains the infimum. A nonzero Weyl tensor at one point therefore certifies **strict positivity on this fixed metric for every centered pencil**, with no numerical size asserted. This applies, in particular, to the explicitly perturbed standard-sphere metric of DIR-E33 with W⁺(0)≠0. The spectral estimate supplies a numerical function of a verified eigenvalue lower bound when its threshold is exceeded. [E33 curvature control](E10_ROUND_INSERTION.md)

**Uniformity in the volume measure.** Fix a smooth non-conformally-round representative g. Let h range over a nonempty family of smooth representatives in [g] for which the normalized volume measures μ_h=dV_h/Vol(h) are uniformly absolutely continuous relative to dV_g. This means that for each η>0 some δ>0 works for the whole family: Vol_g(A)<δ implies μ_h(A)<η for all measurable A. Then inf_h b(h)>0. Bounded normalized volume densities in any fixed Lᵖ(dV_g), p>1, suffice by Hölder. The [zero infimum over an unrestricted conformal class](CENTERED_EXTREMA.md) therefore requires a sequence of normalized volumes violating this condition on a non-conformally-round class.

**Compactness argument.** Suppose smooth centered pencils in such representatives have R_j→0, or consider simply a sequence for the one fixed g. In the former case transport the fields to g by the conformal twistor identification, keeping their parameter frames. The kernel maps f_j:M→S⁴ and R_j are unchanged; on g the transported generators need not be L²-orthonormal. The pointwise jet and degree estimates used below require only fibre generation. Set A_j=df_j and H_j=(∫|A_j|⁴ dV_g)¹ᐟ². In the [spectral calculation](BALANCED_SPECTRAL_BOUND.md) the determinant estimate and its integral precede the Rayleigh inequality. With the common orientation sign σ they give
\[
 H_j^2\le16 V_0+408 H_j\sqrt{R_j},\qquad
 0\le\int_M (|df_j|^4-16\sigma J_{f_j})\,dV_g\le408 H_j\sqrt{R_j}.
\]
In particular H_j stays bounded and the oriented conformality deficit tends to zero. The fixed sign can equivalently be chosen along a subsequence since there are only two signs. Extract a weak W¹,⁴(M,ℝ⁵) subsequence with strong L² and almost-everywhere limit f. Its values lie in the unit sphere almost everywhere. If f were a constant z, then ∫|f_j−z| dμ_j→0: split into the set where |f_j−z|>η and its complement, using boundedness by 2 and the uniform absolute continuity on the first set. Each ∫ f_j dμ_j=0, contradicting |z|=1. For a fixed measure strong L² alone suffices for this step.

In an oriented coordinate chart let G denote the metric matrix and v=√det G. For z∈ℝ⁵ set z'=z/max(1,|z|) and use the integrand on five-by-four derivative matrices D
\[
  v\,|D G^{-1/2}|_{\rm HS}^4-16\sigma\det[z',D_1,D_2,D_3,D_4].
\]
The product-of-singular-values bound makes it nonnegative, including off the unit sphere. At fixed x,z it is a convex function of D plus a linear combination of its maximal minors. Each such minor is a null Lagrangian, by expanding a determinant of exact differentials and integrating a compactly supported variation by parts. This yields quasiconvexity. Continuity and fourth-power growth on each compact chart follow from the smooth positive metric and the clipping. Localize by a smooth nonnegative partition of unity. Acerbi–Fusco, Theorem II.4, now gives weak sequential lower semicontinuity for these nonnegative coordinate integrals. On the sphere their sum is exactly the deficit integral. Its limit inferior is zero, so the deficit density of f vanishes a.e. The sphere constraint gives f·df=0 a.e.; the equality case of arithmetic-geometric mean on four singular values now says df is a similarity of sign σ or is zero a.e. This argument needs no weak continuity of the degree or of the unweighted pulled-back target volume. [Primary hypotheses and localization](E19_WEAK_DEFICIT.md)

Reflect the unit target if needed. The nonconstant limit then has positive Jacobian or zero derivative a.e. Goldstein–Hajłasz–Pakzad, Theorem 4, gives a continuous representative for this extrinsic W¹,⁴ map between smooth oriented four-manifolds without boundary with compact target. Continuity makes it localizable. The fixed sign and equal singular values, including their simultaneous vanishing, satisfy Liimatainen–Salo's Definition 2 of Riemannian 1-quasiregularity. Use the version with nonnegative or nonpositive Jacobian a.e. Their Theorem 4.4 gives a smooth conformal local diffeomorphism (apply successively to smooth metrics). Compactness, connectedness and simple connectivity of the target turn this into a global conformal diffeomorphism. This contradicts the non-conformally-round hypothesis and proves both gaps. In the conformally round case the weak map obtained in this proof is itself a conformal diffeomorphism, with ∫|df|⁴=16V₀. The lower bound by degree on the original sequence and the upper bound above then show convergence of the L⁴ norms of the derivatives. Uniform convexity upgrades weak to strong convergence of df along this subsequence. No convergence of spinor frames is claimed. [Continuity and regularity hypotheses](E20_REGULARITY_AND_CENTER.md)

**Attainment on every representative in the round conformal class.** Transport the exact round pencil to g. Its kernel map f₀ is a conformal diffeomorphism, although its coordinate averages against dV_g can be nonzero. Center (f₀)_*dV_g by a conformal transformation of the target as in E16. The induced source diffeomorphism f₀⁻¹∘T∘f₀ is conformal. Spin pullback of the pencil followed by conformal transport back to g gives exact twistor sections with that centered kernel map (equivalently use the corresponding projective coefficient change). Apply the common smooth normalization to impose the L² Gram condition; R remains zero. This argument uses a compatible lift: an arbitrary pencil with the same kernel map can have positive R. [Standard centering](E16_SPECTRAL_CONTROL.md) · [Round control and compatible lift](E20_REGULARITY_AND_CENTER.md) · [Individual-pencil caveat](../reviews/E09_RIGIDITY.md).

On every hypothetical exotic sphere each fixed g thus has b(g)>0, but every conformal class there would still contain a sequence of metrics with b tending to zero. On standard S⁴ both an exact zero and positive values occur. The sign tests the conformal class, while the positive magnitude depends on the representative. No minimizer at a positive infimum or explicit gap on E33 is obtained here.
