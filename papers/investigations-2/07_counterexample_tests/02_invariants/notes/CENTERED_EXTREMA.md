# Centering, change of volume, and the two smooth-type extrema

27 November 2026 (New York). For Edward B. Baker III, with OpenAI Codex assistance. Lead B06 proof, revision 1, checkout 86b24d9f9c2d. Selected Astra/ultra; exact serving settings unavailable. All metrics and fields smooth. Use [R and conformal transport](PENCIL_RIGIDITY.md), [the concentrating sequence](PENCIL_CONCENTRATION.md), [degree and pullback](E13_SPINOR_PENCIL.md) and the [fixed-metric estimate](BALANCED_SPECTRAL_BOUND.md).

For a fixed metric g let b(g) be the infimum of √R over volume-centered generating pencils with quaternionic L²-orthonormal generators. Define centering by the unit-radius Hopf kernel map f satisfying ∫f dV_g=0. On any smooth oriented homotopy four-sphere,
\[
  0\le b(g)<\infty,\qquad
  \inf_{h\in[g]} b(h)=0,\qquad
  \sup_{g\ \mathrm{smooth}}b(g)=+\infty .
\]
The middle infimum can use representatives of a prescribed total volume. A pair achieving R=0 exists in the conformal class exactly when it is conformally round. Whether the infimum on one fixed low-eigenvalue nonround representative is attained or even positive is a separate question.

**Center any surjective parameter map by changing density.** For smooth surjective f from the compact base to S⁴ let Z(a)=∫ exp(a·f)dV_g on ℝ⁵. It tends to infinity as |a| tends to infinity. For a unit v, f⁻¹{z:v·z>3/4} contains a nonempty open set of positive measure; the scalar product there stays above 1/2 for nearby directions. Finitely many such direction neighborhoods give one positive minimum of the corresponding volumes. This bounds Z(a) below by a constant times exp(|a|/2). At a minimizer, ∇Z=∫ f exp(a·f)dV_g=0. Normalize w=const exp(a·f)>0 so ∫w dV_g equals the desired volume. Then f is centered for w dV_g. Nonzero degree ensures surjectivity for every pencil under study.

**Normalize the fields after centering.** The rank-one projector E*E/m² is I minus the projector onto K. The traceless part of either projector is a fixed linear encoding, up to sign and scale, of the five Hopf coordinates of K. Consequently ∫E*E/m² dV=(V/2)I in the centered measure. Divide both fields by m and multiply by √(2/V) to obtain L²-orthonormal generators for that metric. This common positive smooth factor preserves both R and f.

**Nonempty domain on each metric.** Begin with any smooth generating pair, which exists by [E13](E13_SPINOR_PENCIL.md). Choose the centered measure w dV with mass equal to that of dV. The volume-form isotopy gives φ isotopic to the identity with φ_*(dV)=w dV, so f∘φ is centered for the original g. Indeed interpolate the two positive top forms ω_t with equal integrals, write their difference dα by top-degree de Rham cohomology, and solve ι_{X_t} ω_t=−α. Differentiation of the pullback by its time-t flow gives zero; compactness supplies the full time interval. This is Moser's argument in §4, Theorem 2, pp. 292–293. Parallel transport in the quaternionic line S⁺ along the isotopy identifies the evaluation bundle at φ(x) with the fibre at x. The pulled-back pair has kernel f∘φ. Its normalized generators have finite R by smoothness and m>0. This application asserts existence only; arbitrary volume transport need not preserve the residue in the fixed metric. [Primary text](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~dafr/M392C-2018-MorseTheory/Readings/Moser.pdf)

**Infimum within a conformal class.** Start with the sequence in the fixed-metric concentration proof. For each of its kernel maps choose a centering w as above. Use h=w¹ᐟ²g, so dV_h=w dV_g. Under conformal twistor transport s↦w¹ᐟ⁸βs the kernel map stays fixed and |Bk|/m scales by w⁻¹ᐟ⁴. Hence R is unchanged. The common normalization after centering preserves it again. This gives the stated zero infimum for each conformal class and fixed prescribed volume, with no lower bound needed on the new densities. Rigidity gives the necessity for actual zero; on a conformally round class one can choose its round representative and exact centered round pencil.

**Supremum across metrics.** Colbois and Dodziuk's Theorem 1 supplies smooth metrics of volume one with unbounded first positive scalar eigenvalue on every closed connected smooth four-manifold here. Their proof takes a connected sum with the standard sphere; the smooth type is fixed. Apply the fixed-metric bound, valid for every member of the nonempty centered family. Its right side tends to infinity with λ₁. The bound is compatible with the zero infimum within each individual conformal class because its volume measure changes with the representative. [Published Theorem 1, p. 905 and proof pp. 905–906](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Jozef-Dodziuk/publication/265433300_Riemannian_Metrics_with_Large_l_1/links/54e5f64e0cf2cd2e028b542e/Riemannian-Metrics-with-Large-l-1.pdf)

Both extrema are thus identical on every smooth type under consideration. The spectral bound remains a test on a supplied compact metric. A route retaining sensitivity must specify which geometry or field equation prevents the allowed changes of volume and pencils, or must use information beyond these two extrema.
