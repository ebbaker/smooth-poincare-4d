### Designing a twistor observable beyond the scalar defect

WIN-B05 should compare the all-class infimum with observables that retain the order of transport or vary over a family of fields. A small first trial needs only two based loops and a supplied chiral spin connection. Compute \(\operatorname{tr}(H_1H_2H_1^{-1}H_2^{-1})\) for their holonomies in the defining \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) representation. Two loops contracting inside a flat patch give 2. Test a metric perturbation and a change of loop representatives on that same smooth input. A change of value identifies precisely which geometric data must still be retained. The field-theory proposal has three further steps.

1. **Choose the fields and test one point insertion.** Start with real Riemannian chiral data available for any metric on the candidate. Specify which fields vary, the gauge symmetries, and the equations defining the proposed moduli space \(\mathcal M\). For a cohomological field theory, identify a differential \(Q\) squaring to zero on gauge-invariant observables and a candidate insertion closed under \(Q\). For the characteristic-class construction, determine whether a universal bundle descends to the quotient, taking stabilizers into account. Framing every field at the insertion point trivializes the evaluated bundle there, so specify how that frame is forgotten. If a universal bundle descends, test \(\mu_x=c_2(\mathcal E|_{\{x\}\times\mathcal M})\) against the pullback control in the [overview](../research-overview/OVERVIEW.md). A homotopy sphere has no nonzero two-dimensional homology class for a surface insertion. Point insertions remain available. A numerical pairing of \(k\) point classes needs a cycle of dimension \(4k\), an orientation and control of its compactification.
2. **Vary the metric on a known smooth sphere.** Use the round metric and the repo's perturbation on the same \(S^4\). The round metric carries nontrivial chiral twistor spinors and the non-conformally-round perturbation loses that kernel. A count based on \(P_g\psi=0\) must explain this change. Test whether the insertion moves by a \(Q\)-exact term or extends across a compact oriented family along the metric path. Check contributions at degenerations before inferring equality of the two values. [Exact recognition and perturbation](../../../../investigations-1/03_first_order_twistor/02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/reviews/DIR_B19_GREEN_BOUNDARY_ASSESSMENT_20261124.md)
3. **Test the filling and its boundary state.** If the preceding construction supplies a relative theory, WIN-E08 can test its cylinder map and the gluing of two standard balls with one insertion. Change the boundary spin frame by degree one and reglue with both boundary orientations. The known shifts of the relative Chern numbers must cancel. Determine whether the insertion still affects the glued value. A claim for the closed smooth sphere additionally needs independence under a change of cut, the applicable 06 exclusions, and a way to evaluate or bound the surviving observable on a handle presentation. [Relative Chern calculation, (2)–(4)](../../../../investigations-1/03_first_order_twistor/02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/01_dirac_collar_matching/notes/E12_RELATIVE_CHIRAL_CHARGE_20261125.md) · [06 field-theory audit](../../../06_invariants_and_candidates/research-progress/handoffs/RETURN_INV_E01_20261127.md)

If the proposed check still requires minimizing over all surfaces or metrics, identify what bound would make that minimization avoidable.
