## Off shell and the cost of a comparison

The nonround E33 standard sphere obstructs the ordinary canonical Dolbeault differential. A spacetime complex can still close there. In the chosen five-field abelian algebra \(Q^2=\delta_\phi\), the point insertion \(\phi(p)^2\) represents a nonzero polynomial cohomology class, and the self-dual curvature dependence in the gauge fermion has \(Q\)-exact first metric variation. Evaluating on a flat connection and constant \(\phi=c\) detects the polynomial as \(c^2\). This is also why obtaining a number requires additional input: the action does not damp the scalar, the constant gauge circle remains, and the real auxiliary-field Gaussian has the wrong sign for convergence. The first open statement for a numerical correlation is the existence of a regulated, gauge-compatible pushforward with a justified Ward identity and a specified residual scalar pairing. A further choice would have to make such a number depend on the filling or smooth type. A flat-space perturbative correspondence and a local infrared change of variables currently give no global real map carrying the specified insertion through both descriptions.
