## Closed smooth-source estimate and connected sum

Use the curvature normalization of Bauer–Furuta I §3 and a flat reference spinᶜ connection $A_0$. Over the one-point Picard torus the monopole map takes the form

$$\mu(\psi,a)=\big(D_{A_0+a}\psi,\ d^+a-\sigma(\psi),\ d^*a\big),$$

where the harmonic one-form summand is zero and the scalar target is taken modulo constants. Here $\sigma$ denotes the spinor quadratic map. Complete the domain in $L^2_k$ and target in $L^2_{k-1}$, integer $k>4$ as in I §3. The linear part is $D_{A_0}\oplus(d^+\oplus d^*)$. The residual $S^1$ acts with weight one on spinors, trivially on the form summands. On fixed points, $d^+\oplus d^*$ has kernel $H^1=0$ and cokernel $H^{2,+}=0$ with constants removed from the scalar target. The Dirac index is $(c_1(\mathfrak s_0)^2-\sigma(X))/8=0$. Restriction to fixed points identifies the degree-zero group with $\mathbb Z$ in Bauer's universe, and the linear isomorphism represents $+1$. [Bauer–Furuta I §3 and Prop. 3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204340) · [Bauer II Prop. 2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204267)

For one fixed smooth self-dual source $\eta$ in the same target convention, set $v=(0,\eta,0)$ and $\mu_t=\mu-tv$, $0\le t\le1$. This keeps the Fredholm part and adds a compact constant perturbation. The full homotopy is equivariant because $v$ lies in the fixed subspace. If $\|\mu_t(x)\|_{k-1}\le R$, then

$$\|\mu(x)\|_{k-1}\le R+\|\eta\|_{k-1},\qquad
\|x\|_k\le C_{g,A_0,k}\big(R+\|\eta\|_{k-1}\big),$$

where $C_{g,A_0,k}(S)$ is a radius given by Proposition 3.1 for the target ball of radius $S$. This bounds inverse images uniformly along the interval. With the same Hilbert bundles pulled back to $[0,1]$, Theorem 2.6 and its interval restriction argument give $[\mu_1]=[\mu]$. On the fixed locus the unique reducible is translated to the unique Coulomb solution $d^+a=t\eta$. The estimate depends on the norm of the source and the fixed geometry. A singular source, or a concentrating family lacking a bound in the target norm, needs an admissible domain and a fresh bound before the argument can be used there. [Bauer–Furuta I Thm. 2.6 and proof, §3 Prop. 3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204340)

Bauer II Thm. 1.1 identifies the class of a closed oriented connected sum with the smash product for the induced spinᶜ structures, with the diagonal circle. Thus for any closed oriented spinᶜ summand $(Y,\mathfrak t)$ the $X$ factor acts as the unit under that identification. This gluing uses a closed connected sum with an $S^3$ neck. A relative state for a marked $S^1\times S^2$ cut requires its own construction. [Bauer II Thm. 1.1 and §2, Prop. 2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204267)
