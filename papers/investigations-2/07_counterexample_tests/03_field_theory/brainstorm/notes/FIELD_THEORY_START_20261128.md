## Start at the tube boundary

Take the twisted zero-circle in Gerig's standard $\mathbb R^4$ example as
the initial geometric control. Remove a small tube and truncate at a distant
$S^3$ using the specified asymptotically standard end. Work in smooth
oriented Riemannian signature with the spin-induced $\mathrm{spin}^c$
structure, a fixed pole end and the self-dual convention for the monopole
curvature equation. Keep the zero-circle's negative normal eigenline $L^-$
as a marking. The normal rank-three bundle is oriented and trivial, even
though $L^-$ is Möbius. The actual tube and its inherited spin restriction
are part of the input. The local model and the zero-free standard end are
separate controls. [Gerig, Example 1.7](../../../02_invariants/references/TWISTOR_STRING_BAUER_FURUTA_SOURCES.md) · [First-jet and end data](../../../../02_zero_circles/notes/E03_LOCAL_SOURCES.md)

Begin with the ordinary abelian monopole deformation complex in Coulomb
gauge at the reducible with the trivial determinant connection and zero
spinor. In the reference model use zero curvature perturbation, a
product-collar metric at the cut, and compatible spin restrictions on both
sides. The linear operator to specify is the block $d^*\oplus d^+$ on
imaginary one-forms and the chiral Dirac block on positive spinors, with
infinitesimal gauge, residual scalars and boundary conditions recorded.
Use spectral boundary projections for the linear Fredholm control.
Specify the projection on boundary zero modes and the complementary
conditions or Cauchy-data matching for the two sides. At the outer
$S^3$, keep the end data fixed during the tube experiment and use
compatible boundary data with a standard cap in a closed comparison.
Use the outward-normal-first convention, which gives opposite signs for
the two induced orientations of the same cut. Work over real gauge forms
and complex spinors at this stage. A coefficient system for a state
will be a subsequent choice.

Use the local flat twisted reference obtained from a normal ball over an
interval by the rotation $(x_1,x_2,x_3)\mapsto(x_1,-x_2,-x_3)$ at the
ends, with the negative line along $x_3$, and the untwisted reference with
identity monodromy. Specify the spin lifts by restriction from the
chosen tube before comparing the two. The first check is how the boundary
spin and gauge fields and the marked eigenline jointly transform under a
change of normal frame. A change of spin trivialization can alter the
description of the spectrum. A marking can affect a proposed defect
sector only through the way it enters the fields, symmetries or
admissible boundary conditions. The flat models calibrate the tube;
justify any identification of the original form with the flat
expression separately. Use the signed local model or an
allowed deformation where the source warrants it.

The first experiment ends with the linear data and the gluing comparison
for that single standard tube. Test a cylinder, undo a change of frame
and glue back the actual standard tube to test a proposed relative
state. The zero-free round standard sphere is the control for
forgetting the circle. A two-ball gluing with one point insertion
provides a separate calibration if the chosen theory defines that
insertion and its pairing. A marked boundary signal gives a relative
question. A smooth-type observable needs metric, pole, residue and cut
independence or a comparison valid over the full admitted range.
A continuation across circle birth and disappearance must specify its
deformation, hypotheses and compactness. [Earlier experiment proposal](../../../02_invariants/brainstorm/notes/TWISTOR_STRING_AND_MONOPOLE_MAP.md#a-test-at-the-twisted-circle)

## Decide what could change the closed calculation

The ordinary circle-equivariant Bauer–Furuta class has the common unit
value on all oriented homotopy four-spheres. Connected sum transports that
unit, and a fixed smooth curvature-source translation preserves the
closed class. A norm that diverges in a singular limit gives no uniform
estimate by that argument. Gerig's relative count agrees modulo two
under his asymptotically standard and no-twisted-circle hypotheses.
Reutter identifies the ordinary semisimple oriented closed scalar under
his precise target and algebra hypotheses. Each is a control for a
specific comparison. [Monopole and Gerig sources](../../../02_invariants/references/TWISTOR_STRING_BAUER_FURUTA_SOURCES.md) · [Reutter Corollary B(2) and Theorem A](../../../../06_invariants_and_candidates/research-progress/handoffs/RETURN_INV_E01_20261127.md)

If the boundary construction changes only the source by a fixed smooth
translation, stop that model at the known closed unit. The first possible
extension is one relative or defect boundary condition that retains the
negative line in a defined operator, domain or local system. To select
it, identify the input state space and the tube filling map, the gauge
group and grading, the Fredholm and compactness estimates needed, and
what the relative state measures. If no such choice survives the
standard control, isolate the failed premise and test one alternate
cut-based Floer or cobordism-map construction with its hypotheses
frozen. The boundary three-manifold by itself supplies the same state
space to both fillings. The comparison is of their states or maps
under a specified boundary identification.

## Use twistor theory to constrain the construction

Spacetime topological twists supply the closest comparators. Read
Donaldson–Witten and the infrared monopole description alongside the
distinct Vafa–Witten twist and its partition function. For each, state
the fields and Q-complex, the role of metric variation and reducibles,
and the restrictions that govern the sphere or its cut. The mass
deformation from $N=4$ to $N=2$ and the abelian infrared theory near
a monopole singularity motivate the comparison. A global correspondence
must carry the required reality condition, bundle data, the marked
sector and an observable across these steps. Tree-level amplitudes,
an effective action and a metric-independent correlation function have
different hypotheses and tests. [Twistor-string and spacetime precedents](../../../02_invariants/references/TWISTOR_STRING_BAUER_FURUTA_SOURCES.md)

Use ordinary Dolbeault theory on the integrable round twistor space as
a benchmark. The standard nonround E33 control already fails
$\bar\partial_J^2=0$ on functions for the canonical
$Z=S(\Lambda_g^+)$. An off-shell candidate must define its field space
and differential on this control, then account for closure, the
variation of the metric and possible boundary or quantum anomalies.
Mason's variable almost-complex action is one precedent for revising
the field space. The fallback is a four-dimensional cohomological
description with an explicit test of which part of the twistor data it
retains. Use the [twistor action investigation](../../../04_twistor_action/README.md)
for any proposed use of the exact quartic relative twistor-spinor
action. Its analytic and variational inputs do not supply the
cohomological structure required here.
