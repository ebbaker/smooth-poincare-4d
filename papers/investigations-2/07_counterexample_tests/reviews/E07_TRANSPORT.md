At each first encounter of a paired face in tetrahedron and face order, type 1 joins the next new tetrahedron by the identity. Type 2 uses the next recorded destination and the next recorded index in lexicographic \(S_4\). Record the inverse each time and skip already paired faces. No type 0 occurs. All input digits are consumed, all reciprocal gluings agree, and all three systems admit consistent orientations. The vertex-link triangles, edges and vertices below are counted by face-pairing orbits. Each vertex link is a connected closed orientable surface of Euler characteristic zero. ## The signs of the recorded peripheral map

For a local check of nontriviality I summed the \(2^{16}\) Kauffman bracket states of the named PD. The two uniform smoothings use adjacent slots \((1,2),(3,4)\) and \((2,3),(4,1)\), with weights \(A\) and \(A^{-1}\). Count the loops as components of the smoothed arc graph and give a state with \(L\) loops the factor \((-A^2-A^{-2})^{L-1}\). The coefficients in increasing exponents \(-30,-26,\ldots,18\) are `-1, 3, -5, 6, -5, 3, 1, -3, 5, -6, 5, -3, 1`. Exchanging the smoothing names inverts the variable. Multiplication by a writhe monomial still leaves 13 terms, which proves the named diagram is a nontrivial knot. [Kauffman, *State models and the Jones polynomial*, Topology 26 (1987), §2](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/kauffmanjones.pdf)

Meridional filling of \(Y_A\) gives \(S^3\), so the inverse isomorphism carries its meridian to an \(S^3\)-surgery slope on the nontrivial named knot. Gordon–Luecke's surgery theorem forces that slope to be the named meridian up to sign. The primitive kernel of \(H_1(\partial E)\to H_1(E)\) on each side forces the longitude up to sign. The boundary orientation is induced by the ambient orientation, and both bases have the same intersection convention. The two signs therefore agree. The only possibilities are \(I\) and \(-I\). If the map were \(I\), it would be a combinatorial isomorphism of the oriented triangulations preserving both signed peripheral classes. With one cusp and both curve orientations retained, SnapPy's documented marked signature invariant would be identical. The suffixes `BbBa` and `bBba` differ, excluding \(I\) and forcing \(-I\). This establishes the sign from the recorded strings and the geometry of the two knot exteriors. [Gordon–Luecke, *Knots are determined by their complements*, J. Amer. Math.
