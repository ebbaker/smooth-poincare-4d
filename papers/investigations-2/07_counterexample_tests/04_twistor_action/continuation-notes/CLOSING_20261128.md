## Classical results

1. **Local boundary map for unrestricted spinor traces.** Fix the flat \([-1,1]\times(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)^3\) with its periodic lift, a parallel unit positive spinor \(\sigma\) and \(\psi_*=e^{t/200}\sigma\). All four real components on both ends may be perturbed independently in \(H^{9/2}\). Each sufficiently close pair has a unique critical field near \(\psi_*\) in \(H^5\), depending smoothly on both traces. It is a strict local minimum at fixed trace and is smooth for smooth data. Its outward momenta are the differential of its on-shell action. The graph is exact Lagrangian in the full real Hilbert cotangent space. Matching the two local half-cylinder maps recovers this graph and the global \(H^5\) field; the middle trace is uniquely determined nearby. [Dirichlet proof](../notes/E08_LOCAL_DIRICHLET.md) · [Local minimality](../notes/LOCAL_MINIMUM_AND_SMOOTH_DATA.md) · [Composition](../notes/LOCAL_FULL_TRACE_COMPOSITION.md)
2. **Smooth transmission, including zero defect.** For supplied fields smooth up to each side and stationary there, with common nowhere-zero seam trace, opposite outward momenta in \(H^{-9/2}\) are necessary and sufficient for weak stationarity of the union. They determine its first normal derivative by strict monotonicity. At nonzero defect the bulk equation determines successive normal derivatives. On an open zero-defect patch the tangential twistor equations determine an affine twistor field with the common Cauchy data. Expanding about that field in the normal variable forces all higher normal coefficients of the difference to vanish. The union is smooth everywhere across this flat seam. [First jets and nonzero defect](../notes/E04_GLUING.md) · [Zero-defect patches](../notes/E10_DEGENERATE_SEAM.md)
3. **Exact radial composition.** For log amplitudes \(u_0,u_1\), length \(L>0\), and torus volume \(V\), the stationary radial path has affine logarithm, action \(9V(u_1-u_0)^4/(16L^3)\) and outward covectors \((-p,p)\), \(p=(9V/4)((u_1-u_0)/L)^3\). Consecutive cylinders compose by length-weighted interpolation, including at equal outer endpoints. There the two-dimensional fiber product has a three-dimensional intersection of ambient tangent spaces; clean intersection fails although the reduced relation is smooth. At equal ends the parallel field uniquely attains zero action among all admissible fields. [Graph](../notes/E02_FLAT_TORUS.md) · [Composition](../notes/E04_GLUING.md)
4. **Unrestricted unequal-end infimum.** For the same fixed cylinder and \(d=u_1-u_0\), let \(I(d)\) be the infimum of \(J_g^4\) over all admissible smooth spinors with these constant-ray traces, including compatible isolated zeros. Every \(d\ne0\) gives \(I(d)>0\). For small \(|d|\),
\[
 0\le\frac{9V}{16L^3}d^4-I(d)\le K_{g,L}|d|^6.
\]
After normalizing the mean logarithmic amplitude to zero, every competitor at or below the radial trial is \(O(d^2)\) close to it in \(H^1\). This proves its leading cost over the full field class. [Quantitative lower bound](../notes/E09_UNEQUAL_TRACE_GAP.md) · [Asymptotic and closeness](../notes/SMALL_TRACE_ASYMPTOTIC.md)
5. **One geometric cobordism.** In a chart of \((\mathbb R/16\mathbb Z)^2\), use the one loop of radial trajectories with \(v=\sin\theta,\ p=(9/4)v^3,\ q_t=\cos\theta+(1+t)v\). The selected suspension in the symplectic strip times this torus is an embedded Lagrangian cylinder with end loops \(C_0,C_2\) at height 1. Its plane extension is proper and is cylindrical over both ends. The relative generator has area \(27\pi/16\) and Maslov index two. In the authors' convention the cobordism has arrow \(C_2\leadsto C_0\). The choice of loop, compact target and suspension is additional data. [Construction and source check](../notes/E05_COBORDISM_CONTROL.md)
6. **Variation, zeros and quadratic control.** The outward density for the quartic action is \(4W(\nu)dA\), \(W=|\psi|^{-4}|P\psi|^2P\psi\). Joint conformal transport with prescribed conformal factor preserves its pairing with trace variations. The spinor-induced unit scale gives an equivalent value of the action and varies with the spinor. At a nonvanishing twistor spinor the Hessian is zero. For a family fixing a transverse zero and its compatible first jet the differentiated density stays bounded, and the excision flux is \(O(r^4)\). An incompatible transverse jet costs logarithmically infinite action. On the same unit cylinder at the parallel reference, the one prescribed-scale comparison \(\rho=1\) has full Dirichlet gap \(3\pi^2/16\). The two Hessians refer to different, explicitly normalized actions. [Variation](../notes/E01_VARIATION.md) · [Zeros](../notes/E03_FIXED_JETS.md) · [Quadratic comparison](../notes/E06_FIXED_SCALE.md)
