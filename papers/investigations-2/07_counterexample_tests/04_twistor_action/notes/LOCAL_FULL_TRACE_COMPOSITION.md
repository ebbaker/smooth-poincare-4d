# Local composition for nonradial Sobolev traces at the selected slope

28 November 2026, for Edward B. Baker III with Codex lead assistance. Author-selected GPT-6.2 Astra/ultra; backend telemetry unavailable. Checkout `280376dd7a18`. This corollary uses the single cylinder, its two closed halves, the background \(\psi_*=e^{bt}\sigma\), \(b=1/200\), and the proof of [E08](E08_LOCAL_DIRICHLET.md). Use \(X=H^{9/2}(T^3;S^+_\mathbb R)\) on each component and the real dual \(X'\). The smooth seam trace at the base is \(\sigma\).

**Statement.** Restrict to small neighborhoods of the base field on the cylinder and of its two restrictions on the half cylinders, in their respective \(H^5\) norms. For each outer trace pair near the base pair in \(X^2\), there is a unique nearby middle trace for which the two local Dirichlet solutions have cancelling outward momenta. The middle trace depends smoothly on the two outer traces. Eliminating it gives the local full-cylinder endpoint action and its differential. The union of the two solutions is the full \(H^5\) solution, also for \(H^{9/2}\) traces.

**Proof.** In the conjugated variable \(w=e^{-bt}\psi-\sigma\), all three backgrounds are \(w=0\). E08's proof of the homogeneous inverse applies on each half: the length-one Poincaré constant is smaller, tangential Fourier truncation and normal derivative recovery apply verbatim, and the trace lift uses collars of width less than half the interval length. Thus each piece has a smooth local Dirichlet solution map and boundary action for two independent \(X\) traces. Choose neighborhoods on which all first jets of conjugated fields are uniformly close to the base jet in the essential supremum norm. Small \(H^5\) neighborhoods on the three fixed pieces have this property.

For outer data \(q\), let \(\psi(q)\) be E08's solution on the union and \(r(q)\) its trace at the middle face. Restriction and trace are bounded linear operators at these Sobolev orders, so \(r(q)\) is smooth. Shrink the outer neighborhood to put both restrictions in the two local solution neighborhoods. They are the corresponding critical fields by uniqueness and their common first jet gives opposite outward momenta.

Conversely, two local solutions with the same middle trace and opposite outward momenta glue to a piecewise \(H^5\), global \(H^1\) field. The first distributional derivative has no seam mass because the traces coincide. Integration by parts on each half and the real \(L^2\) flux pairing show that the joined field has zero first variation on smooth fixed-outer-trace tests. These bulk integrals are linear continuous functionals on \(H^1_0\), since fields and first derivatives are bounded and the denominator is bounded away from zero. They vanish on every such test by density. In particular the test can be the difference \(z\) between the joined field and the known global solution in conjugated variables. Along their affine segment the first jet stays uniformly close to the background a.e. on each piece. Every Hessian on the segment differs from the base bilinear form on global \(H^1_0\) by at most \(\varepsilon\|z\|_{H^1}^2\) on the diagonal, by the coefficient estimate in the [local minimum proof](LOCAL_MINIMUM_AND_SMOOTH_DATA.md). Choose \(\varepsilon<c_b/2\), with \(c_b\) E08's global coercivity constant. Subtract the two first variations tested on \(z\) and integrate the Hessian along the segment. This gives \(0\ge(c_b/2)\|z\|_{H^1}^2\), so the fields coincide. In particular the matching pieces recover global \(H^5\) regularity even though only \(H^1\) was needed to compare them.

In the original spinor traces, if \(F_-,F_+\) are the local on-shell actions on the two halves, the middle derivative of \(F_-(q_-,r)+F_+(r,q_+)\) is the sum of the two outward covectors in \(X'\). The critical value at \(r(q)\) equals the full action by additivity. The chain rule gives its full differential with the middle term zero. Reversing the incoming sign yields local composition of the signed canonical relations over this neighborhood.

The actual matching locus is a smooth graph over the two outer trace variables in this chart. In the full Hilbert cotangent model the derivative of the middle momentum residual takes values in \(H^{7/2}\subsetneq X'\): each local solution map is smooth into \(H^5\), its flux is smooth into \(H^4\) in the bulk and hence into \(H^{7/2}\) at the boundary. The residual derivative fails surjectivity onto \(X'\). This accounts for the precise choice of proof of composition. It makes no Fredholm or transversality claim in a different realization of the momentum space. General global composition and the parallel reference field are outside this chart.
