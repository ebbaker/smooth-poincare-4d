## Coordinate ball and signed local control

From POLE E07 §4 and its B04 convention, the marked point is \(p=(q,N)=(0,e_3)\) in the original rounded \(\partial(F\times D^3)\), with ambient order \((X,Y,z_1,z_2,z_3)\). For \(y=(X,Y,y_3,y_4)\), \(v=\sqrt{y_3^2+y_4^2}\), the chart keeps \(q=(X,Y)\) and sends the last two coordinates to \(N=\cos(v)e_3+\operatorname{sinc}(v)(y_3,y_4,0)\). Recovery by \(v/\sin v\) times \((N_1,N_2)\) is smooth near the pole because the factor is even analytic in \(v\), and \(v^2\) is smooth in the north-pole chart. The derivative gives the ordered vectors \((e_X,e_Y,e_{z_1},e_{z_2})\) at \(p\), with ambient outward normal \(e_{z_3}\) preceding them. It therefore has positive sign, which extends across the connected chart domain. For \(|y|<1/4\), the planar distance from either hole boundary is greater than \(1/2\), while the outer boundary is at distance greater than \(15/4\). The polar angle obeys \(v<1/4<\pi/3-5/64\), so the chart misses the enlarged tubes and the rounded corners. The actual removed ball \(|y|\leq 1/16\) is contained in that chart. ## Both ordered seams

Reading the original JSON, the coordinate framing has no turn on either component. The planar word in row 1 is \(x^{-1}yyxy^{-1}y^{-1}y^{-1}\), and in row 2 it is \(x^{-1}yxxy^{-1}x^{-1}x^{-1}\). Each slot traverses \(L_b(\sigma H(7t-k))\), with \(H\) the flat step and \(L_b\) its indicated positive loop. The sphere part winds once with \(\theta=2\pi t\). With \(\epsilon=1/16\), \(m(t)=(\cos 2\pi t,\sin 2\pi t)\), and \(P\) the usual oriented colatitude-longitude sphere chart, substitution in the original gluing gives

\[
 a_i(m(t),n)=\Phi_i(t,n),\qquad
 \Phi_i(t,w)=\bigl(c_i(t)+\epsilon(w_1,w_2),P(\phi_i+\epsilon w_3,2\pi t)\bigr),
 \qquad (\phi_1,\phi_2)=(\pi/3,2\pi/3).
\]

For the cap side of each signed seam, \(s\leq0\), the chart sends \((s,t,n)\) to \(((1+s)m(t),n)\). On the exterior side, \(s\geq0\), it sends the same triple to \(\Phi_i(t,(1+s)n)\). Smooth recovery on the cap uses \(\arg\zeta\) and \(|\zeta|\); recovery on the exterior uses \(\arg(N_1+iN_2)/(2\pi)\), \(\phi=\arccos N_3\), and \(w=\epsilon^{-1}(q-c_i(t),\phi-\phi_i)\). Then \(s=|w|-1\) and \(n=w/|w|\). The bands are disjoint and avoid the poles because \(\epsilon(5/4)=5/64\) is less than half their angular separation and less than their distances to the poles. Longitude therefore recovers the course of the curve even where its planar projection stops or repeats. The inverse operation restores the cradle on \(s\leq0\) using the identical exterior formula for \(\Phi_i\), so the full seam map and framing are the same. ## Winding test and precise dependency

Projection to the original planar domain \(F\) is defined on all of the retained exterior, including its side and rounded faces. A puncture boundary curve projects into the contractible \(|q|\leq1/16\) chart disk. A seam boundary curve of longitude degree \(d\) projects to a loop homotopic in \(F\) to \(c_i(t)\) traversed with degree \(d\). For this homotopy linearly contract \(\epsilon(w_1,w_2)\) in each fiber. The center curves stay at least 1 from either hole center, and every displacement is at most \(5/64\). The homotopy consequently stays inside \(F\), including for the exterior trace at \(s=1/8\). In the basis with positive winding around the left and right holes, \(L_x,L_y\) are the two positive generators. Direct signed counts from the JSON give \([c_1]=(0,-1)\) and \([c_2]=(-1,0)\), determinant \(-1\). If \(Q\) is a compact oriented exterior connector with all boundary curves on the marked puncture or those seam cuts, the pushed-forward boundary of its fundamental chain has class zero. Summing degrees with the induced boundary orientations gives \(d_1[c_1]+d_2[c_2]=0\), hence \(d_1=d_2=0\). This prohibits using one full section disk alone, or just one full section disk on each of the two seams. Opposite-sign traces on one seam satisfy the necessary balance.
