## Unknot control

In the oriented Euclidean unit \(B^4\), fix \(a=1/4\) and a smooth cutoff \(0\leq\chi\leq1\) equal to zero for \(\rho\leq1/2\) and to one for \(\rho\geq3/4\). For \(a\leq\rho\leq1\), set \(z(\rho)=a[1-\chi(\rho)(1-\rho)]\) and take the disk

\[
 (\rho,\theta)\longmapsto \bigl(\sqrt{\rho^2-z(\rho)^2}\cos\theta,\ \
 \sqrt{\rho^2-z(\rho)^2}\sin\theta,\ z(\rho),\ 0\bigr),
\]

with the initial circle collapsed to one point at \(\rho=a\). Near that point the surface is the affine disk \(x_3=a,\ x_4=0\) and \(\rho=\sqrt{a^2+x_1^2+x_2^2}\) has one nondegenerate minimum. For \(\rho>a\), the ambient norm is \(\rho\) and the positive circle radius gives a smooth embedding, with pairwise disjoint levels. Near the boundary the map is radial on the round circle \((\sqrt{1-a^2}\cos\theta,\sqrt{1-a^2}\sin\theta,a,0)\), so it is proper with a product collar in \((\rho,x/\rho)\) coordinates. The disk orientation extends the \((x_1,x_2)\) orientation at the minimum and gives increasing \(\theta\) on the terminal unknot. The latter has a zero-crossing circle diagram. The outward counts are \((b,s,d)=(1,0,0)\) and \(\chi(D)=1\).
