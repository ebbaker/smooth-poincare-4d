**A disk that would immediately answer an existence question.** Dunfield–Gong's Theorem 5.14 says that smooth sliceness of `16n68278` in the standard four-ball would imply an exotic smooth four-sphere. The proof combines the disk complement with the trace of a nonslice zero-surgery friend. The signed knot diagram must come from the named-knot data of §1.19. The DT code in the second row of Table 11 represents a three-component surgery link. A future comparison needs both objects, their distinct roles, the row's sign and the invariant on the friend. The authors have already searched diagrammatic bands using perturbed diagrams, cached ribbon links and connected sums with small ribbon knots. [Dunfield–Gong v1, §§1.19, 2.2–2.6, 2.10, 5.13–5.14 and Table 11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.21825v1)

Try a movie from the empty link outwards to the target that permits one interior radial maximum. A small birth, oriented band saddle or death has an explicit local model. One reason to spend effort on this class is that the movie can encode a disk with a maximum directly. Inserting a cancelling saddle and death also gives such a movie for a ribbon disk. For a control, put that pair into an unknot disk, record which component dies, then reconstruct the entire surface. Before any search, pin down move supports, crossings, intermediate components, band path lengths and total steps so that every proposed movie is representable by a finite string. The expensive part is finding the movie. A reader can replay a particular movie without reproducing its search. An identified ribbon disk found along the way would already suffice. [Repository example of recording framed exterior bands](../../../../../investigations-1/05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/notes/GST_INDEPENDENT_EXTERIOR_BANDS.md)

**A surface in the project's own sphere.** Use one oriented chart in the smooth boundary of 05's marked five-dimensional minus Miller–Schupp handlebody. Excise a smaller closed coordinate ball's interior and retain the boundary parametrization. Try to follow a cap/core patch or a collar patch across the actual marked seams and back to a signed knot diagram in this boundary. A collar-spanning disk for the unknot calibrates the marking. The first informative example would be a connected orientable smooth proper surface of genus \(g\) with one boundary knot \(J\) for which \(|s_{\mathbb Q}(J)|>2g\). A surface certificate alone also has value as an upper bound for the sibling genus profile. The four-dimensional attaching-boundary traces in 05 require their height coordinates and normal framings. Their planar shadows do not determine a three-dimensional knot diagram. [Framed presentation](../../../../05_certificates/notes/E06_PRESENTATION_HANDLES.md) · [01 marked atlas](../../../../01_chiral_transfer_and_pole_forms/notes/E07_MARKED_ATLAS.md) · [Rasmussen, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0402131)

Candidate selection for this route has a mathematical constraint. A homotopy-sphere puncture is an integral homology ball. Its genus bound forces \(|\tau(J)|\leq g\), including \(\tau(J)=0\) for a disk. Thus a disk with nonzero rational Rasmussen invariant is interesting only with a compatible \(\tau\) and an actual smooth disk. A large \(|\tau|\) would disqualify the proposed native surface. Ask 06 to evaluate the two invariants on the exact oriented diagram. [01 puncture-knot filter and primary passages](../../../../01_chiral_transfer_and_pole_forms/research-progress/handoffs/TO_06_PUNCTURE_KNOT_FILTER_20261127.md)

**The opposite direction.** Dunfield–Gong also give `18nh00000601` a smooth disk in a homotopy four-ball. Proving its nonsliceness in the standard four-ball would suffice. A theorem applicable to every homotopy four-ball cannot supply that distinction. Reserve the example until someone specifies an obstruction whose standard-ball premise and values can be checked. Failing every tested ribbon or one-maximum movie leaves smooth nonsliceness open. [Dunfield–Gong, Theorem 5.14 and proof](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.21825v1)

Open the two geometric intakes independently after activation. Keep the standard-ball disk proof, an upper bound on the particular presentation sphere and a recognition result for the particular sphere as distinct outcomes.
