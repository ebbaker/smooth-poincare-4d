## The objects and the two initial tests

All manifolds and embeddings are smooth. A homotopy four-sphere is closed, connected and homotopy equivalent to S⁴. Use oriented manifolds with the outward-normal-first boundary convention. Track knot orientations and mirrors explicitly under boundary identifications. The distinguishing conclusion concerns smooth diffeomorphism type even after forgetting the orientation, since the standard sphere admits a reflection. The surface tests need no metric, preferred chirality or spinor conventions. In a movie use increasing radius toward the boundary of the standard B⁴ and a product collar at the boundary.

Identifying it with any previously marked sphere would require a separate diffeomorphism. |
| A surface in the marked sphere | For W = ΣP minus the interior of a specified small coordinate ball, an oriented boundary knot J and a smooth compact connected properly embedded orientable surface in W with boundary J and genus g, together with an exact rational Rasmussen comparison | If the absolute value of s(J) exceeds 2g, ΣP itself is exotic. |

For the first implication, the disk exterior and the trace of the friend knot glue along the common zero-surgery. The resulting homotopy ball supplies a disk for the friend, which the imported obstruction forbids in the standard ball. The primary theorem packages the construction of the exotic sphere. For the second implication, if ΣP were standard its small coordinate puncture would be a standard four-ball, so the displayed surface would bound the same knot up to boundary parametrization there. The absolute genus bound also accommodates reflection. In neither case does a disk in an unspecified ambient four-manifold supply the premise. ## An invariant that the project could produce

The invariant track starts alongside witness intake. Its first proposed observable assigns to an oriented smooth homotopy sphere Σ the function \(K\mapsto g_\Sigma(K)\). Minimize genus over connected, orientable, smooth proper surfaces with the specified single boundary knot in coordinate punctures. Fix a boundary orientation convention using the same model S³ for every chart. Range over compatible small charts to eliminate any choice of presentation. The standard value is \(g_{S^4}(K)=g_4(K)\). Taking the knots with value at most g gives a nested sequence of sets. A single checked upper bound for the candidate below a checked lower bound for the standard sphere separates the two profiles. The finite table of *bounds* is a practical output even when an exact entry remains open. The classical relative-genus framework and its success on other smooth four-manifolds provide a comparison with prior work. [Manolescu–Marengon–Piccirillo, introduction, Corollary 1.4 and §2](https://web.stanford.edu/~cm5/slice4D.pdf) · [Geometric proposals and probe selection](../brainstorm/notes/WITNESS_ROUTES_20261127.md)

The second proposed observable is intrinsic to the chiral-transfer program. For a triple \((c,p,[u])\), use 01's self-dual pole form with residue direction \([u]\in\mathbb P(\Lambda^-_p)\). A tentative oriented invariant is
\[
  \min_{(c,p,[u])\,\text{with transverse pole form}} \#\pi_0\bigl(Z(\omega_{c,p,u})\bigr).
\]
Here c ranges over all smooth oriented Riemannian conformal structures, and p over the whole sphere. Allow an empty zero set. Leave the minimum undefined if the family has no transverse member. Before use, verify naturality, the common zero-free end for each chosen form, existence of admissible triples on the claimed domain, and behaviour under orientation reversal. The standard sphere has the round zero-free example. Any one transverse example gives an upper bound. A positive lower bound would have to survive variation of conformal class, pole and residue. A ball-supported merge followed by realization in a new conformal class could force the minimum to be at most one throughout its nonempty domain. Check that the relative hypotheses compose before looking for larger values. A separate marked statistic is the normalized area of the closed set of residue directions with a zero for a fixed pair (c,p). An intrinsic test would need control of changes of marking and of its quantifiers. ## Controls, alternatives and ownership

A known standard disk should pass the same birth, saddle, death, endpoint and genus checks required of the target. A deliberately padded movie of that disk tests whether an interior maximum and its cap are handled correctly. On the native side a standard coordinate puncture supplies a boundary and orientation control. An enumeration supports exclusion only if its finite domain is stated and its coverage proved. A timeout or a rejected individual surface records the attempted input and leaves the rest open.

Two alternatives have different bottlenecks. Ordinary smooth nonsliceness of `18nh00000601` would suffice by the same primary theorem, but this knot is smoothly slice in a homotopy ball. A proposed obstruction needs an essential standard-ball premise. For a closed module on ΣP the issue is whether a nonzero comparison survives every relation of the complete handle construction. Each stays in reserve until that prerequisite is explicit.
