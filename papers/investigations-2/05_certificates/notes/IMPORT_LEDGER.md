## Frame and quantifiers

- **Project theorem, replayed by 01 (A1).** For every closed connected oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere M, each smooth Riemannian conformal class c and every p, let H be *all* smooth closed c-self-dual two-forms on M minus p of norm O(ρ⁻⁴), including zero. For each representative g and each η in Λ⁻ at p there exists a unique member with leading term I*η and remainder O∞(ρ⁻²) in positive normal coordinates, and every member occurs. This is a real linear isomorphism. Inversion reverses coordinate orientation. For g′=exp(2f)g the covariant coefficient of a fixed form scales by exp(4f(p)); divide by volume density for the scale-independent parameter. Against source volume the off-diagonal transfer kernel is (2/π²)P_g, where P_g has unit leading coefficient. [Owner normalization and replay](../../01_chiral_transfer_and_pole_forms/research-progress/handoffs/TO_MASTER2_NORMALIZATION_20261127.md)
- **Project theorem, replayed by 01 (A2).** Under precisely these M,c,p hypotheses, a member nonvanishing at **every** point of M minus p implies M is diffeomorphic to S⁴. The proof changes the form on a distant annulus, identifies the resulting end with flat space by a similarity, and uses the prescribed end in the smooth extension over p. [Owner proof and replay](../../01_chiral_transfer_and_pole_forms/research-progress/handoffs/TO_MASTER2_FRAME_20261127.md)
