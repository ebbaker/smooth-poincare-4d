## Conditional finite checks on the rewritten relations

**Certificate of the abstract groups.** For the \(s=0\) indexed
Wirtinger relations, the cone condition makes the three trefoil
meridians \(x_1,x_2,x_3\) involutions, so the common crossing sign
disappears. Set \(x=x_1\), \(y=x_2\). The second equation gives
\(x_3=yxy\). The first is then equivalent to \(xyx=yxy\). The third
follows since \((yxy)y(yxy)=yxyxy=x\). Thus \(r=xy\) has \(r^3=1\)
and \(xrx=r^{-1}\), giving at most six elements \(r^i x^e\) with
\(0\leq i<3\), \(0\leq e<2\). The permutations
\(x_1=(12),\,x_2=(23),\,x_3=(13)\) satisfy the three equations for
both signs and give six distinct images of those words. The indexed
group is \(S_3\). For the cyclic row, the indexed cyclic presentation

\(u^{-r}\). At \(r=4\) its relation is \((u^{-4})^2=1\). Powers
\(1,u,\ldots,u^7\) suffice, and translation by one on \(\mathbb Z/8\)
gives eight distinct images. The old singular meridian has order two
in this model. These calculations certify the groups of the supplied
words. Their use as groups of the actual marked orbifolds depends on
the original link and peripheral identification.
