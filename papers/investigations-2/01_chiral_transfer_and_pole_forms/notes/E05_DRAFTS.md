The Hodge eigenspaces \(\Lambda^\pm\) use the orientation of \(M\). A pole form is closed and self-dual on \(X\), with norm \(O(d_g(p,\cdot)^{-4})\) in a smooth representative \(g\in c\). ## Fibre map and obstruction

*Project theorem, working.* Fix inner products on the source and target of a linear evaluation \(L=\mathrm{ev}_q\). The normalized map \(u\mapsto Lu/|Lu|\) is a diffeomorphism of the unit spheres when \(L\) is invertible, with inverse \(v\mapsto L^{-1}v/|L^{-1}v|\). At rank one or two it takes values in the proper subsphere of \(\operatorname{im}L\), and at rank zero its domain is empty. Where \(Lu\ne 0\), its derivative on tangent vectors is \(v\mapsto \operatorname{pr}_{(Lu)^\perp}Lv/|Lu|\).

For invertible \(L\), choose orthonormal eigenvectors \(e_i\) of \(L^*L\) with positive singular values \(s_i=|Le_i|\). At \(e_1\), the tangent vectors \(e_2,e_3\) have orthogonal images of lengths \(s_2/s_1,s_3/s_1\), so conformality there requires \(s_2=s_3\). At \(e_2\) it also requires \(s_1=s_3\). Conversely, equal singular values make the map the restriction of an orthogonal isomorphism. For a pole basis orthonormal in the fixed source norm, \(Q_{ij}=\langle\omega_i,\omega_j\rangle_g\) because the forms are self-dual. Thus on a whole definite fibre the metric conformality criterion is precisely that \(Q(q)\) be scalar. This calculation is pointwise. The proposal's curvature implication uses an everywhere conformal definite triple of **closed** forms and the parallel-triple argument, whose verification belongs to E03.

*Inherited working project theorem and checked example.* The strict obstruction concerns a single holomorphic local biholomorphism from \(U\times\mathbb{CP}^1\) to the curved correspondence space with projected image in one coordinate neighbourhood and with preservation of the \(\alpha\)-distribution. Compactness of each holomorphic projective line forces its base projection to be constant. The induced base map preserves the null cone and forces \(W=0\). M2 p. 6 asks for coverage of complete target fibres, which alone does not assert that inverse. The obstruction note also verifies auxiliary connection descent for a holomorphic ASD Ricci-flat wave, including its two-patch example with neutral signature on the displayed real slice. That example separates auxiliary descent from the inverse-chart and tangent-spinor requirements. The real sphere map above arises by evaluation and carries no holomorphic distribution-preserving inverse hypothesis.

*Evidence boundary.* The draft use is historical and heuristic. The fibre calculation is conditional on the supplied smooth evaluation family and independent of any theorem in M2 or M3. The existence, normalization, recognition, and global zero-freeness questions retain their separate evidence status in the pole-form investigation.
