## Integrated proof on the original source

**Project theorem (A2).** Let $\omega$ be a zero-free member of the pole space of an oriented smooth marked Riemannian homotopy four-sphere. Since $\omega\wedge\omega=|\omega|_g^2dV_g$, it is symplectic on $X$. By A1 it has a nonzero constant limit $\beta$ in $x=I(y)$ and can be written as $\beta+da$ on the end, with $|a|\le C/r$ and $|da|\le C/r^2$ in Euclidean components. Choose $\chi_R$ equal to one up to $R$ and zero past $2R$, with $|d\chi_R|\le C_0/R$. Retain $\omega$ on the core and use $\beta+d(\chi_R a)$ on the end. These expressions agree on an open overlap. On the annulus the perturbation from $\beta$ has norm at most $C(1+C_0)R^{-2}$. In an orthonormal skew frame for the inherited orientation the matrix of $\beta$ has both singular values equal to $b=|\beta|/\sqrt 2>0$. Taking $R$ large so the perturbation has operator norm less than $b$ gives a path of invertible forms from $\beta$ to the new form at every annular point. The patched closed form is therefore symplectic for the original orientation and equals $\beta$ sufficiently far out.

Choose $Q$ orthogonal so $Q^*\omega_{\rm std}=\beta/b$ and put $z=\sqrt b\, Qx$. The orientation on $X$ is opposite to the coordinate orientation of $x$, and $Q$ reverses the latter. Hence $z$ is a symplectic end chart and sends large spheres to spheres. Excision in $(M,X)$ gives $H_2(X;\mathbb Z)=0$. Use this chart to replace a ball well inside a finite polydisk by the compact core of $X$ in a product of equal-area spheres whose complement of its coordinate divisors is that polydisk. The precise area map and gluing collar are in the [source receipt](../../references/A2_ORIGINAL.md). The two divisors are symplectic with equal area $A$, have trivial normal bundles and intersect once with positive sign. Cutting along the gluing three-sphere in the Mayer–Vietoris sequence gives the two divisor classes as an integral basis for $H_2$ of the closed glued manifold. Every spherical period is thus in $A\mathbb Z$. Apply Gromov's criterion and relative assertion. The resulting product identification agrees with the prescribed one near the divisor. The complement of this neighborhood is compact inside the open polydisk, so an entire outer polydisk collar is in the agreement region. There the identified map and its inverse agree with the prescribed $z$-chart. Paste on that collar to obtain a global diffeomorphism $X\to\mathbb R^4$ equal to $z$ outside a compact set. In target coordinates $w=z/|z|^2$ near the point at infinity and the original smooth chart $y$, this map is $w=b^{-1/2}Qy$. It extends over $p$ with invertible derivative and smooth inverse. Thus $M$ is diffeomorphic to the standard smooth $S^4$.
