# Unique continuation for the marked interface trace

Szarski, “A unique continuation theorem for exterior differential forms on Riemannian manifolds”, Arkiv för Matematik 4 (1962), 417–453. DOI 10.1007/BF02591624. [Primary scan](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/116/6988-11512_2007_Article_BF02591624.pdf) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02591624)

**Import.** On a connected boundaryless Riemannian manifold of class C^{1,1} with locally Lipschitz metric, their theorem applies to a differential form with locally square-integrable coefficients and strong first derivatives in L². In a local coordinate system at some point the integrals of the absolute values of its coefficients on balls must be o(r^a) for each a>0. The sizes of its differential and codifferential must be bounded a.e. on each compact set in terms of its size there, as in (iv). The form then vanishes a.e. on the manifold. The paper's Q denotes the metric quadratic form of the appropriate degree.

Here the manifold considered for continuation is the whole smooth, connected punctured X. A smooth closed self-dual two-form on X is also coclosed, so both left sides vanish identically. If zero on an open set, it satisfies the infinite-order hypothesis at an interior point. Continuity upgrades a.e. vanishing to equality everywhere. This application concerns the transmission of the three-dimensional family to a cap and its boundary. No quantitative unique-continuation constant or pointwise lower estimate is imported.
