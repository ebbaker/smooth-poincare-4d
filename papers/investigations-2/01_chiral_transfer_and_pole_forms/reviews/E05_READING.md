## Real evaluation check

For \(\omega_u(q)\ne0\), differentiation of \(v\mapsto v/|v|\) gives the orthogonal projection to \(v^\perp\) divided by \(|v|\). Applied to \(L=\mathrm{ev}_q\), it yields the derivative in E05. When \(L\) is invertible, normalizing \(L^{-1}\) gives the inverse on the sphere. At deficient rank the image is contained in the unit sphere of the proper image subspace, and at rank zero the map has empty domain. For positive singular values \(s_1,s_2,s_3\), evaluation at the first two singular directions compares tangent lengths \(s_2/s_1,s_3/s_1\) and \(s_1/s_2,s_3/s_2\). Conformality on the whole fibre forces equality of all three singular values, which also suffices. With an orthonormal coefficient basis that is exactly the positive scalar Gram condition by the self-dual wedge identity.

This is a real fibrewise calculation conditional on the evaluation family. It does not use a holomorphic incidence theorem, an Einstein representative, a transport equation, or an identification of complex null trajectories.
