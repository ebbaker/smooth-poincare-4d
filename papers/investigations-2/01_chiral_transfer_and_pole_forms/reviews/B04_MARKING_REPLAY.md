## Analytic premises on the second cap

A1 constructs \(\Omega=Lu=d(A^0+a)\) globally on the punctured manifold. Each fibre \(\{b_0\}\times S^2\) of either cap is compact, closed and disjoint from the pole, so its period is zero by Stokes. On the second cap, the sphere rotation group preserves the metric, its product orientation, and the fibre periods. At fixed base point \(b_0\), isotropy rotations in the vertical tangent plane kill the mixed components of any invariant two-form. Its invariant self-dual subspace is spanned by the unit form \(J=(dV_{\mathbb H^2}+dA_n)/\sqrt2\). Thus the normalized rotation average of \(\Omega\) is \(f(b_0)J\), whose fibre period is \(4\pi f(b_0)/\sqrt2\). The average vanishes. For every scalar \(c(b_0)\), averaging the comparison with \(c(b_0)J\) gives the quantitative obstruction
\[
 \sup_{n\in S^2}|\Omega(b_0,n)-c(b_0)J|_h\ \ge\ |c(b_0)|.
\]
This rules out a strictly positive pointwise margin from uniform approximation by this invariant mode, at every fibre and for every input. The inequality alone gives no zero of \(\Omega\).

For the flat check, write \(w=\tanh(t'/2)e^{i\theta}\) and \(\xi+ib=i(1+w)/(1-w)\). The map is orientation preserving on the disk, which goes to the radius-one hyperbolic ball about \((0,1)\) with \(e^{-1}\le b\le e\). The map \(F(\xi,b,n)=(\xi,bn)\) is a smooth diffeomorphism onto its image and orientation preserving in the order \((\xi,z_1,z_2,z_3)\). Its pulled-back Euclidean metric is
\[
 d\xi^2+db^2+b^2g_{S^2}=b^2h.
\]
Therefore \(\widehat\Omega=(F^{-1})^*\Omega\) is closed and Euclidean self-dual. It is coclosed, so its Cartesian coefficients are harmonic. If \(\beta\) is any constant Euclidean self-dual form, the squared Euclidean norm of \(\widehat\Omega-\beta\) is subharmonic. A full Euclidean seam trace bound \(\varepsilon=\sup_{F(\partial C_2)}|\widehat\Omega-\beta|_{\mathrm{Eucl}}<|\beta|_{\mathrm{Eucl}}\) implies on the closed cap
\[
 |\Omega|_h\ge b^2(|\beta|_{\mathrm{Eucl}}-\varepsilon)
 \ge e^{-2}(|\beta|_{\mathrm{Eucl}}-\varepsilon).
\]
For intrinsic boundary data, a Euclidean self-dual form with tangential restriction \(\tau\) equals \(\tau+\nu^\flat\wedge *_\partial\tau\) in outward-first order. Its full norm is \(\sqrt2|\tau|\) in the corresponding induced metric. This specifies the conversion needed for the boundary error above. The cap frame must be related to the same physical trace at the signed seam. Its constant comparison form does not fix that trace or the input coefficient at the pole.
