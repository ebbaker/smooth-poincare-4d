Each anti-self-dual leading coefficient occurs once, and the quartic pole has a remainder of order at most two in an oriented normal chart. A nowhere-zero member on the punctured manifold implies a diffeomorphism of the original closed smooth manifold with the standard sphere. The proof first makes the end exactly standard, then obtains the diffeomorphism with the prescribed end agreement. [A1](../notes/E01_A1.md) · [A2](../notes/E02_A2.md)

**Project theorem on zeros.** On the standard sphere one can fix the pole and its nonzero coefficient and vary the smooth metric from a zero-free member to a member with a transverse zero circle. At the first parameter with zeros, every zero is nontransverse. Any argument that counts zero circles must either impose transversality or justify it for the family it uses. The smooth openness of zero-freeness in the marked data survives this example.
