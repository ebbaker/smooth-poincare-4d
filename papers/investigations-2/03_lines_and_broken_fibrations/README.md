# Lines and broken fibrations

**Question.** For a pole form with zeros, what do the pseudoholomorphic curves of the closed manifold $M\#\mathbb{CP}^2$ and the broken Lefschetz fibrations attached to the form say about removing its last zero circle?

**Current result (project theorem, replayed).** A single transverse twisted zero circle on the marked input bounds a finite-energy pseudoholomorphic current with one irreducible component of weight one. Its area is the absolute primitive period around the zero circle. Whether its normalization can have one regular boundary sheet, and whether the circle can then be removed, remain open.
