**Project theorem, replayed.** [E06](../../reviews/LINE_E06_PERIOD_REPLAY.md) checks the period and [E07](../../reviews/LINE_E07_CAP_DEGREE_REPLAY.md) checks its application. In the marked construction \(Y=M\#\mathbb{CP}^2\), choose the harmonic realization standard Kähler near the marked positive line. After a cap-relative merger to a single zero \(Z\), the nonzero \(c_1(\det)=3\,{\rm PD}[L_\infty]\) Seiberg–Witten chamber yields a finite-energy current on \(Y-Z\). Its intersection zero with the positive line excludes both intersections with that line and a component equal to the line. Its closure stays in a compact subset of \(V=Y\setminus L_\infty\). In this exact core, every irreducible component has positive area \(n\int_Z\alpha\), where \(d\alpha=\bar\omega|_V\) and \(n\) is the integral degree of its oriented truncated boundary. The total degree has absolute value one. Consequently the current consists of one irreducible, multiplicity-one component; its area is \(|\int_Z\alpha|>0\). This is an existence conclusion. The normalization can still have handles, several approaches to \(Z\) with opposite local orientations, or singular points in its closure. **Heuristic — first attack, owned by SP4-LINE.** Study boundary regularity of that degree-zero component before studying degree-one lines. The first open lemma is the following existential statement. Given the one-circle capped input, allow a deformation of the form away from the marked cap to a standard twisted local model, retaining one transverse circle at the endpoint, and choose an adapted metric standard on the cap and model on the normal double cover. Can these choices be made so that some degree-zero Taubes component has a single smooth embedded half-sheet along every point of \(Z\), descending with boundary degree one and with no exceptional boundary points? Equivalently for the desired conclusion, its normalization acquires one smooth boundary mapping diffeomorphically to \(Z\) with an embedded collar in the ambient manifold. The collar condition is an additional restriction on the image. The finite-energy and algebraic degree tests already obtained apply to each such prepared endpoint.

**Import — scope of the first lemma.** For the exact local form and almost complex structure in Taubes, Thm. 1.8 gives local half-disks off a finite exceptional set. Section 1.c specifies the tube or double-cover coordinates where the theorem applies. In his moduli paper the signed meridional degree on the standard local model is \(p-q_+-q_-\), and the boundary-regular moduli theory assumes regularity. On a twisted tube the lifts and their descent must be accounted for before using sheet labels. A useful first test on the cover is to rule out oppositely oriented axial half-sheets, retain only the one positive sheet over each lifted base point, and exclude exceptional points. Unit signed degree alone does none of those things. A negative outcome for a specified metric would test that choice only. **Heuristic — subsequent steps.** After the boundary lemma one would need to control interior genus and singularities and construct an actual modification of the closed form over a region containing the bounding surface, with the exterior and marked cap fixed. Even an embedded disk with the correct boundary is not yet such a modification. **Import and project deduction — fibration alternative.** Baykur–Saeki can keep the given twisted \(Z\), its local type, and the class of \(L_\infty\) in a pencil with a chosen base point; the proof allows the original line to cease to be a fiber. Baykur separately produces a positive pencil with the actual line a regular fiber, with no requirement to keep this \(Z\). If both requirements held for a simplified one-round pencil with connected fibers, the sphere would be the lower fiber, the higher genus would be one, and the one-point blowup would have two Lefschetz points. The genus-one classification then identifies the *unmarked* odd sphere bundle. Our combinatorial last-circle problem would require a relative move eliminating the round locus, preserving the exceptional section, the line neighborhood, and positive fiber geometry sufficient to reconstruct and uncap the form.
