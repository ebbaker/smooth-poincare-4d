## Capped input and relative degree

**Project theorem (working, conditional on the cap).** Write $Y=M\#\mathbb{CP}^2$, with its positive projective summand and the embedded $\bar\omega$-symplectic sphere $L_\infty$ of square $+1$. Use the [second master's oriented smooth Riemannian conventions](../../../../master-2/research-overview/OVERVIEW.md#fixed-setting-and-conventions) and suppose the capped closed form $\bar\omega$ is transversely near-symplectic, with one twisted circle $Z$ disjoint from the line. Van Kampen and the connected-sum homology sequence give $\pi_1(Y)=0$, $H_2(Y;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z H$, $H=[L_\infty]$, $H\cdot H=1$ and $(b_1,b^+,b^-,\chi,\sigma)=(0,1,0,3,1)$. Thus $Z$ is nullhomotopic and every integral square is nonnegative. In particular $Y$ is smoothly minimal, and there are no symplectic exceptional classes in the circle exterior. For $h=\operatorname{PD}_Y(H)$ we have $[\bar\omega]=a h$ with $a=\int_{L_\infty}\bar\omega>0$.

**Import.** A smooth near-symplectic form with positive rank-three derivative image at its zeros admits a smooth Riemannian metric making it self-dual and hence harmonic. Transversality in $\Lambda^+$ then follows from the derivative rank. This supplies a metric on the capped form under the stated cap hypothesis. [ADK, Proposition 1 and §7.2](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/EMIS/journals/UW/gt/ftp/main/2005/2005-24.pdf).

**Project theorem (working, relative degree calculation).** For a compatible $J$ off $Z$, the canonical class $c_1(K)$ evaluates to $-3$ on $H$. Along $L_\infty$ use a compatible structure preserving its tangent and symplectic normal planes, whose Chern numbers are $2$ and $1$. The compatible structures are homotopic there, so the evaluation also holds for Taubes's metric-induced $J$. The determinant identity in T (6.1) and G2 §1.2 gives, for the relative class $A$ corresponding to a spin$^c$ structure $\mathfrak s$,

\[
2(A\cdot L_\infty)=\langle c_1(\det S^+(\mathfrak s)),H\rangle-3.
\]

Write $k=A\cdot L_\infty$. The determinant class is $(2k+3)h$ and the formal dimension from G1 p. 1798 and G2 (1.2) is $d(\mathfrak s)=k(k+3)$. Relative degree one corresponds to determinant $5h$ and $d=4$. Relative degree zero corresponds to determinant $3h$ and $d=0$. The long exact sequence gives $0\to \mathbb Z H\to H_2(Y,Z;\mathbb Z)\xrightarrow{\partial}H_1(Z;\mathbb Z)\to0$. For the boundary prescribed by Taubes, each $k$ therefore selects one relative class. Its boundary is the oriented generator of $H_1(Z;\mathbb Z)$, whereas the image of the absolute class $H$ has zero boundary. The line pairing specifies the relative degree of the whole current.
