# Invariants and candidate spheres

**Question.** Which field-theoretic invariants does a theorem exclude from distinguishing a homotopy four-sphere from $S^4$, and can an invariant that is not excluded be computed on a named candidate?

**Origin.** Section 6 of the [proposing note](../../master/brainstorm/notes/claude/ALTERNATIVE_FRAMEWORKS_AND_INVESTIGATIONS_2_20261127.md), written in answer to the author's question about methods from topological field theory; the strategy of Freedman, Gompf, Morrison and Walker; and the candidate spheres named in the first master and in [certificates](../05_certificates/README.md).
