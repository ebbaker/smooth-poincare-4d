### What would make a computation on \(\Sigma_P\) worthwhile?

**Heuristic, direct calculation.** 05's framed five-dimensional handles and four-dimensional boundary attachments specify a smooth candidate. For the unmarked invariant one may equivalently choose a four-dimensional lower handle realization of the relators and take its identity double. In that model there are two one-handles, four two-handles, two three-handles and the final cap. The handle formulas allow one- and three-handles. The calculation would sum over cables, impose braid and dotted-annulus relations, and coequalize the two hemisphere maps of each upper attaching sphere. The last four-handle acts by an isomorphism. Even fixed bidegree does not give a general finite bound on the one-handle sum. Neither a relator word nor the rank observed at a single cable cutoff certifies survival under all these maps. A full module computation is unnecessary if a single proved summand differs from the standard one. [Gay–Kirby §4.1](https://msp.org/gt/2007/11-4/gt-v11-n4-p03-p.pdf) · [Manolescu–Walker–Wedrich, Thms. 3.7, 3.10, 4.7 and 4.23](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.04616).

**Project theorem, scoped tests.** The displayed double has no initial geometric one-/two-handle cancellation using any one of its four unchanged two-handle curves. Neither native relator is primitive, as witnessed by noncommuting rational affine representations of its one-relator quotient. The dual meridians are trivial in the lower free group. A slide can change the relator and falls outside the test. Also, an embedded knot trace in a homology four-sphere necessarily has framing zero. The latter fact rules out importing either of Ren–Willis’s \(-1\)-traces as an embedded trace in these candidates. Their exotic pair has boundary and a nonzero second homology class.
