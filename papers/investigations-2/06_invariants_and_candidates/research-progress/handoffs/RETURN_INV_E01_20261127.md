## Imported statements

**Coefficients and domain.** Fix an algebraically closed field $k$. The general theorem imposes no additional characteristic hypothesis. The input is an Atiyah–Segal symmetric monoidal functor $Z:\mathrm{Bord}_4\to\mathrm{Vect}_k$ with the ordinary tensor product of $k$-vector spaces. The domain has all closed smooth oriented three-manifolds as objects and diffeomorphism classes of smooth compact oriented four-bordisms as morphisms. Diffeomorphisms must preserve orientation and the prescribed boundary identifications. A closed oriented four-manifold is an endomorphism of the empty three-manifold and is assigned a scalar in $k$. [§2.1, p. 547](https://doi.org/10.1112/topo.12288).

**Semisimplicity.** Both commutative Frobenius algebras must be semisimple as algebras. The local-operator algebra is $Z(S^3)$, with multiplication from the four-dimensional pair of pants $m_3$ and unit from $D^4$. The fusion algebra is $Z(S^2\times S^1)$, with Frobenius operations from the corresponding three-dimensional bordisms crossed with $S^1$. These algebras are finite-dimensional by duality. Over $k$, a finite-dimensional commutative semisimple algebra is a product of copies of $k$. [Definition 2.1, p. 548, Definition 2.6, p. 550, and proof of Proposition 2.8, p. 551](https://doi.org/10.1112/topo.12288).

**Theorem A.** For such a semisimple $Z$, take connected compact oriented four-bordisms $W,W'\colon P\to Q$ with the same prescribed incoming and outgoing three-manifolds. If there is an integer $n\geq 0$ and an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism of bordisms

$$W\mathbin{\#}n(S^2\times S^2)\;\cong\; W'\mathbin{\#}n(S^2\times S^2),$$

with the sums in the interiors, then $Z(W)=Z(W')$ as linear maps. The proof decomposes $Z$ into indecomposable semisimple summands, uses connected-sum multiplicativity up to the invertible $S^4$ factor, and cancels the invertible $S^2\times S^2$ factor on each summand. [Theorem A and its stabilization footnote, p. 543, Proposition 3.2, pp. 553–554, Theorem 3.5 and proof of Theorem A, p. 556](https://doi.org/10.1112/topo.12288).

**Corollary B.** For such a semisimple $Z$ and closed smooth oriented four-manifolds $M,N$, any of the following suffices for $Z(M)=Z(N)$.

1. An orientation-preserving homeomorphism $M\to N$.
2. An orientation-preserving homotopy equivalence $M\to N$, with $M,N$ simply connected.
3. An orientation-preserving homotopy equivalence $M\to N$, with both universal covers admitting no spin structure. In this case, on a connected manifold $M$, the scalar depends only on $\chi(M)$, $\sigma(M)$, $\pi_1(M)$ and the image $c_*[M]\in H_4(\pi_1(M);\mathbb Z)$ under a classifying map of the universal cover, with the group and homology class compared under the same group isomorphism.

Reutter invokes Gompf for (1), Wall for (2) and Kreck for (3). In (2) the oriented homotopy equivalence identifies the integral intersection forms. [Corollary B, pp. 543–544, and proof in §3.2, p. 557](https://doi.org/10.1112/topo.12288).

**Homotopy-sphere specialization of B(2).** Given a smooth closed oriented homotopy four-sphere $\Sigma$, a homotopy equivalence $\Sigma\to S^4$ has degree $\pm1$. If its degree is $-1$, compose it with a reflection of the oriented standard $S^4$. Then B(2) gives $Z(\Sigma)=Z(S^4)\in k$. This applies for either choice of orientation of $\Sigma$ and requires only the oriented homotopy equivalence and semisimple field theory above. As a second check, $H^2(\Sigma;\mathbb{Z}/2)=0$, $\chi(\Sigma)=2$ and $\sigma(\Sigma)=0$, so the spin case of Corollary 3.9, equation (6), gives $Z(\Sigma)/Z(S^4)=1$ for every indecomposable semisimple $Z$. The unnormalized equality is the statement for general $Z$. [Corollary B(2), p. 543, and Corollary 3.9, p. 557](https://doi.org/10.1112/topo.12288).

**Limits of these imports.** The theorem gives equality of the closed scalar and, under Theorem A's hypotheses, equality of the two specified bordism maps. Application to a construction from link, gauge or other homology requires a realization on the stated bordism category with the stated coefficients and semisimple algebras. A theory with extra tangential structure or another target needs its own applicability argument. Reutter’s statement about all known full theories describes the literature in the 2023 article. He groups Bärenz–Barrett dichromatic state sums, Chaidez–Cotler–Cui Hopf-algebra invariants, Cui’s $G$-crossed braided-category constructions, and Douglas–Reutter fusion 2-category state sums under proven or expected once-extended realizations. Individual coverage of these examples requires checking the hypotheses of Theorem 2.10 or Definition 2.6. [§§1.1 and 1.4, pp. 543 and 546](https://doi.org/10.1112/topo.12288).

**Gluck-twist qualification.** A trivial mapping-cylinder action of the Gluck diffeomorphism of $S^2\times S^1$ is equivalent to $\mathbb{CP}^2$-stability for a semisimple $Z$ (Theorem C). Under that extra condition Corollary D allows arbitrary closed oriented four-manifolds related by an orientation-preserving homotopy equivalence. B(2) already covers the homotopy-sphere specialization without requiring a trivial action on $Z(S^2\times S^1)$. [Theorem C and Corollary D, p. 544, and §4, pp. 559–562](https://doi.org/10.1112/topo.12288).

## Draft entry for the import ledger

**`Reutter2023Semisimple`, Corollary B(2), using Theorem A and Definitions 2.1 and 2.6.** Let $k$ be algebraically closed and let $Z\colon\mathrm{Bord}_4\to\mathrm{Vect}_k$ be an ordinary symmetric monoidal functor on the smooth oriented bordism category whose local-operator and fusion algebras are semisimple. If $M,N$ are closed simply connected smooth oriented four-manifolds and admit an orientation-preserving homotopy equivalence, their scalar values $Z(M),Z(N)\in k$ agree. In particular $Z(\Sigma)=Z(S^4)$ for every smooth closed oriented homotopy four-sphere $\Sigma$. Evidence level is imported theorem, with statement and proof passages checked in the 2023 version of record on 27 November 2026. [Corollary B(2), p. 543, and proof in §3.2, p. 557](https://doi.org/10.1112/topo.12288).
