**Import.** Qiuyu Ren and Hongjian Yang, *The Gorsky–Oblomkov–Rasmussen conjecture over* \(\mathbb Q\), arXiv:2611.10885v1, 7 November 2026. Theorem 1.4(4), equations (2)–(3) and §4.4 give all trigraded dimensions of the classical rational \(\mathfrak{gl}_2\) skein lasagna module of the closed oriented \(\overline{\mathbb{CP}^{2}}\) with empty boundary link. Write \(A_n(t,q)\) for the rational Khovanov Poincaré polynomial of \(T(n,n-1)\) in Theorem 1.4(1). In the Ren–Willis grading conventions, let \(L_\alpha(t,q)\) denote the series for the skein class \(\alpha[\overline{\mathbb{CP}^{1}}]\). The variable \(t\) records homological degree and \(q\) quantum degree. For \(\epsilon\in\{0,1\}\) the residue of \(\alpha\) modulo two, the formulas are

$$
\begin{aligned}
L_0(t,q) &= 1+\sum_{r\geq 1}t^{-2r^2}q^{6r^2-4r+1}A_{2r}(t,q^{-1}),\\
L_1(t,q) &= \sum_{r\geq 1}t^{-2r^2+2r}q^{6r^2-10r+4}A_{2r-1}(t,q^{-1}),\\
L_\alpha(t,q) &= t^{(\alpha^2-\epsilon)/2}q^{-(\alpha^2-\epsilon)/2}L_\epsilon(t,q).
\end{aligned}
$$

The computation resolves Ren–Willis v3, Conjecture 6.15. The proof uses the two-handle formula for the \((-1)\)-framed unknot. After mirroring and reorienting the cables, the symmetrized dotted-annulus transition maps are injective by §4.1. Their successive cokernels give the shifted \(A_n\) terms in §4.4, beginning with the empty-link or unknot term. This gives an exact colimit calculation over \(\mathbb Q\), determining the graded vector space up to graded isomorphism. Its scope is the ordinary rank-two module, while the integral, other-field, higher-rank four-manifold and Lee theories require separate inputs.
