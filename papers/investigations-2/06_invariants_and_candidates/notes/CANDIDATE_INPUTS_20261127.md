**Import applicable to an unmarked closed invariant.** Gay–Kirby §4.1, Lemma 4.7, constructs the presentation sphere of a balanced presentation of the trivial group as the identity double of a four-dimensional 0-, 1-, 2-handle realization of the presentation.
