# Chiral Weyl energy and the smooth four-dimensional Poincaré conjecture

Working paper by Edward B. Baker III, 25 November 2026. [Reading copy](../../../output/pdf/master/chiral_weyl_energy_and_smooth_poincare.pdf) · [Main LaTeX file](chiral_weyl_energy_and_smooth_poincare.tex)

The main file contains the common geometric introduction, recognition and curvature budgets, the one-sided replacement inequality, outstanding questions and the disclosure of language-model use. The proof uses [marked smooth caps](marked_cut.tex), the [first-order splice](first_order_splice.tex), [positive paths and local variational replacement](variational_comparison.tex), [physical volume and joint chiral selection](physical_volume.tex), [complete rescalings and curvature on physical energy scales](complete_limits.tex), [physical Ricci scales](ricci_scales.tex) and the [critical harmonic annulus](critical_annulus.tex). The [immersion criterion](immersion_entry.tex) gives a separate direct recognition route.

The current edition includes the round-boundary spectral form, the exterior height test, conditional recognition from the two surgery orders, simultaneous collar removal and the scale-anchored annulus. The Ricci-scale section also gives a uniform recognition criterion for near-Einstein Yamabe metrics with one small chiral eigenvalue defect.
