# Chiral Weyl energy and the smooth four-dimensional Poincaré conjecture

Edward B. Baker III, working paper, 25 November 2026. [Reading copy](../../../output/pdf/master/chiral_weyl_energy_and_smooth_poincare.pdf) · [Main LaTeX file](chiral_weyl_energy_and_smooth_poincare.tex) · [Companion manuscripts](../README.md#working-papers-for-the-parallel-investigations)

The master gives direct recognition from a positive class or an [induced metric and selected height](immersion_entry.tex). The intrinsic argument goes from [marked topology and the round-boundary form](marked_cut.tex) through the [first-order curvature cost](first_order_splice.tex), [positive paths and local replacements](variational_comparison.tex), and the remaining mass after disk repairs. It analyzes [complete physical blow-ups](complete_limits.tex), [Ricci scales and near-Einstein recognition](ricci_scales.tex) and the [critical physical annulus](critical_annulus.tex).

The [endpoint disk theorem and collar cost](chiral_weyl_energy_and_smooth_poincare.tex) identify the remaining diffuse-repair estimate at the unattained recognition endpoint. The [descent proof](../01_fixed_type_variational_descent/manuscript/endpoint_charge.tex) gives the auxiliary-volume comparison.

The companion papers carry the sharp shape and sign comparisons, the full mixed-surgery proof, the long-buffer cap estimates, the proof of critical shell regularity, and diffuse mass selection. The [physical-volume and joint-selection source](physical_volume.tex) is typeset in the fixed-type descent paper. Its comparison gauge, physical measures and repeatedly cited source path remain available. [Editorial proof and label crosswalk](../research-progress/handoffs/E51_COMPANION_DRAFTS_20261125.md)
