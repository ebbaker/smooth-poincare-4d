Let \(e=E_+=E_-\) be the common closed Weyl energy in the Hilbert–Schmidt convention on real two-forms, \(T=4\pi^2\), and \(I^+(M)=\inf_{Y([g])>0} E_+(g)\). The [existing uniform recognition gap](../manuscript/recognition_gap.tex) leaves only \(T+\varepsilon_*<I^+(M)<2T\) on a hypothetical nonstandard type with entry. ## E · Entry

Write \(s=R_g\), \(Z=\operatorname{Ric}_g-\tfrac{s}{4}g\), and \(A_g=\tfrac12(\operatorname{Ric}_g-\tfrac{s}{6}g)\). In the master normalization \(4\int_M\sigma_2(g^{-1}A_g)\,dV_g=16\pi^2-2e\). Therefore E asks for a class with \(Y>0\) and \(\int_M\sigma_2(g^{-1}A_g)\,dV_g>0\). By Chang–Gursky–Yang this is equivalent to existence on \(M\) of a smooth metric with \(s>0\) and \(\sigma_2(g^{-1}A_g)>0\) everywhere, equivalently \(s>0\) and \(|Z|^2<s^2/12\) everywhere. This implies \(0<2\operatorname{Ric}_g<sg\). Use the Schouten condition itself as the target. Establishing positive scalar curvature alone on arbitrary smooth homotopy spheres is already open. ## R · Rigidity of an attained minimum

**SP4-MASTER-B06 (B, Astra/ultra).** Assume a smooth positive class actually attains \(I^+(M)\) in the surviving band. Positivity is open under smooth perturbations. The Weyl first variation therefore gives Bach-flatness and the second variation is nonnegative on all smooth metric directions, with diffeomorphism and conformal directions removed when studying its strict sign. A nonnegative Hessian is a necessary condition for minimality. Any conjectured negative direction must produce a smooth positive competitor on the same \(M\), with strictly lower energy. A positive class can also be compared by a finite global change. The regularized flow limit supplies criticality for the regularized functional. A stability or minimizing property for that functional would require a separate proof. ## Immersed heights and marked surgery

An embedding in \(\mathbb R^5\) exists on every smooth homotopy four-sphere by the contractible five-manifold and doubling argument. The induced energy bound still has to be constructed.
