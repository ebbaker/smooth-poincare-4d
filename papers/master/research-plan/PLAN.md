## What would prove smooth Poincaré in dimension four?

Fix an arbitrary closed oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere \(M\) and suppose its smooth type is nonstandard. For each such \(M\), it suffices to complete **one** of the following implications. Different constructions may cover different classes of smooth types. A universal conclusion requires a proof that their combined coverage is exhaustive.

1. **Direct recognition.** Construct one smooth positive-Yamabe conformal class on \(M\) with \(E_+\le4\pi^2\), or one global hypersurface immersion \(F:M\to\mathbb R^5\) whose induced class has \(E_+\le64\pi^2/9\). The intrinsic route also accepts $I^+(M)\le T+\varepsilon_*$ with the non-effective uniform gap. Either route contradicts nonstandardness by the master recognition theorems. The immersed shape bound \(\|A^\circ\|_{L^4}^2\le16\sqrt2\,\pi/3\) is another sufficient test. [Intrinsic recognition](../manuscript/chiral_weyl_energy_and_smooth_poincare.tex) · [Immersed recognition](../manuscript/immersion_entry.tex)
2. **Recognition from a subcritical immersed height.** Construct one immersion with \(E_+<8\pi^2\). On a nonstandard candidate, more than three quarters of directions have exactly two index-two points. Select a regular direction and transport its actual framed attaching data through the extremal handle reductions. Prove smooth recognition for the resulting diagram, or show from the supplied geometry that one can choose a direction in a recognized subclass. The measure bound selects a count. Which reduced handle diagram occurs is a further question. This implication uses no Yamabe sign.
3. **Intrinsic descent from positive entry.** Construct a positive class with \(E_+<8\pi^2\). It supplies a sequence of volume-one positive Yamabe metrics \(g_i\) on this very \(M\) with \(e_i=E_+(g_i)\to I^+(M)\) and a fixed margin below \(8\pi^2\). Construct one admissible sequence and subsequence for which the measured cut contradiction, another same-type strict improvement, or independent recognition holds. If working by classification, account for every alternative still possible on the chosen sequence. A hypothetical immersed entry can supply this initial class when its closed Yamabe sign is established. Subsequent minimizing classes can be general.

Here \(E_+\) uses the Hilbert–Schmidt norm on \(\Lambda^+\). Existence of a smooth immersion on every type gives the domain of the first two constructions. Its whole-domain curvature budget is open. Master B02 also retains intrinsic entry constructions. One candidate is a branched-cover metric with a warped transition. In the four product patches tested in E15, fixed tangential area and normal disk area \(O(\varepsilon^2)\) force the chiral cost to diverge for every choice of a common cone angle as \(\varepsilon\to0\). A new model must realize the prescribed smooth type, match the exterior jets, and estimate both the full chiral cost and the global scalar quadratic form. [Common-angle calculation and its hypotheses](../notes/E15_SHEET_ANGLES.md)

## Research priorities after the marked tests

1. Seek one source marking on a separated physical sequence at a scale between the smaller and median chiral radii. It suffices to mark one spherical cone component out to a fixed width depending on the Yamabe floor, ellipticity and anchor distance. Obtain the embedding and the central metric estimates in the original physical gauge. The new proof locates both full energy marks.
2. In parallel, construct an actual retained exterior. For intrinsic entry test the round end form or its scalar Robin sufficient condition and target the strict subcritical energy or inclusive direct threshold. For an immersed exterior keep the angular coverage of each prescribed height filling. On an actual two-middle height record the two surgery orders and their geometric boundary identifications.
3. On diffuse physical sequences, estimate the square sum of collar errors for charged, parametrized standard balls. Compare their total raw charge against that surcharge and the single minimizing excess. This comparison permits an arbitrary retained exterior and requires no disappearance of all complement energy.
4. For bounded physical supercritical Ricci, test the two small defects in the new near-Einstein theorem on the same Yamabe representative. If one survives, the deficit estimate narrows the possible limit but supplies no negative variation. Seek a signed comparison on that survivor or a genuinely different round restart with strict gain.

## Deciding the intrinsic alternatives

- **Separated quantiles.** For one fixed \(0<\theta<1/2\) suppose \(r_i(\theta)/r_i(1/2)\to0\) along a subsequence. The low-full-curvature block is already located. Try the physical transition-shell upper volume test to control long components, then recover a single complete radial marking. For the critical-annulus criterion, fix \(A(1/8,8)\) and its protected trim before taking \(i\to\infty\). In the actual splice coordinates test the distortion and the differentiated coordinate tension after constant physical rescaling. Preserve the two entire mass sets. If one component estimate succeeds while marking fails, retain the improvement and identify the missing global chart. [Cut criterion](../manuscript/critical_annulus.tex) · [Component test](../reviews/E25_ANNULAR_VOLUME.md)
- **Diffuse at the median scale.** At fixed tolerance choose the comparison domains before the source limit. The positive-Ricci gauge locates balls, while the physical metric supplies volume and chiral mass. A different comparison gauge must have its own estimates. Count escaped mass, nonregular mass, excluded atoms and selection error. For a positive same-type competitor \(h_i\), a proved net gain \(e_i-E_+(h_i)>e_i-I^+(M)\) at even one index is enough. If the replacement consumes all the removed mass, change its geometry or compare a whole marked region. [Joint selection](../manuscript/physical_volume.tex) · [Regular disk obstruction](../notes/E17_DIFFUSE_REPAIR.md)
- **Attainment or loss at an end.** A bounded global physical trace-free Ricci \(L^p\) norm for fixed \(p>2\) gives closed attainment. At a nonround minimizer seek rigidity or a genuine negative variation transverse to the conformal and diffeomorphism directions. When round convergence leaves all chiral mass at one exit, track that charged exit and test a strictly decreasing or well-founded quantity against the repeatable disk graft. A complete marked limit, ALE geometry, or a useful gauge is a hypothesis to obtain on the selected sequence. [Ricci-scale theorem](../manuscript/ricci_scales.tex) · [Restart test](../notes/E21_ROUND_RESTART.md)
- **If no useful marked limit is available.** Locate the supercritical physical Ricci failure relative to the charge, and return the uncovered regions to B03/B04. If using [Bach flow](../../investigations-1/02_bach_flow/README.md), require time slices at the same fixed-type infimum with small Bach error in the precise compactness norm. The metric at a curvature maximum has no guaranteed mass on its own scale. A classification of smooth closed limits alone leaves the unmarked case open.
