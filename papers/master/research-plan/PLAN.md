## What would prove smooth Poincaré in dimension four?

For a closed connected oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere $M$, put $e=E_+=E_-$ in the real two-form Hilbert–Schmidt convention, $T=4\pi^2$, and $I^+(M)=\inf_{Y([g])>0}e(g)$. All metrics are Riemannian. The two open assertions are **E**, that every such $M$ admits a positive Yamabe class with $e<2T$, and **S**, that every $M$ admitting one is standard. Together they are equivalent to smooth Poincaré. An independent recognition criterion may settle a type outright. For S the master can seek a geometric cut in an entering class, or use 06's working attainment theorem **X** and the lower gap to reduce S to **R**, the exclusion of a nonstandard attained minimum in $T+\varepsilon_*<I^+(M)<2T$.
