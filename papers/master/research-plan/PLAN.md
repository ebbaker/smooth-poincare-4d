## Proof objective and order of work

Assume a counterexample exists and fix its oriented smooth type $M$. Write $T=4\pi^2$. The first required input is a positive Yamabe class with $E_+<2T$. At this point a positive-class minimizing sequence exists with a fixed energy margin and a uniform positive Yamabe lower bound. Selection of a Yamabe representative in each class preserves the chiral measure. These selections alone give no fourth-order estimate.

The shortest proposed finish is one actual marked separating sphere on one such sequence. The raw sides must each carry a fixed positive amount of chiral energy. If each capped side has positive Yamabe constant and the excess cost on each tends to zero, the two capped energies sum to less than $2T$. Recognition makes one capped piece standard. The positive class on the remaining smooth type $M$ then has energy strictly below its infimum. This would rule out the counterexample. It suffices to exhibit one qualifying sequence on each hypothetical counterexample. Smooth convergence of all such sequences or a bubble tree identity for the whole residual would be stronger.

Run the entry and cut investigations in parallel, with the cut investigation conditional on entry. Start the cut work with two measures on the same sources, namely physical Yamabe probability and the positive chiral curvature measure. Record centers and entire opposite-side sets, and the constants for the two buffers in the gauge used for the splice. If the measured volume balls account for chiral energy, the paper gives a second possible finish. Construct a positive, same-type replacement of each chosen marked ball with controlled *net* energy relative to the raw exterior. If the number is bounded at fixed tolerance, the summed replacement inequality forces the union's energy to zero when the total cost tends to zero. A bound for the chiral energy of the small-volume complement then gives the contradiction. The replacements need only work individually. For a concentrating sequence, test the complement directly.

If no such geometry appears, distinguish chiral concentration from a compact limit before investing in additional regularity. A marked nonflat admissible ALE blow-up on an approximately critical minimizing sequence gives same-type strict descent under the hypotheses in the overview. A whole nonround marked base at minimizing energy can instead carry the entire energy. The latter branch requires a theorem excluding a closed nonround minimizer, or an actual lower-energy competitor. A round limit can lose the smooth type at a puncture and restart at exactly the old infimum. A restart needs a strictly improving quantity or a well-founded termination argument. Each of these alternatives returns to the same original smooth manifold.

At each revision, ask which new theorem enters the shortest complete implication. A local result can stay in its owning investigation until it supplies such an implication or identifies a necessary obstruction. If entry alone proves impossible for the proposed approach, reopen B01: a direct global construction of $W^+=0$ or a positive class with $E_+\le T$ also proves the conjecture and need not pass through the larger energy band.
