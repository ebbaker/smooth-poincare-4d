## Proof objective and order of work

Assume a counterexample exists and fix its oriented smooth type $M$. Write $T=4\pi^2$. The first required input is a positive Yamabe class with $E_+<2T$. At this point a positive-class minimizing sequence exists with a fixed energy margin and a uniform positive Yamabe lower bound. Selection of a Yamabe representative in each class preserves the chiral measure. These selections alone give no fourth-order estimate.

The shortest proposed finish is one actual marked separating sphere on one such sequence. The raw sides must each carry a fixed positive amount of chiral energy. If each capped side has positive Yamabe constant and the excess cost on each tends to zero, the two capped energies sum to less than $2T$. Recognition makes one capped piece standard. The positive class on the remaining smooth type $M$ then has energy strictly below its infimum. This would rule out the counterexample. It suffices to exhibit one qualifying sequence on each hypothetical counterexample. Smooth convergence of all such sequences or a bubble tree identity for the whole residual would be stronger.

Run the entry and cut investigations in parallel, with the cut investigation conditional on entry. Start the cut work with two measures on the same sources, namely physical Yamabe probability and the positive chiral curvature measure. Record centers and entire opposite-side sets, and the constants for the two buffers in the gauge used for the splice. If the measured volume balls account for chiral energy, the paper gives a second possible finish. Construct a positive, same-type replacement of each chosen marked ball with controlled *net* energy relative to the raw exterior. If the number is bounded at fixed tolerance, the summed replacement inequality forces the union's energy to zero when the total cost tends to zero. A bound for the chiral energy of the small-volume complement then gives the contradiction. The replacements need only work individually. For a concentrating sequence, test the complement directly.

If no such geometry appears, distinguish chiral concentration from a compact limit before investing in additional regularity. A marked nonflat admissible ALE blow-up on an approximately critical minimizing sequence gives same-type strict descent under the hypotheses in the overview. A whole nonround marked base at minimizing energy can instead carry the entire energy. The latter branch requires a theorem excluding a closed nonround minimizer, or an actual lower-energy competitor. A round limit can lose the smooth type at a puncture and restart at exactly the old infimum. A restart needs a strictly improving quantity or a well-founded termination argument. Each of these alternatives returns to the same original smooth manifold.

## Next decisions after the physical scale comparison

The initial packet definitions above retain their launch scope. The [live register](../research-progress/REGISTER.md) and [assessment](../research-progress/handoffs/VARIATIONAL_RETURNS_20261125.md) record the revised mechanisms. Use the same volume-one minimizing sequence and its physical measures in the following tests.

1. **B03/B04, two chiral radii.** Fix \(0<\theta<1/2\) and minimize the ball radius over all centers at fractions \(\theta\) and \(1/2\). Their ratio can vanish for some fixed \(\theta\), or the chiral concentration function vanishes uniformly at small multiples of the median in the iterated limit. In the first case the center attaining the smaller radius has at least half the total energy outside its ball of the larger radius. Select a low-full-curvature annulus between these actual sets. Seek one spherical chart across a fixed short log interval with uniform and strong \(W^{1,4}\) comparison and bounded full comparison curvature. Test the marking on **both entire sets**. Preserve the signed excess relative to the incoming strip. Failure of this gauge can lead to a different conformal comparison, provided its local scalar square or full curvature is checked there.
2. **B03, diffuse chiral mass at the median.** Study intermediate radii or a whole marked base. At the maximum-curvature scale every fixed multiple carries vanishing full energy. A bounded collection of such balls cannot hold a fixed chiral fraction. In the diffuse alternative try a finite repair family on regular points with a direct cost-to-incoming-energy estimate. Control escape, nonregular mass and excluded atoms separately in the positive-Ricci distance; supply any transfer to physical median radii. Small total physical volume alone controls none of these terms.
3. **B04, closed attainment and restart.** Test stability of the closed Bach-flat minimizer outside the excluded Einstein range against an explicit compactly supported or transverse-traceless variation on the same type, or another positive competitor. A round base can still pass all its energy to one puncture. Termination requires a decrement or a quantified reduction of the remaining geometric complexity. A shrinking physical diameter, or a high-curvature point with vanishing energy on its own scale, supplies neither.
4. **B02, global entry.** A single common base cone angle fails for all fixed-area simple product patches. Test a construction changing the tangential area and the mixed curvatures, for instance a doubly warped collar with specified outer jets on every sheet. Track \(A_\epsilon/\epsilon^2\), the cost of restoring the tangential geometry, and a lower bound for the global scalar form. If the smooth marked model on arbitrary types cannot be supplied, revisit a handle decomposition with the entire exterior included in both estimates. No branch-cover topology theorem supplies this smooth metric.
5. **Selection alternative.** For independent Bach flows starting near the infimum, prove adequate normalized lifespan versus excess before using a small integrated Bach norm. This is optional for the variational complete-limit route. Keep the direct \(E_+\le4\pi^2\) recognition shortcut available.

Freeze a new E request only once its candidate domain and mathematical test are chosen. B05/E06 require universal entry plus exhaustion of the surviving cases or a direct recognition proof.
