## Exact positive classes in a fixed band

Use smooth closed oriented homotopy four-spheres, with positive-definite Riemannian conformal classes. Write \(T=4\pi^2\), \(e=E_+=E_-\) in the chiral Hilbert–Schmidt convention, \(L=\max(\lambda_{\max}(W^+),\lambda_{\max}(W^-))\), \(\sigma([g])=\int L^2dV\), and \(\Delta([g])=36\sigma([g])-Y([g])^2\). The spectral density is conformally invariant. Fix \(\delta>0\) and let \(\mathcal C_\delta\) be the positive exactly Bach-flat classes on nonstandard smooth types with \(e\le2T-\delta\).

There are only finitely many oriented smooth types represented by \(\mathcal C_\delta\). If it is nonempty, the number

\[
 m_\delta=\inf_{\mathcal C_\delta}\Delta
\]

is attained and strictly positive. On any one represented type the corresponding infimum is also attained and strictly positive. For any fixed \(0\le b<6\kappa_\delta\), the volume-one Yamabe metrics in the exact sublevel \(\Delta\le m_\delta+b\) are sequentially compact modulo oriented diffeomorphisms, allowing the source types to vary. Here \(\kappa_\delta>0\) is the NECK same-type chiral-energy decrement in the fixed band. The identical statement holds with the typewise minimum and restriction to that type. Empty families give vacuous universal statements.

Choose volume-one **ordinary Yamabe minimizers** of the exact classes. They are smooth, and Bach-flatness and both quantities \(e,\sigma\) are conformally invariant. The closed gap puts all sources in \(T<e_i\le2T-\delta\), with \(\sqrt{48\delta}\le Y_i\le8\sqrt6\pi\). The exact compactness alternative applies to variable sources \(M_i\). In its proof the Sobolev and total curvature bounds, normalized volume and Betti-number bound are uniform and carry no fixed-type assumption. When the curvature maxima diverge, the pointed smooth limit at those maxima is nonflat and scalar-flat. The exact critical Ricci equation can also give the order-zero ALE end conclusion directly in this limit: its homogeneous Sobolev inequality gives lower volume growth, and finite \(L^2\) curvature with critical interior estimates gives \(o(r^{-2})\) curvature decay. The differentiated positive end order follows from the scalar-flat obstruction-flat theorem. These steps give the ALE comparison with whole compact truncations that the owning theorem requires. Pass to limits \(e_i\to e\), \(Y_i\to y>0\) and \(\sigma_i\to s\). In the closed branch there are oriented diffeomorphisms from one smooth positive exact limit \((N,h)\) onto all sufficiently late sources of the subsequence. The limiting scalar equals the Yamabe constant \(y\) by passing the Yamabe inequality to each test and using the constant test at volume one. Smooth curvature convergence and continuity of the two top eigenvalues give \(\sigma([h])=s\). The closed limit realizes both the limiting energy and spectral deficit.

In the ALE branch each full truncation of \((X,h)\) has an oriented comparison, and the limit has one ordinary end. The owning theorem compactifies it smoothly by one point to \((\widehat X,\bar h)\), positive and Bach-flat, with

\[
Y([\bar h])\ge y,\qquad T<e_X=E_+(\bar h)\le e-\kappa_\delta.
\]

The one-point completion is orientation-preservingly diffeomorphic to **each** sufficiently late \(M_i\) along the chosen subsequence. For this step the exact theorem uses a whole compact truncation with a buffered normal annulus in the smooth puncture chart and the strict residual \(e-e_X<T\). It straightens and caps the actual exterior with positive Yamabe sign and chiral energy below \(T\). Once that cap is recognized, the filled exterior can be replaced by the actual marked disk. Smooth identification of the completed model and the sources is part of the proof. A punctured local comparison or the existence of a smooth topological compactification alone would leave the diffeomorphism unproved. To transfer the spectral integral, hold a compact truncation \(K\subset X\) fixed and integrate in the smooth scaled source comparison. The measure \(L^2dV\) is unchanged by constant rescaling and by the later conformal compactification. Smooth convergence gives convergence on \(K\). Taking the limit over the source first, then exhausting \(X\), gives \(\sigma_X:=\sigma([\bar h])\le s\), because the added smooth point has measure zero. There is a corresponding quantitative bound on the residual. Pointwise, \(|W^+|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\le6L^2\). Apply this to the **entire actual complement** of the compared \(K\) before taking the source limit. Then exhaust \(X\), which gives

\[
 e-e_X\le6(s-\sigma_X),\qquad
 \Delta([\bar h])\le\lim_i\Delta_i-6(e-e_X)\le\lim_i\Delta_i-6\kappa_\delta . \tag{1}
\]

This does not assume an energy identity for a bubble tree. The comparison on each truncation gives both complementary integrals by subtracting its integral from the single source total, and the conformal extension preserves the limit integrals. The factor six in (1) is \(36/6\) in the stated chiral normalization.

Both branches identify all late members of a subsequence with one fixed oriented smooth type. An infinite list of pairwise distinct types in \(\mathcal C_\delta\) would contradict this by taking one class per type. A sequence with \(\Delta_i\to m_\delta\) cannot have the ALE branch, since its completion belongs to the same band on a nonstandard type and (1) would give \(\Delta<m_\delta\). This proves attainment in the closed branch. The same argument for \(\Delta_i\le m_\delta+b\) with \(b<6\kappa_\delta\) proves the compactness assertion. Restricting to one type is allowed because the ALE completion has that type. Finally, Chen–Zhu's strict and equality classifications imply \(\Delta>0\) for every smooth positive class on a nonstandard homotopy sphere. In the connected-sum classification simple connectivity removes the projective and cylindrical summands. At equality the additional projective complex plane and compact simply connected two-surface product have nonzero second homology. Hence the attained exact minima are strictly positive. ## Uniform isolation near \(I^+(M)\)

For each of the finitely many represented types define

\[
\mathcal A_M=\{E_+([g]):B_g=0,\ Y([g])>0,\ 0<E_+([g])<2T\},\qquad
a_M=\inf\mathcal A_M .
\]

The NECK E15 theorem gives an attained \(a_M\) and an \(\epsilon_M>0\) with \(\mathcal A_M\cap[a_M,a_M+\epsilon_M)=\{a_M\}\). Its near-first-value compactness uses the strict positive same-type decrement, and its local constancy of exact energy follows by analytic Bach gauge and the finite-dimensional gradient inequality. The admissible infimum here is \(I^+(M)\le a_M\), taken over **all** smooth positive classes.

If \(a_M>I^+(M)\), take \(\gamma_M=(a_M-I^+(M))/2\); no exact class meets \(e-I^+(M)<\gamma_M\) there. If \(a_M=I^+(M)\), take \(\gamma_M=\epsilon_M/2\), and any such class has exactly \(e=I^+(M)\). Taking the minimum of these finitely many positive numbers gives a non-effective universal \(\gamma_\delta>0\) for the band. Thus any positive exact class on a nonstandard type with \(e\le2T-\delta\) and \(e-I^+(M)<\gamma_\delta\) attains the true variational infimum. Such minimizers also form a smoothly sequentially compact family modulo oriented diffeomorphisms. After passing to one type, a hypothetical ALE branch would give a positive same-type class of energy strictly below \(I^+(M)\). The threshold gives no existence of exact near-infimum classes. An isolated first exact value above \(I^+\) may be a saddle, and E15 does not give a finite spectrum in the entire band. ## Filled bases and restarts when the input deficit tends to zero

For a physical minimizing sequence on a fixed nonstandard type in the strict band with \(\Delta_i\to0\), assume the whole filled-base hypotheses of the descent manuscript. Each fixed compact in the punctured base has a smooth oriented comparison in a conformal gauge with uniform and strong \(W^{1,4}\) metric convergence, bounded full \(L^2\) curvature there, and a smooth extension of the limit across finitely many marked ordinary points. The strong local chiral matrix-density convergence also implies convergence of the spectral integral on each such compact. The largest eigenvalue is Lipschitz in the self-adjoint block with its Hilbert–Schmidt norm, and the maximum of the two preserves this estimate. Applying it to the volume-square-root densities gives strong local \(L^2\) convergence of the top-eigenvalue maximum density. The ordered exhaustion and transported Yamabe tests give \(\sigma_N\le\lim\sigma_i\) and \(Y_N\ge\lim Y_i=y\). Thus \(\Delta_N\le0\), so the filled base is standard. If its chiral energy were positive, the whole filled-base comparison on a minimizing sequence would identify the completed base with the original nonstandard type and attain \(I^+(M)\) there. This is impossible. Consequently the allowed smooth filled base is conformally round with one actual raw exterior carrying all the limiting chiral energy in each sign under the marked cuts. The local comparison alone cannot locate that exterior without the full puncture markings and the cap argument.
