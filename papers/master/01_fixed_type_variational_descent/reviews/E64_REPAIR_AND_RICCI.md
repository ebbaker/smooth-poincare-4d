### Signed spectral allowance from local Weyl density estimates

For either chirality let \(F_\pm=(\det m)^{1/4}W_m^\pm\) be the trace-free symmetric Weyl matrix in the oriented orthonormalized coordinate frame of the local metric \(m\), represented relative to the common coordinate measure \(dx\). Let \(L(F)=\max_\pm\lambda_{\max}(F_\pm)\) and \(dp=[2(|F_+|^2+|F_-|^2)+3L(F)^2]dx\), the averaged spectral density. On the same measured exterior band \(U\), suppose \(\|F'_\pm-\theta F_\pm\|_{2,U}\le b_\pm\) for a common nonnegative multiplier \(\theta\). A trace-free symmetric three-by-three matrix \(H\) satisfies \(\|H\|_{\rm op}\le\sqrt{2/3}\|H\|_{\rm HS}\): for each eigenvalue the other two sum to its negative and have squares summing to at least half its square. Rayleigh's quotient for the largest eigenvalue and the maximum over the two blocks give
\[
 |L(F')-\theta L(F)|\le\sqrt{2/3}\max_\pm |F'_\pm-\theta F_\pm|_{\rm HS}.
\]
For the augmented density vector \(V(F)=(\sqrt2 F_+,\sqrt2 F_-,\sqrt3 L(F))\), it follows that
\[
 \|V(F')-\theta V(F)\|_{2,U}\le 2\sqrt{b_+^2+b_-^2}.
\]
The coefficient is sharp for this norm estimate, by taking \(F=0\) and a single perturbed block with eigenvalues proportional to \((2,-1,-1)\). Thus the actual exterior increment of the averaged spectral integral is at most
\[
 \left(\sqrt{\int_U\theta^2\,dp}+2\sqrt{b_+^2+b_-^2}\right)^2-p(U).
\]
For a conformally flat fill the added spectral density is zero. With disjoint collars the same bound uses their direct-sum \(L^2\) estimates and common piecewise attenuation on the actual union. The positivity budget still uses the joined \(E_+\) wall or an independent Yamabe test.
