# Smooth four-dimensional Poincaré conjecture

The master asks two questions. Does every smooth homotopy four-sphere admit a positive Yamabe class with chiral Weyl energy below $8\pi^2$? Is every sphere admitting such a class diffeomorphic to $S^4$? Together, entry and recognition are equivalent to smooth Poincaré. Both remain open. [Manuscript and proof locations](manuscript/README.md) · [Logical reduction](manuscript/claude/logical_form.tex) · [Current overview](research-overview/OVERVIEW.md)

The prescribed width-\(1/16\) long-cuff metric on the minus Miller–Schupp sphere exceeds the chiral energy budget in both signs. The native marking and its first derivatives are enclosed over both complete bands. The remaining scalar sign comparison is a four-dimensional integral. For recognition, the specified HMP cross-gluing and Akbulut–Yasui twisted double now carry degree-one witnesses on different native cuts. At an attained minimum, a supplied umbilic \(S^3\) with a Cotton-flat patch suffices. [Current results and proof status](research-progress/handoffs/NATIVE_COST_AND_TWISTED_DOUBLE_20261127.md) · [Master manuscript](manuscript/README.md) · [Continuation](continuation-notes/AFTER_NATIVE_COST_AND_TWISTED_DOUBLE_20261127.md)

The lower recognition gap, fixed-band finiteness under attainment and immersed-height threshold retain their stated hypotheses. The latest independent surgery result covers product doubles of free three-string tangles with a designated boundary-parallel strand and their product closing.
