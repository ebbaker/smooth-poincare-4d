The finite test gives an affordable strictly descending direction wherever the Bach tensor is nonzero in the patch. Its saving on a positive clamped path is at most the minimizing excess. The product-germ example realizes the local mass and raw curvature hypotheses on a standard source in the strict band, and forbids every clamped lowering path below the wall there. It does not realize a nonstandard minimizing sequence. On a large disk, write \(r=2^{-k}\) and let \(Q_i\) denote the original protected disk.
