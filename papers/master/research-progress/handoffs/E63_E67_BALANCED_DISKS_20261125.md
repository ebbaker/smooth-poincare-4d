## Smooth-type comparison and scope

1. **Original disk and connected repair.** E61 provides a small marked disk on each late source with the same sign charged on both whole sides. E63a constructs a second disk carrying nearly the opposite net excess when the first one has an imbalance. Those two signs alone do not give a common local saving. On a clamped shaft of radius \(\rho\) and length \(L\), transfer beyond the weaker raw lobe charges and the shaft mass costs a slice flux bounded by \(C(\rho/L)^{3/4}[\|A\|_4(\|F_g\|_2+\|F_h\|_2)+\|A\|_4^3]\) in its actual product coordinates. A bounded thin connector cannot transfer a fixed excess by itself. The connected first-order cutoff supplies a strict same-type competitor when the measured endpoint estimate beats the infimum; its convex path estimate then keeps the joined class positive. The required estimates on the original core or a coupled replacement are open.
2. **Enlarged disk.** For any fixed \(k\), apply simultaneous bisection in a standard ball chart of the actual exterior. Preserve the E61 disk and its marking strictly inside the new standard disk. With \(r=2^{-k}\), the raw residual for each of \(\lambda^+,\lambda^-,L^2dV,|\mathrm{Rm}_{g_i}|^2dV_{g_i}\) is \(r\) times its original mass outside the protected disk \(Q_i\), up to arbitrary per-source error. This is global smooth engulfment; the small comparison-ball curvature ceiling applies only to the original disk. The two chiral residuals are below \(T\) already at \(k=1\) throughout the band. The actual positive cap must be diffeomorphic to the source. A joined endpoint below \(2T\), with measured division error \(\tau_i\) and capped energy at most its joined exterior charge plus \(d_i\), must save more on the disk side than
   \[
      (1-r)e_i+rE_s(g_i;Q_i)+I-2T-\tau_i-d_i .
   \]
   At \(I=4T/3\) the selected sign has a positive lower bound already at \(k=1\). A single universal depth forces a saving above \(\varepsilon_*\) in the limit throughout the surviving band. A path to the exact matched seam that changes only a final collar of vanishing incoming curvature must therefore reach the wall. A repair changing the charged interior and one acting on the original small marking remain live. The spectral cap allowance uses the two chiral matrix errors at the chosen width on the actual retained band. The finite cap thresholds include equality; the joined wall requires strict inequality.
3. **Entry and marked doubles.** On the eta-zero Mazur face in the contact orientation, \(\ker D=0\), \(\eta_D=\eta_{\rm sig}=0\) and the ordinary chiral APS index is zero on the contractible product-germ filling. The positive-scalar filling would have strictly unstable totally geodesic boundary. For an actual such retained metric \(h\) with boundary in the prescribed class, write \(B=E_+(h;W)+E_-(h;W)\), \(G=\int_W L_h^2dV_h\). Product approximation and the free-trace form give actual positive classes with \(P\to4B+6G\) on each double glued by an orientation-preserving isometry of this exact face. Either \(B\le T+\varepsilon_*\) or \(4B+6G\le8T+\eta\) recognizes the corresponding doubles. A strict \(B<2T\) gives entry. The known example doubles are standard. The spin tests leave the metric on the half unconstructed, and no further smooth type has acquired entry.
4. **Physical shell.** For surviving, uncut geodesics at log radius \(l\), put \(p(l,\vartheta)=J(e^l,\vartheta)e^{-3l}\). A common bound \(\int p(l,\vartheta)^2/p(s,\vartheta)\,d\vartheta\le C e^{2\gamma(l-s)}\), \(\gamma<1\), on a long protected interval gives a selected physical window with full curvature and total radial defect tending to zero. Downward cut-locus jumps are included. This removes the pointwise radial Ricci floor from the supplied-shell criterion. Proper flat compact embeddings at this same scale and the moment estimate on the actual minimizing source remain open. Only together do they produce the marked shell with both complete quantile sets.
5. **Modified Ricci charge.** When \(\Delta_i=36\int L_i^2-Y_i^2\to0\) in a fixed strict band, the small modified-volume sets from E62 carry definite modified trace-free Ricci charge and vanishing scalar and Weyl energy. A complete smooth shrinking limit under locally uniform and strong \(W^{1,4}\) compact-exhaustion markings is Euclidean. The exact end formula \(d_X=16\pi^2(k-2+\sum 1/m_j)\), with \(y\le8\sqrt6\,\pi/\sqrt{m_j}\), excludes multiple ends under \(d_X<y^2/12\). The remaining end is Euclidean and the Euler transgression gives vanishing full energy on fixed compared compacts. Maximum-curvature-scale balls lose full energy uniformly in their centers. An arbitrary small-volume set need not be contained in a marked ball of any shrinking scale.
6. **Exact closed types and restart.** For fixed \(\delta>0\), only finitely many nonstandard oriented smooth types can have a positive Bach-flat class with \(e\le2T-\delta\). In the marked ALE alternative both a smooth compactification on the late source type and its positive class are required. Its deficit drops by at least \(6\kappa_\delta\). Consequently, if the exact family is nonempty its spectral deficit has a strictly positive attained minimum \(m_\delta\). Every fixed closed sublevel below \(m_\delta+6\kappa_\delta\) is smoothly sequentially compact up to oriented diffeomorphisms. The first exact value on a represented type can exceed its all-positive-class infimum. Finiteness and fixed-type isolation give a uniform threshold below which an exact class with energy close to that infimum actually attains it. For arbitrary minimizing sequences a smooth marked filled base at vanishing deficit must be conformally round. One exit carries all limiting chiral and spectral mass. With vanishing inserted and discarded two-sign energies and a retained Yamabe lower bound, the same-type caps can keep the deficit tending to zero. A general signed decrement or termination argument remains open.
