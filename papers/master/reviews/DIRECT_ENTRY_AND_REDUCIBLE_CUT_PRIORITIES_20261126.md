## A change to the master entry problem

Fix a closed oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere \(M\). In the master's real two-form convention, entry means \(Y([g])>0\) and \(e=E_+=E_-<2T=8\pi^2\). Chang–Gursky–Yang give a representative of this class with \(R_g-2\operatorname{Ric}_g(\nu,\nu)>0\) in every unit direction. If \(h\) is the induced metric on a smooth closed two-sided hypersurface \(\Sigma\), \(B^\circ\) its trace-free second fundamental form, and \(H\) its mean-curvature trace, the Gauss equation in that representative gives

\[
 R_h+|B^\circ|_h^2
 =R_g-2\operatorname{Ric}_g(\nu,\nu)+\tfrac23H^2>0 .
\]

Thus a totally umbilic seam in an entering class has positive Yamabe type in its **induced conformal class**. This argument works at any collar width. [Claude's seam argument, §3](../brainstorm/notes/claude/ENTRY_DIRECT_APPROACHES_20261126.md) · [Chang–Gursky–Yang, Theorem A and Corollary B, pp. 712–713](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0409583#page=4)

**For a seam bounding a smooth compact contractible four-manifold \(W\), the conclusion sharpens to \(\partial W\cong S^3\).** Its boundary is an integral homology sphere. Positive scalar curvature allows only \(S^3\) or a sum of signed Poincaré spheres. Chodosh–Maximo–Mukherjee exclude every nonempty such sum as the boundary of a smooth contractible \(W\). For a swap-invariant conformal class on the identity double, a swap-invariant representative has totally geodesic fixed set, and umbilicity persists in the Chang–Gursky–Yang gauge. On the standard Mazur double with face \(\Sigma(2,5,7)\), every such smooth positive class therefore has \(e\ge 8\pi^2\). Reflection exchanges chiralities, so its closed one-sign energy is the sum of the two half energies. Retire the **joint** positive-sign and strict half-budget target for this reflected assembly. The Robin sign of a filling with the prescribed boundary class remains a separate question. A nonumbilic filling must also account for any energy spent when its seam is changed. The conclusion about an \(S^3\) boundary makes no smooth identification of a general contractible filling. [Chodosh–Maximo–Mukherjee, Corollary 2.3 and Proposition 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574) · [Marked double](../../investigations-1/04_hypersurface_immersions/01_immersed_handle_constructions/manuscript/marked_double_exterior.tex) · [Existing Robin test](../../investigations-1/04_hypersurface_immersions/01_immersed_handle_constructions/manuscript/boundary_class.tex)

A bent seam gives a test one can run **before constructing the cap**. With \(\Delta=\operatorname{div}\nabla\), define on the proposed face

\[
 P_{h,B}=-8\Delta_h+R_h+|B^\circ|_h^2 .
\]

An entering completion requires \(\lambda_1(P_{h,B})>0\). Indeed, the displayed Gauss inequality makes the quadratic form positive in the pinched representative. Under an ambient conformal change inducing \(\hat h=v^4h\), the squared trace-free shape scales by \(v^{-4}\), and \(P_{\hat h,\hat B}u=v^{-5}P_{h,B}(vu)\). The sign of the form is therefore available in the *given* boundary metric. Testing on a normalized three-dimensional Yamabe minimizer and applying Hölder gives \(Y(\Sigma,[h])+(\int_\Sigma |B^\circ|^3\,dA_h)^{2/3}>0\). On the prescribed Mazur face \(R_h\) is a negative constant. Even the constant test requires \(\int_\Sigma |B^\circ|^2\,dA_h>-R_h\operatorname{Vol}(h)\). A candidate passing these tests still needs compatible jets, a positive closed conformal-Laplacian form and both whole Weyl integrals. A bound on the bending alone gives no four-dimensional connector cost. ## Which other proposals merit a test

**Folded heights offer a second quantitative target.** For a realized folded map \(f:M^4\to\mathbb R^4\), orient the fold normal away from the local image. Write \(J=\int_{S_{(1,2)}}|\kappa_1\kappa_2\kappa_3|\,dA\) on the parametrized fold, where one curvature is positive and two are negative. In the fold normal form, each such point contributes index two for both opposite normal directions. The Gauss-map area formula gives \(\int_{S^3}m_2(u)\,du=2J\), counting sheets with multiplicity. The mean number of middle handles is \(J/\pi^2\). Thus \(J<2\pi^2\) supplies a Morse height with at most one middle handle and gives direct recognition. Claude's bound \(\int_S |B^\circ_{\rm fold}|^3\,dA<3\sqrt6\,\pi^2\) is a sufficient condition for that strict test. Already \(J<3\pi^2\) supplies a height with at most two. On a hypothetical nonstandard source the fraction with exactly two is at least \(3-J/\pi^2\) in this range. This gives 04/02 a target even when direct recognition fails. The first test should verify the folded tangent bundle for the chosen fold, realize the two sides on the same \(M\), and bound \(J\) or identify a marked direction. The formal existence theorem gives no such curvature bound. The shape measured here is that of the fold immersion in Euclidean space. [Claude, §§5.1–5.3](../brainstorm/notes/claude/ENTRY_DIRECT_APPROACHES_20261126.md) · [Eliashberg–Mishachev, §1.1 and Theorem 1.2.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1108.1270) · [Existing height and handle theorem](../manuscript/immersion_entry.tex)
