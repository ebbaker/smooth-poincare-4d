Run with `bash run_all.sh` (about 30 seconds; needs numpy, scipy, sympy). [output.txt](output.txt) is the log of the last run: 95 `[ok]` lines, 0 `[FAIL]` lines. The first four scripts were written with the note. The fifth was added the same day with the [additions to the master manuscript](../../../../manuscript/claude/umbilic_doubling.tex).

These are floating-point and computer-algebra tests of formulas and constants on explicit examples and random data. They are not proofs. They do not test the topological statements of the note, the cited theorems of Gursky, or anything about Kobayashi's conjecture. The table of cap costs gives the least Euler-Lagrange value found on a scan of the central curvature `K_0` over `[-6, 14]`; the direct minimisation over piecewise constant curvature agreed with it in the six cases tried and never went below it.
