# Checks for the frameworks note and the prism section of the master

27 November 2026. Written by Claude (Anthropic, `claude-fable-5-2`, Cowork session) for Edward B. Baker III, with the [frameworks note](../ALTERNATIVE_FRAMEWORKS_AND_INVESTIGATIONS_2_20261127.md) and the master's section [even_prism_closing.tex](../../../../manuscript/claude/even_prism_closing.tex). The scripts use no repository code. They continue the numbering of the [assessment's checks](../PROGRAM_ASSESSMENT_AND_PATH_FORWARD_20261127_checks/README.md).

| Script | What it checks | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `check_05_prism_groups.py` | The four-generator presentation of the orbifold group in the tube investigation's note E70, by a coset enumeration written for this check. With the meridian of the regular knot killed: order 816, abelianization of order 6, underlying group of order 3, and a surjection onto an explicit model of $(2T\times\mathrm{Dic}_{17})/\{\pm1\}$ built from quaternions. At the longitude $a$: order 6. Abelianizations of the fillings along $a+s\mu$ for $-3\le s\le3$, and orders $\lvert3s-1\rvert$ after killing the two meridians of order two. | 8 ok |
| `check_06_pole_forms.py` | The inversion formula for constant two-forms and the exchange of dualities. For diagonal Bianchi IX metrics: $d(f\sigma_1)$ is closed and self-dual exactly when $f'=2af/(bc)$. A curvature routine for these metrics, validated on flat space, the round sphere and the Fubini–Study metric. For the family $a=r(1+\varepsilon r^2/(1+r^2)^2)$, $b=c=r$: the explicit $f_1$, the asymptotics of $f_2$, and the energies $E_\pm$ for twelve values of $\varepsilon$, with $E_+=E_-$ as a built-in test. | 21 ok |
| `check_07_entropy_and_presentation.py` | The entropies $\lambda(S^k)$, $k\le7$, their closed forms and ordering. The length-fourteen Miller–Schupp presentation of investigation 07 is balanced and presents the trivial group. | 8 ok |

`run_all.sh` runs the three and writes [output.txt](output.txt). The run recorded there used Python 3.13.18, sympy 1.14.0, mpmath 1.3.0 and numpy 2.5.3 in the session's cloud workspace and takes about three minutes.

Limits. `check_05` types the relations from the text of E70 and does not rederive the tangle diagram from which E70 reads them. Its model test checks associativity on a sample and the relations exactly. The two lines marked `info (4)` report that enumerations at the adjacent slopes did not close below 400,000 cosets, which is consistent with infinite groups and proves nothing. `check_06` verifies the pointwise identities and the explicit family; it does not prove Proposition A1 of the note, whose analytic part has no script. Statement (d3) of that script checks where the maximum of the profile sits and its value, from which positivity of $a$ for $\varepsilon>-4$ follows by hand.
