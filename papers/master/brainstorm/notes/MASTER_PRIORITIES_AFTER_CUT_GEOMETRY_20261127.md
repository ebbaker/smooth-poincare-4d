## Proposed opening for the overview

Fix a smooth, closed, connected, oriented Riemannian homotopy four-sphere $M$. Write $T=4\pi^2$ and $e=E_+=E_-$ on $M$, using Hilbert–Schmidt norms on real two-forms. Put $I^+(M)=\inf_{Y([g])>0}e(g)$, with value $+\infty$ if there are no positive classes. On a piece $V$, keep $E_+(V)$ and $E_-(V)$ separately and write $e_V$ for their half-sum. **E** asks whether every such smooth type admits a positive Yamabe class with $e<2T$. **S** asks whether every type admitting such a class is diffeomorphic to $S^4$. Equivalently, S concerns types admitting a metric with $R>0$ and $|\operatorname{Ric}_0|^2<R^2/12$ everywhere. Smooth Poincaré is equivalent to E and S together. An independent recognition theorem can settle a type directly. [Master logical reduction](../../manuscript/claude/logical_form.tex) · [Entry proposition](../../manuscript/chiral_weyl_energy_and_smooth_poincare.tex) · [Chang–Gursky–Yang, Main Theorem](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2002/155-3/p02)

There are two ways to attack S. **Directly**, construct a recognizing cut or sweepout from the geometry available on an entered type. **Variationally**, use 06's working attainment theorem **X** and the recognition gap. A nonstandard entered type would then possess a positive Bach-flat minimum at $T+\varepsilon_*<I^+(M)<2T$. **R** is the open assertion that such a minimum cannot exist. The equivalence of S and R uses these inputs. A direct proof of S would apply even if an analytical step in X needed repair. X would give attainment and, with Chang–Qing–Yang, fixed-band finiteness for the invariant independently of S. Finiteness at each $I^+\le 2T-\delta$, $\delta>0$, permits accumulation at $2T$. The given latitude bounds $\pi/3+5/64<\pi/2$ and $2\pi/3-5/64>\pi/2$ put the two lifted tubes over open hemispheres, including at the proposed switching width $1/16$. The Hopf closing therefore gives, for each band, a reflected class on $S^2\times S^2$ of total Weyl energy $4c_i$. Strict entry would give some $c_i<T$ and hence a double below $4T=16\pi^2$. The Einstein product has total $16T/3$. This would contradict Kobayashi's conjectured global minimum. The implication applies to the specified construction, regardless of the ultimate smooth type of its boundary. One local correction matters for that transfer. With 08's definition of curl, the full $\|\operatorname{curl}B^\circ\|_{3/2}$ can change under conformal rescaling. For flat $h$ and constant $B^\circ=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)$ the curl is zero. At a point where $u=0$, replacing $(h,B^\circ)$ by $(e^{2u}h,e^uB^\circ)$ adds the skew tensor $\varepsilon_l{}^{ik}(B^\circ du)_k$ to the $(i,l)$ component in an orthonormal frame. A nonzero gradient in the first coordinate makes this term nonzero. Pairing with symmetric $B^\circ$ kills it. Thus the estimate for the integral can use $\|B^\circ\|_3\|\operatorname{sym}\operatorname{curl}B^\circ\|_{3/2}$, or a full-curl bound in a declared representative.
