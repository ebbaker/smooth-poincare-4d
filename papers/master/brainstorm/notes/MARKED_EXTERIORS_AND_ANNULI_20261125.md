**B01 and B02. Work first on the part of the metric the recognition theorem uses.** On the intrinsic route one can specify a metric on the actual complement of marked standard balls, already round product at the boundary. The missing sign is measurable on this exterior. Each unit round end contributes the positive boundary operator \(6\sqrt{1-\Delta_{S^3}}\). Positivity of the cheaper scalar Robin form suffices. The compactified energy equals the exterior energy. At or below \(4\pi^2\) this recognizes the type, and strictly below \(8\pi^2\) it gives positive entry. A fixed smooth positive subcritical metric can conversely be flattened on finitely many sufficiently small normal balls with error below its strict margin. Thus this formulation is useful for a construction whose retained metric and marks are supplied. It leaves arbitrary-type entry open. For an immersed exterior, the needed information about the filling can be weaker still. For each direction one may extend just that scalar height with a known bound on its number of middle points. Remaining mandatory index-two directions charge the actual exterior Weyl energy. This recovers the inclusive \(64\pi^2/9\) threshold on the full immersed sphere. For framed quadratic graph collars, the single-direction counts and angular image are explicit. Varying the graph slope also varies the collar data. A fixed nonzero quadratic boundary germ forces a positive middle cost even for a completely different immersed filling. A proposed construction now has two explicit geometric obligations: match the framed exterior, and budget its induced metric. **B05. Route the selected height by its actual intermediate three-manifold.** In the one-sided case with two odd handles at one end, the surgery link is a two-component R-link. An unknot component or tunnel number one recognizes the type. In the mixed case, after framed slides put the lower incidences in the order \((1,0)\). Surgery on the first sublink gives an integral homology sphere. A trivial fundamental group there suffices. The other order gives first homology \(\mathbb Z^2\). Identification as \(\#_2(S^1\times S^2)\) suffices there. The proof transfers the actual outgoing gluing at each step. The original height need not be index-ordered, and an ordered intermediate boundary need not be an affine height level. The count on more than three quarters of directions proves neither surgery property. No immersion of an individual hypothetical nonstandard type or its actual diagram was given. **B03. For separated quantiles, look for one physical annulus at the correct scale.** Fix one physical Yamabe sequence, the attaining center of the smaller quantile and a scale between that radius and the median radius. A chart with bounded ellipticity, low full curvature and a point of its unit sphere within bounded rescaled distance of this center locates both charged sets if its fixed outer radius is sufficiently large. The Yamabe inequality puts all sufficiently distant physical points on the large side. The inner charge and the distance bound then place the full inner ball on the other side. With the central harmonic comparison, or a supplied first-order Euclidean comparison, the original cap argument applies. Thus one source-embedded spherical annulus can suffice inside a branching region. A protected local ball or a chart in an untransferred positive-Ricci or smoothed gauge still leaves a genuine extraction problem. **B03 and B04. For diffuse charge, control collars around actual standard disks.** Only a single combined exterior competitor is needed. A short conformal collar on each parametrized disk has an explicit error in the chiral matrix density. The square sum of those errors controls both the path height and the retained exterior energy. Arbitrary finite numbers of actual disks at each index are allowed. Their discarded cores need no curvature estimate. This permits a genuine gain even with a charged or high-volume complement, provided the removed raw mass exceeds the minimizing excess and the full collar surcharge. The E03 witnesses provide the sets in a positive-Ricci distance gauge. The first-order collar estimates remain to be constructed on the same source. Fixed smooth normal jets in E17 can charge the whole leading savings. An exact evacuated round restart removes zero mass and remains repeatable. **B04. Test closed and almost Einstein survivors in a measured class.** With the subcritical margin, a physical uniform \(L^p\) Ricci bound for some \(p>2\) gives compact marked charts before minimization. Interpolation from small \(L^2\) trace-free Ricci and the Einstein equation in the limit then preserve chiral eigenvalue integrals. The result recognizes any one such Yamabe metric with sufficiently small joint Ricci and one-chirality spectral defect, with a constant depending only on the band, exponent, norm bound and pinching parameter. More strongly, given any positive target energy, reducing the defect threshold forces the tested metric itself below that energy. Taking the target to be the intrinsic direct threshold links this compactness argument to recognition. This uses Catino–Dameno's preprint theorem for the exact limit. Exact Einstein survivors also obey the improved signed determinant inequality in E40. The complementary non-Einstein, poorly pinched and unbounded physical Ricci cases remain.
