## Construction and topology have different inputs

Every smooth homotopy four-sphere admits a Euclidean hypersurface immersion. The construction arm asks for second-derivative control. The master recognizes a class induced by one such immersion if \(E_+\le64\pi^2/9\), irrespective of Yamabe sign. E32 already treats a round joining tube. The next calculation should introduce an actual index-two attachment \(D^2\times D^2\) along a framed \(S^1\times D^2\) and specify the outgoing \(D^2\times S^1\). The matching and exterior estimates must hold for the same immersion. A model with prescribed face data is a useful first result even if general realizability of those data remains open. [Immersion criterion](../../manuscript/immersion_entry.tex) · [Rotational transition](../../notes/E32_ROTATIONAL_TRANSITION.md)

The topology arm can start in parallel under an immersed \(E_+<8\pi^2\) hypothesis on a hypothetical nonstandard type. More than three quarters of directions have exactly two index-two points. Fix one Morse height and its actual level sets. After reducing the extremal handlebodies the \((m_1,m_3)=(1,1)\) case gives a concrete first target: find a framed attaching circle meeting the belt sphere of the 1-handle geometrically once, after justified slides if needed. Cancellation leaves at most one middle handle. Integer incidence alone cannot certify that geometric intersection. The \((0,2)\) case and its dual lead instead to a two-component surgery diagram. There the project should identify a geometric restriction on the framed link imposed by the immersion and energy bound. A general two-component cancellation theorem is not an input. [Middle-handle reduction](../../reviews/E33_INDEX_TWO_HANDLES.md) · [Weyl and indexed Gauss mass](../../reviews/E34_MIDDLE_WEYL_EQUALITY.md)

## Physical extraction and variational descent can proceed independently

Neck geometry already owns physical scale and link selection. Begin with an attaining center and separated chiral radii on one sequence of volume-one Yamabe metrics. A normalized upper volume bound on the inner transition shell would rule out competing long components in the regime of E25. One must still construct the whole radial chart and locate both mass sets beyond its trim. A fixed-distortion harmonic marking, or the controlled approximate version, would then allow the master to pay the cap costs. Local Dirac comparisons become relevant when their overlap maps recover this source marking. [Component test](../../reviews/E25_ANNULAR_VOLUME.md) · [Critical annulus](../../manuscript/critical_annulus.tex)

The fixed-type child should start with diffuse energy. Test a replacement across several marked balls and their intervening region, estimating the difference between removed and inserted chiral energy and the chiral mass on the entire complement. E17 computes why a regular flat-core repair can consume its incoming mass to leading order. If a smooth nonround minimizer is attained, use the same functional to seek a transverse negative variation or a strict competitor. If a round base passes the energy to one exit, any proposed decreasing quantity must distinguish a physically charged exit from the exactly repeatable evacuated disk of E21. Promote the closed-minimizer problem to a separate child when it has its own rigidity mechanism. [Diffuse repair](../../notes/E17_DIFFUSE_REPAIR.md) · [Closed variation](../../notes/E20_CLOSED_VARIATION.md) · [Round restart](../../notes/E21_ROUND_RESTART.md)

For three concurrent sessions, start the immersion construction, two-handle recognition and physical shell extraction. A fourth can take fixed-type descent immediately. A separate dynamical option already has an owner in [Bach flow](../../../investigations-1/02_bach_flow/README.md). Its useful interface would be a sequence at the fixed-type infimum with Bach error small in the norm of a specified compactness theorem. An exact closed Bach-flat limit and a flow slice along an unrelated trajectory do not by themselves give this selection.
