## Upper test from a unit-volume exit

The Yamabe infimum on a punctured smooth compactification equals that of the filled class. In dimension four, cut off bounded smooth tests within distance $2r$ of a filled point with squared gradient cost $O(r^2)$, then use conformal covariance on compactly supported tests away from the point. For a retained open subset on which the cap agrees with the source up to a conformal factor, its compact-test infimum is an upper bound for $Y([c_i])$ and is at least $Y_i$. This latter domain constant needs its own estimate to compare the two closed constants. On a union of disjoint open exits, the compact-test infimum is the minimum of their individual constants when all are positive. The quadratic forms add, and $\|u\|_4^2=(\sum_j\|u_j\|_4^4)^{1/2}\le\sum_j\|u_j\|_4^2$. Testing in one exit gives the opposite inequality for the infimum.
