## The actual gluing and a common framed surgery chart

Use \(W=W_{L_1,0}\) and \(W'=W'_{L_1,0}\) of §2.1 with \(L_1\) the link in Figure 4. In Figure 3 take the ambient \(Z=S^1\times D^3\), represented by the dotted \(U\) (the paper calls this component \(C\) in Figure 4). Substitute \(n=0\) in the source diagram. The lower \(C\) is dotted and \(D\) has coefficient zero. The upper \(D\) is dotted and \(C\) has coefficient zero. Call the remaining framed component \(A\). The framing label on the unremoved \(A\) strand in panels (b) and (c) is \(n-2=-2\). Use that Figure 3(b) push-off, with the same parametrized tube in the common chart on both sides. These are the diagrams with the single spectator and the cork drawn inside the ambient handlebody. The outer \(U\) is present on both sides. [Hayden–Mark–Piccirillo, Figure 3 and caption, pp. 5–7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.05269#page=6)

In the boundary chart replace each dot by its zero filling while retaining every other tube. If \(S\) is surgery on the common link \(U_0,C_0,D_0,A\), this gives maps \(j_\pm:\partial X_\pm\to S\). They identify the common link exterior by the identity and identify the already filled solid tori by their framing coordinates. Fix the parametrized dot exchange by this identification. Its map in the original Mazur coordinates is
\[
 f=\partial I_+\circ j_+^{-1}\circ j_-\circ(\partial I_-)^{-1},\qquad
 f^{-1}=\partial I_-\circ j_-^{-1}\circ j_+\circ(\partial I_+)^{-1}.
\]
Thus \(M=W\cup_f(-W')\). In particular, the upper reader uses the same chart to reverse the \(C,A\) attachments of \(X_+\), which leaves the actual \(U,D\) 1-handlebody. The identity in the common chart specifies this one boundary gluing. An arbitrary reparametrization of the shared integral homology sphere need not preserve the marked cores. Contractibility of \(W,W'\), van Kampen and Mayer–Vietoris give the homotopy-sphere property. Their original separating hypersurface is an integral homology sphere distinct from \(S^3\). Otherwise each of these handlebodies with one 1-handle and one 2-handle would be a ball by reverse Property R and 1/2 cancellation, contrary to their absolute nondiffeomorphism. It follows from the contractible filling and the PSC prime and signed Poincaré-sum obstructions that this original separating hypersurface has no nonzero-degree PSC source. ## Reversal of the two upper attachments

Let \(m_J,\ell_J\) be the meridian and given framed longitude of \(J=C,A,D\) in the common surgery diagram. The 2-handle \(D^2_c\times D^2_{\rm co}\) replaces the attaching solid torus by \(D^2_c\times S^1_{\rm co}\). The latter's meridian is \(\ell_J\) and its product longitude is \(m_J\) on the exterior torus. The reverse framed tube is the outgoing tube with the factors exchanged. In surgery coordinates it can be pushed through the filling collar to a small meridian \(u_J\) of \(J\) with its disk framing. Specifically take a small transverse disk at an isolated arc of \(J\) and two parallel such disks. Their boundary circles are the core and its framed parallel. The annulus in the collar carries the product parallel to this disk parallel and the reverse filling slope to \(m_J\). This works for the spectator with its prescribed framing because the forward slope and the reverse slope are the two different coordinates of the same solid torus exchange. Use the parametrized pullback, with the handle factor swap, for the reverse tube's inverse. [Hayden–Mark–Piccirillo, Lemma 3.1 and proof](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.05269#page=11)

Choose disjoint small balls on isolated arcs of \(C,A\), away from the other three tubes in each case. The meridian tubes \(u_C,u_A\) in those balls lie in the common diagram exterior. This realizes the reverse handles of \(X_+\) in the actual outgoing chart of \(X_-\). They are marked in the preceding level of \(X_-\) by the common exterior. Hence the original middle order \(D,A,u_C,u_A\) can be replaced by \(u_C,u_A,A,D\). All four incoming tubes are on the same boundary of the lower \(U,C\) 1-handlebody. The order change is the identity on their joint exterior and identifies their fillings by the fixed parametrizations. In both orders the complete outgoing boundary \(Y\) is identified with \(\partial H_{U,D}\) by the map \(\gamma\) from the original upper reversal, after transporting by this common-exterior order change. On the \(C,A\) sectors \(\gamma\) is inverse surgery, and on the \(U,D\) boundary fillings and the remaining unchanged exterior it uses the fixed chart. In particular the \(D\) filling and its core are carried through this change. Its inverse uses the forward \(C,A\) surgery chart and the inverse on the unchanged sectors. ## Two relative cancellations

Choose the small ball \(B\) for \(u_A\) along an isolated segment of \(A\) away from a dotted disk for \(C\), the \(U,C\) 1-handle feet and the meridional \(u_C\) attachment. The disk for \(u_A\) meets the chosen segment of \(A\) once. In the incoming lower body \(u_C\) is a small meridian of the actual dotted \(C\). It meets its 1-handle belt sphere once and misses the \(U\) belt sphere. The cancellation applies to \(C,u_C\), now that \(u_C\) is first. Use the usual relative cancellation supported in a neighborhood of these handles, with the complement of the cancellation support fixed in the outgoing comparison. In particular \(B\) is fixed. Strands of \(A\) and \(D\) that pass through the cancellation region are carried by this outgoing diffeomorphism with their entire tubes. Let \(\Xi_-:H_{U,C}\cup u_C\to H^-_1\) be the comparison, where \(H^-_1\cong S^1\times D^3\). Define the returned tubes by
\[
 a=(\partial\Xi_-)A,\qquad b=(\partial\Xi_-)u_A.
\]
Their inverse markings are pulled back by \((\partial\Xi_-)^{-1}\). The cancellation preserves the disk framing of \(b\) and its actual ball \(B\). The ball is used for the \(b\)-only surgery, which leaves a surviving \(a\)-tube rather than requiring \(a\) to avoid the disk.

From above the last \(D\) now has reverse tube \(D^*=\gamma\,o_D\tau\), where \(o_D\) is its actual outgoing embedding and \(\tau\) exchanges the factors. Its inverse on its image is \(\tau^{-1}o_D^{-1}\gamma^{-1}\). The inverse \(C,A\) surgeries in \(\gamma\) leave the entire \(D\) filling sector in place. Its core in \(\partial H_{U,D}\) is the surgery core of the zero filling representing the upper dotted \(D\). It crosses the 1-handle belt sphere once. In the zero-surgery model the latter is the dotted disk in the exterior capped by a meridian disk of the filling torus. The core misses the other belt sphere. The transport uses the product reverse tube, so the top cancellation uses its actual integral framing. Let \(\Xi_+:H_{U,D}\cup D^*\to H^+_1\) be the top comparison to the remaining upper \(S^1\times D^3\). Both cancellations here are geometric 1/2 cancellations read from the corresponding ends, with one transverse intersection on each end. [Milnor, *Lectures on the h-Cobordism Theorem*, Theorem 5.4](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/surgery/hcobord.pdf#page=53)

Write \(P=H^-_1\cup_b h_b\cup_a h_a\). Let \(J:P\to H_{U,C}\cup u_C\cup u_A\cup A\) use \(\Xi_-^{-1}\) on the first piece and identity in the surviving handle coordinates. The boundary map \(\delta\) from the actual level just before \(D\) to \(\partial(H_{U,D}\cup D^*)\) uses \(\gamma\) and the product surgery identification in the \(D\) sector. The complete closing and inverse of the returned diagram are
\[
 q=\partial\Xi_+\circ\delta\circ\partial J,\qquad
 q^{-1}=(\partial J)^{-1}\circ\delta^{-1}\circ(\partial\Xi_+)^{-1}.
\]
The closed comparison to \(M\) is \(J\) on the returned lower piece and \(\Xi_+^{-1}\) on its upper piece under \(\delta\). The \(a\)-first order also gives the same marked closure. If \(F:Y_{ba}\to Y_{ab}\) is the common-exterior order change on the returned pair, its closing is \(qF^{-1}\) and inverse \(Fq^{-1}\). The upper coordinates are transported by \(q\) at the closing, with \(q^{-1}\) for reverse passage. At the actual intermediate cut use the two corresponding face identifications with the same inward-collar convention. ## The degree and recognition

The new counts are \((1,1,2,1,1)\). The \(b\) core has winding zero in the lower \(S^1\times S^2\) because it is in \(B\). The integral handle complex and \(H_1(M)=0\) make the winding of \(a\) a unit. Orient it to make the row \((1,0)\). Surgery on \(b\) first is the disk-framed unknot surgery in \(B\). Replace \(B\) by the punctured \(S^1\times S^2\) in those coordinates and keep the rest of the outgoing marking fixed. This gives an oriented parametrization of the actual \(Z_b\) by \(Y_2=\#_2(S^1\times S^2)\). Use positive product metrics on the two factors and their PSC connected sum, then transport it to a smooth metric \(h\) on \(Z_b\). The marked bicollar can be given \(dt^2+h\) on both sides. For any product slice orient it by the increasing \(t\) normal. Its marked projection to \(Z_b\) is a diffeomorphism of degree \(+1\), its induced scalar curvature is \(R_h>0\), its lapse is one and its shift and second fundamental form vanish. A smooth ambient extension of this germ follows by interpolation with a smooth Riemannian metric outside the bicollar. This supplies B05's PSC source before recognition, without inferring a closed subcritical energy bound for that extension. [Gromov–Lawson, Theorem A](https://www.ihes.fr/~gromov/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/827.pdf) · [05 mixed criterion, \(Z_b\) proof and actual closing](../../investigations-1/05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex#L413)
