## The published gluing

Let \(U\) and \(B\) be the actual dotted unknot and zero-framed unknot in Figure 1 at \(n=1\). Orient them with \(\operatorname{lk}(U,B)=+1\), and use their integral zero longitudes in \(S^3\). Set \(X_-=H_U\cup h_B\) and \(X_+=H_B\cup h_U\), where \(H_U,H_B\) are the actual dotted \(0/1\)-handlebodies. The common boundary chart is \(S=S^3_{0,0}(U,B)\). Let \(j_\pm:\partial X_\pm\to S\) identify the common link exterior by the identity and the two fillings by their zero-framing coordinates. Definition 2.5 gives the boundary comparison \(\theta=j_+^{-1}j_-\). With \(\psi:X_-\to X_+\) induced by the component-exchanging isotopy of the link, the specified cork involution and its inverse are
\[
 f_1=(\partial\psi)^{-1}j_+^{-1}j_-,
 \qquad f_1^{-1}=j_-^{-1}j_+\partial\psi .
\]
The closed marked comparison sends \(X_-\cup_\theta(-X_+)\) to \(W_1\cup_{f_1}(-W_1)\) by the identity below and \(\psi^{-1}\) above. Van Kampen and Mayer–Vietoris give the homotopy-sphere property from contractibility of the fillings. The cork involution is on one filling. HMP use the common boundary of two absolutely nondiffeomorphic fillings. [Akbulut–Yasui, Definitions 2.3 and 2.5 and Theorem 2.4](https://www.gokovagt.org/journal/2012/jggt12-akbuyasu.pdf#page=4) · [HMP marked comparison](REMAINING_NATIVE_PAIR_20261127.md)

## The reverse tube and original upper closing

In \(S\) the upper \(U\)-handle's outgoing solid torus \(D^2_c\times S^1_{\mathrm{co}}\) has meridian \(\ell_U\) and product longitude \(m_U\), the zero longitude and meridian on the original exterior torus of \(U\). Read the upper \(U\)-handle downward by exchanging the factors. The \(U\)-filling sector with a surrounding collar is a product of the \(m_U\) circle with a capped annulus whose outer boundary is the \(\ell_U\) circle. In that product, shrink the reverse tube and isotope its disk into the exterior collar at an isolated arc of \(U\), fixing the \(B\)-sector. This realizes a full reverse tube \(a:S^1\times D^2\to\partial H_U\) in the common exterior. Its core and product parallel there are the boundaries of two parallel transverse disks of \(U\), so \(a\) has disk framing zero in the original diagram. Its core disk meets \(U\) once and is disjoint from the \(B\)-tube. Orient \(a\) to have winding \(+1\). Before reordering, the actual two forward attachments of the concatenation are \(B,a\). [Akbulut–Yasui, proof of Lemma 2.6, p. 62](https://www.gokovagt.org/journal/2012/jggt12-akbuyasu.pdf#page=5) · [05, full inverse surgery coordinates](../../investigations-1/05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex#L81)

Write \(\iota_t\) for the ambient isotopy in \(S\) carrying the parametrized reverse \(U\)-tube to \(a\), and \(S_{\mathrm{rev}}\) for the result of performing the reverse surgery at its original tube. Let \(Y_{Ba}\) be the outlet after \(B,a\). The induced comparison \(\widehat\iota:S_{\mathrm{rev}}\to Y_{Ba}\) uses \(\iota_1\) on the exterior and the identity in the coordinates of the inserted torus. Its inverse uses \(\iota_1^{-1}\) and those same coordinates. The inverse-surgery identification \(k:S_{\mathrm{rev}}\to\partial H_B\) restores the original \(U\)-tube with slope \(m_U\), keeps the \(B\)-filling coordinates and uses the common exterior elsewhere. Its inverse \(k^{-1}\) uses the forward \(U\)-trace identification on the corresponding sector and the identity in the untouched coordinates. Give \(\partial H_B\) the boundary orientation of \(H_B\), and use \(-H_B\) in the oriented assembly. In these upper coordinates the actual closing and its inverse are
\[
 \gamma_{Ba}=k\,\widehat\iota^{-1},\qquad
 \gamma_{Ba}^{-1}=\widehat\iota\,k^{-1}.
\]
The order comparison \(F:Y_{Ba}\to Y_{aB}\) is the identity on the common exterior and on the two parametrized outgoing tori. After attaching \(a\) first the closing becomes \(\gamma_{aB}=\gamma_{Ba}F^{-1}\) and its inverse is \(F\gamma_{Ba}^{-1}\). The \(B\)-filling coordinates pass to the identical coordinates of the upper dotted \(B\). ## Normalization relative to the first cut

Use one framed slide to replace \(B\) by \(b=B\#_{\beta}(-a^+)\), with \(a^+\) the zero-framed parallel of \(a\). Fix an embedded band \(\beta\) between a small arc of \(B\) and the oppositely oriented parallel of \(a\). Choose it in the common exterior with its ends at the prescribed parallels and with the band framing supplied by the two tube framings. A ribbon along a simple arc in the connected exterior gives such a band after shrinking the tube radii if needed. Any such choice works. The winding of \(b\) is \(1-1=0\). Its coefficient in the original \(S^3\) diagram is \(0+0-2\operatorname{lk}(B,a)=0\), since the disk of \(a\) misses \(B\). The returned diagram has row \((1,0)\), counts \((1,1,2,1,1)\) and uses one slide with no stabilizations. [Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson, §2, pp. 2310–2312, framed slides in a filled exterior](https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-4/gt-v14-n4-p13-s.pdf#page=6)

At \(Z_a=\partial(H_U\cup h_a)\), the framed parallel of \(a\) bounds the meridian disk of its surgery torus, extended through the collar up to the chosen parallel. The slide band and this disk give the knot isotopy in \(Z_a\) from the pending \(B\) to the pending \(b\). Adjacent disks effect the same isotopy on the framing parallels. Use the full tube parametrization carried by the isotopy, so an ambient extension \(L_t:Z_a\to Z_a\) gives \(b_a=L_1 B_a\) and \(b_a^{-1}=B_a^{-1}L_1^{-1}\) on its image. The subscript indicates the common-exterior insertion of a pending tube at \(Z_a\). Extend \(L_t\) into the lower collar and through the remaining handle using its coordinates. The outlet map \(L_Y:Y_{aB}\to Y_{ab}\) uses \(L_1\) on the exterior and the identity of handle coordinates in the new filling. Its inverse uses \(L_1^{-1}\) and the inverse coordinate identification. The resulting closing \(q:Y_{ab}\to\partial H_B\) and inverse are
\[
 q=\gamma_{aB}L_Y^{-1},\qquad
 q^{-1}=L_YF\,\widehat\iota\,k^{-1}.
\]
For the \(b\)-first order use the common-exterior and full-torus identification \(G:Y_{ab}\to Y_{ba}\). The closing and inverse are \(qG^{-1}\) and \(Gq^{-1}\). In the \(a\)-first order let \(o_b=L_Yo_B\) be the actual outgoing face of \(b\) in \(Y_{ab}\), and set \(\tau(w,z)=(z,w)\). The upper reverse tube in \(\partial H_B\) and its inverse on its image are
\[
 b^*=q\,o_b\,\tau=\gamma_{aB}o_B\tau,\qquad
 (b^*)^{-1}=\tau^{-1}o_b^{-1}q^{-1}
           =\tau^{-1}o_B^{-1}\gamma_{aB}^{-1}.
\]
At \(Z_a\) the inverse \(b\)-filling identifies the lower boundary of the actual upper piece with the first cut. In the direction from \(Z_a\) to \(\partial(H_B\cup_{b^*}h_{b^*})\), use \(q\) on the identified common exterior and the exchanged coordinates on the restored tube. Use the inverse exterior map and the original handle coordinates for return. The lower outward orientation gives signed collar coordinates \(t=-s_-\) and \(t=s_+\) from the two inward collar coordinates. ## Degree and the metric on the actual seam

The \(a\)-core crosses the belt sphere of the dotted \(U\) once geometrically. In the zero-surgery chart on \(U\), this belt sphere is represented by a spanning disk in the exterior capped with a meridian disk of the zero-filling torus. Geometric 1/2 cancellation gives an oriented comparison \(C_-:H_U\cup h_a\to D^4\). In the ball coordinates the pending tube is \(\partial C_-b_a\) with inverse \(b_a^{-1}(\partial C_-)^{-1}\). Give the unit \(S^3\) its round metric and orientation. The map \(p=(\partial C_-)^{-1}:S^3\to Z_a\) gives degree \(+1\) and PSC domination of this actual mixed cut. One can also give its bicollar the product metric \(dt^2+(\partial C_-)^*g_{S^3}\). Each oriented product slice projects onto \(Z_a\) with degree \(+1\), and has lapse one, shift zero and vanishing second fundamental form. [Milnor, *Lectures on the h-Cobordism Theorem*, Theorem 5.4](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/surgery/hcobord.pdf#page=53) · [05, Theorem 4.2](../../investigations-1/05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex#L413)

For a global entering metric realizing this same seam, \(b^*=\gamma_{aB}o_B\tau\) has core the core of the zero-filling torus of the actual upper dotted \(B\). The isotopy \(\iota_t\) and inverse \(U\)-surgery preserve that full sector. Its core meets the upper belt once by the capped spanning-disk description. The upper body \(H_B\cup h_{b^*}\), read from above, is a ball by geometric cancellation. Compare the two ball parametrizations through the actual \(Z_a\) gluing. Cerf's components theorem for \(S^3\) lets a boundary mismatch of fixed orientation extend over a ball using an isotopy and collars. Thus the two ball comparisons fit smoothly to the standard hemispheres with \(Z_a\) sent to their equator. The pulled-back unit round metric has positive Yamabe sign and \(E_+=E_-=0\). The actual cut \(Z_a\) is totally geodesic with induced scalar curvature \(6\), and its identity map has degree \(+1\). In signed distance the germ is \(dt^2+\cos^2(t)g_{S^3}\) in the selected equatorial coordinates. [Hatcher, *A proof of the Smale conjecture*, opening discussion of Cerf's components theorem, p. 553](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/Papers/SmaleConjecture.pdf) · [Marked disk gluing source record](../../../references/SOURCE_NOTES_JOINT_COLLAR_20261125.md)
