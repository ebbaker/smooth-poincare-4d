## Current materials and status

The inherited manuscript studies full connection descent, a Petrov-type obstruction in holomorphic Ricci-flat ASD geometry, wave realizations, derivative bundles, and relationships to specified classical double-copy constructions.

The Petrov N/O obstruction is stated for a specified holomorphic Ricci-flat ASD setting; it is not a theorem about arbitrary Riemannian four-metrics or every possible transfer. Full connection pullback, partial transfer, derivative-bundle reconstruction, and choice-dependent Ward transfer impose different requirements. The wave-sector identities do not establish interaction-level color–kinematics duality or unrestricted equivalence with general relativity. Their relevance to a positive-curvature Riemannian sphere requires a separate argument.
