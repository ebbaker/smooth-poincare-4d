# Initial geometric investigations

These studies preceded and motivated the smooth Poincaré program. Their questions concern generalized coordinates, connection descent, and double-copy structures; they were not direct investigations of the smooth Poincaré conjecture.

1. [Generalized isothermal coordinates](01_generalized_isothermal_coordinates/README.md): holomorphic wave metrics, direction fibers, and reconstruction.
2. [Chiral descent and double copy](02_chiral_descent_and_double_copy/README.md): transfer conditions, algebraic restrictions, and double-copy interpretations.

Their manuscript and note contents retain the original domains and qualifications. A future application to the Riemannian homotopy-sphere problem requires a separate argument. Existing continuation plans remain optional and unactivated. Folder numbering is local to this collection; task identities and evidence status are unchanged.

The direct smooth Poincaré work is in [investigations-1](../investigations-1/README.md). Shared original draft papers and published literature remain in [references](../../references/README.md).
