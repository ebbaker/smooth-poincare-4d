# Bibliography and attribution audit

These entries support the background and reconstruction tools. The explicit wave-family calculation, its common intermediate surface, and the inverse-chart rigidity proposition should be proved in the manuscript rather than attributed to these sources as though they already stated this formulation. No priority claim is established by this search.

## Recommended bibliography

1. M. F. Atiyah, N. J. Hitchin, and I. M. Singer, **Self-duality in four-dimensional Riemannian geometry**, *Proceedings of the Royal Society of London A* **362** (1711), 425–461 (1978). DOI: [10.1098/rspa.1978.0143](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1978.0143). Bibliography checked against Oxford author/institutional records and the local PDF. Proposition 2.2, journal p. 432 / PDF p. 9, is the precise reference for the Einstein condition and chiral spin-connection self-duality. It does not prove the new nonlocal transfer.

2. Roger Penrose, **Nonlinear gravitons and curved twistor theory**, *General Relativity and Gravitation* **7** (1), 31–52 (1976). DOI: [10.1007/BF00762011](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00762011). Title, author, volume, and pagination checked directly against the supplied PDF. Use for nonlinear-graviton geometry and the rational-curve construction; preserve the distinction between general ASD conformal data and the extra nonprojective/fibration/symplectic data appropriate to Ricci-flat geometry. Do not identify it with the separate short article *The Nonlinear Graviton*.

3. William T. Shaw, **An ambitwistor description of bosonic or supersymmetric minimal surfaces and strings in four dimensions**, *Classical and Quantum Gravity* **3** (5), 753–761 (1986). DOI: [10.1088/0264-9381/3/5/006](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/3/5/006). Author, title, pages, and year checked on the actual article first page, PDF p. 2. The null-curve reconstruction is §2, especially equation (8), journal p. 755 / PDF p. 4. It reconstructs a spacetime point from twistor-curve tangent data; it is not an inverse to the twistor projection at an individual twistor point. The paper discusses its generic denominator condition and singular cases explicitly.

4. R. S. Ward, **On self-dual gauge fields**, *Physics Letters A* **61** (2), 81–82 (1977). DOI: [10.1016/0375-9601(77)90842-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/0375-9601(77)90842-8). The original first page is reproduced in Nigel Hitchin's [Oxford lecture slides](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/lmason/New%20Horizons/Nigel-Hitchin.pdf), and publisher metadata identifies the DOI. Use for the vector-bundle/gauge-field correspondence, with explicit line triviality and splitting hypotheses in the manuscript's own formulation. This is a gauge reconstruction theorem, not a gravitational coordinate theorem.

DOI: [10.1007/BF02684746](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02684746). Official journal archive: [Numdam](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1960__5__5_0/). The explicit two-patch proofs can avoid reliance on this general theorem for the wave family.

## Scope and novelty recommendations

- Describe the result as an explicit construction and derivation. Do not claim a previously unknown metric family: the wave metrics are established objects.
- The transfer construction depends on a preferred presentation and the common intermediate surface. Avoid “canonical” without specifying the preserved projection, coordinates, spin frame, and gauge data.
- The normal bundle calculation should be direct: factor the vertical Jacobian into the standard O(1) transition and a holomorphically trivial matrix factor along each rational curve. Then the nonlinear-graviton interpretation is supported without substituting cohomological dimensions for bundle splitting.
- I did not locate a precise primary citation phrased as “T_vert Z ⊗ O(−1) is the Ward bundle of the unprimed Levi–Civita connection.” The manuscript can establish this explicitly for the wave family by reconstructing the connection from its Jacobian cocycle and comparing it with the torsion-free spin connection. Do not state a general result on the strength of this unsuccessful search.
- The full-fiber inverse-chart obstruction is a self-contained compactness argument. State its hypotheses and prove it; no claim that it is new is needed.
- The general bundle-descent lemma uses a flat alpha symbol, a global holomorphic partial connection, and line triviality. Its linearity conclusion follows from H⁰(CP¹,O(1)); integrability is an additional condition imposing ASD curvature.
- The ten entries above support the cited background. The existing unpublished Baker precursor can be cited separately with its actual manuscript date and status. Add other background only where an actual claim depends on it.

## Editorial audit of the developed manuscript

The final development is coherent: intrinsic parallel-null geometry leads to wave form and spin curvature; explicit twistor first integrals lead to the generalized maps; direct connection transport is then justified over the whole direction sphere by the bundle cocycle; normal-bundle and incidence recovery follow from the same splitting. The inverse-map rigidity theorem and tangent-spinor calculation clarify rather than contradict the positive result. The framed reconstruction and outstanding-challenges sections correctly prevent a claim of a gauge-independent or canonical metric reconstruction.
