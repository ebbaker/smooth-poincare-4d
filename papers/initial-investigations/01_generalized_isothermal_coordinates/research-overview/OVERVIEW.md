Prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance, 21 November 2026 (America/New_York). The setup session’s actual model and effort were not independently recorded; recommendations below are not execution provenance.

## Objective and relation to the program

Preserve and clarify the generalized-coordinate and bundle-reconstruction machinery that motivated the sphere program, with particular attention to domains, direction fibers, and which information survives descent.

The inherited wave manuscript constructs generalized isothermal charts and chiral connection descent for holomorphic metrics with two independent parallel null vector fields. It gives explicit patching and fiberwise bundle splitting and distinguishes these from complete inverse coordinate charts.

## Starting evidence and its limits

This is a local holomorphic construction in a restricted metric class. A parallel null two-plane alone is weaker than two parallel null vector fields. Nonflat examples do not provide Riemannian real slices for the smooth homotopy-sphere problem; the real forms discussed in the audits are neutral. Bundle extension over complete direction fibers does not imply extension of the geometric inverse charts. Canonicity, tangent-spinor identification, and extension to broader curvature classes remain separate questions. The older project overview predates the sphere work and is historical context.

The imported sources retain their working-draft status. Reorganization is not independent checking. The plan begins with a finite audit before asking the next conceptual question; it does not launch a new research campaign.
