Accept the single-curve lemma at its exact scope. Let Z be a smooth real six-manifold with smooth almost complex J0, and let u0:(S2,j)→Z be a smooth embedded regular J0-holomorphic sphere. Fix a compact neighborhood, smooth auxiliary metrics/connection, and the geometric linearization D0:W1,8(u0*TZ)→L8(Hom_anti(TS2,u0*TZ)) with a supplied bounded linear right inverse Q0. There are r,epsilon>0 depending only on this fixed data such that every smooth almost complex J′ satisfying ||J′−J0||C0<epsilon on that neighborhood has a smooth nonconstant J′-holomorphic sphere u=exp_u0(xi), ||xi||W1,8≤r, homotopic to u0 inside the neighborhood. No derivative bound on J′ enters epsilon. No embeddedness of u, uniqueness, smooth dependence or uniform higher estimate is asserted.

The fixed anti-linear projection is essential: it is injective on each nearby anti-linear output space and its derivative at the background zero is exactly D0. Affine dependence on the first derivative gives the quadratic background remainder and the C0 perturbation estimate. The closed W1,8 ball in range Q0 is compact and convex in ambient C0; weak lower semicontinuity proves closedness there. For each fixed J′, uniform coefficient convergence and weak derivative convergence give weak L8 convergence of the residual. Applying Q0 and compact embedding gives the required C0 continuity. This uses no common modulus of continuity over the family of perturbations. Schauder therefore gives a zero of the full equation after choosing r first and epsilon second.

For that individual smooth J′, the full weak equation is u_y=J′(u)u_x. Its difference quotient has an L8 times L8 forcing term in L4. Freeze the continuous coefficient on a sufficiently small disk, apply the constant Cauchy–Riemann L4 estimate and absorb the coefficient error to get W2,4. The note proves that estimate from the L4 Hilbert-transform identity and directional Riesz averaging. The distribution equation Delta u=(partial_y a)u_x−(partial_x a)u_y and the weak Euclidean L4 Hessian estimate then bootstrap smoothness. These later local constants may depend on derivatives of J′. The fixed-point threshold was already selected without them. The tubular homotopy and positive background diameter establish homotopy and nonconstancy.
