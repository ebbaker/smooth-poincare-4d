The result applies to a supplied connected complete smooth nonflat
Ricci-flat ASD ALE four-manifold and a sufficiently large full compact
truncation. Such a truncation cannot smoothly embed, with the stated
orientation, into a closed oriented integral homology four-sphere.
An arbitrary regular piece of an orbifold is outside the statement.

The key integral calculation is stronger than a rational Betti-number
test. If the boundary is a spherical space form and the core embeds,
Mayer–Vietoris gives zero H2 for both sides and splits boundary H1
as their finite H1 groups. Poincaré–Lefschetz duality and UCT identify
the two summands abstractly. Thus boundary H1 is G direct-sum G.
It cannot be nontrivial cyclic.

Positive b2 of the supplied ALE space contradicts the first calculation.
In the b2-zero case, Wright's smooth classification reduces to a cyclic
quotient of a Gibbons–Hawking space. The audited argument supplies the
additional end-group calculation: the deck action preserves the circle
generator, including the k=2 conjugation case; the end extension is
central with cyclic quotient; and a finite abelian group acting freely
and orthogonally on S3 is cyclic by a faithful eigenline character.
Euler characteristic gives k=d and boundary group C_(d squared),
d>1. This contradicts the doubled-group conclusion.
[Wright, arXiv:1102.2442v2, Theorem A and §§2, 7.1–7.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1102.2442v2).

The topology statement and end calculation remain separate from
analytic extraction. E166 checks that cofinal product ends identify
