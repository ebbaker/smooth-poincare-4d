## Whole sphere, distortion and moments

The norm of \(Su\) is bounded away from zero on a fixed spectral
tube. Direct substitution checks that
\(\phi^{-1}(f)=S^{-1}f/|S^{-1}f|\), and the positive path
\((1-r)I+rS\) proves orientation preservation and degree one.
For unit perpendicular \(u,v\), projecting \(Sv\) against the
line \(Su\) gives \(\inf_t|S(v-tu)|\ge\sqrt m\). The largest
stretch is at most \(\sqrt{M_*}/|Su|\), and the smallest at least
\(\sqrt m/|Su|\). Taking their ratio at the same \(u\) bounds it
by \(D_x=\sqrt{M_*/m}\); a middle eigenvector for \(S\) attains
the ratio, including repeated eigenvalues. Individual stretches
are in \([1/D_x,D_x]\). In two positively oriented singular
frames \(d\phi\,j(d\phi)^{-1}-j\) has off-diagonal entries
\(-(r-1)\) and \(r^{-1}-1\), where \(r\ge1\) is that direction's
stretch ratio. Its operator norm is exactly \(r-1\). Using
\(M_*-m\le2|q|\) and the denominator \(\ge2m_*\) proves the
claimed \(D_x-1\le m_*^{-1}|q|\).

The map and inverse differences can be differentiated in the
fixed ambient \(E_x\) with round domain slots. In the intrinsic
target expression \(j^\Phi-j\) one first substitutes
\(u=\phi^{-1}(f)\); the tangent inverse is smooth on its
actual planes, including the poles. The \(\phi,\phi^{-1},j^\Phi\)
expressions have bounded first parameter derivatives of the
required fiber derivatives on the compact \(S\) tube times the
unit sphere. The segment to \(I\) stays in that tube. Subtracting
the \(I\) value and using \(|S-I|\le|q|\) gives \(C_j|q|\)
through each order \(j=0,1,2\), with constants independent of
initial higher derivatives. The inequalities integrate by
E137 (1). [Whole-fiber comparison][e03a] · [Distortion identity][dist].

The unit round fiber has area \(4\pi\). Its real second and
fourth moments are \((4\pi/3)\delta_{ij}\) and
\((4\pi/15)(\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}
+\delta_{il}\delta_{jk})\). For trace-free symmetric \(T\) these
give
\(\int|P_fTf|^2=(4\pi/3-8\pi/15)|T|^2=(4\pi/5)|T|^2\)
and \(\int(P_fTf)\otimes f=(4\pi/5)T\), while a skew
\(\Omega\) gives \(\int(\Omega f)\otimes f=(4\pi/3)\Omega\).
They use the target round area directly. The real gradient
remainder pairs to zero with divergence-free rotations by
integration by parts and with \(P_f a\) by odd/even ambient
parity. Those two subspaces give the real forms of holomorphic
vertical fields as in E03A. The complexified metric with
\(Y^{1,0}=(Y-ijY)/2\) takes half the real squared norm, producing
\(2\pi/5\) for the Hermitian convention. Summing the live base
slots gives the same constants for full tensors. In particular
the norm and squared norm of the remainder have the required
integrated suprema from (1) and \(J\), respectively. [E03A][e03a].

## Horizontal lift, derivative and complete tensor constants

Along \(X^A\), \(D_X^A u=0\). Since \(D^\Gamma=D^A-B\)
on \(E\), differentiation of \(Su/|Su|\) gives
\(\mathcal H_X=P_fL_Xf\) with \(L_X=P_X^\top-B_X\) and
\(P_X=S^{-1}\nabla^A_XS\). The projected scalar part is zero
and \(\operatorname{sym}_0 L_X=T_X\). For the specified
derivative in E137 (8), \(f\) and \(P_f\) are parallel along
\(Y^\Gamma\). The subtraction of \(Z_{\nabla^g_YX}\) is
required for tensoriality in the original base covector slot.
It yields \(P_f(\nabla^\Gamma_YL)_Xf\) and
\(P_f(\nabla^\Gamma_YT)_Xf\), where the \(\Gamma\) and \(A\)
product derivatives have the same live base Levi–Civita part.
The change on an endomorphism \(Z_X\) is therefore exactly
\(-[B_Y,Z_X]\). This lift differs from E09's \(A\)-parallel
labels for its derivative of the remainder. [E03A][e03a] ·
[E09][e09].

For \(g=c\widehat g\), \(c=2K\), a \(k\)-covector
adjoint-valued tensor has native norm
\(\sqrt2c^{-k/2}\) times its old norm. An endomorphism-valued
tensor instead gains \(c^{-k/2}\). In native orthonormal frames
\(\operatorname{ad}(b)=(b\times\cdot)/\sqrt2\). The cross-product
matrix has Hilbert–Schmidt norm \(\sqrt2|b|\) and operator norm
\(|b|\), giving the stated native factors. Applying these
conversions to E03A and E09 proves E137 (6). In particular
\(\nabla^\Gamma B=\operatorname{ad}(\nabla^\Gamma b)\)
because the induced connection preserves the bracket, and
\(\|\nabla^\Gamma B\|_{\rm HS}=|\nabla^\Gamma b|\) in the
full component norms. \(P\)'s norm is unchanged relative to
\(T\)'s, giving (7).

For the commutators the squared sum of
\(|[B_Y,Z_X]|\le2\|B_Y\|_{\rm op}|Z_X|\) runs over independent
\(Y,X\) slots. Thus the Gamma correction costs at most
\(4\sqrt3|T||Z|\) for \(Z=P,T\), using (6).
For \(Z=T\) it gives the \(4\sqrt3|T|^2\) correction below (9).
In the \(P\) identity the multipliers for
\((S\,\cdot+\cdot\,S)^{-1}\) applied to \(ZS,SZ\) have
absolute value at most one. Applying this to the differentiated
relative Sylvester equation gives \(2|\nabla^A\operatorname{sym}P|
+2|P|^2\). The symmetric term costs
\(\sqrt3|\nabla^AT|+2\sqrt3|T|^2\) before the factor two.
Using \(|P|\le2|T|\) and the connection correction gives
\(2\sqrt3|\nabla^AT|+(12\sqrt3+8)|T|^2\).
Adding the exact native \(\nabla^\Gamma B\) norm gives
\((2\sqrt3+6\sqrt2)|\nabla^AT|+
(12\sqrt3+8+36\sqrt2+8\sqrt6)|T|^2\) for
\(\nabla^\Gamma L\), as in (10). Transposition is isometric
and commutes with the metric derivative. These estimates use
the inherited full first derivative of \(T\), with no extra
base derivative of \(T\) or of the comparison.

For the independent fiber differentiation let \(v,w\) be
tangent at \(f\), extend \(w\) with \(\nabla_v^{\rm rd}w=0\)
there, and write \(h=\langle f,Mf\rangle\).
From \(dP_f[v]=-v\otimes f-f\otimes v\),
\(\nabla_v^{\rm rd}Y=P_fMv-hv\). Differentiating this
operator with \(v,w\) as outer and inner slots, using the
ambient \(d_v w=-\langle v,w\rangle f\), gives the three
Hessian terms in E137 (11)'s preceding display.
For complete slot norms take \(f=e_3\) and write
\(M=\begin{pmatrix}A&a\\b^\top&c_0\end{pmatrix}\).
At \(f\), \(Y=a\), \(\nabla Y=A-c_0I_2\), and the Hessian
component at \(e_i,e_j\) is
\(-e_i b_j-\delta_{ij}a-(a_i+b_i)e_j\).
The three full component norms are \(\sqrt2|b|\),
\(\sqrt2|a|\), \(\sqrt2|a+b|\). Hence the derivative and
Hessian norms are at most \(\sqrt3|M|\) and \(4|M|\),
respectively. In particular the proposed
\((1,2\sqrt2,8)\) bounds hold in full tensor norms,
also after summing any live base slots. Applying these bounds
to \(L,T,\nabla^\Gamma L,\nabla^\Gamma T\) gives the stated
orders through two with one specified base derivative.
\(\Gamma\)-parallel motion is an isometry of the fiber,
so it commutes with the round derivative when all round
covariant slots are transported. No evolution in temporal
labels enters these mixed spatial derivatives. [E137][e137].

## Positive chiral scale and time derivative

The old bracket-orthonormal internal components of curvature
gain \(\sqrt2\) in native components, while the two-form norm
has the additional \(c^{-1}\). With
\(\sigma=c\widehat\Sigma/\sqrt2\), the old curvature
decomposition becomes
\(F_\Gamma=-2(W^++s_g I/12)\sigma-2B_{\rm Ric}\sigma^-\)
in the native live curvature coefficients, with the two
triples orthonormal and \(|B_{\rm Ric}|^2=|\operatorname{Ric}_0|^2/4\).
Thus \(F_\Gamma+\sigma\) has norm twice the block norm, and
the scalar target is \(12K/c=6\). Dividing the old E09
pointwise estimate by \(c\) gives exactly
\(\frac12|q|+6|\nabla^AT|+(42+8\sqrt3)|T|^2\).
The three component norm factors \(1,2,4\sqrt3\) follow
from the squared block coefficients. Time integration uses
(1) and \(J\). This estimates the chiral operator
Hilbert–Schmidt \(W^+\) norm, traceless Ricci and scalar
deviation. \(W^-\) does not enter the block. In manuscript
integration, explicitly reserve the ordinary two-form norm
in (12), counting unordered base pairs once, alongside full
component sums for the ordered covariant jets. [E09][e09].

At fixed original \(x,u\) in a time-independent \(E\) frame,
normalization gives \(\partial_\tau\phi=P_f(S_\tau S^{-1})f\).
The entry of \(S_\tau S^{-1}\) is
\((Q_\tau)_{ij}/((s_i+s_j)s_j)\), yielding the multiplier
\(s_i/(s_i+s_j)\) on \(S^{-1}Q_\tau S^{-1}\). This computation
diagonalizes \(S\) only at the evaluation time. The time
norm of the relative velocity and both terms of the live
strain integrand scale by \(c^{-1}\); multiplying by
\(d\tau=c\,dt\) leaves the integral unchanged. E14 therefore
gives \(4512\sqrt2\,J\) for the displayed zeroth-order time
velocity bound, with no quantitative spectral floor for this
step. The target field here is
\(f\mapsto\partial_\tau\phi(\phi^{-1}(f))\), to which the
round jet estimates apply. For fixed source coordinates one
uses ambient \(E\)-derivatives of its composition with
\(\phi\). The Gauss formula expresses the ambient target
derivatives by the round jets plus lower terms, and
\(d\phi,\nabla d\phi\) are uniformly bounded on the spectral
tube. This gives orders through two on the fixed source
with tube-dependent constants. A prescribed moving
\(\Lambda_g^+\) frame would contribute additional frame
velocity. Likewise time-dependent recovery cannot be
differentiated using only same-slice isometry. [E14][e14] ·
[E84][e84].
