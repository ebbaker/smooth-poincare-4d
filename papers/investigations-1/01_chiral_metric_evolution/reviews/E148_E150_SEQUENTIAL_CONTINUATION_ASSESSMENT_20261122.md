# E148/E150 — Sequential value compactness and original continuation

with OpenAI assistance. Lead assessment, actual lead identifiers

Accepted: for a supplied smooth native original flow in the chosen
positive definite component on a fixed smooth SO(3) bundle over
a compact closed oriented four-manifold, smooth definite extension
at a finite endpoint is equivalent to existence of one sequence
of slices approaching it whose connection and curvature values,
after smooth equivariant orientation-preserving automorphisms of
that same bundle, converge uniformly to a continuous positive
definite pair. The connection norm uses differences from one
fixed smooth affine origin.

Only the existence of this sequence is assumed. The theorem neither
constructs it nor deduces it from bounded values. It uses no J bound,
no unnormalized convergence, no uniform derivative bounds on the
automorphisms or their inverses, and no change of bundle or manifold.
The chosen maps need not lie in the identity component. The limit
must remain definite with the same curvature-map sign.

The assessed E138/E140 argument first gives a common original
lifespan theta=3 T_ref/4 for all smooth compatible data in one
small pair-value ball around a fixed smooth compatible reference.
For each datum separately, a smooth graph start identifies the
adjusted solution through zero; its smooth generator has a complete
flow on the compact principal bundle for every closed subslab.
No common bound on these generators is needed to obtain the
common interval. The normalized sequence's uniform pair limit
is compatible in distributions. Choose its positive compact value
tube and radii, one E38 smooth approximation, that reference's time,
and only then a late index. Evolving the normalized datum and
applying (h_n^{-1})^* gives exactly the old datum at that time.
In the adjusted variables the pullback is
(j(s) composed with h_n^{-1})^*. Original smooth uniqueness
identifies the full overlap. The converse uses identity maps
along any sequence tending to a smooth definite endpoint.

The optional positive-strip assertion is for compatible converging
data. Recovery started at identity on a fixed interior strip
converges smoothly. For a recovery j from zero, the strip map is
k(s)=j(s) composed with j(s0)^{-1}; the original path is
j(s0)^* k(s)^* A_adjusted(s). The factor accumulated before s0
is not estimated. No C0-datum continuity of the based original
solution map, and no original recovery from merely continuous
data at zero, is asserted. This distinction is retained in integration.

This supplies a compactness criterion for later geometric work.

of the [general continuation assessment](E138_E143_GENERAL_CONTINUATION_ASSESSMENT_20261122.md).
