## Disposition

Accepted as a theorem of the supplied smooth original native flow
on a fixed smooth principal SO(3) bundle over a compact closed
oriented real four-manifold, with positive definite curvature planes
and the round-compatible curvature-map sign. If a finite endpoint
has finite temporal strain budget
\[
 J=\int_0^{t_*}\sup_M\bigl(|\nabla^A T|_g+|T|_g^2\bigr)\,d\tau,
 \qquad
 T=\tfrac12(Q^{-1/2}\nabla^AQ\,Q^{-1/2})_0,
\]
then the solution extends smoothly, positively definitely, on the
same bundle in the original identification past that endpoint.
Any finite maximal smooth definite lifespan in this component
therefore has infinite J. No near-round, small-budget, or initial
higher-derivative bound is assumed.

The proof also gives the more primitive criterion: a uniform
connection/curvature limit which is continuous, distributionally
compatible and positive definite in this component suffices for
smooth original continuation. Compatibility is automatic for such
a uniform pair limit. The finite-J estimates supply this endpoint.
This criterion is the exact same restart proof with the temporal
estimates omitted, not an additional compactness theorem.
