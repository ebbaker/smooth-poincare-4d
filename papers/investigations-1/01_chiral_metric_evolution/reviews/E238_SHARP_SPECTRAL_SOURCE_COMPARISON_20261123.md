§4.2 text, from (4.5) and Proposition 4.5 through Lemma 4.9's
symbol sequence, including Lemma 4.6, Corollary 4.7 and Remark 4.8.
[Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.5005v2) ·
[Versioned §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1111.5005v2#S4.SS2).

The proposition concerns the second-order principal part of the
linearization of the **same original flow** at a definite connection
\(A\). It factors through \(P_A\) defined in (4.5) using projection
perpendicular to \(Q(A)\) and a positive self-adjoint factor. For
each nonzero covector \(\alpha\), Lemma 4.9 identifies the kernel
of the principal symbol factor as the image of the pointwise base
and internal infinitesimal actions. The image of the factor is
five dimensional. This is a local statement at each point in the
oriented Riemannian reconstruction, applying in the definite
class of the paper without a stationary hypothesis or a spectral
ratio cutoff. The background metric for the symbol is the metric
of \(A\), and the general equation has the live metric.

The symbol acts on connection variations at a single covector.

[Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.11755v2) ·
[Versioned §4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2001.11755v2#S4.SS3).

The lemma starts with a smooth hypersymplectic flow on a compact
four-manifold. The fields are triples of individually closed
symplectic forms in fixed cohomology classes and are self-dual for
their evolving reconstructed Riemannian metric in the positive
triple orientation. There is no sign of an independent \(SO(3)\)
connection in this evolution. The determinant-one wedge Gram matrix
uses the volume of the triple and evolves through the triple equation
(3.1), previously checked in E207. The displayed closed scalar
inequality for its trace has torsion coefficients
\((2-\operatorname{tr}Q/\lambda_i)/3\). Positivity, determinant one,
and the strict trace bound \(2^{5/3}\) give
\(2\lambda_i<\operatorname{tr}Q\) in the subregion. Lemma 4.11
preserves that trace bound from the initial slice on the given
smooth flow's existence interval. It is an upper trace bound for
this matrix, with no ratio threshold in the statement. Theorem 4.10
has a separate sufficiently small initial trace excess and a
global smooth-flow conclusion.

This is the closest scalar-maximum comparison. The branch
all-ratio obstruction concerns an independent connection and the
logarithm of the extremal eigenvalue ratio of a trace-three quotient.
The triple inequality uses torsion and a different principal
operator. Converting a matrix's normalization alone does not
identify those equations. Append the Lemma 4.11 comparison to

[Versioned §1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1407.0840v1#S1.SS1) ·

faithful circle action lifting isometrically to an \(SO(3)\) bundle

orientation-preserving diffeomorphism to \(S^4\) or
\(\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2\), using the orientation defined by the
connection. Positive sign is a consequence reported in §1.2,
with its cited sign proof outside this selection. No metric
evolution or energy stationarity is assumed. Corollary 1.5
addresses a supplied oriented Riemannian metric with an isometric

It concludes the same topology and positivity of \(W^++s/12\).
