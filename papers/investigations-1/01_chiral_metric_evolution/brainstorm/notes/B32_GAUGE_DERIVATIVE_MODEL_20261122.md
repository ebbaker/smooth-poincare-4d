# B32 — Test the derivative integrability needed by ordinary gauge recovery

A rough-data system theorem can give derivatives of size t^{-1/2} and t^{-1} from continuous initial values. The gauge generator uses first spatial derivatives, so its speed may be time integrable while a direct bound for the derivative of its ordinary flow needs time-integrable second derivatives. Continuity alone does not make the displayed t^{-1} bound integrable. This does not prove failure of gauge recovery: cancellation or a geometric identity may be better than this sufficient estimate.

Freeze a flat scalar heat test. A Dini modulus is sufficient for integral ||D² heat_t f||infinity, by differentiating the Gaussian and subtracting f(x). To show the exact limitation of continuity, test disjoint bumps of shrinking scale, heights 1/j and centers tending to one point. Their sum is continuous, but the supremum Hessian can contribute 1/j on disjoint diffusion windows. This differs from E25's L4,1 principal forcing estimate and concerns a separate C0-to-ordinary-flow sufficient condition. Do not infer an actual Fine-flow singularity or nonexistence of a limiting automorphism.
