# B81 — Check whether known four-dimensional compactness can remove injectivity

22 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI assistance. Conceptual B decision by the selected lead; actual lead identifiers unavailable. This is a literature applicability question, not a claimed improvement to the criterion.

The geometric criterion being assembled in E159 still supplies injectivity and diameter bounds. On a smooth homotopy four-sphere, the native original flow preserves a positive total volume and the real transport bound controls Ricci and W+, though not W−. Anderson's R29 discussion after Theorem 2.2 indicates orbifold degeneration under weaker volume/diameter/Ricci hypotheses in dimension four. A bounded source check should establish exactly whether existing compactness and bubble results prohibit such degeneration on the stated topology when the positive Weyl block is also uniformly bounded.

The suggested mechanism is not assumed: at shrinking curvature scales a bounded Ricci and W+ tensor would vanish, suggesting Ricci-flat anti-self-dual ALE bubbles if the relevant bubble theorem actually gives those objects. A bubble carrying a compact two-cycle of nonzero self-intersection would contradict H² of a homotopy sphere. But finite quotients may have b2=0, so one must not replace all Ricci-flat ASD ALE spaces by simply connected hyperkähler gravitational instantons. Their end groups, orientability, spin and possible embedding of the end boundary into a homology four-sphere matter. Rational-ball or quotient cases must be retained as potential obstructions rather than dismissed by simple connectivity of the ambient manifold.
