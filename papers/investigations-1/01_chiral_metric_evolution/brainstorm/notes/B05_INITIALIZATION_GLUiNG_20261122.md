# SP4-CME-B05 — local definite pieces and the global gluing obligation

22 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI assistance. Selected lead model/effort unavailable, preserved. Conceptual assessment after the foundation audit, first nonlinear equation and valid real sphere geometry.

On an arbitrary smooth homotopy four-sphere one may choose an auxiliary smooth Riemannian metric and its chiral rank-three bundle E. Local coordinates and bundle trivializations allow local round model connections on small balls. This supplies local definite data, not a global definite connection. The O(1) first-jet benchmark constructs charts only in its integrable round setting. The real O(2) comparison starts with an existing definite A and therefore cannot itself provide the missing A without a new construction.

Assess one proposed initialization mechanism: transport local round data into charts and patch their connection forms. Exact cocycle-compatible connection forms glue, and definiteness is then a local property. But specifying such compatible definite forms is precisely the missing global existence requirement. A partition of unity on the affine space of connections does produce a global connection; positivity of its curvature is not an affine condition. If A1=A0+a, then

F_(A0+t a)=(1−t)F_A0+t F_A1−t(1−t)[a wedge a]/2.

The quadratic term is uncontrolled by endpoint definiteness. Freeze E06 to test this obstruction in the smallest possible class: two gauge-equivalent round-positive definite connections on the same bundle whose arithmetic midpoint loses definiteness at one point. A proposed construction chooses a round-normal gauge at p with F_12=−K e3, a gauge transformation g(p)=I with a_1=2sqrt(K)e1 and a_2=−2sqrt(K)e2, and evaluates the midpoint. Endpoints are the same geometry in different gauges; the example addresses naive affine averaging, not every gluing method. It may be made global on standard S⁴ with a gauge supported in a trivializing ball; that is a validation example on a known sphere, not initialization of an unknown sphere.

The next useful input for a positive construction would be a gauge-covariant overlap correction with quantitative control of d_A a and [a,a], plus a cocycle solution on the actual bundle, independently of a preexisting definite connection or ASD metric. No such overlap theorem is supplied by the present real transport identity. This is a scoped obstruction map for one gluing route, not a general impossibility result or a topological classification.
