## Decision

Make the nonlinear definite-connection defect-gradient flow the primary
analytic object. Keep the full smooth four-dimensional Poincaré problem
as the motivating objective. Seek intermediate hypotheses from the actual
evolution, regularity and compactness estimates. Do not preselect a small-Q
sphere theorem as the required first result.

The earlier [special-case assessment](SPECIAL_CASE_TARGETS_20261122.md)
remains a useful historical comparison with known classifications.
Its intrinsic small-defect proposal becomes one candidate to test, rather
than the selected research target. Established sphere cases can be used
as validation cases even if their diffeomorphism conclusion is already
known. Separate a new convergence or continuation theorem from a new
classification theorem.

1. **Foundation:** complete the existing finite audit SP4-CME-E01. Keep its
   six claims and scope intact; use its result to prevent a normalization
   or compatibility error from propagating into the nonlinear work.
2. **Exact nonlinear structure:** SP4-CME-B01 should freeze one calculation,
   normally Q_t with the induced metric and compatibility terms visible.
   Specify the gauge, sign, K normalization and derivative order. Identify
   the principal operator and all terms obstructing a useful sign estimate.
   A separate compatibility evolution can be the next dependency if needed;
   it is not silently added to an already frozen packet.
3. **Continuation:** seek geometric hypotheses sufficient to extend a
   solution. Separate staying inside the definite region from controlling
   reconstructed geometry. Do not treat |F_A|²_ĝ = 6K² or fixed total
   volume as curvature or noncollapse estimates.
4. **Tests:** recover round damping and published local stability as
   normalization/analytic benchmarks. Select any stronger intrinsic or
   symmetric case only after verifying that its proposed conditions apply
   to the connection flow and can be preserved. A known manifold
   classification alone does not close this test.
5. **Long-time endpoint:** address compactness and exclusion of nonperfect
   stationary limits. Energy dissipation alone does not supply either.
6. **Initialization on unknown smooth structures:** retain a separate
   obligation to construct appropriate definite data on arbitrary smooth
   homotopy four-spheres, or explicitly replace this formalism's starting
   hypothesis with a justified broader mechanism.

The immediate frozen E02 packet must still contain only one finite
