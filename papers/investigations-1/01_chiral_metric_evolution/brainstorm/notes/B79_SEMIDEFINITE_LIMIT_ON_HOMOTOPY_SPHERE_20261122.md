# B79 — Test whether topology excludes a semidefinite compact limit

22 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI assistance. Conceptual B decision by the selected lead; actual lead identifiers unavailable. This is a proposed finite proof test, not an assessed theorem.

E155's bundle identifications and the bounds on b, its full covariant derivative and Q's first derivative do not use a quantitative Q floor until the final positivity step. Remove that floor while retaining the supplied positive C1 metric limit and uniform live |T|+|nabla^A T|. The same construction should yield a continuous compatible connection/curvature pair and nonnegative trace-three Q limit. The question is whether such a degenerate limit is impossible on a closed simply connected oriented four-manifold with H²(M;R)=0, in particular a smooth homotopy four-sphere.

The proposed mechanism is kernel transport. From D_A Q=2S(T−tr(QT)I/3)S and |Q|<=3, the quadratic-form derivative is bounded by 4|T| times Q. Along any fixed curve and A_n-parallel vector, Gronwall controls endpoint quadratic forms in both directions with constants independent of the least eigenvalue. After the normalized connections converge uniformly, parallel transports converge along each fixed curve. The limiting kernel should therefore have constant rank and be A_infinity-parallel.

At constant rank, its projection can be made C1 from the parallel equation with the continuous connection. The continuous distributional curvature must preserve the kernel. If Q has rank two, the stabilizer of its kernel line in so(3) has curvature along that line, whereas F=−sqrt(Q)sigma has internal image equal to its perpendicular plane: a contradiction. If Q has rank one, the parallel image line admits a global parallel unit section on the simply connected base. Its scalar curvature form is closed and self-dual with nonzero constant norm in the positive limiting metric. Stokes and H²=0 would exclude it. Trace three excludes rank zero. Rank three gives the desired positive limit.

E157 will check all low-regularity, bundle and rank arguments, especially passage of the quadratic inequality and Bianchi, regularity of a parallel projection, and the C1 closed-form/Stokes step. If any premise fails, retain the E155 floor and report the exact obstruction. No smooth limiting connection, convergence of gauges, source compactness theorem, global flow estimate or solution of initialization may be presumed. The manifold hypotheses are essential; the rank-one limit may correspond to a symplectic class on other manifolds. The target is only a refinement of conditional compactness on this specified topology, not a general exclusion of degeneration.
