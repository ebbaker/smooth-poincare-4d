The proposed proof uses the curvature-normalized chiral isomorphism E to Lambda+ for each slice and a canonical positive metric isometry from one fixed reference metric to phi_n* g_n. This identifies the pulled-back connections on one fixed bundle so the transformed Gamma_n depends only on the converging metric and its first derivative. The trace-three Q and its first covariant derivative are bounded by the strain dictionary; its fixed-chart derivative includes the bounded b and Gamma terms. Arzela–Ascoli and F=−sqrt(Q) sigma then provide uniform pair convergence, a positive floor and weak curvature compatibility. The construction must explicitly verify the direction of every bundle map and that no uniform derivative bound on the chiral identifications or original diffeomorphisms is silently needed.
