# B74 — Value compactness modulo symmetries at finite time

22 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III
with OpenAI assistance. Conceptual B decision by the selected lead;
actual model and effort unavailable.

The assessed E138–E143 proof starts an original flow for one common
positive time from every smooth compatible datum in a fixed value
ball about one smooth reference. Individual recovery maps may have
large derivatives; those do not change the available interval.
This suggests a continuation criterion useful for any later real
coordinate reconstruction compactness theorem.

Test precisely this implication: if a supplied original smooth
definite flow at a finite endpoint has a sequence of slices tending
to that endpoint which, after smooth orientation-preserving bundle
automorphisms of the same fixed bundle, converges in connection and
curvature C0 values to a continuous positive definite pair, then
the original flow extends. No bounds on derivatives of those
automorphisms should be needed. The fixed positive limit gives
one reference and one time; one sufficiently late normalized slice
can be evolved, recovered, and pulled back by its one fixed
normalization. Smooth original uniqueness then handles the overlap.

This uses only one convergent sequence, not compactness of the
entire family. Its contrapositive excludes positive C0 accumulation
modulo these symmetries at a finite maximal endpoint. It does not
construct normalizations or prove compactness from bounded pointwise
curvature. State it as a conditional compactness criterion, not
an a priori theorem or a general-data placement result. If the
proof requires a hidden uniform recovery estimate, identify that
exact dependency rather than adding it silently.

Delegate the bounded implication as E148. Inputs are the
[uniform radius](../../notes/138_UNIFORM_VALUE_RADIUS_LOCAL_THEOREM_20261122.md),
[original restart](../../notes/140_CONDITIONAL_FINITE_BUDGET_ORIGINAL_RESTART_20261122.md)
and [combined assessment](../../reviews/E138_E143_GENERAL_CONTINUATION_ASSESSMENT_20261122.md).
The fixed-base internal gauge group alone is not the full group
used here, and no change of base manifold or bundle is allowed.
The same-slice curvature/metric naturality and smooth uniqueness
are inherited. No new primary theorem is proposed for import.
