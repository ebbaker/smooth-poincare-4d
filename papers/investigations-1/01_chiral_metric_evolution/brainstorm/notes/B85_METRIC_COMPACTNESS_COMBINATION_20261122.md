# B85 — Conditional combination of metric compactness dependencies

22 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III
with OpenAI assistance. Conceptual B decision by the selected lead;
actual model/effort unavailable. The exact BKN source input is checked
and the supplied-core topology proof is assessed. The pre-ALE E165
construction is still under independent audit. Its conclusion must
remain an explicit conditional premise in the next packet.

Test the resulting static implication on a fixed smooth closed
oriented homotopy four-sphere: absolute Ricci and W+ bounds, positive
volume lower bound and diameter upper bound should imply positive
C1 metric compactness modulo same-base smooth diffeomorphisms.
If minimum harmonic radii tend to zero, E165 would give a smooth
complete nonflat Ricci-flat ASD limit with Euclidean volume growth,
finite energy and smooth embeddings of fixed compact subsets.
BKN would make it ALE, and the supplied-core obstruction would
contradict the embedding of a sufficiently large full truncation.

The complementary case needs a proof too. A positive harmonic-radius
lower bound and bounded diameter give finitely many charts. General
bounded-Ricci limits need not be smooth. Construct the comparison
diffeomorphisms with one fixed smooth approximating manifold as the
reference, or explicitly justify the smoothing of the limiting atlas.
Do not silently use smooth Einstein regularity on this unscaled
sequence. One possible route uses the cutoff-coordinate Euclidean
embeddings from E165: uniform C2 convergence, compactness, curvature
and separation give a common tubular neighborhood, and one fixed
smooth embedded approximant supplies the smooth target for the
projection. Its inverse maps should give C1 metric convergence.
Establish the needed global injectivity and surjectivity, rather
than assuming a collection of local charts gives diffeomorphisms.

E168 will carry out this fixed combination, conditional on an explicitly
named H_pre equal to the frozen E165 conclusion. It will also spell
out the flow consequence: native volume is conserved, and a bound
on Psi gives Ricci, W+ and strain bounds. Thus a supplied original
flow on a homotopy four-sphere would extend at a finite endpoint
if Psi and diameter are simultaneously bounded on one late sequence.
No injectivity lower bound, full-curvature supremum bound or
normalizer-derivative estimate should be claimed as a separate
consequence. The intended result is positive C1 metric compactness
and then the already assessed pair/flow continuation criterion.

The lead will discharge H_pre only after the E167 audit and critical
review. This packet does not justify an a priori estimate, long-time
convergence or suitable initial data on arbitrary homotopy spheres.
