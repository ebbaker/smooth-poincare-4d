# B84 — Test a minimum harmonic-radius alternative to bubble trees

22 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III
with OpenAI assistance. Conceptual B decision by the selected lead,
actual model/effort unavailable. This is a proposed static compactness
mechanism, not an established improvement to the flow criterion.

The source search has not supplied a complete bounded-Ricci terminal
bubble-tree theorem with the required embeddings. R29's proof of
Theorem 2.2 instead suggests rescaling at a global minimum of the
harmonic radius on the closed base. After normalizing that minimum
to one, every point has a common harmonic-coordinate radius. This
may directly give a complete smooth Ricci-flat pointed limit,
avoiding intermediate orbifold points. The injectivity premise in
R29 was used to force flatness through lines; here nonflatness would
be retained, followed by a separate ALE theorem and the E162
conditional topology exclusion.

The proposed exact metric inputs are a fixed smooth closed oriented
homotopy four-sphere, uniform absolute Ricci and W+ bounds, positive
volume lower bound and diameter upper bound. Bishop–Gromov supplies
scale-wise volume lower bounds and an upper total volume bound;
four-dimensional Gauss–Bonnet supplies finite total curvature
energy. Rescaling should send Ricci and W+ to zero and preserve
energy. These elementary consequences do not themselves prove
pointed smooth-manifold compactness, continuity of the normalized
harmonic radius or the ALE conclusion.

Separate two bounded E dependencies. E164 reads the actual
Bando–Kasue–Nakajima ALE theorem for a complete Ricci-flat limit
with maximal Euclidean volume growth and finite critical curvature
energy. Preserve all decay, dimension, simple-connectivity or end
hypotheses rather than inferring them from later citations.
E165 tests the global minimum harmonic-radius construction up to
the complete nonflat smooth Ricci-flat ASD limit with Euclidean
volume growth, finite energy and embeddings of every fixed compact
subset. It must show exactly where the R29 injectivity assumption
can be removed from the compactness part, distinguish C1,alpha and
weak/strong W2,p convergence, justify nonflatness and regularity,
and retain any missing standard compactness input as a stated gap.
It may use an explicitly conditional ALE premise only after the
pre-ALE construction is established. No combined singular-free
compactness or flow improvement is accepted before both dependencies
and the topology argument are assessed.
