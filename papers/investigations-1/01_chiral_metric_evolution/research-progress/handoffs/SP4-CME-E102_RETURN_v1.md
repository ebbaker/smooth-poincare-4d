## Disposition

The static defect estimate holds on the stated actual round
value tube with neither fixed slice equation imposed. For
\(q=Q(A)-I\) and \(\ell=\|T(A)\|_{L^{4,1}(g_A)}\),
\[
 \|q\|_{W^{1,(4,1)};0}+\|q\|_{C^0}\le C\ell .
\]
The ordinary connection Laplacian on \(\operatorname{Sym}_0(E)\)
has principal symbol \(|\xi|_{g_0}^2I_5\) and trivial kernel.
The curvature spans the adjoint fiber, so a parallel
symmetric endomorphism commutes with every infinitesimal
rotation and is scalar. Trace-freeness gives zero.
Its separate divergence inverse uses the actual rank-five
induced connection blocks, with no kernel projection term.
The E88/E89 cutoffs and annular estimates apply after this
substitution and give the common \(L^2,L^8\) weak inverse,
then \(L^{4,1}\to W^{1,(4,1)}\) by layers and the ray
estimate for continuity.

The value tube gives fixed/live metric and density
comparison. Strain inversion gives the live bound
\(|\nabla^AQ|\le6|T|\).
The exact error in \(D_0q\) is
\(-[\operatorname{ad}(a),q]\).
After fixing the geometric radius and reference inverse
constants, shrink the tube so that
\(C_L C_{\rm ad}\|a\|_\infty\le1/2\).
Absorption uses only the values of \(a\) and \(\beta\),
with no estimates on their derivatives.

For \(\ell\le\delta/C\), \(0<\delta<1\), the eigenvalues of
\(Q\) lie in \([1-\delta,1+\delta]\) everywhere. Zero
strain implies \(Q=I\). The eigenvalue bound and live
Lorentz strain norm are invariant under internal gauges
and transfer to a supplied fixed allowed base pullback
by naturality. The \(W^{1,(4,1)}\) estimate holds for the
stated fixed reference and value-tube representative.
The calculation supplies no uniform reference-derivative
bound under arbitrary base pullbacks, full critical-pair
bound, finite slice, temporal budget, persistence or
arbitrary-sphere initialization. No new primary source
