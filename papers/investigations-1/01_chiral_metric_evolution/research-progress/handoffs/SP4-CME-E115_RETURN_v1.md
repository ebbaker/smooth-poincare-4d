## Disposition and derivative passage

The frozen E112 linear example rules out any uniform upper bound
of the form \(J\le C\|T(A_{\rm in})\|_{L^4(G_{A_{\rm in}})}\)
on a fixed positive radius of smooth data small in connection and
curvature values, even with a separate fixed small initial \(L^4\)
threshold. This implication is in the established smooth round
value basin, using native geometry and time.

Fix the adjusted existence time and its half-time splice.
Shrink the upper annular scale once, independently of \(N\),
so all E112 native windows \(I_j=[\alpha r_j^2/4,\beta r_j^2/4]\)
lie before the splice. The own-scale error and damping remain
controlled and the off-scale sums depend on ratios. Their first
\(N\) windows \(W_N\) lie in a compact positive-time strip
\(I_N\). Fix \(N\) and vary \(A_0+\varepsilon a_N\).
The exact initial pair is polynomial in \(\varepsilon\), with
tangent \((a_N,d_0a_N)\). Joint Fréchet smoothness in E42 gives
operator-norm continuity of \(D_u\partial_s^\alpha\Psi\).
Taylor expansion is uniform on the compact lifted parameter
ball through \(|\alpha|=2\), and the fixed E63 evaluation
patches give the derivative in \(C(I_N;C^2)\).
The untransformed derivative in \(X_{\mathcal T}^\circ\)
also supplies its continuous value trace at zero.

Differentiating the compatible adjusted connection equation
on positive-time strips gives a linear physical term and a
tangent orbit correction, since the reference generator is
zero. The exact naturality \(Q(g^*A_0)=I\) implies that
\(DQ_{A_0}\) kills the correction. The native composition
in E112 gives \(q_\tau'=-(4\mathcal L+12)q'\), with initial
value \(DQ_{A_0}(a_N)\). Uniqueness identifies \(q'=q_N\)
and the linear strain is \(T_N=\tfrac12D_0q_N\).
On \(I_N\), \(T/\varepsilon\) converges in fixed spatial \(C^1\)
and the full \(\nabla T/\varepsilon\) converges uniformly to
\(D_0T_N\), with the live metric converging in \(C^2\).
The initial live \(L^4\) norm divided by \(\varepsilon\)
converges to \(\|T_N(0)\|_{4,G}\) by the initial smooth curve
and uniform volume convergence. On the head, exact per-slice
recovery pullbacks transfer the full live derivative norm and
its spatial supremum. No differentiability or uniform
derivative bound for those maps is required.

Under the proposed bound, drop its nonnegative square term
and integrate only over \(W_N\). First send
\(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) at fixed \(N\). This gives
\[
 c_*\sqrt N\le\int_{W_N}\|D_0T_N\|_{\infty,G}\,d\tau
                   \le C\|T_N(0)\|_{4,G}\le C C_*N^{-1/4}.
\]
Only now send \(N\to\infty\), contradicting the fixed constant.
The calculation uses heat time \(s=4\tau\) inside E112 and
the original native clock for the budget. No nonlinear
remainder uniform in \(N\), unbounded \(J\) near zero or
failure of an arbitrary continuity modulus follows.
Only the two named E115 files were written, and no new
primary source was inspected.
