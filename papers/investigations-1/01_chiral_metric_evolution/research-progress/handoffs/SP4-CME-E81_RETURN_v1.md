## Disposition

Hamilton III.1.1.1 requires a smooth tame map on an open domain
and a jointly smooth tame inverse family of the derivative there.
III.1.1.3 gives a smooth tame local right inverse when the
derivative instead has a jointly smooth tame right-inverse
family throughout the neighborhood. E79's round-kernel
restriction and forcing annihilator fit the input and output
blocks of Hamilton II.3.3.3 at the scalar elliptic level.
The full orbit chart differential has the E79 candidate
right inverse after undoing pullback and using the actual
horizontal lift. This route allows the full automorphism
parameter and needs no two-sided invertibility or finite
chart integrating the restricted complement. The two-sided
route would need a genuine restricted chart and control
of its tangent spaces. Both routes require their stated
smooth tame chart, action and derivative-solver hypotheses.
The separate \(H^m\) estimates of E79 leave that verification
open. A smooth local section would give placement in a
smooth neighborhood. Placement with constants under
value-only or critical-regularity assumptions is separate.
No nonlinear slice theorem is claimed.

Fine v2 gives the actual infinitesimal action in §3.2,
the linear elliptic gauge in §3.3, and time-dependent gauge
correspondences in §§4.2–4.3. The paper's checked material
does not provide a finite static slice for E71's fixed
conditions. Only the named review and return were edited.
