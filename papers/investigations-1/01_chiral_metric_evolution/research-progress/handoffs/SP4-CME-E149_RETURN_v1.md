## Result

**Bounded no match.** Six focused searches and selected passages
in four primary works did not identify the assessed finite-\(J\)
or continuous positive \(C^0\) pair-endpoint criterion for Fine's
exact unadjusted (4.1), or the uniform value-radius and
reference-dependent time refinement for its adjusted pair
system. This finding concerns the searched texts and terms.
It supplies no general a priori strain bound or novelty conclusion.
