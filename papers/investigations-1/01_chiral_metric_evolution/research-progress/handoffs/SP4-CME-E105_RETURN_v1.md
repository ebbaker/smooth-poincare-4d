## Disposition

Pass at the frozen scope. The ordinary induced connection Laplacian
on \(\operatorname{Sym}_0(E)\) has scalar metric principal symbol
on the rank-five fiber. Its curvature action is the commutator
with the internal infinitesimal rotations, which span the full
skew algebra at each point. A parallel symmetric endomorphism is
therefore scalar, and trace-freeness gives the trivial kernel.
The resulting coercive \(L^2\) divergence inverse uses neither
slice equation nor a kernel subtraction. Its actual metric flux,
lower blocks and cutoff signs match E102. The checked E88/E89
annular method extends to these blocks, yielding \(W^{1,8}\) on
the same weak inverse and the Lorentz derivative bound by arbitrary
vector layers. The ray estimate yields the fixed \(C^0\) endpoint.

The strain inversion has Hilbert–Schmidt operator norm at most
\(2\lambda_{\max}(Q)\le6\). Live-to-fixed conversion for its one
base covector slot has factor \(2^{1/4}\sqrt{3/2}\), with metric
and volume comparisons supplied by curvature values alone. The
exact error in \(D_0q\) is \(-[\operatorname{ad}(a),q]\), a
trace-free symmetric endomorphism in each base slot. After fixing
the reference constants, the tube can be shrunk to absorb this
error through \(\|a\|_\infty\). This proves the static coefficient
pinching with neither \(d_0^*a\) nor \(S_0^*a\) imposed. In particular,
zero strain gives \(Q=I\) in the stated definite component.

Eigenvalue pinching, sup defect and live Lorentz strain transfer
under smooth internal gauges and a supplied smooth orientation-preserving
bundle pullback. The fixed-volume value norm of the defect is internally
invariant. The derivative formula and a local family of internal
gauges verify failure of invariance of the derivative norm when
the reference is held fixed. The stated derivative estimate is
for a representative in the fixed value tube. This audit supplies
no uniform fixed-reference derivative bound under arbitrary base
pullbacks, temporal persistence, or full critical-pair control.
