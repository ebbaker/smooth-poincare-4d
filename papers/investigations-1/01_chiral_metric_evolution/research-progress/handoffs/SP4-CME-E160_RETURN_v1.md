## Disposition

**Retain the supplied injectivity premise.** The five exact frozen
inputs matched. Four distinct discovery and metadata queries were
run, and selected passages of three primary works were inspected.
The checked material does not give a singular-free compactness
theorem for the exact fixed homotopy four-sphere sequence with
uniform \(|\operatorname{Ric}|,|W^+|\), positive volume lower bound
and diameter upper bound.

- **R29, Anderson author PDF.** Theorem 2.6, (2.17), and the
  proof discussion, printed pp. 9–10, give an orbifold GH
  limit with regular-set \(C^{1,\alpha}\) convergence. The
  proof discussion also asserts nontrivial Ricci-flat ALE
  rescalings near singular points and topologically essential
  embeddings. This is more than an orbifold-only discussion,
  while exact nested extraction and smooth compact-core/end
  embeddings are not spelled out there. Ricci and \(W^+\)
  vanish in an oriented regular blow-up with the indicated
  convergence since their norms scale by \(\lambda_i^{-2}\).
  This establishes the chirality of the supplied regular part,
  without supplying the required smooth ALE leaf by itself.
  [R29 primary PDF][r29].
- **Wright.** *Quotients of gravitational instantons*,
  arXiv:1102.2442v2, 29 April 2011, Theorem A, §§2–3 and
  7–8, and Corollary B checked in the versioned primary PDF.
  The theorem is for smooth oriented nonflat
  non-simply-connected Ricci-flat ASD ALE manifolds, with
  positive ALE order and the stated derivative decay,
  and no assumed spin or Kähler hypothesis. It gives cyclic
  isometric Gibbons–Hawking quotients and a Kähler conclusion.
  The smooth \(b_2=0\) quotients remain candidates, with
  deck degree \(d\ge2\) and end group order \(d^2\).
  Published metadata were verified separately.
  [Wright v2][wright] · [Publisher record][wrightpub].
- **Kapovitch–Lott.** *On noncollapsed almost Ricci-flat
  4-manifolds*, *American Journal of Mathematics* 141(3),
  737–755 (2019), DOI 10.1353/ajm.2019.0015. Theorem 1.1,
  Corollary 1.2 and §§2.2–2.4 checked in the author-hosted
  published text. Its proof first allows order-four Ricci-flat
  ALE orbifold nodes and then treats smooth leaves in a
  finite tree. The theorem and the proof's global spinor
  mechanism need spin with nonzero \(\widehat A\), and the
  scalar or Ricci pinching specified there. A homotopy sphere
  has \(\widehat A=0\). The result does not apply here.
  [Published primary text][kaplott].

The narrow next dependency is an exact bounded-Ricci extraction
theorem giving a nonflat smooth ALE leaf with the full compact-core
and end-section embeddings under these hypotheses, together with
a topology check excluding the remaining \(b_2=0\) smooth quotients,
or a directly stated singular-free theorem. Ambient simple
connectivity does not imply that of a bubble. A nonzero
self-intersection excludes an embedded \(b_2>0\) core on the
ambient homotopy sphere. The review gives only a conditional
end-homology test for \(b_2=0\), and does not assert the necessary
embedding or exclusion. No no-collapse theorem was proved or
imported, and no source file was downloaded. Relevant source
discoveries and exact inspection upgrades were sent to the lead
for canonical registration. Only the assigned files were written.
