The proposed threshold is sufficient. On a smooth closed
oriented homotopy four-sphere with the assumed smooth
round-positive definite connection, the pointwise
\(\mathfrak R_K\leq K\) gives
\(s\geq(12-4\sqrt3)K>0\) and \(|W^+|_{\rm HS}\leq K\).
The Yamabe constant of the reconstructed conformal class is
positive by the conformal scalar formula and compact Sobolev
inequality. The curvature-span isomorphism \(E\cong\Lambda^+\),
\(\langle p_1(E),[M]\rangle=2\chi+3\tau=4\), and
\(\mu=(4\pi^2/3)p_1(A)\) in the manuscript's internal pairing
give \(\operatorname{Vol}=8\pi^2/(3K^2)\).

Thus \(E_+\leq8\pi^2/3\) in the chiral Hilbert–Schmidt norm,
and \(E_-=E_+\) by the zero signature.
The total four-index Weyl energy is \(8E_+\leq64\pi^2/3<32\pi^2\).
The published Chang–Gursky–Yang Theorem A, journal p. 107,
eq. (0.3) and Remarks 1–2, was read directly in the Numdam PDF.
The theorem applies with this strict inequality and positive
Yamabe constant. It gives \(S^4\) or \(\mathbb{RP}^4\) up to
diffeomorphism, and \(\pi_1(M)=0\) excludes the latter.
[Published theorem and norm convention][cgy].

For fixed \(A\), \(K\to K'\) sends \(g\) to \((K/K')g\), and
\(\mathfrak R_K\) to \((K'/K)\mathfrak R_K\). The condition
therefore depends on \(\mathfrak R_K/K\), with no benefit from
changing \(K\). The result is a static sufficient criterion
using a known theorem. It asserts neither existence of data
satisfying the condition on an arbitrary homotopy sphere nor
preservation or convergence of the flow.
