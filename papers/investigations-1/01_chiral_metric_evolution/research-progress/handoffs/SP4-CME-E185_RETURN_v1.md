## Disposition

**Pass.** E182 supplies the exact \(H_{\rm var}\) of B90.
E183's varying-base energy and universal initial small-\(\Psi\)
thresholds follow under that premise. No mathematically necessary
repair or new premise was found. E183 remains historically
conditional as written and the lead owns its separate discharge.

- At a vanishing minimum harmonic radius the comparisons use each
  target's own distance and harmonic charts. The full energy is
  uniformly bounded because \(\chi(M_i)=2\). Compact embeddings
  in individual targets give the intrinsic-ball harmonic-coordinate
  contradiction for a flat limit. The nonflat limit satisfies BKN
  and its full smooth ALE core contradicts the integral homology
  sphere obstruction in each separate target.
- The positive-radius case places the separate bases in one finite
  Euclidean space. The tube and displacement margins are set before
  choosing one smooth late reference. The actual \(P_i\) are
  globally injective on each source and surjective onto the connected
  reference. Their smooth positive inverses yield strong
  \(C^{1,1/4}\) and weak \(W^{2,8}\) convergence to a positive
  metric in one reference atlas. Common smooth type is a conclusion.
- The native characteristic volume satisfies
  \(v_i=(8\pi^2/3)\langle p_1(E_i),[M_i]\rangle\ge8\pi^2/3\)
  by positivity and integrality. Together with the floor-free
  Ricci, \(W^+\) and diameter bounds it meets \(H_{\rm var}\).
  The polar maps lift the base diffeomorphisms to smooth
  \(H_i:P_{\rm ref}\to P_i\). Their pullbacks preserve native
  energy, \(\sup\Psi\), diameter and the curvature-map sign.
  E177 is applied only after both reference objects are fixed.
  Inverse pullback transports the actual original solution and
  its positive-time entry and fixed round limit back to each
  original bundle, with the same native clock.
- The universal small-\(\Psi\) condition gives diameter
  at most \(2\pi\) by the index form. The B90 model ball volume
  upper bound and half-minimum threshold give strict energy
  below \(\delta_0/4\), hence below \(\delta_0\).

The thresholds are non-effective. They give no uniform entry
time, derivative bound on comparison maps, datum-independent
higher constants or construction of qualifying data. No shared
edit, new source search, build or numerical test entered the audit.
