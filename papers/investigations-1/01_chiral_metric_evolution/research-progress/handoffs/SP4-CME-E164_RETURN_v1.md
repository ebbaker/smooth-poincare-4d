## Result

Bando–Kasue–Nakajima Theorem (1.5), p. 314, gives ALE order four
for a smooth complete connected Ricci-flat Riemannian four-manifold
with positive Euclidean volume growth at a fixed point and finite
full-curvature \(L^2\) integral. The one-end conclusion follows in
the proof on p. 335. The p. 313 definition gives a smooth quotient
end chart with finite \(\Gamma\subset O(4)\) acting freely on \(S^3\).
If the manifold is oriented, \(\Gamma\subset SO(4)\). Remark (1.8)(1)
gives \(D^k(h-\delta)=O(\rho^{-4-k})\) on the chart cover for each
\(k\ge0\). The faster-than-quadratic full-curvature decay used by
Theorem (1.1) is obtained in the proof. Simple connectivity at
infinity appears in the separate rigidity remark.
[Published scan](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/Courses/MAT683/201001/MAT683S10.pdf).

This discharges the ALE source premise for a B84 candidate once its
hypotheses have been supplied. The implication concerns the candidate
itself. The theorem for exact Einstein sequences is the distinct
Theorem (5.5).
