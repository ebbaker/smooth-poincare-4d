## Disposition

The B56 construction works on the actual round chiral bundle.
The time-one base flow \(\psi_n\) of the compactly supported
cutoff field is an exact shear on the inner cube, as is its
negative-time inverse with opposite sign. The identity on a
chart collar gives a smooth equivariant lift \(G_n\) and
inverse on the fixed principal bundle. In fixed chart
comparisons the base and bundle maps and their inverses
have displacement \(O(n^{-7/4})\), \(C^1\) error
\(O(n^{-3/4})\), and second derivative growth comparable
to \(n^{1/4}\).

The actual connections \(A_n=G_n^*A_0\) have curvature
\(G_n^*F_0\). For fixed \(r_0=\iota_{u_0}F_0\) and
\(r_n=G_n^*r_0\), exterior covariant derivative naturality
gives \(d_{A_n}r_n=G_n^*d_0r_0\). Fixed-chart pullback
estimates, with all fixed cutoff and frame terms,
give \(O(n^{-1/4})\) convergence of both differences
\((A_n-A_0,F_{A_n}-F_0)\) and
\((r_n-r_0,d_{A_n}r_n-d_0r_0)\)
in localized \(B^{1/2}_{8,1}\).
The fixed \(N^\perp\) normalization of E79 gives
\(\phi_n=0\) and residual zero exactly, since
\(r_n=S_{A_n}u_n\) with \(u_n=\psi_n^*u_0\).
Its horizontal output differs from \(u_0\) by
\(O(n^{-3/4})\) in \(C^0\) and grows in \(C^1\) with
lower and upper bounds comparable to \(n^{1/4}\).
This gives failure of horizontal \(C^1\) continuity of
the selected E79 solver in this joint critical topology
at \((A_0,r_0)\).

The connections are perfect with \(Q=I\) and \(T=0\).
Their original Fine flow is stationary. The distinct
auxiliary flow of the actual horizontal lift is
\(G_n^{-1}\circ\Theta_{0,t}\circ G_n\), with inverse
obtained by \(t\mapsto-t\). Both maps differ from their
reference counterparts in \(C^1\) by \(O_T(n^{-3/4})\)
on \(|t|\le T<\infty\). This example concerns only the
chosen right inverse and input topology. It does not
settle other solvers, finite slice placement or general
nonlinear critical results. No new primary source was
used. Only the named note and return were edited.
