connected oriented real four-manifold and fixed principal
\(SO(3)\) bundle, a supplied smooth native original flow in
the positive-plane round-compatible component extends
smoothly and definitely in its original identification if
some sequence \(t_n\to t_*<\infty\) has simultaneous uniform
whole-fiber distortion and real transport density bounds,
positive injectivity-radius lower bound and finite diameter
upper bound in the live metrics. At a finite maximal
smooth definite lifespan every such sequence is excluded.

\[
 k^2=4\pi/5,\qquad B=(1+4\sqrt3)P_0/k^2,\qquad
