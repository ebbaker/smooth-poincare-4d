## Disposition

E71 passes within its fixed native round linear scope. The native
curvature isometry gives \(S^*S=\tfrac32I\) and
\(\sigma_2(d^*\Pi d)(\xi)=\tfrac23|\xi|^2I\). The compact form
realization has \(H^2\) domain and finite smooth kernel. An
\(L^2\) connection difference gives an \(H^{-1}\) forcing that
annihilates the kernel, and the weak inverse gains one derivative
in the vertical parameter. The exact orbit-plus-slice decomposition
gives the closed orbit sum, its parameter ambiguity and the
self-adjoint \(L^2\) residual projection. E71's integer \(m\ge0\)
bounds all hold. The audit also records the extension of the bounds
to negative integers by Sobolev duality.

The active pullback tangent for the fixed \(A_0\)-horizontal lift
is \(SX+\iota_X da+d(a(X))\), with exact bracket cancellation.
The inverse pullback reverses the tangent. The localized
oscillations give an unbounded \(H^m\)-to-\(H^m\) derivative
in the flow parameter as \(a\) varies for one fixed smooth \(X\).
The derivative test and the linear inverse leave the nonlinear
slice question open. I found no mathematical gap in E71 as
stated. No new primary source was inspected. Only the named
review and return were edited.
