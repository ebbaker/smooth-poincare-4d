## Disposition

Four search queries and four selected primary passages were checked.
None of the selected passages gives the same closed \(7/2\) stationary
theorem, effective stationary-kernel and weighted-flow estimates, or
all-\(r>1\) local ratio obstruction for the trace-three connection
defect energy. The finding is bounded to those passages.

- Fine's same-equation Proposition 4.5 and Lemma 4.9 concern the
  connection linearization at one covector, with gauge and base
  action in the symbol kernel. This differs from a nonlinear
  stationary derivative-form or scalar Hessian calculation.
  [Fine v2 §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1111.5005v2#S4.SS2).
- The published Fine–Krasnov–Panov interpretation of Theorem 4.14
  excludes intermediate critical points of the connection **volume
  action**. Conjecture 4.16 separately proposes deformation to such
  a critical point. [Published §4.3, p. 309](https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2014/20-18v.pdf#page=17).
- The Fine–Yao passage is Lemma 4.11 and (4.8)–(4.10).
  The scalar maximum principle preserves the strict trace bound
  \(2^{5/3}\) on a smooth compact hypersymplectic triple flow,
  with its determinant-one Gram normalization.
  [Fine–Yao v2 §4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2001.11755v2#S4.SS3).
- Fine–Panov Theorem 1.3 supplies the topology comparison under a
  faithful lifted circle action preserving a definite connection.
  Corollary 1.5 instead starts with an isometric action and a chiral
  curvature block inequality. The inspected preprint is sole arXiv
  v1, posted 3 July 2014. Journal metadata are recorded separately
  from the uninspected publication body.
  [Fine–Panov v1 §1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1407.0840v1#S1.SS1) ·
  [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.0840).

The existing canonical source records suffice. The review suggests
precise passage and consumer additions, and preserves R43 and R44
at their inherited inspection levels. No new theorem or proof-audit
