## Disposition

**The exact bounded-Ricci smooth terminal extraction remains
unverified.** This is a narrow source applicability and access
result. It supplies no combined topology, noncollapse or metric
compactness theorem. The present injectivity premise is retained.

- Reopened the published R32 §§2.3–2.4, pp. 745–747 and
  references p. 754. The precise citations are Anderson–Cheeger
  [AC91, Remark 3.1] and Bando [Ba90a, Proposition 2].
  Kapovitch–Lott's proof distinguishes intermediate Ricci-flat
  ALE orbifold nodes from smooth manifold leaves. Its theorem
  and the global hyperkähler inference use the nonzero spin
  index. [R32 published primary text][kl].
- Anderson–Cheeger, *Diffeomorphism finiteness for manifolds
  with Ricci curvature and \(L^{n/2}\)-norm of curvature bounded*,
  *Geometric and Functional Analysis* 1 (September 1991),
  231–252, DOI 10.1007/BF01896203, is the directly cited
  bounded-Ricci original. The primary publisher preview and
  author bibliography verify the identity. The PDF redirected
  to the preview and the EUDML route returned 403, so
  Remark 3.1 and its neighboring proof were not verified.
  [Publisher record][ac].
- Bando, *Bubbling out of Einstein manifolds*, *Tohoku
  Mathematical Journal*, second series, 42(2), 205–216
  (June 1990), DOI 10.2748/tmj/1178227654. Theorem A, p. 205,
  takes smooth Einstein metrics with bounded Einstein
  constants, diameter, volume and full \(L^{n/2}\) curvature
  bounds, and names a selected smooth ALE blow-up.
  Proposition 2, p. 210, allows a nonflat Ricci-flat ALE
  **orbifold** from the annular rescaling. Propositions 3–4
  and Theorem 1, pp. 210–212, give neck and finite assembly
  statements under the Einstein premise. The original
  preprint was used to clarify extraction typography.
  [Published journal PDF][bando].
- Bando's separately published *Correction and addition*,
  42(4), 587–588 (December 1990), DOI 10.2748/tmj/1178227575,
  changes the small-energy conclusion of Theorem 2 from
  Euclidean to flat, and justifies Proposition 2's
  nonflatness and finite termination in the Einstein
  setting. [Published correction][corr].
- R29's Theorem 2.2 proof, (2.5)–(2.9), uses the
  global-minimum harmonic-radius scale, complete pointed
  manifold convergence, and elliptic Ricci-flat upgrading.
  It uses the assumed injectivity bound to obtain lines,
  and does not state the sought nonflat ALE alternative
  under B83. Its harmonic-coordinate mechanism is a
  traceable ingredient for a separate derivation.
  [R29 author PDF][r29].

The remaining dependency is a checked no-injectivity extraction
under the supplied two-sided Ricci bound, local volume
noncollapse and full-curvature energy, giving a smooth complete
nonflat ALE manifold with compact core and finite end-collar
embeddings into the original smooth approximants. On its
oriented regular charts the scaled \(W^+\) goes to zero when
the stated metric convergence controls second derivatives
weakly. A separate ALE theorem may provide end asymptotics
after a direct pointed construction. No such theorem beyond
the three-work bound was inspected here.

Six discovery queries were used. Three new works were selected
for primary inspection, counting the separate correction, with
Anderson–Cheeger accessible only at metadata level. Short
geodesics and Bando–Kasue–Nakajima stayed discovery-level
candidates. The review records exact access routes and passage
locators. Relevant discoveries and inspection upgrades were sent
to the lead. No source bytes were retained. Only the designated
review and return were written.
