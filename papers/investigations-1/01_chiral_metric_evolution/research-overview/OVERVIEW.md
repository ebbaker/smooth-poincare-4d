## Objective and relation to the program

Develop analytical control of connection-driven metric evolution near the round four-sphere, and identify the additional geometric estimates needed to address arbitrary smooth homotopy four-spheres.

The inherited manuscript studies definite-connection curvature reconstruction, its exact metric velocity, the linear transverse-traceless correspondence with normalized Ricci flow, and nonlinear compatibility and degeneration issues.

## Starting evidence and its limits

The correspondence with Ricci flow is linearized near the round metric and has a specified factor-four time rescaling. It does not identify the full nonlinear flows. The fourth-order metric gradient of the pulled-back energy is a different evolution. The manuscript’s ASD-existence characterization of standard smooth four-spheres is an equivalent target based on classical results, not an existence proof. Initial definite connections on arbitrary homotopy four-spheres, quantitative definiteness preservation, curvature and noncollapse estimates, compactness, and global convergence remain open. The finite-density degeneration example establishes an algebraic limitation, not a singular flow trajectory.

The imported sources retain their working-draft status. Reorganization is not independent checking. The plan begins with a finite audit before asking the next conceptual question; it does not launch a new research campaign.
