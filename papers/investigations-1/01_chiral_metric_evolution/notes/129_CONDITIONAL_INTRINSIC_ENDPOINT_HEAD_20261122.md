## Fixed setting and conditional conclusion

Use the smooth closed oriented standard round Riemannian \(S^4\),
its positive perfect chiral \(\mathrm{SO}(3)\) connection \(A_0\),
native round reference metric \(G\) of sectional curvature \(1/2\),
and negative-Killing internal pairing. The fixed product derivative
\(D_0\) couples \(A_0\) to the native Levi–Civita connection on
the base slots. The time \(\tau\) is native. On the existing
E42/E50 compatible adjusted head write
\[
 U=(a,\zeta)=(\mathcal A-A_0,F_{\mathcal A}-F_0),\qquad
 q=Q(F_{\mathcal A})-I,\qquad g=g^{\rm F}_{\mathcal A}.
\]
For the actual smooth initial data put
\[
 v=\|A_{\rm in}-A_0\|_{\infty;0}+\|F_{A_{\rm in}}-F_0\|_{\infty;0},
 \qquad
 \ell=\|T(A_{\rm in})\|_{L^{4,1}(g^{\rm F}_{A_{\rm in}})} .
\]
The Lorentz convention is
\(\|z\|_{4,1}=4\int_0^\infty \mu_{|z|}(\lambda)^{1/4}\,d\lambda\)
with the measure and tensor metric named by each norm.

Use E125 (1) on the compatible head, now assessed at this
exact structural scope. The two E128 conclusions remain
conditional as formulated in its frozen request: the mixed
coefficient source estimate in the E53/E57 critical spaces
and the third weighted pair jet bound in the E42 setting.
The written E128 return was read when available and agrees

There are a fixed \(h>0\) and \(v_*>0\), independent of
higher norms of the datum and of \(\ell\), such that for
\(v<v_*\) the following bounds hold under the stated premises:
\[
 E_h(q)\le C\ell,\qquad
 \int_0^h\sup_{S^4}
        \bigl(|\nabla^A T(A)|_{g^{\rm F}_A}+|T(A)|_{g^{\rm F}_A}^2\bigr)
         \,d\tau\le C\ell .                                      \tag{1}
\]
The defect \(q\) in the first bound is on the fixed adjusted
head. The second bound is for the existing original smooth
flow \(A\) recovered on that head. No slice equation is
imposed. There is no smallness requirement on \(\ell\).

## Initial defect in the critical trace space

Fix \(s=1/2\) and the E57 finite atlas, bundle frames, supported
partition cutoffs and zero extensions to the unit tori for
\(\mathcal E=\operatorname{Sym}_0(E)\). If \(q_\alpha\) denotes
the resulting localized component array of a section \(q\), put
\[
 \|q\|_{\mathcal B^s}=\sum_{\alpha,j}2^{sj}\|\Delta_jq_\alpha\|_8,
 \quad
 M_h(q)=\sum_{\alpha,j}2^{sj}
                   \sup_{0\le\tau\le h}\|\Delta_jq_\alpha(\tau)\|_8,
 \quad
 H_h(q)=\sum_{\alpha,j}2^{(s+2)j}
                   \int_0^h\|\Delta_jq_\alpha(\tau)\|_8\,d\tau .
\]
Write \(E_h=M_h+H_h\) and \(F_h=L^1(0,h;\mathcal B^s)\).
The dyadic sum of the integrals in \(F_h\) is exactly this
Bochner norm by Tonelli. The positive-time supremum of E128
equals the supremum including zero for the smooth path here,
since each block is continuous at zero.
[E57, (1)–(3)][e57] · [E128 request, norms][req128].

In the fixed native value tube, E102 with no horizontal or
vertical slice equation gives
\[
 \|q(0)\|_{W^{1,(4,1)};0}+\|q(0)\|_\infty\le C\ell .
\]
Initial recovery is the identity. Thus \(q(0)\) is the actual
initial Gram defect, with the live strain norm of the given
datum on the right. In a supported E57 cutoff, the ordinary
derivative of its local components is a fixed bounded linear
combination of the components of \(D_0q(0)\) and \(q(0)\).
The latter terms include both the cutoff derivative and
fixed connection terms. There are no seam terms after zero
extension, and the fixed coordinate density relative to \(G\)
is bounded above and below on the support. In the Lorentz
layer formula such a density comparison contributes only a
fixed factor with exponent \(1/4\). Hence on each unit torus,
\[
 \|q_\alpha(0)\|_8\le C\|q(0)\|_\infty,\qquad
 \|\partial q_\alpha(0)\|_{4,1}
     \le C\bigl(\|D_0q(0)\|_{4,1;0}+\|q(0)\|_{4,1;0}\bigr).
\]
Applying E53 (4) and summing the fixed atlas gives
\[
                   \|q(0)\|_{\mathcal B^{1/2}}\le C\ell.           \tag{2}
\]
In particular the inhomogeneous low block is controlled by
the value term. [E102, (1) and absorption][e102] ·
[Lead assessment of E102][a102] · [E53, (4)][e53].

## Fixed operator and absorption

Fix the E42 head length \(\mathcal T\), reduced in the original
construction to at most one, and the positive E57 horizon
\(h_0\) for the specific fixed operator below. Choose once
\(0<h\le\min(\mathcal T/4,h_0)\). This lies inside the
already recovered smooth head before its fixed splice.
For each smooth compatible datum, E50's individual smooth
comparison with the E42 path makes that adjusted path smooth
through zero. The short comparison used to prove smoothness
may depend on the datum; it does not determine \(h\) or the
value threshold. The path is smooth at later times up to
and beyond \(h\), since \(h\le\mathcal T/4\).
Its \(q\) is therefore smooth on the compact closed slab
\([0,h]\). In particular each supported coordinate piece
has bounded spatial \(W^{4,8}\) norm there. The fixed annular
inverse fourth derivative bound of E57 gives
\(\|\Delta_j q_\alpha\|_8\le C2^{-4j}\|q_\alpha\|_{W^{4,8}}\)
for high blocks. This proves finite \(M_h,H_h\) and a strong
continuous \(\mathcal B^{1/2}\) trace by uniform block tails,
since both dyadic weights have exponents below four.
This is a finiteness justification for each datum before
absorption; their higher spatial norms are not used in a
threshold or in the final a priori constants.
[E50, regularity at zero, (8)–(10)][e50] ·
[E42, compatible passage][e42] · [E57, strong path class][e57].

On \(\mathcal E\) set \(\Delta_0=G^{ij}(D_0^2)_{ij}\), the
nonpositive rough Laplacian. At \(U=0\), \(Q=I\) and
\(\mathscr L_I(Z)=4Z\) on trace-free symmetric endomorphisms,
since \(\operatorname{tr}Z=0\). Therefore E125 (1) has
exactly the form
\[
 q_\tau=(4\Delta_0-12)q+
             P^{ij}(x,U)(D_0^2q)_{ij}+B^i(x,U,D_0U)D_{0,i}q
                         +C(x,U,D_0U)q ,                           \tag{3}
\]
where \(P^{ij}=g^{ij}\mathscr L_Q-4G^{ij}I_{\mathcal E}\).
The coefficient \(P\) is smooth in values and vanishes
at \(U=0\). The lower arrays \(B,C\) are E125's affine
arrays in \(D_0U\) with value parts vanishing there.
The \(-12q\) is the stated native mass; the small reaction
change is inside \(Cq\). The fixed operator has smooth
coefficients and leading rank-one symbol
\(4|\xi|_G^2 I_{\mathcal E}\) in the positive-symbol
convention of E57. Its sign as a diffusion generator is
nonpositive. Thus it is the exact fixed linear operator
to which the E57 estimate applies here.
[E125, (1) and native conventions][e125] ·
[E57, hypotheses and assessment][e57] · [E57 assessment][a57].

The pending third jet gives
\(\|D_0^m U(\tau)\|_\infty\le C_b v\,\tau^{-m/2}\)
for \(0\le m\le3\) on \(0<\tau\le h\).
If its derivative is denoted by \(D\) in E42, changing to
\(D_0\) adds fixed lower derivatives up to each order,
absorbed by the weights for \(\tau\le1\).
The bound at order zero keeps the whole path inside the
fixed small positive tube for sufficiently small \(v\).
When differentiating \(P(x,U)\), any term at derivative
order \(m\) either has a factor \(U\) from the vanishing
value at \(U=0\), or has at least one derivative of \(U\),
with summed derivative order at most \(m\). For \(B,C\)
the derivative-free parts have the same property. The
terms involving \(D_0U\) have summed derivative order
at most \(m+1\), including a factor at that increased
order when derivatives hit \(D_0U\). Smooth fixed
coefficient bounds and \(v,\tau\le1\) therefore give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \|D_0^m P(\tau)\|_\infty
       &\le C_3 v\,\tau^{-m/2}, &&0\le m\le3,\\
 \|D_0^m B(\tau)\|_\infty+\|D_0^m C(\tau)\|_\infty
       &\le C_3 v\,\tau^{-(m+1)/2}, &&0\le m\le2 .
 \end{aligned}
\]
These are precisely the E128 bounds on the fixed atlas and
bundles, after fixed frame changes as covered in that premise.
They do not use \(\ell\) or estimates for the unknown
critical defect norm. Consequently the pending product
estimate gives, for the remainder \(f\) in (3),
\[
                \|f\|_{F_h}\le C_{\rm prod}C_3 v\,E_h(q).
\]
E57 (1) can now be applied with the already verified smooth
solution and strong trace, giving
\[
 E_h(q)\le C_{\rm lin}\|q(0)\|_{\mathcal B^{1/2}}
            +C_{\rm lin}C_{\rm prod}C_3 v\,E_h(q).
\]
Fix \(v_*\) small enough for the E102 tube, original E42/E50
basin, pending E128 datum radius, and the coefficient bounds
above, and impose
\(C_{\rm lin}C_{\rm prod}C_3 v_*\le1/2\).
All constants in this choice are fixed before choosing a
datum. Absorption and (2) prove the first part of (1).

## Full strain derivative and the squared-strain factor

Set
\(\mathcal G_Q Z=\tfrac12(Q^{-1/2}ZQ^{-1/2})_0\),
a smooth internal endomorphism operator on the fixed
positive tube. Let \(X_i=D_{0,i}q+[\operatorname{ad}(a_i),q]\).
Because \(q\) is an endomorphism-valued zero-form, its
live first derivative has no base Levi–Civita correction,
and the exact strain is \(T_i=\mathcal G_Q X_i\).
In the following identity \(D_0\) differentiates all base
and internal slots and
\(\mathcal C^k{}_{ji}=(\Gamma(g)-\Gamma(G))^k{}_{ji}\)
is the difference of the two base connections:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 (\nabla^{\mathcal A}T)_{ji}
 &=\mathcal G_Q((D_0^2q)_{ji})\\
 &\quad+(D_{0,j}\mathcal G_Q)X_i\\
 &\quad+\mathcal G_Q\big(
         [\operatorname{ad}((D_0a)_{ji}),q]
           +[\operatorname{ad}(a_i),D_{0,j}q]\big)\\
 &\quad+[\operatorname{ad}(a_j),T_i]-\mathcal C^k{}_{ji}T_k .
 \end{aligned}                                                     \tag{4}
\]
This is the full ordered derivative with the extra base
slot \(j\). The last term is negative because \(T_i\)
is a covector in its base index. The preceding internal
commutator acts on the endomorphism slot. The reference
connections preserve the bracket, identity and trace
projection. This gives the indicated coefficient derivative
and no further derivatives of \(a\) or of the base connection.

The map \(g(x,U)\) is smooth in the curvature values,
equals \(G\) at \(U=0\), and is uniformly equivalent to
\(G\) throughout the small value tube. The exact expression
\[
 \mathcal C^k{}_{ji}
  =\tfrac12 g^{k\ell}
        ((D_0)_jg_{i\ell}+(D_0)_ig_{j\ell}-(D_0)_\ell g_{ji})
\]
uses the reference Levi–Civita derivative on metric
components. It is affine in \(D_0U\) with derivative-free
part vanishing at \(U=0\), and has size
\(C(|U|+|D_0U|)\). The same bound holds for \(D_0q\)
by value reconstruction and \(q(x,0)=0\).
In particular \(D_{0,j}\mathcal G_Q=(\mathcal G_Q)'[D_{0,j}q]\)
is a lower coefficient affine in the first pair jet.
All terms in (4) beyond the smooth leading
\(\mathcal G_QD_0^2q\) can thus be written as coefficients
smooth in the values and affine in \(D_0U\) multiplying
\(D_0q\) or \(q\). Their derivative-free parts vanish at
\(U=0\). For example the term that is quadratic in \(D_0q\)
uses one \(D_0q\) as a first-pair-jet coefficient, with
the other \(D_0q\) retained as the defect derivative.
This confirms the bound in the live tensor norm
\[
 |\nabla^{\mathcal A}T|_g
   \le C|D_0^2q|_0+
        C(|U|+|D_0U|)(|D_0q|_0+|q|_0).                            \tag{5}
\]
The uniform live/reference norm comparison uses only
the curvature values and is made after (4), so it does
not omit the moving Levi–Civita term.

Next, the exact strain gives
\(|T|_g\le C(|D_0q|_0+|a|_0|q|_0)
          \le C(|D_0q|_0+|q|_0)\).
Independently of any defect critical estimate, E119's
assessed weighted value and first-jet bounds give
\[
 \|q(\tau)\|_\infty\le Cv,\quad
 \|D_0q(\tau)\|_\infty\le Cv(1+\tau^{-1/2}),\quad
 \|T(\tau)\|_{\infty,g}\le Cv(1+\tau^{-1/2}) .
\]
Use this last bound for only one factor of the square.
Together with the exact strain bound it gives
\[
 \|T(\tau)\|_{\infty,g}^2
 \le Cv(1+\tau^{-1/2})
       \big(\|D_0q(\tau)\|_\infty+\|q(\tau)\|_\infty\big).          \tag{6}
\]
Thus neither the square term nor (5) requires a small
Lorentz norm for the initial strain. The factor \(v\)
in (6) comes from the independent background derivative
estimate. [E119, first estimate in (1)][e119] ·
[E119/E122 assessment][a119] · [E84, full live tensor][e84].

## Time-frequency split and exact original transfer

For a localized block write
\(d_{\alpha j}(\tau)=2^{j/2}\|\Delta_j q_\alpha(\tau)\|_8\).
E53 (2) and the E57 reconstruction give
\[
 \|D_0^k q(\tau)\|_\infty
    \le C\sum_{\alpha,j}2^{kj}d_{\alpha j}(\tau),
          \qquad k=0,1,2 .
\]
Indeed the local Bernstein gain to sup is \(2^{j/2}\);
derivatives of fixed reconstruction factors or frames
give lower powers, also bounded by \(2^{kj}\) for
\(j\ge0\). The low block is included at frequency one.
As a result
\[
 \int_0^h\|D_0^2q\|_\infty\,d\tau\le C H_h(q).
\]
For \(k=0,1\), the unweighted contribution is also
bounded by \(H_h\). For the additional weight
\(\tau^{-1/2}\), split at \(\tau_j=2^{-2j}\).
The integral of
\(\tau^{-1/2}2^{kj}d_{\alpha j}(\tau)\)
on \(0<\tau\le\min(h,\tau_j)\) is at most
\[
 2\cdot2^{(k-1)j}
              \sup_{0\le\tau\le h}d_{\alpha j}(\tau).
\]
On the remaining portion, \(\tau^{-1/2}\le2^j\), so its
cost is at most
\(2^{(k+1)j}\int_0^h d_{\alpha j}(\tau)\,d\tau\).
Since \(k\le1\), the first coefficient is bounded and
the second is at most \(2^{2j}\). The low block has
\(\tau_0=1\ge h\) and uses only the early bound.
Summing the nonnegative quantities proves
\[
 \int_0^h(1+\tau^{-1/2})
       \big(\|D_0 q(\tau)\|_\infty+\|q(\tau)\|_\infty\big)\,d\tau
                       \le C E_h(q).                              \tag{7}
\]
No integral of a pointwise \(1/\tau\) bound is used.
[E53, Bernstein (2)][e53] · [E57, localization and reconstruction][e57].

By (5), the independent first pair jet estimate, and (7),
the adjusted derivative-supremum integral is at most
\(C H_h(q)+Cv E_h(q)\). Equations (6) and (7) control
the square-supremum integral by \(Cv E_h(q)\).
The integrated spatial supremum of their sum is
bounded by the sum of those two integrals. This gives
the adjusted bound \(C(1+v)E_h(q)\le C'\ell\).

At each time in the recovered E50 head, the original
connection is a smooth orientation-preserving bundle
pullback of the adjusted one. Its live metric is the
corresponding base pullback. The full strain and full
covariant derivative pull back on every base slot and
by internal isometry. Thus the scalar pointwise
\(|\nabla^A T|+|T|^2\) composes with the base
diffeomorphism, and its spatial supremum agrees
exactly on the two slices. The time \(\tau\) is the same
native time on both paths. This uses equality at each
time, without differentiating the recovery map in time
or estimating its derivatives uniformly over data.
The adjusted bound therefore implies the second inequality
of (1) on the original head. This establishes only that
head bound, conditional on the stated inputs, and uses
the existing smooth basin and its unchanged splice.
[E50, (8)–(10)][e50] · [E84, (3)–(5)][e84].
