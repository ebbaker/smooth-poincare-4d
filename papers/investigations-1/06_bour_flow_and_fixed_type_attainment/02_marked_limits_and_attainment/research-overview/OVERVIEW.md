## Objects and target

Fix a smooth closed connected oriented homotopy four-sphere \(M\) with a smooth Riemannian positive-Yamabe class below the wall. Use the Hilbert–Schmidt norm on the Weyl operators on \(\Lambda^\pm\), the four-index norm on full curvature, \(T=4\pi^2\) and \(Y_{\rm rd}=8\sqrt6\pi\). Write

\[
E_\pm(g;U)=\int_U |W^\pm_g|_{\rm HS}^2\,dV_g,
\qquad I=I^+(M)=\inf_{Y([g])>0}E_+(g),
\qquad D(g)=\int_M|\operatorname{Ric}_{0,g}|^2\,dV_g.
\]

On a closed homotopy sphere \(E_+=E_-\) by the signature identity. Track both restricted energies on pieces and both budgets under exhaustion. The round type has the round class as a minimizer. The proposed nonstandard-type argument assumes \(T\le I<2T\), chooses \(\alpha_j\downarrow0\) and Yamabe initial metrics with \(E_+(g_{0,j})-I\le\alpha_j^2\), and works in a fixed upper band \(E_+\le2T-\delta\) for sufficiently small \(\alpha_j>0\). The desired endpoint is a smooth closed Bach-flat positive class on the original \(M\) with \(E_+=I\).
