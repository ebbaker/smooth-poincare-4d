## Order of work

1. Freeze one hypothetical nonstandard entered \(M\), one gap below \(2T\) and one sequence of positive unit-volume Yamabe starts within \(\alpha_j^2\) of \(I^+(M)\). Audit §8 of Bour and the specific results it uses. Reconcile the dissipation coefficient in §8 with the stated gradient equation and identify which uses require only finiteness. Record at each step the constants for fixed \(\alpha_j\) and the estimates that persist in \(j\). A failing import can be replaced by a theorem with checked hypotheses or a local proof of the precise missing estimate. [Flow and energy plan](../01_regularized_flow_and_energy/research-plan/PLAN.md)
2. On the first uncut flows test the full Ricci energy ceiling at every smooth time. Identify the common positive Yamabe and Sobolev information for outputs in their actual gauges. Independently derive both the signs and constants before applying an end formula. The end-group, transfer and regularity source comparisons can be prepared in parallel on explicitly assumed input.
3. Follow **both** fixed-parameter outputs. For a blow-up, establish the end hypotheses, exclude multiple Euclidean ends using the Ricci budget if available, and identify a one-ended compactification with the entered type. For a compact critical output, derive the constant-scalar equation and its uniform elliptic control as \(\alpha_j\downarrow0\). Pass to subsequences as necessary and cover both closed and rescaled limits of the critical sequence. Each compactification needs an independent test of its smooth type and positivity. [Marked limits plan](../02_marked_limits_and_attainment/research-plan/PLAN.md)
4. Compare the complete energy of the resulting positive metric on \(M\) with \(I\). Freeze a theorem or the first uncovered case and have a reader reconstruct the argument from its primary sources and branch receipts. The master can promote fixed-type attainment only once every alternative needed for a minimizing sequence yields the asserted conclusion. Strengthening this to arbitrary starting energies or a fixed-\(\alpha\) restart theorem is optional.
5. If a multi-ended case actually survives, decide whether a single restart can be specified with a positive same-type cap, controlled \(D,S,\mathcal F^\alpha\) before and after the Yamabe reset, and a strict decrement giving a finite count for that fixed \(\alpha\). If the obstruction lies at a one-ended marking or in the vanishing-parameter estimates, send that specific premise to the relevant owner. A failure at one step alone cannot settle attainment.
