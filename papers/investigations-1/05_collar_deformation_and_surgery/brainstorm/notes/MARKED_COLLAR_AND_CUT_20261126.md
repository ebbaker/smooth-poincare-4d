**From surgery to deformation.** Specify a smooth oriented source $(M,g)$, the embedded bicollar $\Phi:S^3\times(-w_L,w_R)\hookrightarrow M$, the angular marking and its inverse, normal convention, conformal gauge and actual available widths on the two sides. **From deformation to surgery.** Return a smooth path $g_s$ for $0\le s\le1$ agreeing with the source near the outer faces, a matched round product near the middle up to a specified conformal factor, and the marked maps on both faces. Give the peak joined chiral energy and signed increments on each full side in both chiralities, including every change of gauge, connector and end that changes the energy. Identify all metric changes outside the original collar. If using induced metrics throughout, supply the immersions realizing them. An intrinsic collar path can instead use the intrinsic positive-Yamabe argument.

**A finite numerical decision once the geometry is supplied.** Write $e_i=E_+(g_i;M)$, $d_i=e_i-I^+(M)$ for a member of a fixed-type positive minimizing sequence, and $m_L,m_R$ for the raw $E_+$ on its two complete sides. If a result supplies the opposite chirality, use it consistently on both sides and on the oriented closures. Each closed homotopy sphere has equal chiral totals. On an open side the two integrals can differ.

- Let $\gamma_j\ge0$ bound the surcharge of cap $j$ over $m_j$. For positive caps of combined energy at most $8\pi^2$, at least one is standard. An attained positive metric of energy at most $4\pi^2$ recognizes $M$ directly. A sequence of unattained bounds must use the master's infimum theorem at its stated threshold.
