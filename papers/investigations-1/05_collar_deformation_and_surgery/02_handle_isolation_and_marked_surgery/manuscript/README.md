# Smooth recognition from marked handle cuts

The draft follows the framed attaching tubes through the two mixed orders and the first three-handle reduction. It derives recognition tests on physical intermediate levels and the finite comparison on the separately supplied charged disk.

It proves cancellation at the penultimate level, recognition along successive zero-winding cuts and a relative slide on a nonlocal two-attachment family. It measures PSC bands both on the Mazur double and on a returned rank-two cut. [Joint assessment](../../reviews/NONLOCAL_BANDS_AND_PHYSICAL_TRANSPORT_20261126.md)
