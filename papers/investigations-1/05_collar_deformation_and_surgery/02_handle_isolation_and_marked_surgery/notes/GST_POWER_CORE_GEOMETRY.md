The retained zero-framed power component \(s=Q^*\) has word \(x^4y^{-3}\) in the belt basis, up to cyclic permutation, orientations and naming of the generators. The construction below concerns the knot \(s\) before filling. ## The model and its disk

Take solid tori \(V_x\) and \(V_y\) with annuli \(A_x\subset\partial V_x\) and \(A_y\subset\partial V_y\) whose cores have longitudinal degrees \(4\) and \(-3\), in the chosen orientations. Their other slope coefficients can be any integers that make the slopes primitive. Identify the annuli with \(A=(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)_\theta\times[-1,1]_t\), using coordinates in which the longitude projections on the respective tori read \(4\theta\) and \(-3\theta\). These coordinates are available on annular neighborhoods of linear slope curves. The standard middle-tunnel description of a torus-knot exterior fits this model, by cutting the genus-one Heegaard torus of \(S^3\) along an annular neighborhood of the torus knot. The complementary annulus glues the two solid tori along the matching slopes.

Split \(A\) into rectangles \(R\) and \(R'\) by cutting at two values of \(\theta\). Glue \(V_x\) and \(V_y\) by the three-dimensional \(1\)-handle \(R\times I_u\) using \(R\times\{0,1\}\). Call the resulting genus-two handlebody \(H\). Completing the annular gluing adds \(R'\times I_u\), a three-dimensional \(2\)-handle with disk coordinates \((\theta,u)\) and thickness coordinate \(t\). Its attaching curve \(c=\partial(R'\times I_u)|_{t=0}\) on \(\partial H\) follows the two slope cores outside \(R\) and crosses the two sides of the bridge at \(t=0\). Write \(D(H)=H\cup_{\partial H}(-H)\) for the oriented double. It is \(\#_2(S^1\times S^2)\), with \(c\) as an embedded knot on the seam. This gives the pair model \((D(H),c)\).

To relate this model to a doubled proper arc, close a proper arc \(a\subset B^3\) by a trivial arc in the complementary ball to obtain \(T_{3,4}\). Adding the exterior of that trivial arc along the twice-punctured sphere just lengthens an annular collar. Thus \(B^3\setminus\operatorname{int}\nu a\) is the torus-knot exterior. The product disk \(a\times I\subset B^3\times I\) bounds the doubled knot \(T_{3,4}\#\overline{T}_{3,4}\). Its exterior is the product of the arc exterior with \(I\). Taking products with \(I\) in the annular model above describes this disk exterior by a four-dimensional thickening of \(H\) and one two-handle attached along \(c\) on \(D(H)\), with the product framing. This identifies the candidate model with the ordinary doubled-arc disk construction before its single handle is attached.

Put four meridian disks \(P_0,\ldots,P_3\) in the winding-three torus and three meridian disks \(Q_0,Q_1,Q_2\) in the winding-four torus. Choose their longitudes to be \(-i/4\) and \(j/3\), respectively. At the annuli the disks on both sides then have precisely the same twelve intervals \(\{\theta=\ell/12\}\times[-1,1]_t\). The interval at \(\ell/12\) pairs \(P_{\ell\bmod4}\) with \(Q_{\ell\bmod3}\). Glue each matching pair by the strip \(\{\ell/12\}\times[-1,1]_t\times I_u\) in the annular product. Choose \(R\) with just \(\ell=0,\ldots,5\) in its interior. Its six strips join the seven disks along the path

\[
P_3-Q_0-P_0-Q_1-P_1-Q_2-P_2.
\]

The union of the seven disks with these six strips is a properly embedded disk \(D\subset H\) after rounding corners. In an oriented annular product the two families of meridian disks can be oriented coherently on each side, and the orientation of one family can be chosen so that every strip joins compatibly. Each of the six strips in \(R'\times I_u\) has two attaching intervals on \(\partial D\), one in each solid torus. Each interval crosses \(c\) once, at \(t=0\). The two attaching intervals of an oriented strip induce opposite intersection signs at their crossings. The sides of \(R\times I_u\) at the \(\theta\)-cuts are disjoint from all seven disks and all six retained strips. This accounts for exactly twelve points of \(\partial D\cap c\), with algebraic count zero.

Double \(D\) along its boundary and smooth at the seam to obtain \(S\subset D(H)\). The smoothing keeps transversality to \(c\), since its tangent plane has the normal of the seam and the tangent of \(\partial D\) there. A core loop in the winding-four solid torus on one side of the double meets \(S\) three times coherently. Thus \(S\) is nonseparating. Orient the basis using \(c=(4,-3)\) to read the intersection vector up to overall sign as \((3,4)\). If a pair diffeomorphism \(\Phi:(Y,s)\to(D(H),c)\) is supplied, \(\Phi^{-1}(S)\) attains the native lower bound, with closing carried by the original boundary marking.

In the model of winding degrees \(n+1\) and \(-n\), use \(n\) meridian disks on the first side and \(n+1\) on the second. The matching strips are indexed by \(\ell\bmod n(n+1)\) and join \(P_{\ell\bmod(n+1)}\) to \(Q_{\ell\bmod n}\). The first \(2n\) strips give the path \(P_n,Q_0,P_0,Q_1,P_1,\ldots,Q_{n-1},P_{n-1}\). The remaining \(n(n-1)\) strips give \(2n(n-1)\) transverse intersections in opposite-sign pairs. The disk and sphere assertions follow by the same argument.

## The diagram comparison still needed

A sufficient diagram certificate would separate the single retained power component into the boundary sum of two slope curves in the complements of the dotted unknots. The two joining strands must follow the sides of a rectangle joining the solid tori. For example, if the diagram simplifies to two isolated full-twist boxes between one strand of \(s\) and one of the respective dotted circles, joined by an untwisted two-strand bridge, each closed one-dot subtangle is a \(T(2,2m)\) link. Its two components are parallel \((1,m)\) torus curves. Relative to one dotted component, the slope of the other is \(\lambda_{\rm dot}+m\mu_{\rm dot}\). In the complementary solid torus this becomes a curve of longitudinal degree \(m\) and meridional coefficient of magnitude one. Zero surgery on each dotted circle gives the double of that solid torus, and the bridge describes the rectangular bridge of the annular model.

In the literal left panel the \(k\) and \(-k\) boxes lie on facing parts of the dotted ovals, while the power-component strands run in the intervening corridor.
