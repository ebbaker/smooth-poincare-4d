## Doubled tangles

A source with an actual free three-string tangle in \(B^3\) would suffice if a chosen strand \(a\) is unknotted and the exteriors of the two retained subtangles \((a,b)\) and \((a,c)\) are each irreducible with incompressible **whole boundary**. For a subset \(J\) of the strands, let \(E_J\) denote the drilled ball. Restore each drilled product as \(D^2\times I_s\) attached on \(\partial D^2\times I_s\); taking the product with a second interval \(I_t\) makes this a four-dimensional reverse 2-handle with attaching region \(\partial D^2\times I_s\times I_t\). Thus in the forward direction the boundary operation is integral surgery on the doubled strand with its doubled product framing, and the level at \(J\) is the double of \(E_J\). After \(a\) it is \(Y_1\) and after all three it is \(Y_3\). The upper body is the specific \(E_{abc}\times I_t\), with boundary identified with the doubled exterior by the product. The upper tube for each reverse handle is the actual \(\partial D^2\times I_s\times I_t\); its core runs around the seam annulus of the drilled strand. A proper disk system of the genus-three \(E_{abc}\) supplies the doubled belt spheres, and the product boundary identification has the inverse that forgets the doubling notation. For either two-strand exterior the irreducible-boundary gluing argument proves its double irreducible: remove sphere intersections with the seam by incompressibility and use irreducibility on the two halves. The required boundary includes the lateral strand annuli as well as the punctured outer sphere.
