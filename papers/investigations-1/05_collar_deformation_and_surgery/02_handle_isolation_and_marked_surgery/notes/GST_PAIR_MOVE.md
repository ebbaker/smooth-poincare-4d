## A Figure 6 band with both knots nontrivial

Choose a thin zero-framed parallel $Q^+$ in the complement of $U\cup Q$. After forgetting the original $Q$, the link $C=U\cup Q^+$ is isotopic to $U\cup Q$: the product annulus of the parallel supplies an isotopy of the moving component away from $U$. Put $X=S^3\setminus\operatorname{int}\nu C$. The full zero surgery on $C$ is $Y_2$ by Figure 5. Filling the tori gives a surjection
\[
  \pi_1 X\twoheadrightarrow F_2=\pi_1(Y_2).
\]
It induces an isomorphism on integral first homology, since the surgery kills the two zero longitudes of the algebraically split link. In particular the two based meridians map to an abelianization basis of $F_2$.

Use the character $\chi:F_2\to\mathbb Z/2$ taking both meridians to $1$. It is nonzero. From any free basis choose one with both basis elements odd for $\chi$: if exactly one is odd, multiply the even one by it. Sending these two free generators to $(12)$ and $(23)$ gives a surjection $\rho:\pi_1 X\twoheadrightarrow\mathfrak S_3$ whose sign character is $\chi$ after filling. Thus the two meridians have transposition images.

Fix mouths on $U$ and on $Q^+$, with the local band germs of Figure 6. Choose paths from a basepoint to the exterior points of the mouths and a tentative connecting arc. If $a\in\pi_1 X$ is the loop through this arc closed by the two basing paths, the band relation will identify $m_U$ with $a m_{Q^+} a^{-1}$, up to meridian orientation. Choose $s\in\mathfrak S_3$ with
\[
  \rho(m_U)=s\,\rho(m_{Q^+})\,s^{-1}.
\]
For definiteness $s$ may be the first such permutation in any fixed enumeration. Prepend a based loop of image $s\rho(a)^{-1}$ to the connecting path using its $U$ basing path. The loop can be chosen as a lift of a word in the two chosen free generators, then perturbed off the additional $Q$ by general position. Fix the mouth germs and represent the resulting relative homotopy class by a smooth embedded arc in the complement of all three cores. This is possible in dimension three by general position for an interval, and the arc can meet only the prescribed attaching arcs after the tubes are shrunk. Thicken it to an oriented saddle band in that complement, extending the local germs and using the framed parallel $Q^+$. The core path, character and conjugating condition specify a class of choices for $\beta$, with the same conclusion for every such choice. The framing on $V$ is the framed-slide framing from Figure 6.

Let $Z$ be the exterior in $S^3\times[0,1]$ of the pair-of-pants movie of this band, from $C$ to $V$, forgetting the stationary $Q$. The saddle contributes one index-two handle to the exterior from the $C$ end. Its attaching loop is the band sum of the two meridians. With the chosen basings this is $m_U a m_{Q^+}^{-1}a^{-1}$ for an oriented saddle, up to the common orientation convention. Thus $\rho$ factors onto $\mathfrak S_3$ through $\pi_1 Z$. Reversing the movie still has just a saddle, so the exterior also has a single index-two handle from the $V$ end. The map $\pi_1(S^3\setminus\nu V)\to\pi_1 Z$ is surjective. It follows that the knot group of $V$ surjects onto $\mathfrak S_3$ and is nonabelian. In particular $V$ is nontrivial. The other component $Q$ is the original nontrivial square knot, the connected sum of the two mirror trefoils. ## Individual prefixes

For a zero-framed knot $K\subset S^3$, the trace on $D^4$ with a single 2-handle is simply connected with zero intersection form and $H_2=\mathbb Z$. In particular $H_2(S^3_0(K))\to H_2(D^4\cup h_K^2)$ is an integral isomorphism. The [previous penultimate-prefix check](../../reviews/LAST_HANDLE_READER.md#actual-prefix-hypotheses-and-repeated-reductions) therefore applies. A PSC certificate gives one $S^1\times S^2$ factor and a residual sum of signed Poincaré spheres. Compressing along the product factor kills the trace generator and gives a smooth contractible filling of the residual sum. The obstruction to such a filling leaves $S^3_0(K)\cong Y_1$, and Gabai's Property R then gives $K$ the unknot. Conversely zero surgery on the unknot gives $Y_1$ with its product PSC witness. ## Trace maps and the closed one-sided presentation

Write $T_6,T_5,T_0$ for the cobordisms from $S^3$ of the selected Figure 6 pair, the left panel of Figure 5, and the split unlink, respectively. A slide of a moving tube $e_a$ over an anchor $e_b$ has the following trace comparison. Order $b$ first and use the actual collar of its outgoing level. The longitude $b^+$ bounds a disk in the outgoing filling torus joined to the parallel by an exterior annulus. Compress the band detour across that disk, using parallel disks to carry the frame. This gives a tube isotopy of the two versions of $a$ in the $b$-surgered level. Extend the isotopy to an ambient $J_t$ there, with the endpoint normal coordinates chosen by the framed slide. From the old trace to the new one, use identity through the $b$ handle, $(y,t)\mapsto (J_{\eta(t)}y,t)$ along the last collar, and identity on the last handle coordinates. Here $\eta$ goes from zero to one and is constant near the ends. The inverse uses $J_{\eta(t)}^{-1}$ and the reversed coordinate identifications. This procedure gives a diffeomorphism $F_6:T_6\to T_5$ using the inverse of the Figure 6 slide, then $F_5:T_5\to T_0$ using Figure 5, including the final isotopy of its right panel. The final isotopy and inverse carry both frames. Every trace map is identity at the incoming end. The correspondence on reversed handles can also be read from GST Lemma 2.1, which exchanges the moving and anchor roles on dual knots. ## Relative block in a parametrized ball

Fix $r\ge 0$ and an oriented parametrized ball $B\subset Y_r$. Choose the puncture in the $S^3$ diagram away from the active cores, band cores, and the core trajectories in the splitting isotopy. These form a compact set of dimension at most two. Take a small puncture ball there, extend the core isotopy by a cutoff away from it, and use sufficiently narrow ribbons and framed tubes. Use the resulting ball to place the selected Figure 6 link, its framed parallels and the two bands in $\operatorname{int}B$. The same supports and outgoing face disks are available in the local trace on $B$. Apply the comparison there and extend by identity on $(Y_r\setminus\operatorname{int}B)\times[0,1]$. Its outgoing marking gives $Y_{r+2}$ with the two new product summands distinguished. The inverse uses the same product collar outside $B$ and the inverses of the two local trace maps.

External incoming tubes disjoint from $B$ are fixed. For finitely many other disjoint tubes with movable cores, a small ambient perturbation away from the original active tubes makes the spectator cores disjoint from the intermediate active cores and the finitely many chosen band core paths. Narrow the ribbons and tubes so they miss each other. In a slide ordered with its anchor first, the compression runs through the anchor's new solid torus and a neighborhood of the narrow band, a parallel taken sufficiently close to the anchor, and its annulus. Spectator tubes lie in the surviving exterior, and the slide isotopy can be taken disjoint from them in this region. During the splitting isotopy carry the spectator coordinates with the ambient isotopy. If $P$ is the resulting coordinate change on a survivor patch, the transported tube is $P e_j$, with inverse $e_j^{-1}P^{-1}$ on its image. Preparatory spectator isotopies are part of the marking, with their reversals in the inverse comparison. These choices preserve each framing. For a tube specified on the outgoing level, transport instead by $\phi_B e_j$, using the outgoing trace map $\phi_B$, with inverse $e_j^{-1}\phi_B^{-1}$.

When the outgoing level closes directly by the actual rank-$(r+2)$ upper body, the marked splitting sends the two local dual cores to the last two product generators and misses the other $r$ belt spheres. The upper boundary re-marking extends as in the rank-two case. The two cancellations replace that upper body and the two local attachments by a rank-$r$ body with the closing induced by the product cancellation, with its inverse in reverse order. If further handles intervene above the block, the local conclusion is the relative trace equivalence with their attaching data transported. A net cancellation in the complete presentation then requires the identified geometric belt-sphere intersections with those further data in place.
