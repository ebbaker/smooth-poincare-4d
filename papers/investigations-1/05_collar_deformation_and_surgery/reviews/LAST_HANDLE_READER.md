The check covers smooth oriented manifolds, one integral framed surgery, and its application to a specified prefix of a marked handle presentation.

**Result.** For every integer $r\ge0$, if a single integral surgery on a knot $K\subset Y_r=\#_r(S^1\times S^2)$ gives $Y_{r+1}$, then $K$ bounds a smooth embedded disk and the given framing is its zero framing. The outgoing surgery core is a product generator in a marked new summand. When the outgoing level is closed by the actual rank-$(r+1)$ upper body, the last 2-handle cancels one upper 3-handle on the original closed smooth type. The PSC prefix criterion works for both the specified one-lower presentation and the one-sided presentation. The latter continues to require a further prefix test for another cancellation in general.

## Surgery, trace group and zero slope

Give the trace $X$ its single forward 2-handle and its single reversed 2-handle along the actual dual knot $K^*$. The forward and reverse presentations of $G=\pi_1(X)$ are respectively

$$
 F_r/N\quad\text{and}\quad F_{r+1}/\langle\!\langle K^*\rangle\!\rangle,
 \qquad N=\langle\!\langle K\rangle\!\rangle.
$$

The reverse presentation yields $\operatorname{rank}H_1(G)\ge r$. In the forward presentation any nonzero abelianized class of $K$ would give rank $r-1$. Thus $K$ abelianizes to zero and $H_1(G;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z^r$. It follows that the dual relator's abelianization is nonzero and primitive, since its quotient is torsion free of rank $r$. In the reverse presentation complex the boundary of the only 2-cell is that abelianized vector. The map $\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z^{r+1}$ is injective. Attaching cells of dimension at least three to form a $K(G,1)$ preserves the vanishing of $H_2$, so $H_2(G;\mathbb Z)=0$. This computes the homology of the group separately from that of the physical surgery cobordism.

Since $N\subset[F_r,F_r]$, Hopf's formula gives $N/[F_r,N]=0$. Set $\gamma_1F_r=F_r$ and $\gamma_{j+1}F_r=[F_r,\gamma_jF_r]$. The equality $N=[F_r,N]$ puts $N$ in every $\gamma_jF_r$ by induction. The residual nilpotence of a free group then gives $N=1$. This works for $r=0$ as well. One can see the residual nilpotence from the Magnus embedding $x_j\mapsto 1+X_j$ in units of noncommutative integral formal power series. The degree filtration carries each $\gamma_j$ to units congruent to 1 modulo degree $j$, and the intersection of those filtrations is trivial. [Hopf's formula, Loeh Theorem 1.5.1](https://loeh.app.ur.de/teaching/grouphom_ss19/lecture_notes.pdf) · [Magnus, Math. Ann. 111 (1935), 259–280](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01472217)

For the compact exterior $E=Y_r\setminus\operatorname{int}\nu K$, the pair sequence by excision has $H_2(Y_r)\to H_2(Y_r,E)\cong\mathbb Z$ given by intersection with $K$. It is zero because $[K]=0$. The subsequent relative group $H_1(Y_r,E)$ vanishes. Thus the meridian $\mu$ generates an injected direct summand $\mathbb Z$ of $H_1(E)\cong\mathbb Z^{r+1}$, with quotient $H_1(Y_r)$. The Seifert longitude $\lambda$ vanishes in $H_1(E)$ because the truncated oriented Seifert surface bounds it. Integral framing is a slope $\lambda+n\mu$ up to overall sign. Homology of the Dehn filling is the quotient by that slope, so its rank is $r+1$ only at $n=0$. This uses the actual longitude of the null-homologous knot. Any two Seifert longitudes agree, since each is a parallel of the knot and their difference is an integer multiple of the injected meridian.

## Prime reduction and return of the disk

An embedded nonseparating two-sphere in the exterior cuts off an $S^1\times S^2$ factor by cutting along that sphere and capping the two new boundaries by actual three-balls. Both the meridional and zero filling are along the unchanged torus, so they commute with every such removal. In particular the capped exterior stays connected and the sphere is nonseparating in each current filling. This process terminates after at most $r$ steps, since each removal reduces the first Betti number of the meridional filling by one. It removes all $S^1\times S^2$ factors of the exterior. Kneser–Milnor uniqueness on the closed fillings gives $Y_s$ and $Y_{s+1}$ for the final two fillings, where $s=r$ minus the number removed. The first filling contains the knot, with its neighborhood unchanged. Its fundamental group is a free factor of $\pi_1(Y_r)$ via the connected-sum description and the knot class comes from that free factor. A retraction onto it proves the residual knot null-homotopic. Its zero slope is still the same longitude. For instance the residual knot is null-homologous, and the inclusion of its exterior as a free summand on first homology preserves the slope with zero class. [Milnor, *A Unique Decomposition Theorem for 3-Manifolds*, Amer. J. Math. 84 (1962), 1–7](https://doi.org/10.2307/2372800)

Ni's published Theorem 1.1 applies to a nontrivial null-homotopic knot in a closed connected oriented three-manifold whose knot complement has no $S^1\times S^2$ summand. It excludes any such summand in its zero surgery. Apply the contrapositive in $Y_s$, since its zero surgery is $Y_{s+1}$. The punctured residual manifold still needs a disk. Let $D$ be the smooth spanning disk in the closed residual manifold. The finitely many parametrized balls used for sphere capping are disjoint from a fixed tubular neighborhood of the knot. In the residual manifold, shrink these balls by isotopy and move them into disjoint small coordinate balls away from $D$, using paths in the complement of the knot neighborhood and the other balls. The complement remains connected, and isotopy extension can fix the knot neighborhood throughout. Pull back $D$ by the resulting ambient diffeomorphism. The pulled-back disk misses all the original cap balls and survives the reverse gluings. A regular neighborhood of that spanning disk puts the original knot in a ball as an unknot. Its disk framing agrees with the longitude already checked. [Yi Ni, *Null-homotopic knots have Property R*, Math. Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 175 (2023), 217–223, Theorem 1.1, p. 217](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~yini/Published/NullPropR.pdf#page=1)

The literature check found the published rank-one special case inside the proof of Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson Proposition 3.2, pp. 2313–2314. The proof puts the last knot into a ball as an unknot in the surgered $S^1\times S^2$ before converting its isotopy into link slides in $S^3$. Gabai's Property R covers $r=0$. Hom–Lidman Corollary 1.2 covers null-homotopic knots in prime rational homology spheres. For $s>0$ the residual case above uses Ni's theorem, with the trace-group reduction supplying null-homotopy and the subtraction of spectator factors supplying the exterior premise. The bounded search did not find a separate direct all-rank statement of the exact input/output claim, and establishes no priority for the corollary. [Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson, *Fibered knots and potential counterexamples to the Property 2R and Slice-Ribbon Conjectures*, Geometry & Topology 14 (2010), Proposition 3.2 and Theorem 1.1](https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-4/gt-v14-n4-p13-s.pdf#page=9) · [Hom–Lidman, *Dehn surgery and nonseparating two-spheres*, Open Book Ser. 5 (2022), Corollary 1.2](https://msp.org/obs/2022/5-1/obs-v5-n1-p07-p.pdf#page=2)

## Upper marking and cancellation

The zero-framed local model takes the outgoing $D^2\times S^1$ to the solid torus in the new $S^2\times S^1$ summand, preserving its core. To see the intersection directly, glue the meridian disk of this outgoing solid torus to a Seifert disk in the original local unknot exterior. The capped sphere intersects the core once, transversely, and is the product sphere of this summand in the two-solid-torus model. A choice of summand marking $\lambda:Y_{r+1}\to\partial Q_0$ to the standard upper body sends this sphere to one belt sphere and the core away from the other belt spheres. The marking sends the actual tube with its framing using the framed local model. Equip $\partial Q_0$ with the side convention of the actual top. If $\gamma:Y_{r+1}\to\partial Q$ is the supplied upper identification, then $\lambda\gamma^{-1}$ extends to a diffeomorphism $H:Q\to Q_0$ by Laudenbach–Poénaru. This acts only on the upper body. For the outgoing face $o:D^2\times S^1\to Y_{r+1}$, the reversed attaching tube is $\gamma o\tau$, with $\tau:S^1\times D^2\to D^2\times S^1$ interchanging the factors. Its inverse on its image is $\tau^{-1}o^{-1}\gamma^{-1}$. The body change and its inverse transport the entire tube with those same handle coordinates.

Read from the top. The reversed 2-handle attaches to the standard upper body with a single transverse intersection with one belt sphere. The Morse cancellation removes this 1/2 pair in the reversed decomposition. It gives an identification $\Psi$ from the actual upper piece (including the last middle handle) to a standard rank-$r$ 1-handlebody in that reading direction. If $\delta$ is the original identification from the lower prefix boundary to the incoming boundary of that upper piece, the new closing is $(\partial\Psi)\delta$, with inverse $\delta^{-1}(\partial\Psi)^{-1}$. Gluing the identity on the original prefix to $\Psi$ gives a diffeomorphism of the original whole closed assembly to the returned one. Thus the decrease is one forward 2-handle and one forward 3-handle. This uses the geometric intersection condition in Milnor's first cancellation theorem. [Laudenbach–Poénaru, *A note on 4-dimensional handlebodies*, proof of Theorem A, pp. 341–342](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/bsmf.1741.pdf#page=6) · [Milnor, *Lectures on the h-Cobordism Theorem*, Theorem 5.4, p. 48](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/surgery/hcobord.pdf#page=53)

## Actual prefix hypotheses and repeated reductions

Let $W$ be the actual penultimate prefix in a reduced handle presentation of an oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere. With one lower 1-handle and $m$ middle handles, normalize the algebraic winding row by framed slides and assume the penultimate prefix contains its winding-one component. Then $W$ is simply connected and $H_2(W)=\mathbb Z^{m-2}$ by the 0/1/2 handle chain complex. In the one-sided case with no lower 1-handle, $W$ is automatically simply connected by its 0/2 description and $H_2(W)=\mathbb Z^{m-1}$. In both cases the intersection form of $W$ vanishes because it is computed by interior cycles also intersecting in the enclosing $M$ with $H_2(M)=0$. Thus the integral pair sequence, Poincaré–Lefschetz duality and $H_1(W)=0$ give an isomorphism $H_2(\partial W)\to H_2(W)$ and $H_1(\partial W)\cong\mathbb Z^r$ for $r=m-2$ or $r=m-1$, respectively.

If the actual boundary $\partial W$ receives a nonzero-degree map from a closed oriented three-manifold with PSC, the classification gives a sum of exactly $r$ product factors and spherical space forms. The torsion-free first homology makes the remaining spherical sum an integral homology sphere, so it is a sum of signed Poincaré spheres or $S^3$. Pull back the factor spheres and attach auxiliary 3-handles to $W$ along their products with normal intervals. They kill an integral basis of $H_2(W)$, and the pair sequence gives no new homology. The result is simply connected and acyclic, hence contractible. Its boundary is precisely the residual sum because those compressions remove the product factors. Proposition 4.2 leaves the empty Poincaré sum. Therefore $\partial W\cong Y_r$. The auxiliary compression serves to identify this boundary; the closed assembly uses $W$ itself. [Chodosh–Maximo–Mukherjee, arXiv:2407.05574v1, Propositions 2.2 and 4.2, Corollary 2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574v1)

With one lower 1-handle the original upperbody has rank $m-1$ by Euler characteristic. The specified PSC prefix yields the transition $Y_{m-2}\to Y_{m-1}$, and the cancellation returns one lower 1-handle and $m-1$ middle handles. With no lower 1-handle the original upper body has rank $m$. The specified PSC prefix gives $Y_{m-1}\to Y_m$ and returns a one-sided presentation with $m-1$ middle handles. Each earlier prefix stays in the unchanged lower part. As long as further actual successive prefix boundaries meet the stated simple-connectivity and PSC hypotheses, the argument can be reapplied using the new upper closing. In particular a certified chain can reduce to the known base counts. An isolated boundary identification at an earlier prefix leaves any intervening collective framed surgery to be analyzed. A single penultimate certificate gives a one-handle reduction and supplies neither automatic certificates of further prefixes nor a general link-slide statement.
