## Result on the actual cut

Let $M$ be a smooth oriented homotopy four-sphere with one handle in each of indices $0,1,3,4$ and two 2-handles. Normalize the winding row by marked slides to $(1,0)$. Let $Y$ be the actual intermediate boundary with the winding-zero component $K_0$ attached first. If a smooth closed oriented 3-manifold with a Riemannian metric of positive scalar curvature admits a map of nonzero degree to $Y$, then $Y\cong \#_2(S^1\times S^2)$ and $M\cong S^4$. The condition includes a positive-scalar-curvature metric on $Y$ itself. The normalized winding-one order and passage to the dual height are distinct choices from this reversal of the two attachments. ### Boundary and auxiliary filling

Write $U$ for the dotted unknot and order the full ordinary surgery matrix as $U,K_1,K_0$. The matrix is $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1&0\\1&d&b\\0&b&c\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Its cokernel is $H_1(S^1\times S^2;\mathbb Z)$ from the actual upper end. Its determinant is $-c$, hence $c=0$. Replacing the dot by zero in the two-component prefix $U_0\cup K_0$ gives the same marked boundary $Y$. The ordinary surgery trace $V$ of this link has one 0-handle and two 2-handles, so $\pi_1(V)=1$ and its only nonzero reduced homology is $H_2(V;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z^2$. The linking matrix for this prefix is zero. It gives the zero intersection form on $H_2(V;\mathbb Z)$ and $H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z^2$. The physical lower piece still has its dotted 1-handle. [Pinned marked-height paper, mixed boundaries](../../04_hypersurface_immersions/02_two_handle_recognition/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_morse_heights.tex)

CMM Proposition 2.2 gives an oriented connected-sum decomposition of $Y$ into spherical space forms and $S^1\times S^2$ factors. If the source is disconnected, one component has nonzero degree. The integral first homologies of these summands add directly, so there are exactly two $S^1\times S^2$ factors and each spherical summand has trivial $H_1$. For a spherical summand this is the vanishing of the finite fundamental group's abelianization and, by Poincaré duality, gives integral homology sphere homology. Kervaire's Theorem 2 identifies the trivial group and the binary icosahedral group as the only possibilities. For the diffeomorphism types, the spherical residual $\Sigma$ is itself a positive-scalar-curvature integral homology sphere by the spherical metrics and connected-sum preservation. CMM Corollary 2.3 now gives $\Sigma=S^3$ or a connected sum of signed Poincaré spheres. This uses the corollary on $\Sigma$. [CMM, arXiv:2407.05574v1, Proposition 2.2 and Corollary 2.3, pp. 4–5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574v1#page=4) · [Kervaire, Theorem 2, p. 67](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/kervsmoo.pdf)

Pull back the two disjoint factor spheres $\{p_i\}\times S^2$ from this decomposition, with the connected-sum balls disjoint from the spheres. They form an integral basis of $H_2(Y;\mathbb Z)$. In the pair sequence, the term $H_3(V,Y;\mathbb Z)\cong H^1(V;\mathbb Z)$ vanishes. Under Poincaré–Lefschetz duality and the universal coefficient isomorphism $H_2(V,Y;\mathbb Z)\cong \operatorname{Hom}(H_2(V;\mathbb Z),\mathbb Z)$, the map from $H_2(V;\mathbb Z)$ is the zero intersection form. Exactness therefore makes $H_2(Y;\mathbb Z)\to H_2(V;\mathbb Z)$ an integral isomorphism. The two spheres supply a basis simultaneously, which is stronger than individual primitivity.

Attach two smooth 3-handles along these spheres in $\partial V$ with product neighborhoods. Surgery replaces each $S^2\times(-1,1)$ by two balls. In the chosen connected-sum decomposition each compression removes an $S^1\times S^2$ factor, giving the connected boundary $\Sigma$. The new filling $V'$ has the same fundamental group as $V$. The connecting map $H_3(V',V;\mathbb Z)\to H_2(V;\mathbb Z)$ is an isomorphism because its two columns are the two sphere classes. The pair sequence gives acyclicity and the simply connected CW type gives contractibility by Hurewicz and Whitehead. CMM Proposition 4.2 applies directly to the compact smooth $V'$. It forces $\Sigma\cong S^3$ and gives a homeomorphism type for $V'$. The proof uses the boundary conclusion and requires no complete metric on the interior of $V'$. [CMM, Proposition 4.2, p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574v1#page=10)

### Actual gluing and residuals

The identification of $Y$ supplies the cut hypothesis of `thm:mixed` on the original trace. In its $Y_2$ case both actual halves are $\left(S^1\times D^3\right)\natural\left(S^2\times D^2\right)$ by the isotopy in the proof of Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson Proposition 3.2. The isotopies carry the framed tubes. With the actual gluing written in the resulting boundary coordinates, the theorem replaces one interior sphere by a circle and uses the boundary extension over the two-1-handlebody to identify the surgered manifold with $S^1\times S^3$. Simple connectivity of $M$ forces the inverse surgery circle to generate $\pi_1(S^1\times S^3)$. Its homotopy to the product circle gives an isotopy by parametric general position in dimension four, and boundary extension over the one-1-handlebody handles the actual inverse surgery framing. This gives the smooth recognition of $M$. [Pinned `thm:mixed`](../../04_hypersurface_immersions/02_two_handle_recognition/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_morse_heights.tex) · [Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson, Proposition 3.2, pp. 2313–2314](https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-4/gt-v14-n4-p13-s.pdf#page=9) · [Laudenbach–Poénaru, Theorem A and proof, pp. 337, 341–342](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/bsmf.1741.pdf)

With one lower 1-handle, one minimum and maximum, and any number $n_2\ge2$ of middle attachments, $n_3=n_2-1$. After normalizing to $(1,0,\ldots,0)$, the full ordinary matrix has top block $A=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, off-block rows $(0,b)$, and winding-zero block $C$. The full boundary has first homology of rank $n_3$, so the matrix has rank two. The bottom-right entry of $A^{-1}$ vanishes, making the Schur complement equal to $C$ and forcing $C=0$. Thus the same compression proves $Y=Y_2$ when a selected winding-zero handle goes first and its actual cut receives the same positive-scalar-curvature degree input. Proposition 3.2 then isolates that handle as a zero-framed unknot in a ball in the lower level. Ambient isotopy carries every remaining framed tube and induces the outgoing boundary map for transporting subsequent attachments. The resulting isolation supplies no geometric 2/3 cancellation with arbitrary subsequent handles. Those require additional information about the entire upper trace. [Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson, proof of Proposition 3.2](https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-4/gt-v14-n4-p13-s.pdf#page=9)

The implication for the mixed trace is conditional on the degree input at this actual cut. Existence of an abstract positive-scalar-curvature source with nonzero degree is equivalent here to $Y\cong Y_2$, since $Y_2$ itself admits positive scalar curvature. This check supplies no such physical metric or map and no universal order-selection or strict handle-count reduction with arbitrary residuals.
