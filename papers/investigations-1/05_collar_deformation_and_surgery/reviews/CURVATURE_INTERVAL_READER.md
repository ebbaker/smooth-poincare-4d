## The marked profile and its discrepancy

$$
\rho'^2+\cosh^2(\sqrt\lambda\rho)Z'^2
=\frac{r'^2+r^2(u^2+\lambda)}{1+\lambda r^2}=1.
$$

Thus the meridian is embedded, and in regular cylindrical coordinates $Z=\mathcal Z(y/B_f)$ with $\mathcal Z'>0$ gives the marked filling map on the whole planar ellipsoid. Its angular coordinate is identical to the physical one. All these maps depend smoothly on $\lambda$ in compact positive intervals. The outward radial component of the unit normal is $\sqrt{1-\rho'^2}$. Applying it to $\log r$ gives the repeated curvature $U_\lambda=\sqrt{u^2+\lambda}$.
