Let $X\cong Y\times[-1,1]$ be the actual smooth compact oriented four-dimensional band about either normalized mixed cut. Let $g$ be the smooth band metric with $R_g\ge\rho>0$, $d>0$ its intrinsic width, and $a,b$ the infima of $\operatorname{div}_{\partial X}\nu_{\rm out}$ on the two boundary components in order. If $Y$ fails the topological recognition condition for its order, the two prior checks imply that $Y$ admits no positive-scalar-curvature metric. Each closed separating hypersurface in the product has an oriented component whose projection to $Y$ has nonzero degree. Thus the degree argument rules out a separating hypersurface admitting positive scalar curvature and supplies the hypothesis of Räde's §2.A comparison. [Winding-one check](POSITIVE_FACE_READER.md) · [Winding-zero check](REVERSE_ORDER_READER.md) · [Räde, arXiv:2104.10120v4, 23 May 2022, §2.A and Lemma 6.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.10120v4)

Räde's Remark 1.4 specifies the second fundamental form for the inner normal with the sign making a Euclidean sphere positive. In these conventions its trace is the outward tangential divergence. Indeed, on $dt^2+\varphi^2h$ the lower outward normal is $-\partial_t$, giving mean $-3\varphi'/\varphi$, while the upper one is $+\partial_t$, giving $+3\varphi'/\varphi$. For $\varphi(t)=\cos(2t)^{1/2}$, the model boundary means are $+3\tan(2\ell_-)$ and $-3\tan(2\ell_+)$. [Räde, Remark 1.4, Theorem 2.7 and §3.A, equation (2)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.10120v4)

Set $c=\sqrt{\rho/12}$. For $c^2g$ the scalar curvature is at least $12$, the width is $cd$, and the end infima are $a/c,b/c$. Corollary 2.9 gives $a+b<0$. Write $\alpha=\arctan(a/(3c))$ and $\beta=\arctan(b/(3c))$, using the principal branch. Oddness and strict monotonicity give $\alpha+\beta<0$, so $\ell_-=\alpha/2$ and $\ell_+=-\beta/2$ lie in the required order strictly inside $(-\pi/4,\pi/4)$. They saturate the boundary comparisons and give the shortest allowed model interval, since an allowed lower endpoint satisfies $\ell_-\le\alpha/2$ and an allowed upper endpoint satisfies $\ell_+\ge-\beta/2$. Theorem 2.7 now gives $cd\le-(\alpha+\beta)/2$, or

$$
\arctan\!\left(\frac{2a}{\sqrt{3\rho}}\right)
+\arctan\!\left(\frac{2b}{\sqrt{3\rho}}\right)
+\sqrt{\frac{\rho}{3}}\,d\ \le 0 .
$$

A strictly positive left side therefore certifies recognition for the supplied mixed band. Equality in this finite-end estimate remains compatible with the comparison. The general equality rigidity expected in Remark 2.4 lies outside Theorem 2.7. With finite $a,b$ the displayed estimate also gives $\sqrt{\rho/3}\,d<\pi$, in agreement with Corollary 2.25. Hence equality at $\rho d^2=3\pi^2$ already certifies recognition by the separate width-only condition. [Räde, Theorem 2.7, Remark 2.4 and Corollaries 2.9 and 2.25](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.10120v4)

For a normalization check in physical units, let $\kappa=\sqrt{\rho/3}$, take a flat $h_0$ on $T^3$, and put $g=dt^2+\varphi(t)^2h_0$ with $\varphi(t)=\cos(\kappa t)^{1/2}$ on a compact subinterval $[t_-,t_+]\subset(-\pi/(2\kappa),\pi/(2\kappa))$. Writing $p=\varphi'/\varphi=-\tfrac{\kappa}{2}\tan(\kappa t)$, the scalar curvature is $-6p'-12p^2=3\kappa^2=\rho$. The means are $a=+\tfrac{3\kappa}{2}\tan(\kappa t_-)$ and $b=-\tfrac{3\kappa}{2}\tan(\kappa t_+)$. The width is $t_+-t_-$, since every crossing curve has length at least its total change in $t$ and a vertical segment realizes it. The two arctangents are $\kappa t_-$ and $-\kappa t_+$, so the finite-end estimate is an equality on each strip. The unrestricted endpoint is approached as the strips exhaust the interval of positive warping. The model verifies sharpness for these bands. Selection of a physical mixed band and equality rigidity for its specific topology remain outside this check. [Räde, Remark 1.8 and §3.A, equations (1)–(3)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.10120v4)
