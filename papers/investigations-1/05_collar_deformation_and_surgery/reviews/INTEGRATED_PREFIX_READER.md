## Penultimate level at arbitrary lower and upper rank

Take the reduced handle presentation on a closed oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere. With \(p\) lower 1-handles, \(q\ge1\) upper 3-handles and \(m=p+q\) middle handles, the penultimate \(W\) has the 0/1/2 spine with \(m-1\) two-cells. Simply connectedness gives \(H_1(W;\mathbb Z)=0\) and hence \(H_2(W;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z^{q-1}\) from the handle chain complex. Its pairing vanishes because the interior intersections can be computed in \(M\), where all oriented two-cycle classes are zero. Duality gives \(H_3(W,\partial W)=H^1(W)=0\), and \(H_2(W,\partial W)=H^2(W)=\mathbb Z^{q-1}\). The zero intersection map then makes \(H_2(\partial W)\to H_2(W)\) an isomorphism and \(H_2(W,\partial W)\to H_1(\partial W)\) an isomorphism. No restriction on \(p\) enters.

Nonzero-degree PSC domination gives a connected sum of spherical and product factors. The torsion-free computation of \(H_1(\partial W)\) gives exactly \(q-1\) product factors and makes the spherical residual an integral homology sphere. This residual admits PSC by connected-sum preservation. The product factor spheres give an integral basis of \(H_2(W)\). Auxiliary 3-handle compression kills that basis without changing the fundamental group, so the resulting smooth filling is contractible. The Chodosh–Maximo–Mukherjee obstruction then makes the residual \(S^3\). This argument identifies \(\partial W\) and says nothing about a standard smooth type for the auxiliary filling. It leaves the original lower piece and tubes in the assembly. [Proposition 2.2, Corollary 2.3 and Proposition 4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.05574v1)

In the single integral step \(Y_r\to Y_{r+1}\), the trace group is both \(F_r/N\) and a quotient of \(F_{r+1}\) by one relator. Its first homology has rank at least \(r\) from the dual presentation, so the forward relator has trivial abelianization and the resulting first homology is exactly \(\mathbb Z^r\). The dual relator has nonzero primitive abelianization. The presentation complex then has injective \(C_2\to C_1\) and passing to a classifying space by higher cells preserves \(H_2=0\). Hopf's formula gives \(N=[F_r,N]\). The filtration argument in the draft is valid. In the Magnus expansion of a reduced syllable word, the monomial that takes one letter from each successive syllable has distinct adjacent indices. It cannot receive a term with two letters from one syllable, so its coefficient is the product of the nonzero exponents. Together with the degree-raising property for commutators this proves residual nilpotence and \(N=1\).

The longitude is zero in homology of the knot exterior, whose meridian contributes an independent \(\mathbb Z\) because the knot is null homologous. The rank of the filled first homology therefore forces the longitude slope. Remove product summands of the exterior with its peripheral torus fixed. Both fillings commute with these removals, and prime uniqueness gives residual fillings \(Y_s\) and \(Y_{s+1}\). A free-factor retraction preserves null-homotopy of the residual knot. The extra product summand of the zero filling is exactly what excludes a nontrivial residual knot under Ni's Theorem 1.1. Shrinking and moving the attaching balls off a spanning disk returns a disk in the original incoming level and keeps the actual slope. This also covers \(r=0\). ## Zero-winding tower

In the direction with one lower 1-handle, normalize the winding row by genuine framed slides, place the sole winding-one attachment last, and certify each of the preceding \(m-1\) winding-zero levels in that single ordered trace. After converting the dot to zero for the boundary calculation, the dot and last component form an invertible two-by-two block. The full matrix has rank two since its \((m+1)\)-generator cokernel has rank \(m-1\). The dot links none of the other components algebraically, and the entry of the inverse block in the winding-one position is zero. Thus the Schur complement is precisely the remaining linking block, which vanishes. Each pure prefix including the converted dot has zero matrix. The associated comparison traces give \(Y_{j+1}\) after the \(j\)-th pure cut. This use of ordinary traces is confined to boundary recognition.

The integral single-knot result applies to each physical step \(Y_j\to Y_{j+1}\), so its collar-extended isotopy carries the actual future framed tubes, and the original lower piece becomes \((S^1\times D^3)\natural\natural_{m-1}(S^2\times D^2)\). The upper piece is independently \(\natural_{m-1}(S^1\times D^3)\natural(S^2\times D^2)\) from the dual step \(Y_{m-1}\to Y_m\). Interior surgery on the last sphere keeps the boundary marking and changes the upper piece to the rank-\(m\) 1-handlebody. The boundary extension is applied to that surgered body. In the split gluing the resulting closed type is \(S^1\times S^3\), with the other summands doubling complementary faces of \(D^2\times D^3\) to \(S^4\).

The actual inverse circle's complement has fundamental group \(\mathbb Z\) by codimension-three general position. Inverse surgery kills its represented class, so the original simple connectivity forces a generator. In a one-parameter homotopy of circles, the equal-time pair-and-parameter domain has dimension three and the target diagonal has codimension four. Relative transversality and the corresponding derivative test give an isotopy to a product generator, up to orientation. The two oriented normal framings differ by a loop in \(SO(3)\) whose rotation extends over \(S^1\times D^3\), so both return the standard sphere. A certificate only on the last pure prefix identifies the upper part but gives no inductive identification of the earlier lower attachments. This argument recognizes the closed tower using interior surgery. It does not supply a local 2/3 cancellation on the unsurgered pure prefix, where the dual knot is null homologous. ## Physical comparisons

- **Mazur band.** The metric lives on the specified unstabilized meridian-first trace in the original gluing, with the PSC concordance on one side of the central slice. Index-one extension attaches along two 3-balls and preserves product end germs. The PSC path on the actual \(Y_2\) connects the two pulled-back end metrics. Its slow concordance contributes \(O(D^{-2})\) to scalar curvature relative to the slice scalar. The separate product has intrinsic width \(L\), scalar floor \(\rho=\min R_h\), zero outward means and a stable central slice of projection degree one. In an eigenframe with Ricci eigenvalues \(r_j\), the chiral Weyl eigenvalues are \(-\tfrac12(r_j-R_h/3)\) for both signs. The product adds \(L\int|\operatorname{Ric}_h^0|^2/4\) in each sign and adds half of this to each complete side, with the fixed concordance counted on its own side. The coefficient is positive because \(Y_2\) has infinite fundamental group and a positive Einstein three-metric would have positive constant sectional curvature. Large widths cross the energy ceiling. No subcritical bound is established at the first qualifying width. The construction supplies no quantitative width on a minimizing or immersion-selected source.
