For a smooth oriented homotopy four-sphere with counts $(1,1,3,2,1)$, fix the normalized marked presentation and attach the winding-one component $a$ first and one of the two winding-zero components $b$ next. Write $W_a,W_{ab},W_{abc}$ for the successive lower pieces and $Y_a,Y_{ab}$ for the first two boundaries. Suppose each of those two actual cuts receives a nonzero-degree map from a smooth closed oriented 3-manifold admitting a Riemannian metric of positive scalar curvature. The sources for the two maps can differ. The test gives $M\cong S^4$.

## Cancel the first pair and carry both framings

The winding-one relation kills $\pi_1$ of the lower 1-handlebody, and the cellular $2\to1$ differential of $W_a$ is a unit. Thus $W_a$ is contractible and $Y_a$ is an integral homology sphere. CMM Proposition 2.2 gives the positive-scalar-curvature decomposition, Corollary 2.3 restricts it to signed Poincaré spheres, and Proposition 4.2 applied to $W_a$ gives $Y_a\cong S^3$. Reversing handle $a$ gives surgery from this $S^3$ to the actual lower $S^1\times S^2$. Its integer framing is zero by first homology. The dual knot is unknotted by Property R. Scharlemann's reverse formulation identifies the original attaching core up to isotopy in the actual lower boundary with the product circle. Carry the framed tube by that isotopy. Its single transverse intersection with the lower belt sphere gives the cancellation by Milnor's Theorem 5.4. [CMM, arXiv:2407.05574v1, pp. 4–5 and 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574v1) · [Gabai, Corollary 8.3 and Remark 8.5](https://www.scribd.com/document/1026013102/2-Gabai) · [Scharlemann, Theorem 10.1, p. 612](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~mgscharl/papers/suturedman.pdf#page=56) · [Milnor, Theorem 5.4, p. 48](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/surgery/hcobord.pdf#page=53)

Let $\gamma:D^4\to W_a$ be the comparison from that product cancellation, with the framing isotopy carried back to the given marking. If $u_b,u_c:S^1\times D^2\to Y_a$ are the actual residual framed embeddings after the first attachment, set $v_j=(\partial\gamma)^{-1}\circ u_j$ for $j=b,c$. The embeddings $u_j$ themselves come from the original tubes by the complement identification across the surgery on $a$. Attaching along $v_b$ or along both $v_b,v_c$ gives traces $V_b,V_{bc}$. The comparisons $\Psi_b:V_b\to W_{ab}$ and $\Psi_{bc}:V_{bc}\to W_{abc}$ use $\gamma$ on the ball and the identity on the corresponding handle models. If $\varphi:\partial W_{abc}\to\partial\mathcal H_2$ is the original upper gluing to $\mathcal H_2=\natural_2(S^1\times D^3)$ read from the top, the new gluing is $\varphi\circ\partial\Psi_{bc}$. The supplied map $f_{ab}$ to $Y_{ab}$ becomes $(\partial\Psi_b)^{-1}\circ f_{ab}$ on the residual prefix and has nonzero degree. ## Identify the second prefix and close the trace

The ordinary surgery linking matrix of $v_b,v_c$ is zero, because its cokernel is $H_1(\partial\mathcal H_2;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z^2$. In particular $V_b$ is a zero-framed one-knot trace. It is simply connected, $H_2(V_b;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z$, and its intersection form vanishes. Its boundary has $H_1=\mathbb Z$. CMM Proposition 2.2 then makes that boundary $(S^1\times S^2)\#\Sigma$, with $\Sigma$ an integral homology sphere assembled from the spherical summands. Corollary 2.3 applied to $\Sigma$ gives a possibly empty sum of signed Poincaré spheres. The pair sequence makes $H_2(\partial V_b;\mathbb Z)\to H_2(V_b;\mathbb Z)$ an isomorphism since the preceding term is $H^1(V_b)=0$ and the following map is the zero intersection form. Attach one auxiliary 3-handle on the pullback of the factor sphere, which generates that group. Its relative boundary map kills $H_2$ integrally, and its attachment preserves simple connectivity. The resulting smooth filling is contractible with boundary $\Sigma$. CMM Proposition 4.2 gives $\Sigma\cong S^3$, hence $Y_{ab}\cong S^1\times S^2$. Independently, Gabai's primeness of zero surgery already removes $\Sigma$ once the $S^1\times S^2$ summand has been identified. [CMM, Proposition 2.2, Corollary 2.3 and Proposition 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574v1) · [Gabai, Corollary 8.3](https://www.scribd.com/document/1026013102/2-Gabai)

Property R now makes $v_b$ an unknot in the actual residual link. Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson Proposition 3.2 applies to this two-component link with total surgery $\#_2(S^1\times S^2)$ and gives framed slides to the zero-framed unlink. If $\Theta:\natural_2(S^2\times D^2)\to V_{bc}$ is the traced comparison in the unlink coordinates, the assembly is along $\varphi\circ\partial\Psi_{bc}\circ\partial\Theta$. Laudenbach–Poénaru's Theorem A applies to that map and gives the smooth $S^4$. The first cut alone supports a strict reduction from three to two middle handles on the same $M$. Identifying the resulting two-component trace here uses the second cut. A larger residual link requires another recognition input. [Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson, Proposition 3.2, pp. 2313–2314](https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-4/gt-v14-n4-p13-s.pdf#page=9) · [Laudenbach–Poénaru, Theorem A, p. 337](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/bsmf.1741.pdf#page=2)
