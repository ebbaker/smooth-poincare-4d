# Deformation of marked spherical collars

The draft derives the two-width interpolation in a physical marking, its leading signed energy cost and the jet estimates for approximate faces. It gives the compactness hypotheses under which vanishing face defect would contradict disk charge.

It measures both isolated connectors along the actual charged-disk deformation, gives a finite-width cap sign test, and evaluates the trace-free mismatch on a separate curved reference family.
