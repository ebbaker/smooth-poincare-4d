| Sean Curry and A. Rod Gover, *An introduction to conformal geometry and tractor calculus, with a view to applications in general relativity*, arXiv:1412.7559v2. [Canonical record R86](../../../../../references/SOURCE_NOTES_SEC_20261124.md) · [Author text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.7559) | §4.1, equation (32) and Proposition 4.1 identify the conformal curvature obstruction. In dimension three the three-covariant Cotton tensor is conformally invariant and detects local conformal flatness. Remark 6.5 and the discussion of totally umbilic hypersurfaces in §6 give the minimal scale along the face. These passages were checked for the [face functional](../research-overview/OVERVIEW.md). The branch chooses the boundary scale and normal derivative in its own marking. |
| N. H. Kuiper, *On conformally-flat spaces in the large* (1949). [Inherited developing-map record](../../../../../references/README.md#activated-foundation-audit-22-november-2026) · [Publication](https://doi.org/10.2307/1969587) | For the smooth compact simply connected three-sphere, a conformal developing map is a local diffeomorphism onto an open and closed image in the round sphere, hence a covering and then a diffeomorphism. Use the inherited developing-map argument. Kuiper's original proof is an inherited citation. Agreement with the angular marking is a further condition. |
| [Cost of the first-order interpolation](../../../../master/manuscript/first_order_splice.tex) · [Coordinate costs in the physical marking](../../../../master/01_fixed_type_variational_descent/manuscript/disk_collar_comparison.tex) · [Strict positivity wall and short marked cut](../../../../master/manuscript/variational_comparison.tex) | The sections identified in the parent table give the first-order estimates in a supplied chart, disk-side interpolation costs and the strictly subcritical joined path test. These manuscript statements were read for their domain, width and conformal gauge. The proposed estimate starting from approximate face data is open. |
| [Three-axis Weyl samples](../../../03_first_order_twistor/01_sections_and_connections/manuscript/twistor_sections_and_chiral_weyl_curvature.tex) · [Spinorial Weyl identity](../../../03_first_order_twistor/02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/manuscript/dirac_twistor_and_yamabe_rigidity.tex) · [Dirac transmission and measured fields](../../../03_first_order_twistor/02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/01_dirac_collar_matching/manuscript/dirac_transmission_on_marked_collars.tex) | The sections manuscript tests the real positive Weyl block in three axes. The matching paper requires the actual spinor on the physical collar and independent bounds for its differentiated defect. These are inherited identities and conditional estimates. |
| [Immersed energy and topological recognition](../../../../master/manuscript/immersion_entry.tex) · [Preceding immersion and bulk discussion](../../../../master/brainstorm/notes/WEYL_PRESCRIPTION_IMMERSION_SPINORS_AND_BULK_FLOW_20261126.md) | The intrinsic path may leave the metrics induced by a supplied Euclidean hypersurface immersion.
