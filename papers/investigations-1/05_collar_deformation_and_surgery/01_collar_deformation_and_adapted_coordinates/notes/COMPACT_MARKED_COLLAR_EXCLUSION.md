# Small face defect on a precompact family of physical collars

26 November 2026. For Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance. Lead selection GPT-6.2 Astra/ultra, independent serving metadata unavailable. SP4-CDS-DEF-B02. [Source versions](../../references/README.md) at c8fb08dc.

On an oriented embedded three-sphere use the defect
\[
 \mathcal D_g(S)=\int_S(|\operatorname{Cotton}(h)|_h+|K^\circ|_h^3)\,dA_h.
\]
The three-covariant Cotton tensor is conformally invariant. The trace-free second fundamental form scales by $e^u$ under $g\mapsto e^{2u}g$. Including the area factor, both integrals are conformally invariant. [Face definitions and cited passages](../references/README.md)

**Claim.** Consider embeddings $\Phi_i:S^3\times[-1,1]\to M_i$ about specified oriented separating faces, carrying both complete sides and their physical markings. Suppose the physical pullbacks after smooth positive conformal multiplication are precompact in the smooth topology on this closed slab, with positive definite limits. Uniform ellipticity and bounds for all coordinate derivatives in a fixed finite atlas suffice. If $\mathcal D_{g_i}(S_i)\to0$, then on a subsequence there are paths to a round product up to conformal scale with $o(1)$ absolute energy increments on each complete side, uniformly along the path in both chiralities. The angular transition to round coordinates must be carried by the application. For capping a parametrized disk every such smooth transition extends.

**Proof.** Pass to $p_i=e^{2u_i}\Phi_i^*g_i\to p_\infty$. Both face integrals converge, so the limiting face is umbilic and its Cotton tensor vanishes. Local conformal flatness and conformal development on the simply connected compact $S^3$ give a round metric $\eta=e^{2v_0}h_\infty=\vartheta^*\gamma$ on the original parameter sphere. The developing map is a diffeomorphism by the covering argument. Compose with a round reflection if necessary to fix orientation. Prescribe the value $v_0$ and normal derivative $-H_\infty/3$ of one function $v$ along this face. Normal flow for $e^{2v}p_\infty$ defines a common chart $F(x,t)$, with $F(x,0)=(x,0)$, inside the original slab. On a smaller closed slab $m_\infty=F^*(e^{2v}p_\infty)=dt^2+h_t$, with $h_0=\eta$ and $\partial_t h_0=0$. The actual metrics $m_i=F^*(e^{2v}p_i)$ converge in $C^2$ to $m_\infty$, including the mixed and normal coefficients.

Put $\delta_i=\|m_i-m_\infty\|_{C^2}$ in the product background $b=dt^2+\eta$. Choose subwidths $w_{i,L}=(\delta_i+i^{-1})^{1/3}$ and $w_{i,R}=w_{i,L}/2$ for late indices. Any two widths there tending to zero with $\delta_i/w_{i,j}^2$ bounded suffice. On each side use
\[
 m_{i,s}=b+(1-s\chi(|t|/w_{i,j}))(m_i-b).
\]
The cutoff is one near zero and zero before the outer face. The zero-, one- and two-jet bounds for $m_\infty-b$ are respectively $O(w^2),O(w),O(1)$. Up to order two the errors for $m_i-m_\infty$ are $O(\delta_i)$. Twice differentiating this full four-tensor and using uniform ellipticity gives bounded curvature including the mixed terms. Each changed half has volume $O(w_{i,j})$ for every interpolant and the incoming metric. Its full-side increment therefore has absolute value at most $Cw_{i,j}$ in each sign for all $s$.

Push forward by $\Phi_i F$, and multiply back by the inverse of the composed conformal multiplier. Near both outer faces this recovers $g_i$ exactly. In the original central marking $x$ the round tensor is $\eta$. The round coordinate is $z=\vartheta(x)$, with $x=\vartheta^{-1}(z)$ for reassembly. The physical points are $\Phi_i F(x,t)$ throughout. For a disk the cap uses this boundary transition, extended over a standard ball by the sphere diffeomorphism extension in the [marked cut proof](../../../../master/manuscript/marked_cut.tex). For a trace application one must carry every framed tube and the gluing by the same coordinate change. If a particular protected region must keep its metric, take both strips in the allowed complement. This application of precompactness needs admissible subwidths with the stated error-to-width bound there, for example uniform clearance in the common slab. The gauge contributes zero signed Weyl cost.

**Consequence on the original charged disks.** On a positive minimizing sequence $e_i\to I^+(M)<2T$ on a hypothetical nonstandard type, the joined peak is at most $e_i+o(1)<2T$ for large $i$. Stretching gives positive caps on both sides. The capped exterior is diffeomorphic to the original $M$ and in sign $\sigma$ has energy $e_i-E_\sigma(g_i;D_i)+o(1)$. A fixed lower disk mass $a>0$ in the selected sign gives a contradiction at a sufficiently large *finite* index. Accordingly, on every precompact subsequence as above of the original protected charged collars, $\liminf\mathcal D_{g_i}(S_i)>0$. Otherwise a further subsequence would yield the contradiction just proved. At one exact face the [finite calculation](../../02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/notes/CHARGED_DISK_BUDGET.md) gives $E_+(g_i;D_i)=E_-(g_i;D_i)\le e_i-I^+(M)$. The same angular extension removes the need for the disk's original angular chart already to be round coordinates in that fixed-source result. A common precompact physical gauge and a numerical defect lower bound are not provided by disk selection.
