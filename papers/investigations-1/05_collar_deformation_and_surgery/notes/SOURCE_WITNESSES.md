The companion's `cor:thin-raw-collar` provides actual additional geometry. For each fixed $\eta,\zeta,r_*>0$, it selects a fixed comparison radius $r<r_*$ and $b>0$ for its late sources. There is a smooth marked $D_i$ with an actual two-sided bicollar $A_i$, all inside one comparison ball $B_i(r)$, and

$$

 \nu_i(B_i(r))<\zeta,\qquad

Here $B_i(r)$ uses the positive-Ricci comparison distance, $\nu_i=|\mathrm{Rm}_{g_i}|_{\rm tens}^2dV_{g_i}$ uses the original physical metric and the ordered four-index tensor norm, and $\lambda_i^\sigma$ is its chiral measure. The proof fixes an inner comparison ball of positive selected charge, encloses its compact preimage by three nested disks in an open smooth model ball, and uses the middle sphere and two other spheres as the seam and the native collar faces. The inverse and a buffer exist for each source chart. The construction gives neither a lower bound on usable physical Gaussian widths nor a uniform first-order comparison in a normalized annular chart. The floor $b$ can depend on $\eta,\zeta,r_*$. The corollary permits reselection of the disk and radius when these tolerances change. Consequently it supplies a fixed positive charge at fixed tolerance, with no conclusion that the same charge bound and marking survive a diagonal limit $\eta\downarrow0$. ## Width and cost on that source

For a joined path ending in a matched round germ on the original marked seam, let $P_i^\sigma$ bound its increase in total energy, and let $G_i^\sigma$ bound the endpoint increase on the complete $V_i$. The path ceiling $e_i+\min_\sigma P_i^\sigma<2T$ makes the round caps positive by stretching on the original closed type. A separately certified positive same-type cap also suffices for the exterior comparison. The angular identification and inverse must give the same oriented $M$ after replacing the parametrized ball. At the endpoint the energy of the capped exterior is $e_i-m_i^\sigma+\Delta_V^\sigma$, because the round fill adds zero in either sign. Hence

$$
 \Delta_V^\sigma\ge m_i^\sigma-d_i,\qquad
 \Delta_V^+-\Delta_V^-=m_i^+-m_i^-.
$$

A certified upper bound $G_i^\sigma<m_i^\sigma-d_i$ in either sign contradicts the infimum. For an annulus-only change, write $S=E_\sigma(g_i;A_i\cap V_i)$. If $\beta$ bounds the exterior chiral density error in $L^2$ after subtracting the leading attenuated density, then $G_i^\sigma=2\sqrt S\,\beta+\beta^2$ suffices as an upper bound. The finite comparison requires any such certified error bound to satisfy

$$
 \beta\ge\sqrt{S+m_i^\sigma-d_i}-\sqrt S
 \quad\text{when }m_i^\sigma>d_i.
$$

In particular if $S\le\eta m_i^{s_*}$ and $d_i\le m_i^{s_*}/2$, the lower bound is at least $(\sqrt{\eta+1/2}-\sqrt\eta)\sqrt{m_i^{s_*}}$. This uses a relaxed raw-shell bound and no favorable attenuation. At fixed tolerance the lower bound stays positive on the selected late sources. Small raw mass can improve the upper-bound formula when accompanied by small error. It gives no upper bound on the error itself. A cap recognized by the actual marked exterior's scalar quadratic form, with its full signed energy, can be compared without the joined ceiling.
