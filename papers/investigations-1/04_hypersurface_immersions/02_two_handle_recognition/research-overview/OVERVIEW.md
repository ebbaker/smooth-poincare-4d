## Fixed setting and inherited evidence

Let \(M\) be a closed, connected, oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere. Fix a smooth immersion \(F:M\looparrowright\mathbb R^5\) and the induced Riemannian metric \(g=F^*g_{\rm Eucl}\). Use the orientation-compatible unit normal \(\nu\) and the shape convention \(d\nu=-dF\circ A\). For the self-dual Weyl operator on \(\Lambda^+\), use \(E_+(g)=\int_M |W^+|_{\rm HS}^2\,dV_g\). On a homotopy sphere the two chiral energies agree. Integrals and all counts are on the domain, including distinct preimages at image crossings. The working hypothesis is a supplied immersion with \(E_+(g)<8\pi^2\) on a hypothetical nonstandard \(M\). The master already recognizes each supplied immersion with \(E_+(g)\le 64\pi^2/9\), so only \(64\pi^2/9<E_+(g)<8\pi^2\) can remain under that hypothesis. The induced class needs no Yamabe sign premise for these conclusions. [Master immersion theorem](../../../../master/manuscript/immersion_entry.tex) · [E34](../../../../master/reviews/E34_MIDDLE_WEYL_EQUALITY.md)

For \(u\in S^4\) such that both \(u\) and \(-u\) are regular values of \(\nu\), the height \(h_u=\langle F,u\rangle\) is Morse. Write \(n_i(u)\) for its original Morse counts, \(\Omega_2=\{x:A_x\text{ has two positive and two negative eigenvalues}\}\), \(I_2=\int_{\Omega_2}\det A\,dV_g\), and \(\omega=|S^4|=8\pi^2/3\). The inherited indexed area formula gives \(\omega^{-1}\int_{S^4}n_2(u)\,d\sigma=2I_2/\omega\). Every Morse function on a hypothetical nonstandard \(M\) has at least two index-two points. The Weyl bound in the working energy range gives mean \(n_2<9/4\). Therefore more than three quarters of directions have exactly two index-two points. The orthonormal-frame corollary can select four orthogonal directions with that count. Each selection controls \(n_2\). It leaves the remaining count distribution and all attaching and belt data to be obtained separately. ## Marked presentations and the two cases

Record the gradient-like data and all comparison maps used to express the selected height as an ordered handle decomposition. Milnor's rearrangement supplies an auxiliary ordered Morse presentation. Its ordered levels require a separate comparison if a proposed estimate uses original height levels. Denote the reduced counts by \(m_i\). Then \(m_0=m_4=1\), \(m_2=2\), and \(m_1+m_3=2\). A marked decomposition records the lower \(0\)–\(1\) handlebody \(P\), the ordered or jointly attached index-two handles with framed attaching embeddings, the upper dual \(0\)–\(1\) body \(Q\), and their actual collared boundary identifications. If diagrams are used, supply identifications with these objects and retain the outgoing surgery marking after every move.

The mixed case calls for a geometric intersection number of one, with the framing and slide or isotopy specified. A signed incidence of \(\pm 1\) may allow several transverse intersections and leaves cancellation open. A band for a slide must be embedded in the marked level with interior disjoint from the other attaching data as required by the slide. An ambient crossing between different sheets of \(F\) cannot supply such a band. The one-knot theorem in E33 becomes available only after obtaining the relevant one-middle-handle presentation. ## Open implications

The master energy estimate supplies a measured direction set under the conditional energy hypothesis. This branch still needs a mechanism that gives the geometric handle data or establishes a usable extra hypothesis for one named class. A proof for a supplied mixed presentation leaves height selection open unless every admitted immersion is proved to have such a presentation with the needed marking. A proof for one two-component class must establish that the relevant heights meet its added hypotheses before it can be used to exhaust the one-sided case. Any bridge from the actual heights to ordered handle levels must specify its maps and the data they preserve. Quantitative existence of a suitable \(F\) on arbitrary smooth types remains with the [construction arm](../../01_immersed_handle_constructions/README.md) and [master](../../../../master/README.md).
