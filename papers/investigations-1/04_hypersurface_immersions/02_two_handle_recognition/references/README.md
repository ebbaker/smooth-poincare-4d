| John Milnor, *Lectures on the h-Cobordism Theorem* (1965), Theorem 4.8 gives the auxiliary self-indexing presentation. Theorem 5.4 applies to a pair of consecutive indices whose two spheres in the common intermediate level meet transversely at exactly one point. It gives a product cobordism with the Morse data fixed near the end levels. [E33 source use](../../../../master/reviews/E33_INDEX_TWO_HANDLES.md#one-minimum-and-one-maximum-with-the-original-middle-count) | Theorem 4.8 checked in the [author text scan](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/surgery/hcobord.pdf). Theorem 5.4 statement inspected in a [search-indexed transcription](https://www.cjhb.site/Files.php/Books/Topology/John%20W.Milnor/%28PUP%29%20Lectures%20on%20h-cobordism%20Theorem%20%28v3-2023%29.pdf) after the corresponding scan passage was unavailable through retrieval. |
| David Gabai, *Foliations and the topology of 3-manifolds III*, Corollary 8.3 and Remark 8.5 give Property R for zero-framed surgery on one knot in \(S^3\) yielding \(S^1\times S^2\). Corollary 8.6 gives the case of one 2-handle and one 3-handle on \(D^4\) with \(H_2=0\). Martin Scharlemann, *Generalized Property R and the Schoenflies Conjecture*, Corollary 2.2, gives an independent statement for a homology four-sphere with one 2-handle and no 3-handles in the PL category. [Smooth application](../../../../master/reviews/E33_INDEX_TWO_HANDLES.md#the-one-middle-handle-consequence) | Gabai's statements checked in a [reproduction of the published text](https://www.scribd.com/document/1026013102/2-Gabai) after the [publisher link](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214441488) could not be retrieved. Scharlemann's category and Corollary 2.2 checked in the [journal text](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/43163). |
| François Laudenbach and Valentin Poénaru, *A note on 4-dimensional handlebodies*, Theorem A, p. 337. Gluing \(\natural_p(S^2\times D^2)\) to \(\natural_p(S^1\times D^3)\) by any boundary diffeomorphism gives the smooth \(S^4\). The existing one-middle result uses \(p=1\). A prospective two-component result must first identify the trace for \(p=2\). | Theorem A statement checked in the [journal text](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/bsmf.1741.pdf). |
| Allen Hatcher, *A proof of the Smale Conjecture*, opening theorem and discussion, p. 553. The components result for smooth diffeomorphisms of \(S^3\) gives the disk extension used in the two-disk case. [Canonical record R122](../../../../../references/SOURCE_NOTES_JOINT_COLLAR_20261125.md) | Checked the opening discussion through a search excerpt. The [author PDF](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/Papers/SmaleConjecture.pdf) returned a size limit on direct retrieval. |
| Robert E. Gompf, Martin Scharlemann and Abigail Thompson, *Fibered knots and potential counterexamples to the Property 2R and Slice-Ribbon Conjectures*, *Geom. Topol.* 14 (2010), §2, pp. 2307–2310. Framed band sums use the parallel copy determined by the framing. Conjecture 1 isolates the general slide-to-unlink question. Proposition 2.2 forces zero framings and vanishing pairwise linking numbers when surgery yields \(\#_n(S^1\times S^2)\). These are background for the one-sided case. | The slide description, Conjecture 1 and Proposition 2.2 checked in the [journal text](https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-4/gt-v14-n4-p13-s.pdf). |
