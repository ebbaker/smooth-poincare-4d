# Chiral Weyl energy and Morse heights of immersed homotopy four-spheres

[Working manuscript](chiral_energy_of_hypersurface_immersions.tex) by Edward B. Baker III, revised 26 November 2026. The paper proves the pointwise and integrated curvature comparisons, the inclusive immersed recognition threshold, the subcritical height count, and the exterior angular criterion. It distinguishes the induced metric's Yamabe sign from the sign and energy requirements for intrinsic entry.
