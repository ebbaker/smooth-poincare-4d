# Research plan for immersed index-two handle constructions

25 November 2026. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance. GPT-6.2 Sol/ultra was requested for this setup. Actual serving model and effort are unavailable. [Fixed inputs and conventions](../research-overview/OVERVIEW.md#inputs-and-versions) · [Live SP4-IMM-HDL register](../research-progress/REGISTER.md) · [Model workflow](../../../../../GPT_RESEARCH_MODEL_WORKFLOW.md)

Begin with one concrete marked index-two attachment and supplied compatible face germs. Use $E_+$ in the induced orientation and operator normalization of the [overview](../research-overview/OVERVIEW.md). A bounded construction must track the actual framing and a full-domain application must use one immersion throughout.

| ID and type | Recommendation and reason | Prerequisite and finite scope | Deliverable, completion and escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SP4-IMM-HDL-B01 · B | Astra/ultra. The immersion ansatz and the class of admissible germs determine what can be estimated. | On activation, select one explicit marked handlebody, one framed embedding and one pair of collar germs. Compare a saddle graph and a trace built from supplied collar germs, within this one marked attachment. | Record the chosen rounded domain, framing, common-corner compatibility, expressions or construction route, parameter range, and budget to check in E01. Include any claimed height direction and level sets. Freeze E01 only when the data and test are fixed. If neither route yields compatible germs, record the failed premise and choose the next local mechanism in B work. |
| SP4-IMM-HDL-E01 · E | GPT-6.2 Sol/ultra. With the model and parameter range fixed, rank, face matching and curvature are explicit checks. | One B01 model and its two matching regions, with the written E request fixing the allowed parameter range and constants. | Give a smooth rank-four map with actual framed face agreement and a bound for the integral on each of the three regions. Prove uniformity in the frozen range and record determinant and shape costs when required by the target. Check any local height claim using the given direction on the resulting map. Return a scoped failure to B01 if rank, corner or framing fails or if a different ansatz or target is needed. |
| SP4-IMM-HDL-B02 · B | Astra/ultra. Marked exterior extension and an attainable global threshold require a geometric choice. | Requires assessed E01 evidence. Select one concrete supplied closed completion on the originally marked domain, or identify the missing exterior data. | Record the same-immersion extension method, actual inside–outside marking, retained exterior, leakage or modification terms, and one achievable quantitative target to audit. Give the intended inequality for the full energy and, if positive-Yamabe entry is the target, a candidate sign argument. If a matching exterior is unavailable, name the exact unresolved extension premise and keep E02 dependent. |
| SP4-IMM-HDL-E02 · E | GPT-6.2 Sol/ultra. A supplied exterior and a fixed extension method permit an explicit budget and dependency audit. | Requires a frozen B02 completion. One assembled immersion on one marked domain, with both matching regions and the actual exterior. | Prove smooth assembly on the given domain. Report the full-domain energy and any required $J$, shape or Yamabe estimate in the same normalization, with any uncovered or changed region charged explicitly. Apply a recognition or entry criterion only if its hypotheses have been checked, and state whether the result is a single model or a conditional class. Return the remaining estimate or geometry to B02 if the stated criterion or extension is unavailable. |
| SP4-IMM-HDL-B03 · B | Astra/ultra. Adapting to other markings or several handles changes the geometric premises. | Requires an assessed first local model or scoped failure. Choose one next framing, overlapping attachment or class of exterior germs from that evidence. | Identify precisely which fixed-domain estimate would persist and which compatibility or budget estimate is missing. Reserve any execution packet until its scope and completion test are fixed. |

Freeze numerical work separately if later needed. Independent checks should state their common premises and reproduce the rank, marking and budget checks relevant to each claim. Route a verified actual height, its induced face markings and any proved global energy bound to [two-handle recognition](../../02_two_handle_recognition/README.md). The [immersion parent](../../README.md) integrates shared geometric evidence and forwards qualifying full-domain results to the [master](../../../../master/README.md).
