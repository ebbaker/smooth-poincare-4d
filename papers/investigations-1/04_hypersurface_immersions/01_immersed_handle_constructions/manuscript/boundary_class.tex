\section{Filling the marked boundary class}
\label{sec:marked-boundary-class}

The actual half \(W=W^-(0,3)\) in the \(n=1\) diagram has one
handle of each index \(0,1,2\). It therefore has a smooth
positive-scalar-curvature metric with mean-convex boundary by
Sweeney \cite[Proposition 1.2]{Sweeney2026Contractible}.
The boundary deformation of
B\"ar--Hanke \cite[Definition 3.1 and Theorem 3.7]{BaerHanke2023Boundary},
with second fundamental form zero as its target, yields a PSC
metric with totally geodesic boundary on the same half. It fixes
the induced boundary metric \(\gamma_0\). Its terminal Fermi
collar is \(dt^2+(1-Ct^2)\gamma_0\), which extends evenly to a
smooth PSC metric on the marked identity double. The induced
class here is that of the input metric supplied by Sweeney's
proposition. The prescribed class \([\gamma(v_*)]\) requires a
separate extension.

\begin{proposition}[Boundary trace criterion]
\label{prop:marked-robin-trace}
Fix a smooth metric \(\gamma\) in the boundary marking of \(W\).
A smooth PSC metric with totally geodesic boundary in \([\gamma]\)
exists if and only if some smooth PSC extension \(g\) of the
exact metric \(\gamma\) satisfies
\[
 6\langle f,\mathcal N_g f\rangle
       +2\int_Y H_g f^2\,dA_\gamma>0
 \qquad (0\ne f\in H^{1/2}(Y)).
\]
Here \(H_g\) is the trace of the second fundamental form for
the outward unit normal \(\nu\). The operator
\(\mathcal N_g f=\partial_\nu u_f|_Y\) is given by
\((-6\Delta_g+R_g)u_f=0\), \(u_f|_Y=f\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
PSC gives a positive Dirichlet form and hence a unique \(u_f\).
Let \(J_g=-6\Delta_g+R_g\). The Robin form
\[
 Q_g^{\rm Rob}(u)=\int_W(6|du|_g^2+R_gu^2)\,dV_g
                    +2\int_Y H_g u^2\,dA_\gamma
\]
splits into the trace form in the statement and the positive
Dirichlet remainder on \(u-u_f\). If the trace form is positive
then the lowest Robin eigenvalue is positive, by the trace
inequality and compactness in interior and boundary \(L^2\).
Its positive first eigenfunction \(u\) satisfies
\(\partial_\nu u+(H_g/3)u=0\). Thus \(u^2g\) has strictly
positive scalar curvature and zero mean curvature.
B\"ar--Hanke's deformation with target second fundamental
form zero preserves its induced metric. Their mean curvature
convention is the trace divided by three, with the same sign
as ours \cite[proof of Lemma 2.18 and Theorem 3.7]{BaerHanke2023Boundary}.

Conversely, let the filling be PSC and totally geodesic.
Normalize its boundary metric to \(\gamma\) by a positive
conformal change on the half, obtaining \(h\). Green's formula
and conformal covariance give
\(Q_{b^2h}^{\rm Rob}(\phi)=Q_h^{\rm Rob}(b\phi)\) for smooth
\(b>0\), so \(Q_h^{\rm Rob}\) is positive. In particular the
Dirichlet realization of \(J_h\) is positive. Solve
\(J_hw=1\) with \(w|_Y=1\). Testing the negative part and
using Dirichlet positivity gives \(w\ge0\). An interior zero
would give \(J_hw\le0\) there. Thus \(g=w^2h\) is smooth
and PSC with exact boundary metric \(\gamma\) and positive
Robin form. Testing it on \(u_f\) proves the claim.
\end{proof}

The sign in Proposition~\ref{prop:marked-robin-trace} is also
the positivity of the relative Yamabe invariant with fixed
boundary class. In each extension conformal class the Robin
sign gives the relative Yamabe sign, using a minimal-boundary
representative, the Sobolev inequality and the first eigenfunction
test \cite[\S\S1.1 and 1.3]{AkutagawaBotvinnik2002Relative}.
Shi--Wang--Wei \cite[Theorem 1.1 and Remark 2.1]{ShiWangWei2022FillIns}
supply PSC extensions of the exact
\(\gamma(v_*)\) on this \(W\) with no guarantee of nonnegative
mean curvature. The boundary conformal Laplacian has negative
first eigenvalue since \(R_{\gamma(v_*)}=-a^2(2+v_*^2/2)\) is
constant. As a result the fixed-boundary Robin sign is the
remaining existence test.

\begin{proposition}[Spin obstruction in the homogeneous family]
\label{prop:homogeneous-spin-crossing}
The first zero crossing \(v_c\) in the marked family \(\gamma(v)\)
satisfies \(2/11\le v_c^2\le2\). For \(0<v<v_c\) the boundary
operator is invertible and the APS index on a product-germ
contractible filling is zero. For every \(v\ge v_c\) the boundary
kernel or this APS index is nonzero. Accordingly, no such
\([\gamma(v)]\) with \(v\ge v_c\) admits a PSC totally geodesic
filling on the smooth oriented contractible half.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Use \(H,V,\omega,D_v\) from Section~\ref{sec:eta-zero-filling}
on the fixed Haar \(L^2\) space of complex spinors. The symmetric
elliptic operator \(H^2+V^2\) has finite-dimensional smooth
eigenspaces, each preserved by \(H\) and \(V\) because they
anticommute. Split each into their joint square eigenspaces
\(H^2=\mu I,\ V^2=\nu I\), with \(\mu\ge0\) and
\(\nu\ge(a/22)^2\). On such a space,
\((H+v^{-1}V)^2=(\mu+v^{-2}\nu)I\); its sign multiplicities are
constant for \(v>0\) by continuity. The branches of \(D_v\)
are therefore of the form
\[
  \pm\sqrt{\mu+v^{-2}\nu}-av\omega/4 .
\]
When \(\omega=1\), every crossing belongs to a plus branch
and is strictly downward. When \(\omega=-1\), every crossing
belongs to a minus branch and is strictly upward. A crossing
with \(v\le M\) requires
\(\mu+\nu\le\max(1,M^2)(aM/4)^2\), giving finitely many
crossings on any compact parameter interval. The constant
spinors in the global frame have branch
\(a(2-v^2)\omega/(4v)\), of complex multiplicity at least two.
Hence there is a crossing at \(v=\sqrt2\), and the small-fiber
gap in Section~\ref{sec:eta-zero-filling} bounds \(v_c\) from
below.

Extend the boundary path to smooth product-germ metrics on one
fixed contractible \(W\). On intervals with invertible boundary
Dirac operators the APS index is constant. At a crossing,
isolate its finite-dimensional boundary spectral subspace
and continuously identify the other spectral subspaces on
the two sides. The trace map has a right inverse. The APS
domain gains or loses the crossing subspace, so the index
changes by the signed dimension of this subspace. The
absolute index is therefore positive at every invertible
parameter after the first crossing, since the initial index
is zero and the jumps have a common sign. At the crossing
parameters the boundary kernel is nonzero. Both necessary
tests follow for totally geodesic PSC fillings by
Propositions~\ref{prop:free-trace-spin}
and~\ref{prop:totally-geodesic-half-filling}.
\end{proof}

The eta-zero scale \(v_*^2=\sqrt{159}/11-1\) is below
\(2/11\). A separate curvature test excludes zero Weyl
energy at this scale. In the \(\gamma(v)\)-orthonormal
left-invariant frame, the intrinsic Schouten tensor
\(P=\operatorname{Ric}_\gamma-(R_\gamma/4)\gamma\) satisfies
\(P_{33}-P_{11}=P_{33}-P_{22}=a^2(1+v^2)\).
Koszul's formula gives
\(\langle\nabla_1 e_2,e_3\rangle=-av/2\) and
\(\langle\nabla_2 e_1,e_3\rangle=av/2\). Thus the Cotton
component \((\nabla_1P)_{23}-(\nabla_2P)_{13}
=a^3v(1+v^2)\) is nonzero. For a locally conformally flat
four-metric with totally geodesic boundary, the ambient
Schouten tensor restricts to the intrinsic one by Gauss.
The ambient Schouten Codazzi identity restricts to the
intrinsic Cotton identity. This would force the boundary
Cotton tensor to vanish, a conformally invariant condition.
Therefore every smooth totally geodesic filling of
\([\gamma(v_*)]\) has \(E_+(W)=E_-(W)>0\), using the
eta-zero equality of Section~\ref{sec:eta-zero-filling}.
The sign of the Robin form at the prescribed scale and
the upper energy budget for recognition remain unresolved.
