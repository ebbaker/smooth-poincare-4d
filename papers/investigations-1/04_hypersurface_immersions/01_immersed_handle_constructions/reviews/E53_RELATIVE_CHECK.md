## Degree and index integrals

Equality of the actual immersion maps in the whole marked seam neighborhoods makes the inserted references a smooth immersion on the original closed domain. The compatible unit normal identifies the oriented plane \(dF(T_pM)\) with \(T_{\nu(p)}S^4\). Euler classes of the oriented isomorphism \(TM\simeq\nu^*TS^4\) give \(2\deg\nu=\chi(M)=2\). The identity \(d\nu=-dF\circ A\) gives signed Jacobian \(\det A\) since the dimension is even. Critical Gauss values and seam images have spherical measure zero. Each reference Gauss map is injective into its hemisphere and has positive determinant, so its \(N_j\) subtracts one positive preimage per direction in \(N_j\). The relative signed count on \(C^\circ\) is \(1-\ell(u)\). Wherever \(\ell(u)=3\) and this count applies, the negative preimages outnumber the positive by two.

At a critical point of height \(u\), the Hessian is \(g(A\cdot,\cdot)\) for \(u=\nu\) and its negative for \(u=-\nu\). Reversing a four-dimensional index sends \(j\) to \(4-j\), fixing index two and preserving odd parity. The reference patches therefore contribute only middle points, in number \(k(u)=\ell(u)+\ell(-u)\). The area formula gives \(J(C)=\frac12\int(m_1+m_3)\), since odd index corresponds to \(\det A<0\) and both normals are counted. Likewise \(I_2(C)=\frac12\int m_{2,C}\) and \(\frac12\int k=\sum_j s_j=S\). These integrals use the unnormalized unit \(S^4\) measure \(\omega_4=8\pi^2/3\). Substituting \(m_2=m_{2,C}+k\) into the Morse Euler sum gives the claimed identity, including the nonnegative correction \(\tfrac12\int (m_0+m_4-2)\). Multiplicity throughout is on the domain. [Onti–Vlachos, arXiv v3, §3, equation (5), printed p. 11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.07349#page=11)

On a nonstandard type the inherited \(m_2\ge2\) gives \(I_2(C)\ge D_C\). The identity \((2-k)_+=2-k+(k-2)_+\) gives \(S+D_C=\omega_4+\frac12\int(k-2)_+\). The inherited exterior theorem gives \(E_+(C)>\frac83D_C\), with strictness from an open middle-signature region along any reference seam. With empty seam family the nonstandard Morse bound gives positive exterior middle mass and hence an open region. Substituting these bounds in \(B^2(C)\ge6E_+(C)+\frac{16}{3}J(C)\) gives \(B^2(C)>\frac{64}{3}D_C+\frac{16}{3}S\). The sufficient recognition upper bound for \(J\) is strict and the one for \(B^2\) is inclusive. For three coincident one-sign images of area \(s\), the common image and its antipode have total measure \(2s\), with \(k=3\) there and zero elsewhere. Thus \(D_C=\omega_4-2s\) and the necessary bound on a nonstandard type is \(J(C)\ge\omega_4+s\). ## Standard completion and sign

The three cutoff supports lie in disjoint horizontal balls of radius \(2r\) centered at \(5jr e_1\). They enter a fixed upper chart for small \(r\). The original graph has vanishing constant and linear terms at zero. The bracket in the new graph formula therefore has size \(O(r^2)\), first derivative \(O(r)\), and second derivative \(O(1)\). The cutoff derivative factors \(r^{-1}\) and \(r^{-2}\) preserve these bounds for the full change. The horizontal differential has rank four. The upper and lower spherical graphs were separated at the affected horizontal points by \(2\sqrt{1-|X|^2}\), so the \(O(r^2)\) height change preserves global injectivity. Outside the supports the closed embedding is unchanged.

The rounded disk has \(|z|\le\sqrt2<3/2\), so \(X=r(a_j+z)\) lies in the plateau of its cutoff with a buffer around the whole seam. The graph is exactly \((ra_j,0)+F_{r,r}(z)\). At \(\delta_0<\lambda<1/2\), the equations at heights \(\pm r^2\lambda\) give the two product faces and the inherited angle and framing coordinates. For each fixed \(r\), Sard's theorem on the glued height permits simultaneous regularity at both chosen levels. The three saddle centers have a common critical value zero, which does not interfere with regularity at those levels.

The one-sign Gauss map of each reference in gnomonic coordinates is \(p=-rDz\), with Jacobian \(r^4\). For the spherical coordinate map \(p\mapsto(p,1)/\sqrt{1+|p|^2}\), the metric has three eigenvalues \((1+|p|^2)^{-1}\) transverse to \(p\) and one eigenvalue \((1+|p|^2)^{-2}\) along \(p\). This gives area density \((1+|p|^2)^{-5/2}\) and \(s_r=|\Omega|r^4+O(r^6)\), with the same actual image for all three references. The exterior thus satisfies \(3s_r\le J(C_r)=O(r^4)\). Every modified point belongs to a set of induced volume \(O(r^4)\) with uniformly bounded graph shape. The untouched round part contributes zero density to \(E_\pm,B^2,J\). In particular \(E_+(C_r)\to0\) and \(D_{C_r}\to\omega_4\), so the actual exterior meets the recognition criterion.

The closed metric is uniformly equivalent to the original round metric. Its scalar curvature is 12 off the supports and is uniformly bounded on them, so \(\|R_{g_r}-12\|_{L^2(g_r)}=O(r^2)\). Compact Sobolev and Hölder give
\[
 Q_{g_r}(f)\ge 6\|df\|_2^2+12\|f\|_2^2-O(r^2)\|f\|_4^2
 \ge c\|f\|_4^2
\]
for fixed \(c>0\) and sufficiently small \(r\). This verifies positive conformal Yamabe sign on the completed standard sphere, including the negative-scalar patches. For a fixed immersion on an unclassified type, the analogous chart insertion changes exterior and total energy only by \(O(r^4)\). The original strict energy gap above \(64\pi^2/9\) on a putative nonstandard type therefore persists for small \(r\). Quantitative control of the starting immersion and actual global attaching data remain separate premises there.
