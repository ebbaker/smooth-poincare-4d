In the standard-side mapping torus of \(Y^\circ=Y\setminus\operatorname{int}B^3\), use the pages at disk angles 0 and \(\pi\). In the core chart \(S^2\times D^2\) connect them by \(S^2\) times the entire real diameter. The endpoint identifications are the native parametrizations, or the common exterior maps \(a_{18}=a_0 T^9\). They preserve the page angle, and their fiber rotations at each endpoint do not affect the three-manifold type. With product collars at the cuts this gives a smooth closed embedded two-sided hypersurface \(L\cong Y\#(-Y)\). Opposite oriented pages glue along their boundary spheres. It also describes a hypersurface on the target with its own cap, although its second fundamental form depends on the inserted metric. [Kuhrman §§2.2–2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.03798v1#S2.SS3)

The aspherical Brieskorn homology sphere \(Y\cong-\Sigma(2,3,7)\) does not admit positive scalar curvature, and the same holds for \(L\). One group proof uses its circle fibration over the hyperbolic triangle orbifold. The regular fiber gives an infinite central cyclic subgroup (pull back to the contractible hyperbolic orbifold cover). A lift of a hyperbolic element of the triangle group commutes with that fiber and generates a copy of \(\mathbb Z^2\) with it. This subgroup injects into \(\pi_1(Y)*\pi_1(-Y)=\pi_1(L)\). The PSC classification of oriented integral homology three-spheres permits only sums of signed Poincaré spheres and the standard sphere. Their groups are virtually free and have no \(\mathbb Z^2\). [Kuhrman §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.03798v1#S4.SS1) · [Chodosh–Maximo–Mukherjee, Corollary 2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574)

Suppose the **standard-side background** is conformal on the entire marked cap to the product of the hyperbolic disk and unit sphere, using the stated polar angles. The diameter is a geodesic, so the cap part of \(L\) is umbilic. If the *two entire prescribed exterior pages* are also umbilic in that background, then all of \(L\) is umbilic by smooth matching. The entry seam condition would give its induced conformal class positive Yamabe sign, a contradiction. Thus this attempted background fails even on the standard smooth sphere at every model radius. The assumption can for instance be imposed by metrics product in normal time near these two pages away from the gluing and matched umbilically in the germs. No reflection of the whole manifold is required. More generally the inherited necessary inequality on this explicit \(L\) reads
\[
 Y(L,[h])+\Big(\int_{L\cap E}|B^\circ|^3\,dA_h\Big)^{2/3}>0
\]
because the cap diameter contributes zero. Here \(h\) is the metric induced on the complete double page. This forces bending in the retained exterior of any entering standard-side class with this core model. It supplies no universal positive numerical cost; \(Y(L,[h])\) depends on the actual seam data. A throughput to the target would still have to break the obstructed deck symmetry there, preserve the literal framing or carry the chosen boundary isotopy and metric, price all regions in both chiralities and establish positivity on the closed target. A fitted flux coefficient does not supply any of these.
