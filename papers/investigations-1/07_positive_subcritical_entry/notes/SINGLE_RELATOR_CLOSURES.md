# Each transition band has equal, strictly positive chiral costs

26 November 2026, for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance. Selected lead Astra/ultra, actual serving settings unavailable. B02 deduction on the precisely marked exterior in the [funnel construction](FUNNEL_EXTERIOR.md). This adds a single-surgery comparison for each of the two fixed bands. No orientation symmetry or sign of the conformal Laplacian is assumed.

Let \(N=\partial(F\times D^3)=\#_2(S^1\times S^3)\) have the Weyl-flat metric of the funnel construction. For \(i=1,2\), perform exactly the \(i\)-th marked surgery and keep its chosen smooth band \(U_i\) and positive-facing hyperbolic cap. On the other marked tube retain the original smooth metric of \(N\) right through the core. Call the resulting oriented closed four-manifold \(N_i\). This alternative is available for **every** smooth choice of the transitions matching the stated end germs. The second band's metric is never used in \(N_i\). Thus
\[
 E_\pm(N_i)=E_\pm(U_i)
\]
with the orientation induced from the same exterior as the final two-surgery closure.

The original \(\chi(N)=-2\). Replacing \(S^1\times D^3\) by \(D^2\times S^2\) adds 2 to the Euler characteristic. The boundary \(S^1\times S^2\) has Euler characteristic zero. Removing an embedded circle in dimension four preserves \(\pi_1\) by transversality, and adjoining the cap kills exactly the normal closure of the corresponding relator. Its exponent-sum row is primitive and nonzero, so \(b_1(N_i)=1\). Thus \(\chi(N_i)=0\), \(b_2(N_i)=0\) by duality, and \(\tau(N_i)=0\). The closed signature formula in the real chiral Hilbert–Schmidt convention gives
\[
 E_+(U_i)-E_-(U_i)=12\pi^2\tau(N_i)=0.
\]
The [longitude holonomy test](HYPERBOLIC_EXTERIOR.md) proves strictly positive combined cost on that band; hence each of its two equal costs is strictly positive. Define \(c_i=E_+(U_i)=E_-(U_i)>0\). The complete energy condition for this marked construction is exactly \(c_1+c_2<8\pi^2\). An explicit upper bound, or a positive uniform lower bound large enough to rule out entry, still needs proof. The closed Yamabe sign belongs to the actual two-surgery metric. Single-surgery closures are used only to read the signed costs.
