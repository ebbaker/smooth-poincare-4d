## Miller–Schupp candidate

Use the length-14 presentation
\[
P=\big\langle x,y\mid x^{-1}y^2x=y^3,\quad x=yx^2y^{-1}x^{-2}\big\rangle.
\]
Take the ordered relators $r_1=x^{-1}y^2xy^{-3}$ and $r_2=x^{-1}yx^2y^{-1}x^{-2}$. These have lengths $7,7$. Their exponent rows on $x,y$ are $(0,-1)$ and $(-1,0)$, so the presented group is perfect. Miller–Schupp's Theorem 1 then implies triviality since the conjugate powers $2,3$ are coprime. Their theorem applies to every $n>0$ and word of $x$-exponent zero in $\operatorname{MS}(n,w)=\langle x,y\mid x^{-1}y^nx=y^{n+1},\ x=w\rangle$. The original source is Charles F. Miller III and Paul E. Schupp, *Some presentations of the trivial group*, Contemp. Math. **250** (1999), 113–115. [Publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/250/03848) · [Checked manuscript, pp. 1–3](https://hcmuaf.edu.vn/giangvien/data/file/Some%20presentations%20of%20the%20trivial%20group.pdf)

For the oriented smooth boundary construction, start with $V_1=\natural^2(S^1\times D^4)$ and realize the two conjugacy classes by disjoint oriented smooth attaching circles in $\partial V_1=\#^2(S^1\times S^3)$. Their normal three-plane bundles are oriented and trivial. Once a spin structure on $V_1$ and actual circles are chosen, the two normal framing classes on each circle differ by the generator of $\pi_1SO(3)=\mathbb Z/2$. Exactly one lets the spin structure extend across that two-handle. The exponent matrix is invertible mod $2$, so changing the spin structure on $V_1$ realizes each prescribed change of the two extension parities. The checked algebra sources give words and no geometric framing or parametrized tubes. The spin rule supplies a framing convention for a chosen geometric realization.

The five-dimensional thickening $H_P$ has $\pi_1=1$ and the homotopy type of its two-dimensional presentation complex. The exponent matrix also gives acyclicity, so $H_P$ is contractible. Turning it upside down makes the boundary inclusion an isomorphism on $\pi_1$ since all dual handles have index at least three. Duality gives the homology of $S^4$, and the Hurewicz and Whitehead theorems give the homotopy sphere $\Sigma_P=\partial H_P$ with the outward-normal-first orientation. The diffeomorphism type of the unmarked thickening is determined by the presentation. Homotopic disjoint attaching circles are isotopic in the four-dimensional attaching boundary. A twist of the normal four-plane coordinates around a one-handle core changes the spin structure on that generator and hence accounts for the framing choices after allowing a diffeomorphism of the one-handlebody. Gay–Kirby §4.1 also identify the boundary with the smooth identity double of any four-dimensional contractible $0,1,2$ realization of $P$. An actual such realization marks the seam and can depend on its diagram. [Gay–Kirby, *Constructing Lefschetz-type fibrations on four-manifolds*, GT **11** (2007), p. 2095, Lemma 4.7 and Remark 4.9](https://msp.org/gt/2007/11-4/gt-v11-n4-p03-p.pdf)

A sequence of Andrews–Curtis (AC) moves to the trivial presentation gives smooth cancellation of the five-dimensional one-/two-handle pairs and identifies the boundary with the standard $S^4$. Stable AC triviality is sufficient too, since a generator–relator stabilization can be realized by adding a canceling pair. Gabai's recognition problem applies to this precise $n=2$ family. For its contractible thickenings, standardness of the boundary is equivalent to standardness of the five-ball by the five-dimensional result cited in Gabai's Remark 13.2(i). [Gabai–Naylor–Schwartz, *Doubles of Gluck twists: A five-dimensional approach*, Lemma 3.4](https://web.math.princeton.edu/facultypapers/Gabai/Doubles.Gluck.pdf) · [Gabai, *3-Spheres in the 4-Sphere and Pseudo-Isotopies of $S^1\times S^3$*, arXiv:2212.02004v2, Remarks 13.2(i), 13.5(ii) and Problem 13.6, p. 62](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.02004v2)

Shehper et al. §3.3 list $P$ as the minus choice in a pair of length-14 presentations for which their bounded search gave no path to the trivial presentation or to $\operatorname{AK}(3)$. The same paper proves AC triviality for $n=1$ with all words of $x$-exponent zero, for $w=y^{-1}xyx^{-1}$ with any $n>0$, and for $\operatorname{MS}(2,y^{-k}x^{-1}yxy)$ for all $k\in\mathbb Z$. Those cases give recognized boundaries by handle cancellation. [Shehper et al., arXiv:2408.15332v2, Theorem B and §§3.3–3.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15332v2)

## Akbulut–Kirby controls

For $n\ge 1$, use
\[
\operatorname{AK}(n)=\big\langle x,y\mid xyx=yxy,\quad x^n=y^{n+1}\big\rangle.
\]
The braid relation conjugates $x$ to $y$ by $xy$. The common element $z=x^n=y^{n+1}$ is central, so conjugating $x^n$ gives $y^n=z=y^{n+1}$. Thus $y=1$ and then $x=1$. Akbulut–Kirby's original four-dimensional diagram is Fig. 28, p. 383, with two one-handles and two two-handles. The authors simplify the read-off relations (1), (2) to $xyx=yxy$ and $x^5=y^4$. Exchanging $x,y$ gives the displayed $n=4$ convention. The framed diagram marks this four-dimensional handlebody. The cap on the original four-dimensional diagram and the boundary of its five-dimensional presentation thickening require their own geometric identifications. [Akbulut–Kirby, *A potential smooth counterexample in dimension 4*, Topology **24** (1985), §2, Fig. 28 and equations (1)–(5)](https://math.berkeley.edu/~kirby/papers/Akbulut%20and%20Kirby%20-%20A%20potential%20smooth%20counterexample%20in%20dimension%204%20to%20the%20Poincar%C3%A9%20conjecture%2C%20the%20Schoenflies%20conjecture%2C%20and%20the%20Andrews-Curtis%20conjecture%20-%20MR0816520.pdf)

Gabai's Remarks 13.5(ii) record that Gompf recognized the five-dimensional thickenings of the Akbulut–Kirby presentations as standard $B^5$. For the original $n=4$ word pair Gay–Kirby directly record the standard boundary in Remark 4.9. Meier–Zupan Theorems 1.1–1.2 and Corollary 1.3 also recover standardness of the specified Gompf $H(n,k)$ and Cappell–Shaneson $\Sigma_m$ four-spheres by identifying actual framed surgery links with the generalized-square-knot hypothesis. This link theorem requires a four-dimensional presentation with no one-handles, two two-handles and a generalized square knot component of the attaching link. An abstract group presentation supplies no such link description. [Gompf, *Killing the Akbulut–Kirby 4-sphere*, Topology **30** (1991), 97–115](https://doi.org/10.1016/0040-9383%2891%2990036-4) · [Gabai, arXiv:2212.02004v2, Remarks 13.5(ii)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.02004v2) · [Gay–Kirby, Remark 4.9](https://msp.org/gt/2007/11-4/gt-v11-n4-p03-p.pdf) · [Meier–Zupan, *Generalized square knots and homotopy 4-spheres*, JDG **122** (2022), preprint Theorems 1.1–1.2, Corollary 1.3 and §8.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.08527)

The $n=1,2$ Akbulut–Kirby cases are AC trivial. For $n\ge 5$, Shehper et al. prove a shortening from length $2n+7$ to $n+11$, leaving AC triviality unresolved by that shortening. The published Lisitsa article records why the earlier claim of stable triviality for $\operatorname{AK}(3)$ does not follow from the cited intermediate result. These algebraic limitations coexist with the smooth recognition of the corresponding five-dimensional thickenings. [Shehper et al., arXiv:2408.15332v2, Theorem A and §3.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15332v2) · [Lisitsa, *Stable Andrews-Curtis trivialization of AK(3) revisited*, J. Comput. Algebra **16** (2025), 100041, final published abstract](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaca.2025.100041)
