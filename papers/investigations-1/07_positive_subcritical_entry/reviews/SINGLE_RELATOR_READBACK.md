## The two smooth closings

The original oriented boundary \(N=\partial(F\times D^3)\) carries the smooth Weyl-flat metric of the funnel construction. On the pants face it is \(g_F+g_{S^2}\) with curvatures \(-1,+1\). The conformal factor on the funnels equals one near their joins to \(F\), and the tail form \(B^{-2}(dv^2+\sin^2v\,g_{S^2}+\ell^2d\eta^2)\) extends as a smooth round polar metric times a circle across \(v=0\). Weyl-flatness extends there by continuity. All framed tubes and bands lie on the compact pants face, with the radius \(9/8\) at the outer end of each band inside the extended marking of radius \(5/4\). For \(N_i\), start with this \(N\), replace only the interior of \(\Phi_i(S^1\times D^3)\) by its disk cap using \((e^{2\pi it},p)\mapsto\Phi_i(t,p)\), and use the chosen transition on \(U_i\). The signed collar coordinate is \(s=t'-1\) on the cap and framed radius minus one on the exterior. Near \(s=0\), the cap and band share the exact germ \(ds^2+\sinh^2(1+s)d\theta^2+g_{S^2}\). At \(s=1/8\), the band agrees with the exact pants product near the join. The other tube, including its core and the entire region occupied by its band in the two-surgery metric, carries the original smooth metric of \(N\). Equivalently, if starting from the two-surgery metric, undo that cap by the inverse marked surgery and restore the original metric on that band as well. Choose the orientation on each filling extending the retained orientation, as obtained by attaching the corresponding five-dimensional handle to \(F\times D^3\). The identity on \(U_i\) from this closure to the two-surgery metric preserves that orientation and the metric, so it preserves the Hodge star and both sign labels. The cap itself is Weyl-flat. ## Signature and the two energies

The decomposition of \(N\) into \(F\times S^2\) and the three boundary-circle products with \(D^3\) gives \(\chi(N)=(-1)2=-2\), since the added pieces and their common faces have Euler characteristic zero. The inclusion of each common face into its circle-disk piece induces an isomorphism on the fundamental group, so van Kampen gives \(\pi_1(N)\cong\pi_1(F)\). Each surgery removes a piece with Euler characteristic zero and adds a cap with Euler characteristic two, so \(\chi(N_i)=0\). Transversality moves loops and their homotopies in \(N\) off the deleted core, and the core complement retracts onto the exterior of the tube. Adjoining the simply connected cap kills exactly the normal closure of \(r_i\) by van Kampen, because the fundamental group of the attaching face is generated by the longitude with image \(r_i\) in the presurgery group. The exponent-sum rows \((0,-1)\) and \((-1,0)\) give \(b_1(N_i)=1\) in both cases. Connectedness and oriented Poincaré duality give \(b_2(N_i)=\chi(N_i)-2+2b_1(N_i)=0\), hence \(\tau(N_i)=0\). [Relators and framings](../notes/MARKED_CONNECTION_ASSEMBLY.md)

For the coefficient, in an oriented orthonormal frame the skew curvature forms satisfy \(p_1(\Omega)=\frac{1}{4\pi^2}\sum_{a<b}\Omega_{ab}\wedge\Omega_{ab}\). Applying the Hodge star to each two-form converts the sum into the difference of the squared norms of the self-dual and anti-self-dual columns of the curvature operator times the positive volume form. The two off-diagonal blocks are transposes and have equal squared norms. The diagonal blocks are \(W^\pm+(R/12)I\), so trace freeness cancels their scalar cross terms and the scalar identity terms cancel in the difference. This yields \(4\pi^2p_1(\Omega)=(|W^+|_{\rm HS}^2-|W^-|_{\rm HS}^2)dV\). Hirzebruch's closed signature formula gives \(E_+(U_i)-E_-(U_i)=12\pi^2\tau(N_i)=0\). The characteristic form is integrated on the smooth closed \(N_i\). The identity with band integrals uses its pointwise support, so no boundary correction is needed. [Hirzebruch, §4, p. 11, equation (16)](https://hirzebruch.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/84/6/HirzebruchEtal-ProspectsInMathematics-AM70.pdf)

## Strictness on each band

An outer longitude at fixed framed radius and fixed framed sphere point projects to a small constant translate of the corresponding relator path in the pants. Scaling the translation to zero stays within the marked face. The sphere projection contributes no holonomy. Both relators are nonidentity already by their abelianization. The pants double across its geodesic boundary is a closed hyperbolic surface, and the folding retraction injects the pants group into the surface group. Its hyperbolic deck action on \(\mathbb H^2\) is faithful. The map \((x,z,p)\mapsto(x,zp)\) is an injective conformal chart for the product with the unit sphere, so every nonidentity deck isometry acting on the first factor induces a nonidentity Möbius transformation there. Thus the outer longitude has nonidentity conformal holonomy. The marked inner longitude extends in its simply connected Weyl-flat disk cap, where its holonomy is the identity. [Longitude argument](../notes/HYPERBOLIC_EXTERIOR.md) · [Schoen–Yau, pp. 47, 49–50, conformal charts and development](https://math.jhu.edu/~js/Math646/schoen-yau.conformal.pdf)

If the combined integral vanished on \(U_i\), continuity of its nonnegative integrand would make the Weyl tensor vanish throughout that band. The band and its two exact end germs would be locally conformally flat. Its two longitudes are freely homotopic through the framed annulus, so their developing holonomies would be conjugate, contrary to the two values above. This proves \(E_+(U_i)+E_-(U_i)>0\) and makes each equal per-sign cost \(c_i\) strictly positive. In the two-surgery closure the strict energy condition is then \(c_1+c_2<8\pi^2\).
