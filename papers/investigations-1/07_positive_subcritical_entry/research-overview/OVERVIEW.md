## What would establish entry

Fix a closed, connected, oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere $M$. Use smooth Riemannian metrics and the Weyl operators on real self-dual and anti-self-dual two-forms. For either sign write $E_\pm(g;U)=\int_U |W_g^\pm|_{\rm HS}^2\,dV_g$. Signature zero gives equal **closed** energies $e=E_+(g;M)=E_-(g;M)$. Reversing orientation exchanges the signs. The two integrals on an individual piece may differ. Set $T=4\pi^2$. Entry requires a single closed conformal class with $Y(M,[g])>0$ and $e<2T$, equivalently a positive class with $E_++E_-<16\pi^2$. Positivity can be proved by the first eigenvalue of the closed conformal Laplacian $-6\Delta_g+R_g$, with $\Delta=\operatorname{div}\nabla$. Alternatively, a smooth global metric with $R>0$ and $|\operatorname{Ric}_0|^2<R^2/12$ certifies both conclusions. Positive Yamabe sign and positive integrated $\sigma_2$ give such a representative by Chang–Gursky–Yang. The class is chosen before any minimizing sequence. Specify the embedded relator circles and their normal three-plane framings in the four-dimensional attaching boundary. Its simply connected acyclic spine is contractible. The point and circle surgeries on the four-dimensional boundary have codimension at least three and give the existence of a positive-scalar-curvature metric. ## A test on the proposed seam

For a smooth closed two-sided embedded hypersurface with induced metric $h$, trace $H$ and trace-free second fundamental form $B^\circ$, the pinched representative has $R-2\operatorname{Ric}(\nu,\nu)>0$ for every unit normal. Gauss gives $R_h+|B^\circ|^2=R-2\operatorname{Ric}(\nu,\nu)+2H^2/3>0$. For the **given** seam data the sign of the form of $P_{h,B}=-8\Delta_h+R_h+|B^\circ|^2$ is conformally invariant. If $\hat h=v^4h$, then $P_{\hat h,\hat B}u=v^{-5}P_{h,B}(vu)$. Entry therefore requires $\lambda_1(P_{h,B})>0$. Testing on the three-dimensional Yamabe minimizer gives $Y(\Sigma,[h])+(\int_\Sigma |B^\circ|^3\,dA_h)^{2/3}>0$. For constant negative $R_h$, testing on $1$ also requires $\int_\Sigma |B^\circ|^2\,dA_h>-R_h\operatorname{Vol}(h)$. Passing a test leaves both the four-dimensional sign and the connector costs to prove. A totally umbilic seam has positive induced Yamabe class under entry. If it bounds a smooth compact contractible $W$, the classification of positive-scalar-curvature integral homology spheres and the obstruction to bounding a contractible four-manifold imply $\partial W\cong S^3$. This conclusion identifies the boundary only. On the smooth identity double of the marked Mazur manifold $W^-(0,3)$ with face $\Sigma(2,5,7)$, every swap-invariant positive conformal class must have $e\ge8\pi^2$. A swap-invariant representative has totally geodesic seam, and reflection interchanges chiralities. Its closed $e$ equals $E_+(W)+E_-(W)$ on that representative. This rules out the joint reflected positive-sign and strict half-budget test at every collar width. The prescribed Robin sign remains open on its own. An asymmetric or nonumbilic trial requires compatible germs, a positive closed class and new signed costs.
