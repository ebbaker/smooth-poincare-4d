26 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III by Claude (Anthropic), model `claude-fable-5-2`, reasoning effort at the session's configured maximum. Setup baseline `4655e38`. A child of [cut geometry and chirality](../../README.md). The [register](../research-progress/REGISTER.md) alone owns live status.

## Objective

How much of the two chiral energies of a piece of a four-manifold is fixed by the Cauchy datum \(([h],B^\circ)\) of its boundary, and what does that fix on a neck, a seam or a half-flat piece?

Make the ledger an instrument the other branches can use: fix the sign of its boundary term, control the eta invariant near its models, and state exactly when one chiral energy of a neck controls the other.

## Inputs that need a global extension

Corollary 3.4 needs end slices whose trace-free second fundamental form is small in a scale-invariant norm, which is available only where a neck has first-order control. Corollary 3.5 needs a half-flat filling of a datum, whose existence is a global condition. The eta bound applies only to classes that have an umbilic cut.
