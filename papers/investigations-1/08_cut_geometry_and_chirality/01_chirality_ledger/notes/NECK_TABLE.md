## What is measured at the two ends

The metrics are smooth and Riemannian, with orientation-preserving comparisons and chiral Hilbert–Schmidt curvature-operator energies. Orient both slices by the increasing collar normal and apply outward-first at the boundary of the collar, so the initial slice enters with minus sign. The signature of a cylinder is zero because the map from relative to absolute middle cohomology has zero image. Write \(h_j\) for the induced metric, \(B_j^\circ\) for the corresponding trace-free second fundamental form, and \(\mathcal T_j=\int\langle B_j^\circ,\operatorname{curl}_{h_j}B_j^\circ\rangle\). The bound from Theorem 3.1 and Corollary 3.4 (`thm:ledger`, `cor:collar`, lines 230–306 and 363–380) is

\[
 |E_-(C)-E_+(C)|\le 12\pi^2|\eta(h_1)-\eta(h_0)|+|\mathcal T_0|+|\mathcal T_1|.
\]

The estimate uses the odd signature eta on even forms in the product-boundary convention \(\tau=\int p_1/3-\eta\), with \(p_1=(|W^+|^2-|W^-|^2)dV/(4\pi^2)\). The product-boundary hypothesis and the signature convention were checked in [Atiyah–Patodi–Singer I, Theorem 4.14, p. 66](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/aps001.pdf). For the difference on a product-germ interpolating cylinder see [Atiyah–Patodi–Singer II, (4.6)–(4.7), p. 420](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/aps002.pdf).

**Smooth comparison criterion.** On fixed-size end windows in the marked unit cylindrical gauge, suppose the ambient metric differs from \(dt^2+\gamma\) by at most \(\epsilon_j\) in \(C^2\), with \(\gamma\) a fixed unit round metric on the same oriented link at both ends. Restriction gives \(\|h_j-\gamma\|_{C^2}=O(\epsilon_j)\). The unit normal and \(B_j^\circ\) are smooth expressions in the metric and its first derivatives, giving \(\|B_j^\circ\|_{C^1}=O(\epsilon_j)\). Hölder in this gauge gives \(|\mathcal T_j|=O(\epsilon_j^2)\). For \(\eta\), join \(\gamma\) to \(h_j\) by \(ds^2+\gamma+\chi(s)(h_j-\gamma)\) on a fixed unit strip, with \(\chi=0,1\) on end germs. The strip has zero signature, and each Weyl block is \(O(\epsilon_j)\) since the round product is conformally flat. Product APS gives \(12\pi^2|\eta(h_j)-\eta(\gamma)|=O(\epsilon_j^2)\). This works for a fixed round quotient as well, with its own round eta. No estimate on the length between the two end windows is needed. The \(L^3\) norm of \(B^\circ\) is conformally invariant, and the contracted integrated transgression is conformally invariant. The full row-wise curl acquires a derivative-of-conformal-factor term. Use the Hölder bound in one specified gauge rather than transporting its second factor as a conformal invariant.

**Table key.** Yes means the specified original or modified faces have a small error in the ledger. Missing means the quoted estimates do not supply the separate terms in the smooth comparison criterion on the prescribed raw faces. No cut means the source has yet to supply both actual marked faces. A conditional row retains the comparison, embedding, and marking hypotheses of its source.

## Consequences for the weaker comparisons

**One fixed raw face and a splice.** If the fixed splice has raw Weyl masses \(q_\pm=o(1)\) and error \(aK+d^2+d+a=o(1)\), keep the raw metric on a germ of the raw slice and use the exact round product on the other germ. The interpolated substrip is a product topologically. Its eta at the product end is the round value and its transgression there vanishes. The ledger applied to this substrip yields

\[
 \big|12\pi^2(\eta(h_{\rm raw})-\eta(\gamma))+\mathcal T_{\rm raw}\big|
 \le E_+(\mathrm{splice})+E_-(\mathrm{splice})=o(1)
\]

in the slice orientation. This comparison uses the two **individual** new energies, with the cutoff constant on the end germs. If each weak end has such a splice to the same round model, the difference of the combined terms tends to zero, so transfer on the original collar follows. This does not provide the separate bound for either term. See [the interpolation and splice estimate, lines 224–246 and 249–296][03].

**Separate fixed-face estimates from the strong Sobolev route.** On a neighborhood of a fixed smooth face, suppose there is strong ambient \(W^{2,2}\) convergence to a smooth fixed metric in the marking with uniform ellipticity. This is more than the weak cap premise. In the fixed reference frame, the adapted tangential Levi–Civita connection difference \(A\) and the difference of second fundamental forms are smooth coefficient expressions in \(g\) and first derivatives, obtained by orthonormalizing the tangent frame and taking the compatible normal. Those expressions converge strongly in \(W^{1,2}\) on the window. In differentiating them, the principal differences are controlled by \(D^2(g-g_0)\) in \(L^2\), quadratic first derivative differences by \(D(g-g_0)\) in \(L^4\), and coefficients multiplying smooth model terms by \(g-g_0\) in \(L^2\). Uniform ellipticity bounds the coefficient functions and their derivatives on the range of the metrics. Restriction gives strong \(H^{1/2}\) convergence for \(A\) and the second fundamental form in adapted frames.

On the three-dimensional face, \(H^{1/2}\hookrightarrow L^3\). In the Pontryagin transgression for the intrinsic connections, \(\int\operatorname{tr}(A\wedge d_0A)\) is bounded by \(C\|A\|_{H^{1/2}}^2\) by \(H^{-1/2}\) duality. The model curvature term is linear and the cubic term is bounded by \(C\|A\|_3^3\). Product-germ APS identifies the resulting transgression with the eta difference. Likewise \(\sum_i\int B_i^\circ\wedge(d^\nabla B^\circ)_i\) pairs \(B^\circ\) with its distributional reference derivative and has one extra \(L^3\)-cubic connection term. This gives \(\mathcal T\to0\) for an umbilic model without claiming a separate \(L^{3/2}\) bound for the full curl. All metrics in this argument are smooth; the Sobolev estimate bounds their ordinary signed integrals. It is applicable to the interior developed comparison from the differentiated-tension route and to the optional strong \(W^{2,p}\) Dirac hypothesis on interior faces. See [the transgression calculation, lines 255–287][08], [the critical annulus, lines 73–118][C] and [the protected Dirac strip, lines 266–285][D].

**Separate fixed-face estimates from \(C^1\) and pointwise curvature.** Use the normalized physical metric on a fixed compact round cone band once it is identified by the extra radial and marking hypotheses. Its round model faces are umbilic. On each fixed face the ambient \(C^1\) convergence gives \(\|A\|_\infty+\|B^\circ\|_\infty=o(1)\), where \(A\) is the intrinsic Levi–Civita connection difference in bundle-isometric frames, and it also bounds the full second fundamental form and the face area. Gauss and the uniform ambient full-curvature bound imply \(\|\Omega\|_\infty=O(1)\) for the induced connection curvature. Codazzi bounds the symmetric curl of \(B^\circ\) by the ambient curvature. That symmetric part is the one paired with \(B^\circ\), so \(\mathcal T=o(1)\). Write the intrinsic relative Pontryagin transgression as a constant times \(\operatorname{tr}(A\wedge(\Omega+\Omega_0)-A^{\wedge3}/3)\), using \(\Omega=\Omega_0+d_0A+A\wedge A\). Its integral tends to zero by the bounds on \(A\), curvature, and face area. Product-germ APS gives \(\eta(h)-\eta(\gamma)\to0\), where \(\gamma\) is the fixed round face metric at that radius. The scale change between two round cone faces preserves the reference eta. This proves the two estimates before the splice. The proof uses both assembled \(C^1\) convergence and the pointwise curvature premise of [the protected physical window][W].

**Separate estimates when the faces may move.** With \(C^0\) and first derivatives \(L^4\)-small on each of two fixed-width windows and ambient full curvature bounded in \(L^2\) there, Fubini and Markov select one slice in each window where the first derivative error is \(L^4\)-small and the ambient curvature is \(L^2\)-bounded on the slice. Use a fixed smooth model, with round metric and umbilic slices. The first jets bound \(B^\circ\) and the intrinsic connection difference \(A\) in \(L^3\) on the chosen faces. Gauss bounds the intrinsic curvature in \(L^{3/2}\) using the ambient curvature and second fundamental form. Codazzi gives the same bound on the symmetric curl of \(B^\circ\), the part that pairs with \(B^\circ\). Hölder on the intrinsic Pontryagin transgression gives a vanishing eta error, and the contracted curl term also tends to zero. These choices require end windows in the available marking that avoid the complete marks, and a \(W^+\) estimate on the collar with the newly selected ends. A weighted or only bulk bound with no such end windows is insufficient for this selection.

The fixed-face strong Sobolev bound and the movable-face test are derived comparisons for lead review. Both require a marked chart and metric estimates beyond Dirac transmission. For transfer back to 03 and 06, the direct smooth comparison applies wherever the manuscripts provide buffered \(C^2\) control. The protected-window comparison supplies separate estimates on fixed raw faces when its additional marking hypotheses hold. The combined splice error is available on the weak small-curvature cuts after retaining the raw end placement. The two types of marked identification in `lem:mark` of 06 already give both end estimates.
