## After the first horizon

The rows above are the first horizon. The directions below serve the components and are listed in the order in which they are likely to matter; the lead may replace them. A statement marked † was derived while this note was written and has not been read independently: it is a first packet for a Sol reader, not an input.

- **The bound at an arbitrary cut** (L4). † The proof of Corollary 3.3 uses only \(\tau(V)=\tau(V')=0\) and the opposite signs of the ledger on the two sides, so at every cut of a homotopy four-sphere \(e\ge\lvert12\pi^2\eta(\Sigma,[h])+\mathcal T(\Sigma)\rvert\), and with the estimate of Corollary 3.4, \(e\ge12\pi^2\lvert\eta\rvert-\lVert B^\circ\rVert_{L^3}\lVert\operatorname{curl}B^\circ\rVert_{L^{3/2}}\). With L3 this gives, for each link of Section 3 whose round value is at least \(\tfrac23\), an explicit neighbourhood of the round umbilic datum that no class in the band contains, and for the cyclic groups of orders three and four the least energy, \(\tfrac23T\) and \(\tfrac32T\) at the round datum, of a class with such a cut.
- **The ledger along a foliation** (L3). † Theorem 3.1 between two leaves and Proposition 3.2 on each leaf give \(\tfrac{d}{dt}\bigl(12\pi^2\eta(\Sigma_t)+\mathcal T(\Sigma_t)\bigr)=4\int_{\Sigma_t}\langle\mathcal E,\mathcal H\rangle\,\phi\,d\sigma\), with \(\phi\) the lapse and the sign fixed by L1. This is the first variation that LED-E03 needs, and the test set checks it.
- **The estimate that needs no new analysis** (L3). † By Corollary 3.4 a collar \(C\) between two cuts gives \(12\pi^2\lvert\eta(\Sigma_1)-\eta(\Sigma_0)\rvert\le E_+(C)+E_-(C)+\lvert\mathcal T(\Sigma_1)\rvert+\lvert\mathcal T(\Sigma_0)\rvert\). So \(\eta\) is Lipschitz for the conformally invariant distance given by the least Weyl energy of a collar with umbilic ends. LED-B01 decides whether that distance, or a norm of the Cotton tensor, is what the necks of 03 and 06 control.
- **Quotients of left-invariant metrics** (L5). † For a metric on \(S^3\) invariant under a free action of \(\Gamma\), the difference \(\eta(S^3/\Gamma)-\eta(S^3)/\lvert\Gamma\rvert\) does not depend on the metric: up to the factor \(-1/\lvert\Gamma\rvert\) it is the rho invariant of the regular representation, which the second paper of Atiyah, Patodi and Singer shows to be independent of the metric. It equals the round value, because \(\eta\) of the round sphere vanishes. LED-E04 then needs only \(\eta\) on the left-invariant metrics of \(S^3\), which is the computation of LED-E01 with three parameters in place of one.
- **Round space forms beyond left quotients** (L5). The fixed point formula quoted in Section 3 extends to every free linear action. The seams child will ask for the space forms whose round value is below \(\tfrac23\) when it extends the atlas.
- **Half-flat pieces** (L6). This stays reserved. The useful step is to state, in the terms of Remark 3.6, exactly which filling theorem a chirally split class would need, and to send that statement to 03.
