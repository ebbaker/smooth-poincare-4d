## The marked exterior gap

- Let \(X\) be a smooth compact exterior with the rational homology of a circle and connected smooth umbilic boundary \(S^1\times S^2\). If its reflective double has positive Yamabe sign and \(e_X=0\), \(X\cong S^1\times D^3\) as a manifold with boundary. Smooth conformally flat half-charts reflect across the seam and the weak form transfers its positive sign to the smooth reflected class. Bour–Carron, Corollary 1.3(iii), gives the conformal cylinder quotient. Lifts of the seam are great equators times the line. They intersect if distinct, so there is one component and the hemisphere monodromy is a rotation. Ding–Geiges §2, Proposition 2, supplies the boundary-map generators, all of which extend over the exterior, so every smooth \(S^2\times D^2\) filling is standard. The inversion family kills the meridian quotient and both of its homotopy-sphere fillings are standard by the periodic pointed action. Identity monodromy leaves \(C_m\) after filling for odd \(m>1\), so Problem 3 requires the homotopy-sphere or normally-generating-meridian hypothesis. For a noncyclic exterior one has \(Y(DX)^2\le96e_X\). The stronger strict spectral pinching \(36\int_{DX}\max(\lambda_{\max}(W^+),\lambda_{\max}(W^-))^2\,dV<Y(DX)^2\) gives a meridional generator of \(\pi_1(X)=\mathbb Z\) and the unmarked \(DX\cong S^1\times S^3\).
