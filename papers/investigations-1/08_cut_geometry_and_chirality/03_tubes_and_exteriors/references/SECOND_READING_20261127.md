# Sources for the second marked-prism and quaternionic calculations

27 November 2026, prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI assistance. Baseline bc2c1bb. Actual physical and signed-energy comparisons are derived in the branch notes. The following passages identify where outside results enter.

- APS I (4.14) and II (2.12) give the product-boundary signature and round quotient defect. The exact lens lift, corrected orientation and nonproduct transgression are checked in [E35](../notes/E35_COVER_CAP.md), [E46](../reviews/E46_CAP_COVER.md) and [E55](../reviews/E55_RESOLUTION_REPLAY.md). [APS I](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/aps001.pdf) · [APS II](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/aps002.pdf).
- Sung, Theorem 1.4 and §3.3, gives inequalities for compact oriented four-orbifolds with positive Yamabe sign. The positive collar, point group, orientation, Betti numbers and the two vanishing topological coefficients are checked in [E49](../notes/E49_ORBIFOLD_CAP.md) and [E50](../reviews/E50_ORBIFOLD_CAP.md). [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.03808).
- Gromov–Lawson 8.4 and Schoen–Yau's incompressible surface test exclude positive scalar curvature on the closed double of the prism geodesic complement. Nonflatness uses the first Betti number of its actual finite cover. [Exact sources and proof](../reviews/E45_TORUS_END.md).
- Plotnick–Suciu Proposition 5.1, Teragaito's stated list, and the order-seven preprint have different parameter and marking hypotheses. [E39 source table](../notes/E39_PRISM_FILLINGS.md). Mecchia–Seppi describes the single-mirror boundary and tangle model of spherical Seifert orbifolds. The core recognition tracks the particular projected section. [Published source copy](https://arts.units.it/retrieve/handle/11368/2838654/163924/mecchia-seppi-revised.pdf).
- Kronheimer–Mrowka–Ozsváth–Szabó Theorem 1.1 requires orientation-preserving identification with the same-slope unknot surgery in \(S^3\). It checks the conditional inference in E54. The core proof supplies a separate direct identification. [Primary theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0310164).
- Bakas–Sfetsos equations (2.14)–(2.16), Schroers–Singer §1.4 and Atiyah–LeBrun §5.2 fix the elliptic expressions and real as opposed to covered bolts. [E52 and its primary links](../notes/E52_MONOPOLE_ORBIT.md). The conic Fubini–Study orbit metric uses the direct projective calculation cross-checked against Li Example 3.1 in [E42](../reviews/E42_CONIC_REPLAY.md).
