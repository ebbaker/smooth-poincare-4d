# B14 · The Gluck twist on the identified even two-knot

27 November 2026, prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI assistance. Lead settings unavailable. The group obstruction on the original regular torus and the winding-seven test leave the odd smooth type open. The even result gives an embedded two-sphere in a known ambient smooth sphere with an explicit punctured-fibre complement. A self-diffeomorphism of that complement implementing the Gluck map on its boundary would identify the two fillings. Start with a moving orthonormal frame at the puncture and the evaluation sequence for a family of fibre maps. Check whether the twist is a loop derivative or a discrepancy between a path and its image under the marked monodromy. The latter requires retaining central deck elements even when their derivatives agree. E80 and E81 test this restricted approach. If it fails in the bundle category, a general relative handle move is still available. [Even transfer](../../reviews/E76_RECOGNITION_TRANSFER.md) · [cable decision](B13_ORTHOGONAL_CABLING.md).
