## Curvature, energy and the E01 certificate

Use (e^0=ds,e^1=a\,d\psi,e^2=d\vartheta,e^3=\sin\vartheta(d\phi+b\,d\psi)). Put \(H=a'/a\), \(c=\cot\vartheta\), \(v=F\sin\vartheta\), and \(w_1=F'\sin\vartheta\). Then \(de^1=H e^{01}\) and \(de^3=c e^{23}+v e^{01}\). The nonzero upper-triangular Levi-Civita connection forms satisfying \(de^i+\omega^i{}_j\wedge e^j=0\) are

\[
\omega^0{}_1=-H e^1-\tfrac12v e^3,\quad
\omega^0{}_3=-\tfrac12v e^1,\quad
\omega^1{}_3=\tfrac12v e^0,\quad
\omega^2{}_3=-c e^3.
\]

Using \(H'=-K-H^2\) and \(c_\vartheta=-1-c^2\), the curvature matrix in the ordered frame \((01,02,03,12,13,23)\) has diagonal \((K-3v^2/4,0,v^2/4,0,v^2/4,1)\). Its nonzero upper entries at \((01,03),(01,23),(02,13),(03,12)\) are \(-w_1/2,-cv,-cv/2,cv/2\), respectively. The Ricci diagonal is \((K-v^2/2,K-v^2/2,1,1+v^2/2)\) with off-diagonal \(\mathrm{Ric}_{13}=-w_1/2\). This gives \(R=2(K+1)-v^2/2\) and, on squaring the matrix and subtracting \( |\mathrm{Ric}|^2/2-R^2/12\), gives

\[
|W|^2_{\rm HS}=(K+1)^2/3-2(K+1)v^2/3+v^4/3+3c^2v^2+w_1^2/4.
\]

The two angle integrals give \(4\pi^2\), and the volume factor for the remaining integral is \(a\sin\vartheta\). Integrating the five terms against \(\sin\vartheta\) and dividing by two for the mean gives (6.3), including the factor \(\pi^2\). This derives the identities for arbitrary smooth profiles in this ansatz and uses their continuous or one-sided values where a coordinate pole or piecewise smooth jet occurs.

For E01, differentiating \(a=s p(s^2/4)\) gives \(a'=p+2xp_x\) and \(a''/a=(6u+20vx+42kx^2)/(4p)\), with \(u,v,k\) now the three coefficients in the E01 polynomial. The two linear endpoint equations give \(a(2)=A\), \(a'(2)=-A\cos\theta\). The companion \(b'=aF\) integrates to \(b(2)=-1/2\) because \(a\,ds=2p\,dx\) and \(J=\int p(1-x)\,dx\). Also \(b'(2)=0\). These are the signed boundary jets in the fixed product frame. The polar disk smoothness follows from \(a/s=p(s^2/4)\), \(p(0)=1\), and \(b=s^2h(s^2)\) for a polynomial \(h\). The sphere rotation field and its metric dual are smooth at the spherical poles, so the angular cross term extends there.

The interval script uses exact `Fraction` endpoints and encloses every division away from zero. The inverse endpoints, general multiplication, squared-interval form and outward rounding are enclosure operations. The alternating bounds enclose both arctangents and the trig endpoint values. The integration bounds are sums over the full partition of \([0,1]\), with each summand an enclosure of the density for every point in its cell.  \qquad e_{\rm cap}/\pi^2\in[38411/5000,19281/2500].

The interval is for the explicit E01 competitor. The original floating estimate of \(7.6271\) used a different radial profile. Here \(w\) is the E01 conformal factor. For a radial function the divergence formula is \(\Delta_g=a^{-1}\partial_s(a\partial_s)\), which in \(x=s^2/4\) gives \(xw_{xx}+(1+xp_x/p)w_x\). The jets \(w(2)=\sin\theta\) and \(w_s(2)=\sin\theta\cos\theta\) match the exterior \(\sin r\) via \(dr/ds=\sin r\). Thus the cap uses \(w^2g\) and the exterior uses its global product representative, with \(R=6\). In the joining collar, smoothing \(a,b,w\) preserves the inequalities \(a,w>0\), and their second derivatives remain uniformly bounded. Scalar curvature is affine in their second derivatives with uniformly continuous coefficients on the bounded jet set, plus a lower-order term. As a result, it differs from the radial convolution of the two positive one-sided scalar curvatures by a uniform \(o(1)\). On the cutoff transition it converges in \(C^0\) as a curvature expression of metrics converging in \(C^2\). ## Fixed-half-turn model and marked filling

Here is an explicit check of the row geometry. Let \(\Gamma=\langle (z_1,z_2)\mapsto(\omega z_1,\omega^{-1}z_2)\rangle\), with \(\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}\), on the unit sphere in \(\mathbb C^2\), and let \(I(z_1,z_2)=(-z_2,-z_1)\). The maps

\[
U=(z_1^3+z_2^3)/2,\quad V=(z_1^3-z_2^3)/2,\quad W=z_1z_2
\]

identify the quotient of \(\mathbb C^2\) by \(\Gamma\) with \(U^2=V^2+W^3\). Indeed \(z_1^3=U+V\), \(z_2^3=U-V\), and the choices consistent with \(z_1z_2=W\) form one \(\Gamma\)-orbit. Off the origin the same formulas have smooth local inverses modulo \(\Gamma\), using a local cube root of one nonzero coordinate. On each nonzero radial ray, \(|V|^2+|W|^2\) increases strictly from zero to infinity, since the terms scale by the sixth and fourth powers and cannot both vanish. Consequently the link cut out by \(|V|^2+|W|^2=1\) identifies smoothly and equivariantly with the round lens quotient as a manifold. Projection to \((V,W)\) is the double branched cover of the unit three-sphere, with deck involution \(U\mapsto-U\), induced by \(I\). The branch set is \(V^2+W^3=0\), with fixed positive magnitudes and phase windings 3 and 2, hence a trefoil. The spherical displacement of every nontrivial \(\Gamma\) element is \(2\pi/3\). The round injectivity radius is \(\pi/3>8\pi/25\). The descended \(I\) fixes the projection of the great circle \(z_1=-z_2\) and acts by a half turn on its normal plane at a fixed point.

For a smooth classical knot and nonzero twist number, the complement fibres over the circle with punctured cyclic branched cover and canonical deck monodromy. Fibre closures are smooth bounded punctured covers. This matches the \(k=2\) exterior above. The algebraic branched model also fixes an isometric realization of its specific monodromy, which is the geometric premise for the interval certificate.

In the chosen angular framing, the boundary circle at a normal pole fixed by \(I\) is the suspension generator \(t\). The pole can be connected to the center along an \(I\)-fixed radius in the deleted ball. The punctured fibre has group \(\mathbb Z_3\), and its monodromy inverts the generator, so the exterior group is \(\mathbb Z_3\rtimes_{-1}\mathbb Z\). Filling kills \(t\), after which the quotient of \(\mathbb Z_3\) has relation \(r=r^{-1}\) and is trivial. Since the filled smooth manifold has \(\chi=2\), simple connectivity and closed oriented duality make it a homotopy four-sphere. The exterior group also distinguishes its cap core from an unknot. The exterior suspension and the cap weights \((2,1)\) give the effective smooth circle action of parent Proposition 6.2(2) for this marking.
