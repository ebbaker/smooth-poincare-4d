## Geometry and electric acceleration

Use the specified marked prism quotient of the unit quaternions by \((u,z):q\mapsto u q z^{-1}\), \(u\in Q_8,\ z\in C_{17}\) on the complex circle generated by \(i\). Its normalizing isometry lifts as \(q\mapsto b q j^{-1}\) with \(b=(1+i+j+k)/2\). Composing with any deck map changes the left factor to \(ub\) with real part \(\pm1/2\), and changes the right factor to an element with real part zero. The displacement of such a double action is at least the difference of their quaternion angles, here \(\pi/6\). This follows by writing the two factors as \(\cos\theta+\sin\theta\,n\), since the relative real part is maximized by aligning their imaginary axes. Taking \(p j p^{-1}=(i+j+k)/\sqrt3\) gives equality and realizes the great-circle motion \(p\mapsto p\exp(-j\pi/6)\). Thus \(d=\pi/6\) for the marked arc in this construction. The right deck action bounds the injectivity radius above by \(\pi/17\). Conversely the minimum nonzero difference for left deck angles \(0,\pi/2,\pi\) and right deck angles \(2\pi\ell/17,\ 0\le\ell\le8\), is \(\pi/34\). This bounds the injectivity radius below by \(\pi/68\) and makes the original \(R=2/100\) available along the arc. The larger-radius statement below requires the shell to be embedded at every center.

Let \(s(t)\) be the signed displacement along the arc and \(v(t)=s'(t)\), with \(\int_0^1v\,dt=d\) and \(v(0)=v(1)=0\). Take the smooth fixed unit-speed profile \(V\) on the initial shell, agreeing with the unit transvection \(Z\) on the inner germ and vanishing on the outer germ. Transport it by the local round isometries \(I_{s(t)}\) along the great circle, and let \(\Psi_t\) be the actual flow of \(v(t)V_{s(t)}\), where \(V_s=(I_s)_*V\). The physical metric is round on the shell. Its bent inner part is transported isometrically by \(I_s\), and the flow agrees with that isometry there. Pull back by \(\Psi_t\) to get the fixed punctured fibre path \(h_t\). The metric is time-independent in the pulled-back inner bend, and its trace-free Ricci tensor vanishes on the open round region regardless of the time dependence there. Consequently the intrinsic trace-free Ricci tensor and the time curvature terms have disjoint supports. With the E26 notation this gives \(B=\int|M|^2\ge0\).

Write \(k=\operatorname{sym}\nabla V=\tfrac12\mathcal L_Vh_{\rm rd}\) on the initial shell and \(k_s=(I_s)_*k\). For the moving shell \(K=\tfrac12\partial_t h_t=v\,\Psi_t^*k_s\). Since \(\partial_t k_s=-v\,\mathcal L_{Z_s}k_s\) and the pullback derivative adds \(v\,\mathcal L_{V_s}k_s\), the electric tensor
\[
 U=(-\partial_t K+HK)^\circ,\qquad H=\operatorname{tr}K,
\]
equals the pullback from \(-v'k^\circ+v^2U_0\) on the fixed round shell, with
\[
 U_0=\bigl(-\mathcal L_{V-Z}k+(\operatorname{tr}k)k\bigr)^\circ.
\]
All spatial norms, inner products and volume in the fixed shell are the round ones. In the squared integral the cross coefficient is therefore a constant spatial pairing multiplied by \(\int_0^1 v'v^2\,dt=[v^3]_0^1/3=0\). Dropping the nonnegative quartic term and using
\[
 d=\int_0^1(1/2-t)v'(t)\,dt,\qquad
 \int_0^1(v')^2\,dt\ge 12d^2
\]
gives \(C=\tfrac14\int|U|^2\ge3d^2\int|k^\circ|^2\) on the single exterior. Resting values suffice for this integration. Flat endpoint speed germs are available as in the lead's smooth path and ensure its stationary mapping-torus jets.

## Momentum stress and an exact flux bound

Set \(x=2\tan(r/2)\) for Euclidean stereographic radius and \(h_{\rm rd}=\Omega^2\delta\), \(\Omega=(1+x^2/4)^{-1}\). Let \(n\) be the Euclidean unit radial vector, \(P\) the unit displacement at the center, and \(f=n\cdot P\). The Euclidean tensor
\[
 T_{ij}=\frac{3}{2x^2}\{n_iP_j+n_jP_i-\delta_{ij}f+n_i n_j f\}
\]
is trace-free. Its divergence is zero on the annulus: the first and fourth terms have zero divergence using \(\partial_i(x^{-2}n_i)=0\) and radial constancy of \(n,f\); the second and third give \(x^{-3}(P_j-3fn_j)\) and its negative before the constant factor. In three dimensions, direct substitution of the conformal Christoffel symbols gives \(\operatorname{div}_{h_{\rm rd}}(\Omega^{-1}T)=\Omega^{-3}\operatorname{div}_\delta T\) for trace-free \(T\). Thus \(\widetilde T=\Omega^{-1}T\) is trace-free and divergence-free.

For completeness, the transvection in unit polar coordinates has dual form \(f\,dr+\sin r\cos r\,df\). In the sphere model \(q=\cos r\,q_0+\sin r\,n\), the rotation vector is \(\cos r\,P-\sin r\,f q_0\), which pairs with the radial and spherical directions as stated. As a vector in the Euclidean stereographic coordinates, write \(P=fn+w\) with \(w\) tangent to the coordinate sphere. Then
\[
 Z=(1+x^2/4)fn+(1-x^2/4)w,
 \qquad T(n,\cdot)=\frac{3}{2x^2}(2fn+w)^\flat_\delta .
\]
The factors from \(\widetilde T\), the round unit normal and round surface measure cancel. For the normal away from the center the flux is exactly
\[
 \frac32\int_{\mathbb S^2}
       \left[2(1+x^2/4)f^2+(1-x^2/4)(1-f^2)\right]\,d\omega=8\pi.
\]
The sign reverses at the inner face of the shell when the shell's outward normal is used. The flux against \(V\) on the outer face is zero. Integration by parts thus gives \(\left|\int\langle k^\circ,\widetilde T\rangle\right|=8\pi\), with no additional factor from the symmetrization of \(k\).

In the orthogonal splitting into \(n\) and its tangent plane, the tensor inside braces has squared Euclidean norm \(2+4f^2\). Its spherical integral after the prefactor is \(30\pi/x^4\). Write \(x_0=2\tan(r_0/2),\ x_R=2\tan(R/2)\). Including the \(\Omega^{-6}\) squared norm factor and the \(\Omega^3\) volume factor gives
\[
 \int|\widetilde T|_{h_{\rm rd}}^2\,dV_{h_{\rm rd}}
 \le 30\pi\sec^6(R/2)(x_0^{-1}-x_R^{-1})
 \le 30\pi\sec^6(R/2)/x_0 .
\]
Cauchy–Schwarz gives
\[
 \int |k^\circ|^2\,dV_{h_{\rm rd}}
   \ge \frac{32\pi}{15}\,x_0\cos^6(R/2),
 \qquad
 C\ge \frac{32\pi}{5}d^2x_0\cos^6(R/2).
\]
Only the boundary values of the fixed \(V\) were used in the flux bound. In particular the gradient correction of [E30's prescribed test](../research-progress/handoffs/E30_REQUEST_R1.md) has these values, since \(d(\sin r\cos r\,f)+2\sin^2r\,f\,dr=Z^\flat\) on the inner germ.

## Radial mass and the full exterior budget

Let the smooth bend be \(d\ell^2+F(\ell)^2g_{\mathbb S^2}\), with \(F>0\), totally geodesic inner boundary and exact round outer germ at radius \(r_0\). Choose the last zero of \(F'\) before the round outer face, and write \(a>0\) for \(F\) there. The zero exists by the inner and outer derivatives. On the last increasing segment the mass \(\mu=F(1-(F')^2)\) satisfies
\[
 \mu'=\tfrac12 R_h F^2 F'\ge0,\qquad
 \mu_{\rm inner}=a,\qquad
 \mu_{\rm outer}=\sin r_0(1-\cos^2r_0)=\sin^3r_0 .
\]
In particular \(0<F'\le1\) on the open increasing segment and \(a\le\sin^3r_0\). Earlier changes of sign of \(F'\) do not enter.

On this segment use \(F\) as coordinate and set \(k_s=(1-(F')^2)/F^2\), \(k_r=-F''/F\). The two tangential Ricci eigenvalues are \(k_r+k_s\), and the radial one is \(2k_r\). Thus \(A=\tfrac14\int|\operatorname{Ric}_h^\circ|^2\) is at least
\[
 \frac{2\pi}{3}\int F^2(k_r-k_s)^2\,d\ell
 =\frac{\pi}{6}\int_{a}^{\sin r_0}
           \frac{F^4}{F'}\left(\frac{dk_s}{dF}\right)^2\,dF .
\]
Here \(dk_s/dF=2(k_r-k_s)/F\). The endpoints of \(k_s\) are \(1/a^2\) and \(1\). Cauchy–Schwarz bounds this last integral using \(\int_a^{\sin r_0}F' F^{-4}\,dF\le1/(3a^3)\), giving \(A\ge\pi(1-a^2)^2/(2a)\). The shape is fixed up to its transport in time, so time integration on \([0,1]\) leaves this lower bound unchanged.

Write \(A_0=\pi(1-a^2)^2/(2a)\) and \(C_0=(32\pi/5)d^2x_0\cos^6(R/2)\). Since \(B\ge0\), the E26 formula gives
\[
 e_X(L)\ge A_0 L+C_0/L^3\ge\frac4{3^{3/4}}A_0^{3/4}C_0^{1/4}.
\]
The minimum on the right occurs at \(L^4=3C_0/A_0\). With \(d=\pi/6\), the fourth-power comparison is
\[
 \left(\frac{e_X(L)}{8\pi^2/3}\right)^4
 \ \ge\ \frac{(1-a^2)^6\cos^6(R/2)}{240\pi^2}\frac{x_0}{a^3}.
\]
For \(R\le\pi/17<1/5\), \(a\le\sin^3r_0\le r_0^3<1/125\) and \(x_0\ge r_0\). Thus \(x_0/a^3>5^8\). The inequalities \(1-a^2>1-1/15625\), \(\cos(R/2)>199/200\) and Bernoulli give each of the sixth-power factors greater than \(1/2\). Using \(\pi^2<10\), the right side exceeds \(5^8/9600>(5/2)^4\). In particular the exterior exceeds \(8\pi^2/3\) at every base length, even before any sign restriction on \(g_L\).

The comparison agrees with the [lead's derivation](../notes/MARKED_PRISM_17.md) on the norm, flux, support separation, acceleration coefficient and mass inequality. It is an obstruction to a constant-lapse, zero-shift path with unit-round exterior shell, isometrically transported radial bend and one fixed spatial extension moved by the co-moving isometries with resting speeds. The cancellation and acceleration lower bound use those last two hypotheses. Compatible nonresting endpoint speeds, changing spatial shapes, arbitrary exterior metrics and other path constructions remain outside this test. No smooth recognition or homotopy-sphere energy upper bound follows.
