# The Atiyah–Hitchin orbit on the native projective-plane disk

The physical monopole quotient has the required projective-plane bolt, quaternionic principal orbit, short collapsing axis and extending subgroup of order four; its oriented cover has a sphere bolt. The physical rotation period is \(\pi\), quaternionic period \(\pi/2\), and normal arclength slope four. The terms 'Atiyah–Hitchin manifold' and 'double cover' vary by author. [Schroers–Singer §1.4](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10134877/1/2004.02759v2.pdf#page=4) · [Atiyah–LeBrun §5.2](https://scgp.stonybrook.edu/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/Atiyah-Lebrun-Curvature-Cones-and-Characteristic-Numbers.pdf).

Put \(m=k^2,\ P=E-(1-m)K,\ Q=K-E\), where \(K,E\) are complete elliptic integrals. With \(A\) the collapsing length the three products, in a common scale, are \(AB=KQ,\ AC=KP,\ BC=KE\). The first two functions \(Q,P\) are \(m\) times the integrals of respectively \(\sin^2\theta,\cos^2\theta\) against \((1-m\sin^2\theta)^{-1/2}d\theta\), \(0\le\theta\le\pi/2\). Pair complementary angles to get \(Q>P\), and \(E>P\). Thus \(B\) is longest and \(A/C=K/E-1\) strictly increases from zero to infinity. At its unique crossing \(E/K=1/2\), so \(B/A=1/(2m-1)\). [Bakas–Sfetsos (2.16)](https://cds.cern.ch/record/300377/files/9604003.pdf#page=7).

In \((2E-K)/(\pi/2)=1-\sum_{n\ge1}d_nm^n\) the coefficients \(d_n=((2n+1)/(2n-1))(\binom{2n}{n}/4^n)^2\) decrease. For the partial sums \(S_N\), rational evaluation gives \(S_{16}(33/40)+d_{17}(33/40)^{17}/(1-33/40)<499/500\) and \(S_{12}(5/6)>1007/1000\). Hence \(3/2<B/A<20/13<\sqrt3\). No regular orbit has zero shear since \((\log(A/C))'\ne0\). An arbitrary conformal identification of homogeneous face metrics would be a homothety by the constant nonzero Cotton norm on the target and the conformal law in three dimensions. For \(\mu_i=2l_i/(l_jl_k)\), the Ricci difference is \((\mu_i-\mu_j)(\mu_i+\mu_j-\mu_k)\). The second possibility gives two zero Ricci eigenvalues; the target has two repeated negative ones. Thus a matching orbit must have two equal lengths and the required ratio. A new filling based on this metric would have to change the orbit shapes and jets.
