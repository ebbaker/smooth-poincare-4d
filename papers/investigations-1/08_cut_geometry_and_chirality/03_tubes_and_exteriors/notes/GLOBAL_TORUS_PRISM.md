The geodesic has length \(\pi\) in the round fibre. The period cell generated by \((\pi,\pi),(0,2\pi)\) has area \(2\pi^2\). On an embedded annulus write \(h=dr^2+F^2ds^2+G^2d\theta^2\) with \(F=\cos r,G=\sin r\). A transported rigid inner germ moves by \(d=\pi/6\). Smooth longitudinal and angular translations depending on radius and time have mean energy \(AL+B/L+C/L^3\) at base length \(L\). Here \(B\ge0\) because the radial bend is stationary where it is nonround. The round shear contributes a diagonal electric tensor \((-2,1,1)(F^2v_r^2+G^2w_r^2)/3\). Integrating its square and applying Hölder gives
\[
 C\ge {\pi^2 d^4\over3}\left[\int_{r_0}^{R}(\cos^5 r\,\sin r)^{-1/3}dr\right]^{-3}.
\]
The magnetic cross integral vanishes by integration by parts on the actual period lattice. The stationary bend has \(A\ge(8\pi/3)\cot\rho\) even with spatial scalar curvature of either sign. With \(\rho\le r_0<R\le\pi/34\), one gets \(e_X>32>8\pi^2/3\) for every base length. This excludes full round solid-torus support and arbitrary through-running shear profiles of this form. ## A finite test on a spatially changed solid torus

Fix a metric on the untouched geodesic complement \(N\) with an exact flat product torus near its end. The 68-component link lift makes its boundary incompressible. The smooth double of \(N\) admits neither positive scalar curvature nor a flat metric. A long-double eigenfunction argument using the difference of the conformal coefficients eight and six produces a compactly supported negative test on \(N_\infty\). It can be chosen invariant under a static gluing isometry. [Proof and topological hypotheses](../reviews/E45_TORUS_END.md).

Write \(Q_N(u)=\int_N(6|du|^2+R_h u^2)\). Restrict that negative test to \(N\). For an orthonormal flat torus eigenbasis with eigenvalues \(\mu_j\) and boundary coefficients \(b_j\), minimization on the end gives
\[
 \kappa=-Q_N(u)-6\sum_{\mu_j>0}\sqrt{\mu_j}|b_j|^2>0.
\]
The constant mode has infimum zero on the half-line. On a finite collar of length \(S\) extend to zero at the far end. The harmonic modes are linear for the constant and hyperbolic sines for the rest. The total numerator is exactly
\[
 -\kappa+6|b_0|^2/S
 +12\sum_{j>0}{\sqrt{\mu_j}|b_j|^2\over e^{2S\sqrt{\mu_j}}-1}.
\]
It is negative for \(S>6\|b\|^2/\kappa\). A nonpositive trial already rules out strictly positive Yamabe sign, including equality. Extend by zero, take the static time-independent test, and reflect on the actual double. The necessary length depends on the core and torus metric. A moving core or varying end metric needs a separate estimate. For a doubly warped restoration with \(G=\sin r,\ R_h\ge0\), the local maximum used to bound \(\min F\) is the maximum on the interval from the minimizer to \(R\). The weaker positive-double test without spatial scalar sign is in E40. [Independent finite test](../reviews/E53_FINITE_TORUS.md).
