# Local conformal-flatness obstruction on every quaternionic tube marking

27 November 2026 for Edward B. Baker III, OpenAI assistance, lead serving setting unavailable. Input bc2c1bb, [ledger](../../01_chirality_ledger/research-progress/handoffs/TO_TUBE_QUATERNIONIC_MARKING_20261127.md), and the half-chart and Liouville constructions in [E03](E03_EQUALITY.md). This argument does not prescribe a normal circle, a homogeneous metric, or a curvature sign for either side.

**Intrinsic statement.** Every smooth locally conformally flat conformal structure on the quaternionic link \(Q=S^3/Q_8\) has \(|\eta|=3/4\) in the APS signature normalization. For a smooth locally flat conformal atlas in dimension three the transition maps are Möbius by Liouville (the Schouten proof in E03 also works in dimension three). Continue charts along paths in the simply connected universal cover; transition maps are uniquely determined by any open subset, and subdivision of a path homotopy proves independence of its continuation. This gives a local conformal diffeomorphism from the universal cover, a compact three-manifold, into round \(S^3\). Its image is open and closed. The proper local diffeomorphism is a covering, hence a global diffeomorphism since the target is simply connected. Its deck transformations form a finite group of Möbius transformations. Conjugate this group into \(O(4)\): realize the Möbius group as the isometry group of the hyperbolic four-ball, average the finite orbit of a future unit timelike vector in the hyperboloid model and normalize. The average is timelike since the future cone is convex, so the group fixes an interior point. In polar coordinates centered there its action is orthogonal.

The nonidentity central involution in a free orthogonal \(Q_8\) action has no \(+1\) eigenspace, hence is \(-I\). The images \(I,J\) of the quaternion generators are anticommuting orthogonal complex structures. For a unit \(v\), the frame \(v,Iv,Jv,IJv\) is orthonormal, and the action in that frame is the standard four-dimensional quaternionic representation. The conjugating frame may reverse orientation. The round signature eta has magnitude \(3/4\), from the six noncentral group elements with quarter-turn angles and the central zero cotangent term in the APS formula. Conformal invariance preserves this magnitude. [Primary passage recorded by the ledger](../../01_chirality_ledger/references/PRIMARY_20261127.md).

**Consequence for both sides.** A locally conformally flat one-sided collar up to a smooth umbilic \(Q\) gives the induced metric a locally flat conformal structure. In flat coordinates the umbilic face is locally a sphere or a plane by Codazzi. The same conclusion follows if each point of the face has such a one-sided chart. An umbilic \(Q\) in the strict homotopy-sphere band requires \(|\eta|<2/3\). Thus neither side has a Weyl-flat collar of the entire face. On *each* side, *every* collar of the whole seam has strictly positive total Weyl energy. This does not give a uniform positive lower bound independent of the collar width.

There is a second local test. Suppose one side has a smooth positive conformal scale, nonzero up to the entire face, in which the metric is Einstein and half-conformally flat in a collar. The mean principal curvature \(\kappa\) of the umbilic face is constant by contracted Codazzi and \(\operatorname{Ric}(\nu,\cdot)=0\) tangentially. Full Codazzi gives \(R_{\nu abc}=0\), and the Einstein Schouten tensor is a scalar multiple of the metric, so \(W_{\nu abc}=0\). The Hodge star identifies normal-tangent bivectors with tangential bivectors. In the chiral basis the vanishing mixed entries give equality of the two Weyl blocks under that identification. The vanishing of either block therefore gives \(W=0\) on the face. Gauss now gives \(K_\Sigma=R_g/12+\kappa^2\) for every tangent plane, constant on the connected face. The same round eta contradiction applies. In fact it suffices to know the Einstein metric on a collar and vanishing of one Weyl block *on the face*. For a merely half-flat metric Cotton/Ricci data may vary; equality of the Weyl blocks on the face does not in itself show that its intrinsic Cotton tensor vanishes.

This excludes all regular conformally flat one-sided exteriors, and all regular half-flat Einstein one-sided exteriors, for this class of projective-plane seams. A Ricci-flat gravitational-instanton cap would have to break one of the stated hypotheses near the entire face. A singular Einstein scale vanishing on the face is a different boundary problem. Non-locally-conformally-flat intrinsic Berger data passing eta are genuine possibilities, as [E14](E14_SHORT_AXIS.md) shows at the positive-scalar cap level.
