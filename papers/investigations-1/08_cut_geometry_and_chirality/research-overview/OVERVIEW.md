## The recognition question

Let \(M\) be a closed, connected, oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere with a smooth Riemannian metric. With \(W^\pm\) acting on the real chiral two-forms, put \(E_\pm(U)=\int_U|W^\pm|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\), \(e_U=(E_+(U)+E_-(U))/2\), \(e=E_+(M)=E_-(M)\), and \(T=4\pi^2\). An entering class has positive Yamabe sign and \(e<2T\). The Chang–Gursky–Yang representative has \(R>0\) and \(\sigma_2(A)>0\), where \(A=(\mathrm{Ric}-Rg/6)/2\). In this gauge \(P=R-2\mathrm{Ric}(\nu,\nu)>0\). The sphere statement (S) asks whether every smooth type admitting such a class is standard. Universal existence of an entering class is a separate problem in 07 and the master. [Master manuscript](../../../master/manuscript/chiral_weyl_energy_and_smooth_poincare.tex) · [source hypotheses](../references/README.md)

**The first priority is to find a positive-scalar-curvature source mapping with nonzero degree to one of the two original cuts of a reduced mixed handle presentation.** Keep the framed attachments, their normalized windings \((1,0)\), the order of attachment, and the original upper closing. On a presentation with handle counts \((1,1,2,1,1)\), 05's marked recognition theorem identifies \(M\) from such a source on either the winding-one or winding-zero cut. A product collar provides the map by projection. For a disconnected homologous separator, verify the sign on a component whose map has nonzero degree. The existence of a suitable metric band or map for an untreated diagram is open, as is reduction of a general smooth homotopy sphere to these marked presentations. [B01 decision](../brainstorm/notes/B01_MARKED_RECOGNITION_20261127.md) · [05's theorem](../../05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex)

On one fixed entering gauge and smooth isometric Euclidean embedding \(F\), all closed cuts obey \(Y(\Sigma,[h])\ge C(g,F)-\|B^\circ\|_{L^3(h)}^2\). The constant is positive and explicit in the ambient normal curvature and second fundamental form of \(F\). The integrated cubic shear, including the lapse and tangential shift on a specified marked collar, can therefore select a positive slice. In the round conformal class transported to the \(n=1\) Mazur double, **every** embedded copy of the winding-one boundary \(\Sigma(2,5,7)\) obeys \(\int|B^\circ|^3\ge9\sqrt2\,\pi\). On the opposite meridian-first cut of that same known-standard double, the master supplies an embedded positive source and a degree-one map. The final upper level \(S^1\times S^2\) cannot dominate the winding-zero target of first Betti number two. A degree-one map from it to the winding-one homology sphere would itself force the target to be \(S^3\). ## Rigidity at a minimizing class

A **free cut** is a smooth umbilic \(S^3\) whose induced conformal metric is round. If a smooth positive class minimizes \(e\) over the positive classes on its smooth type, has \(e<2T\), and admits a free cut with **both** first-jet-matched hemisphere-capped classes strictly positive, the original class is conformally round. The matched caps have exact side energy at \(C^{1,1}\) regularity. Their positive smooth approximants have convergent energies. A summand with limiting energy below \(T\) is standard, so the other capped side is a competitor on the original smooth type. Minimality gives zero Weyl energy on the removed open side. The Bach-flat equation and unique continuation give global roundness. [Derivation and cited theorems](../notes/MARKED_BAND_AND_MINIMIZER_20261127.md)

For a near-minimizer on a nonstandard type, retain the deficit \(\delta\), the two positive cap signs and the strict sum \(e+c_1+c_2<2T\). The discarded side \(i\) has \(e_{V_i}\le c_j+\delta\), where \(c_j\) is the half-sum cost on the *surviving* side. This gives a second recognition route if a balanced nearly free cut can be produced with both signs and strict savings. Global attainment belongs to 06.
