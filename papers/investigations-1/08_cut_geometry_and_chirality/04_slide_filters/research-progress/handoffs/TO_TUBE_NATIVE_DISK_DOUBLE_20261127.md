Here \(D_K\subset B^4\) is the core of the lower forward \(K\)-handle in the dual picture, properly embedded in the pictured upper ball. With \(W_K=B^4\setminus\operatorname{int}\nu D_K\), its closed boundary is \(S^3_0(K)\). The copy of \(E_K=S^3\setminus\operatorname{int}\nu K\) on that boundary surjects onto its filled boundary group and thence onto \(\pi_1W_K\), by reversal of its 0/1/2 handle structure. The double of the disk has exterior \(X_K=W_K\cup_{E_K}\overline W_K\). Its fold to either half induces an isomorphism of groups, since van Kampen identifies two copies by the same surjection. The physical knot meridian of \(K\) is the core of the zero-surgery solid torus, up to orientation and isotopy in that torus. It is the belt of the removed forward handle, carried past the other reversed attachment. This is exactly the surviving word in the table.

**Closing.** Double the collared product normal disk bundle of \(D_K\) with its reflected copy, matching fibers by the identity. This produces \(S^2\times D^2\); identify \((u,e^{it})\in S^2\times S^1\) to the corresponding normal direction on \(\partial X_K\). In these product coordinates the inverse reads the same base point and normal phase. Its circle factor is the meridian above, and the \(S^2\) factor has trivial \(\pi_1\). This is the original tubular gluing in the doubled \(B^4\), giving the specified standard \(S^4\). No alternative Gluck filling is required.

For each sample take \(\rho:G\to SU(2)\) from its recorded formula, then \(\operatorname{Ad}\rho:G\to SO(3)\) acting on the imaginary quaternions. The projective images are nonabelian as well. This assertion uses the actual generators: the test pairs have nonparallel axes and at least one factor has nonzero real part, ruling out both commutation and anticommutation upstairs. The images give flat bundles on \(X_K\) and the boundary action is \(\operatorname{Ad}\rho(\mu_K)\). If a rotation angle is desired its principal value is \(2\arccos|\operatorname{Re}\rho(\mu_K)|\). The relation sent to \(1\) is the other handle's attaching word. Triviality of that relation does not force trivial meridional holonomy.

This candidate has a different cut from the one used for its non-domination certificate: the one-tube seam is \(S^2\times S^1\), whereas the obstructed cut is \(\partial W_K=S^3_0(K)\). For an immediate split-zero terminal \(A\) with no lower 1-handles the actual exterior has group \(\mathbb Z\): its boundary \(Y_1\) surjects onto it by dual handles, and its first homology is \(\mathbb Z\) by the pair sequence for its tubular splitting in a homotopy sphere. That marking cannot carry any of the listed nonabelian images. A Fuchsian identification, a normal connection of a chosen metric, positivity of the double or an energy estimate would require additional geometric data.
