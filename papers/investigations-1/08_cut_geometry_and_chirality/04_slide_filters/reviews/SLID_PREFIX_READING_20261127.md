## Why a dominated cut forces a primitive word

Let \(Y_j=\#_j(S^1\times S^2)\), with \(Y_0=S^3\). Consider a smooth oriented actual unstabilized rank-two successor of the marked GST reverse diagram, with both framed middle tubes and its endpoint markings carried through the slides. For one chosen upper attaching core \(C\), order the reverse attachments as \(C\) then \(D\), with intermediate level \(Z_C\). Turning the two-handle cobordism around while keeping the marks gives
\[
Y_2\xrightarrow{C}Z_C\xrightarrow{D} S^3,
\qquad
S^3\xrightarrow{D^*}Z_C\xrightarrow{C^*}Y_2 .
\]
The forward prefix in \(S^3\) uses the forward counterpart of the **other** reverse label \(D\). In the full forward link both components are integral. GST Lemma 2.1 identifies the transported forward link as the surgery-dual pair, with the moved and stationary roles interchanged under reversal. Its full linking matrix has cokernel \(H_1(Y_2;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^2\). A two-by-two integral matrix with cokernel of rank two is zero. In particular the ordinary single-handle trace of the actual \(D^*\) from \(D^4\) has zero matrix and boundary \(Z_C\) (up to boundary orientation). [GST, §2, Lemma 2.1 and Figures 3–4](https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-4/gt-v14-n4-p13-s.pdf#page=4) · [05, marked reverse diagram](../../../05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex#L2325)

Here PSC domination means a map of nonzero degree from a closed oriented Riemannian three-manifold of positive scalar curvature. Under this hypothesis, 05's zero-matrix lemma applied with \(j=1\) gives \(Z_C\cong Y_1\). Its compression step uses the *ordinary forward trace* \(V=D^4\cup h^2_{D^*}\). Since \(V\) is simply connected with zero intersection form, \(H_2(\partial V;\mathbb Z)\to H_2(V;\mathbb Z)\) is an isomorphism. The PSC-domination decomposition has one product factor by \(H_1(Z_C;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z\). All spherical factors have trivial first homology. A 3-handle along the product sphere kills the integral generator of \(H_2(V;\mathbb Z)\) and leaves a smooth simply connected acyclic filling of the remaining sum of signed Poincaré homology spheres. That filling is contractible, so the residual sum is \(S^3\) by the smooth boundary obstruction. [05, zero-matrix lemma](../../../05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex#L372) · [Chodosh–Maximo–Mukherjee, arXiv:2407.05574v1, Propositions 2.2 and 4.2 and Corollary 2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574v1)

Now apply the rank-increasing single-knot theorem to **\(C^*\) in the actual \(Z_C\)**. Its 2-handle filling is integral and nonmeridional, and takes \(Y_1\) to \(Y_2\). Meier–Schirmer–Zupan, Theorem 5.1, places the knot in a ball as an unknot and gives the disk framing for the one-rank increase. Under this identification the reverse core \(C\) is the core of the new zero-surgery solid torus. In the resulting \(Y_1\#(S^1\times S^2)\) it crosses a sphere of the new factor once, and its class generates the new cyclic free factor. Its original attaching word \(w_C\) is therefore primitive in \(F_2=\pi_1(Y_2)\), independently of the choice of complete upper belt system, basing or orientation. [05, consecutive-rank surgery lemma](../../../05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex#L711) · [Meier–Schirmer–Zupan, arXiv:1507.06561, Theorem 5.1, preprint pp. 9–10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.06561#page=9)

Thus \(w_C\) nonprimitive excludes PSC domination of \(Z_C\) for every nonzero degree. This uses the same \(C\) for the upper word and the upper-first cut. The converse \(w_C\) primitive \(\Longrightarrow Z_C\cong Y_1\) would require a separate argument. The geometric type \(Z_C\cong Y_1\) already implies PSC domination by the identity.

Meeting an upper belt once implies algebraic primitivity. To export a word obstruction, the proof must rule out the primitive classes themselves. The theorem headlines about geometric cancellations are supplemented here by what their proofs actually exclude.

## When a representation gives a direct certificate

The actual upper partial trace \(U_C\), formed by attaching \(C\) to the upper rank-two 1-handlebody, has \(\pi_1(U_C)=F_2/\langle\!\langle w_C\rangle\!\rangle\). A homomorphism \(\rho:F_2\to SU(2)\) descends to this trace precisely when \(\rho(w_C)=1\). If it descends with nonabelian image, the word cannot be primitive, since killing a primitive element gives an infinite cyclic group. Equivalently, \(\pi_1(Z_C)\to\pi_1(U_C)\) is surjective by the dual decomposition of \(U_C\) relative to \(Z_C\), which starts in index two, so \(Z_C\cong Y_1\) would force cyclic images. The generic filters kill a designated **original** relator and compare values on the moved word to those on its primitive candidate using that quotient. The frozen fourth-band comparison uses a homomorphism on \(F_2\) and a scalar bound on the possible products. These comparisons furnish the algebraic nonprimitivity premise through the inherited proofs. Descent to the actual partial trace requires checking the actual successor relator. [05, generic and frozen fourth-slide arguments](../../../05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex#L2802)

For an entering conformal class on a smooth oriented homotopy four-sphere, with positive Yamabe type and each chiral Hilbert–Schmidt Weyl energy strictly below \(8\pi^2\), Theorem 4.1(2) gives positive scalar curvature on every closed umbilic cut. Such a cut would dominate its own diffeomorphism type by a map of degree \(\pm1\). Hence every marked \(Z_C\) excluded above is ruled out both as an umbilic representative of that level and as the diffeomorphism type of any closed umbilic cut in such a class.
