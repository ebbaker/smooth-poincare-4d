## The two native certificates

Let \(Y_j=\#_j(S^1\times S^2)\), \(Y_0=S^3\), and \(H_j=\natural_j(S^1\times D^3)\). Take the zero-framed GST link \(Q\cup V_n=L_{n,1}\) in \(S^3\), with the indicated \(Y_2\) closing, and \(n\ge3\). Here \(Q\) is the square knot and \(V_n=T_{n,n+1}\#\overline{T}_{n,n+1}\). In the belt basis of \(H_2\), the marked reverse words are \(r=yxy(xyx)^{-1}\) for \(V_n^*\) and \(s=x^{n+1}y^{-n}\) for \(Q^*\). Attaching one handle from above undoes that forward handle and leaves the other forward surgery. **Why the two rows exclude domination.** The lower side is the ordinary zero trace of the first knot. Its one-component linking matrix is zero. Lemma 3.1 of 05 implies that a nonzero-degree map to its boundary \(Z\) from a closed oriented PSC three-manifold forces \(Z\cong Y_1\). In this lemma one compresses the product factors in the PSC decomposition of the boundary of the simply connected comparison trace. The resulting integral homology sphere bounds a smooth contractible four-manifold and the signed Poincaré-summand obstruction eliminates all its nontrivial factors. The upper side \(W=H_2\cup h^2_w\), for \(w=r\) or \(s\), has handles of index 0, 1 and 2. Starting instead at its entire boundary \(Z\), the dual relative handles have indices at least two. Consequently \(\pi_1 Z\twoheadrightarrow\pi_1 W=F_2/\langle\!\langle w\rangle\!\rangle\). If \(Z=Y_1\), every such image is cyclic. A matched nonabelian image gives the contradiction. The forward trace itself has trivial fundamental group. No conclusion about domination follows from an arbitrary nonabelian image of an arbitrary three-manifold group.

In the first row \(p=xyx,\ q=xy\) give \(p^2=q^3\), \(x=q^{-1}p,\ y=p^{-1}q^2\). Quotienting by the common power yields \(C_2*C_3\). For the second row the corresponding quotient is \(C_{n+1}*C_n\). Both are nonabelian for every \(n\ge3\). Thus both native cuts fail domination throughout this family. In the two explicit \(n=3\) braid samples \(p=i\) and \(q\) has nonzero \(j\)-component. In the other three \(x=(1-i)/\sqrt2\) and \(y\) has nonzero \(j\)-component. The displayed pairs do not commute. The detailed [representation table](REPRESENTATIONS_AND_EXTERIORS_20261127.md) records these as exact identities. An independent topological check uses the fact that each native cut is a bundle over the circle with fiber a closed surface of genus \(2\) or \(n(n-1)\). Its universal cover is contractible, excluding PSC domination by the three-dimensional domination theorem.

The two braid samples apply on the \(Q\)-first side for every \(n\ge3\). For samples 1, 3 and 4 the generator axes are distinct, so \(x^{n+1}=\pm y^n\) forces both powers into the center. The individual conditions are \(4\mid n+1\) and \(3\mid n\). For \(n=3+12t\) the signs are \((-1)^{1+3t}\) and \((-1)^{1+4t}\). The recorded \(SU(2)\) samples therefore factor on the \(V_n\)-first side exactly at \(n\equiv3\pmod{24}\); their \(SO(3)\) projections factor exactly at \(n\equiv3\pmod{12}\). This describes the fixed samples. Changing lifts is a separate choice. In particular the actual rank-two \(L_{4,1}\) carries the two braid samples on its \(Q\)-first side. The all-\(n\) topological obstruction above remains valid on both cuts.

## Transfer through an actual reverse slide

At an actual unstabilized successor in this rank-two trace, write \(Y_2\xrightarrow{C} Z_C\xrightarrow{D} S^3\) for its two reverse surgeries. Read it forward, transporting the two dual markings. Its two-component integral surgery matrix in \(S^3\) vanishes because its cokernel is \(\mathbb Z^2\). Thus \(Z_C\) is the ordinary zero surgery on \(D^*\). If it is PSC-dominated, Lemma 3.1 of 05 gives \(Z_C=Y_1\). The remaining forward surgery on \(C^*\) now takes \(Y_1\) to \(Y_2\). Meier–Schirmer–Zupan, Theorem 5.1, makes \(C^*\) a zero-framed unknot in a ball. Its dual \(C\) meets the new \(S^2\) factor once, hence its word in \(F_2\) is primitive, also in the original belt basis. This proves **nonprimitive upper word \(\Rightarrow\) non-PSC-dominated cut after that upper attachment**. The implication uses a physical framed successor and its opposite forward knot. A signed history by itself specifies no three-manifold. A filter may compare the image of a successor word to that of the unique candidate primitive conjugacy class and prove they differ. This does not require the filter representation to kill the successor word. Only the native table (or a separately verified factorization) puts that representation on the actual four-dimensional side. The proof uses the previously checked word exclusions of 05; this reading performs no enumeration. ## Exterior and curvature consequences

For either native component \(K=Q,V_n\), GST §8 identifies its proper slice disk in their pictured \(B^4\). In the forward handle coordinates this disk is the first handle core; removing its normal handle leaves the other dual handle attached to \(H_2\). Thus \(W=B^4\setminus\operatorname{int}\nu D_K\) (with the boundary collar understood) is the **marked upper side** of its row. Write \(E_K=S^3\setminus\operatorname{int}\nu K\). Its inclusion in the zero filling \(Z_K\) surjects on \(\pi_1\), as the added solid torus adds only a slope relation; the inclusion of \(Z_K\) in \(W\) also surjects. Double the proper disk across its boundary. The resulting smooth sphere in \(S^4\) has trivial oriented normal bundle, and its one-tube exterior is \(X_K=W\cup_{E_K}\overline W\). The mirror is glued by identity. By van Kampen the group is \(G*_{\pi_1 E_K}G=G\), since the two maps are the same epimorphism under reflection. Folding identifies it with precisely \(G_r\) or \(G_s\). A small normal circle to the doubled disk is the original knot meridian, represented by the surviving reverse belt word up to conjugacy and inversion. This realizes the flat holonomies on named exteriors with seam \(S^2\times S^1\). The seam differs from \(\partial W=S^3_0(K)\). The construction requires no assignment of a metric, of a Fuchsian quotient or of an energy to \(X_K\). [GST §8, p. 2334](https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-4/gt-v14-n4-p13-s.pdf#page=30)

On any smooth oriented homotopy four-sphere, let \([g]\) have positive Yamabe type with \(E_+=E_-=e<8\pi^2\) in the Hilbert–Schmidt curvature-operator convention. For a two-sided umbilic hypersurface, Theorem 4.1 of 08 gives a metric of positive scalar curvature on it. Indeed, in the entering gauge \(R_\Sigma=R-2\operatorname{Ric}(\nu,\nu)+2H^2/3-|B^\circ|^2\) with \(R-2\operatorname{Ric}(\nu,\nu)>0\). A PSC metric would supply the identity map of degree one. Hence each excluded topological cut type is absent as a closed umbilic hypersurface of **every** such class, regardless of the embedding; existence of an entering class with a given handle foliation is a separate matter. Independently, every smooth compact product band on an excluded \(Z\), with intrinsic end distance \(d\) and \(\rho=\min R_g>0\), satisfies \(\rho d^2<3\pi^2\) by Räde's Corollary 2.25 at dimension four, scaling by \(\rho/12\).
