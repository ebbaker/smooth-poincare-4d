## Conventions and recognition implications

Write $Y_j=\#_j(S^1\times S^2)$, $H_j=\natural_j(S^1\times D^3)$, $A_j=\natural_j(S^2\times D^2)$, and $F_j=\pi_1Y_j$. A cut is the boundary of the actual lower sublevel, oriented by its outward normal. The upper side has the opposite induced boundary orientation and is read from above using the actual closing $\gamma$ and each outgoing face $o_i$, with reversed tube $\gamma o_i\tau$ where $\tau$ interchanges the disk and circle factors. An outgoing comparison $P$ transports tubes to $Pt$ and the fixed closing to $\gamma P^{-1}$, with inverse $P\gamma^{-1}$. Reordering the two fillings and reading the trace in reverse are different operations. [05 manuscript, §1 and equations labeled `eq:dual`, `eq:reglue`](../../../05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex#L81)

PSC domination means a nonzero-degree map from a closed oriented Riemannian three-manifold of positive scalar curvature. When a collection of oriented separator components projects to the cut with total degree one, some component projects with nonzero degree. For connected closed oriented targets the prime factors permitted by PSC domination are spherical space forms and $S^1\times S^2$. The resulting target admits PSC, with the reverse implication given by its identity map of degree one. For a smooth compact contractible filling, the spherical-sum obstruction forces $S^3$ on the boundary if the boundary admits PSC. The subsequent smooth identification of a particular filling requires its handle hypotheses. [Chodosh–Maximo–Mukherjee, Proposition 2.2, Corollary 2.3 and Proposition 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574#page=4) · [Gromov–Lawson connected-sum input, 05 source receipt](../../../../../references/SOURCE_NOTES_COLLAR_SURGERY_20261126.md)

For an ordinary integral zero-matrix trace on $D^4$, `lem:zero` forces $Y_j$ from PSC domination. The $j$ reducing spheres of the $S^1\times S^2$ summands map to an integral basis of $H_2$ of the trace by the pair sequence. Adding auxiliary 3-handles along these spheres leaves a compact contractible filling of the remaining homology-sphere sum. The same proof applies to a simply connected 0/1/2 sublevel with zero form. This step gives a boundary diffeomorphism. The pertinent marked surgery, cancellation, or gluing argument is needed for the smooth piece. ## Terminal pairs with inlet $Y_1$ and outlet $Y_3$

Take a smoothly marked integral pair $Q,K$ in $Y_1$ with actual joint surgery $Y_3$. For a one-sided terminal closure start with the zero-framed unknot $A$ on $D^4$ and end with the actual $H_3$. Let $Z_J$ be the native cut after $A$ and just $J$, with $J=Q$ or $J=K$. The joint ordinary three-component matrix in $S^3$ vanishes since its cokernel is $\mathbb Z^3$. Each actual lower trace at $Z_J$ is simply connected, with zero form and $H_1(Z_J)=\mathbb Z^2$. Its upper side has group $F_3/\langle\!\langle d_{\mathrm{other}}\rangle\!\rangle$ for the reverse core of the other component. The boundary surjects onto that side group. Consequently PSC domination at $Z_J$ is equivalent to $Z_J\cong Y_2$, and equivalently $J$ is the disk-framed local unknot by the rank-increasing surgery theorem. A different lower $H_1$ body can supply the same marked inlet in a mixed presentation. Then the dotted-to-zero replacement preserves those individual boundary surgeries and the cut test, while the actual lower group stays $\mathbb Z$. The joint outlet has boundary group $F_3$. [05 manuscript, zero-matrix lemma and §5](../../../05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex#L872) · [Meier–Schirmer–Zupan, Theorem 5.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.06561#page=9)

If $J$ is local, $Z_J=Y_1\#S^3_0(J)$ and $\pi_1 Z_J=\mathbb Z *\pi_1(S^3_0(J))$. The degree-one pinch to the zero surgery gives a second argument that local nontrivial knots fail the PSC test. In the stationary-filled cobordism alone, the incoming boundary group is $G*\langle t\rangle$, with $G=\pi_1 S^3_0(Q)$, and adding $K$ gives $(G*\langle t\rangle)/\langle\!\langle K\rangle\!\rangle \cong F_3/\langle\!\langle d_K\rangle\!\rangle$. The incoming boundary group, the latter single-attachment group, and the full simply connected lower trace group enter different assertions. A quotient used to detect a capped return word in $G$ can be pulled back by the fold of this free product to $G$, which kills the $\mathbb Z$ factor. For the Figure 5 lower return the recorded affine quotient to $A_4$ detects the nonidentity return in $G$. This is a marked return obstruction. The separate zero-trace criterion and Property R exclude the stationary-first cut. ## Native GST rank two and the doubled-product control

The marked native GST diagram is $L_{n,1}=Q\cup V_n$ for $n\ge3$, with $V_n=T_{n,n+1}\#\overline{T}_{n,n+1}$ and joint outlet $Y_2$. The basis $x,y$ belongs to the actual upper $H_2$ given by Figure 9, transported through the bracketed ambient Kirby moves in Figures 10–13. Its two reverse attaching words are $r=yxy(xyx)^{-1}$ for the dual of $V_n$ and $s=x^{n+1}y^{-n}$ for the dual of $Q$, up to conjugacy and orientation. Reversing one of the two surgeries from the joint outlet retains the surgery on the other forward component. ## Geometric consequence and audit boundary

For any row where the actual closed oriented cut fails PSC domination, that diffeomorphism type cannot occur as a closed umbilic hypersurface of a smooth positive-Yamabe conformal class on an oriented homotopy four-sphere with $E_+=E_-<8\pi^2$ in the parent's chiral Hilbert–Schmidt norm. Umbilicity survives changing to the entering gauge, whose induced metric on that hypersurface has positive scalar curvature, giving degree one by the identity. For a certified marked winding-one mixed cut the proof of 08 Corollary 4.4 uses the actual smooth cut data in the mixed theorem. A successful PSC test for another cut gives the consequence in its own row. Marked product bands and conformal comparisons on physical source disks in 05 have their own premises. A chosen physical disk has spherical boundary by its parametrization. Its metric comparison and full costs require the specified embedding and do not furnish a comparison to a native one-relator cut.
