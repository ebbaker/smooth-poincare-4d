**GEO-B01.** Test the unique maximal-area principal orbit, perturbed into both sides before starting ordinary mean curvature flow. The falsifiable claim is that the pointwise entering condition excludes a codimension-two collapse in this family. The round join is a smooth test. If the event occurs, first change the cut to a meridian of the circle action. Then use levels of the length of its Killing field times an angular coordinate function. The input is a specified action and a choice of angular phase. On a generic entering gauge finding a replacement for that input is open.

**GEO-B02.** Test the missing first eigenvalue both by balancing against the Jacobi ground state and by building the opposite sign in a homogeneous collar. Then bring in the genuinely global least-area property, first on the join using the angular sweepout. A second negative direction in the area Hessian would contradict index one. The conformal Laplacian and the Jacobi operator have different potentials, so the existence of that direction has to be tested.

**GEO-B03.** The principal-orbit claim already fails on the round join. Replace the starting surface by an unconstrained minimizer for a prescribed-mean-curvature functional. Test separately an independently marked band, a small ball, and the same orbit family with explicit forcing. The stop test is a positive cut and the fate of the subsequent ordinary flow in the example.
