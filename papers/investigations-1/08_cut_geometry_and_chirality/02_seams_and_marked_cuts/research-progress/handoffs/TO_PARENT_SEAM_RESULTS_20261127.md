**§4.** For one fixed smooth entering gauge and isometric Euclidean embedding, set \(p=\min(R-2\mathrm{Ric}(\nu,\nu))\), \(K=\sup_{P^3}|\mathrm{tr}_{P^3}\mathrm{II}_F|\), \(m\ge2\) the Euclidean codimension of the cut and \(b_m=3[(3+m)\omega_{3+m}/(m\omega_m)]^{1/3}\). Every closed cut satisfies \(Y(\Sigma,[h])\ge C-\|B^\circ\|_3^2\) with \(C=b_m^2/\max(2,K^2/p)>0\). This gives the winding-one corollary under \(\|B^\circ\|_3^2<C\) for *some representative of its diffeomorphism type*. Existence of such a cut is a separate research target. **§5, abstract and §6 opening.** The atlas applies to PSC connected sums with free rank \(k\). In an entering class with both positive doubles, \(k=0\) gives two rational homology balls. \(k=1\) gives complementary rational \(S^2\)- and \(S^1\)-type sides, with the \(4T/3\) lower and \(2T/3\) strict upper bound. No \(k\ge2\) occurs. Restrict the pure-seam and tube assertions to the specified marked models. The double Yamabe sign is the first Robin sign for \(L=-6\Delta+R\), \(3\partial_\nu+H_{\rm out}\). For a double of group order \(d\) one also has \(e_V\ge T(\chi(V)-1/d)\), with reciprocal zero for an infinite group. A single spherical prime leaves the sphere and quaternionic candidates. A nontrivial PSC integral homology-sphere seam requires \(119T/60\le e<2T\). Keep limits of reflected classes distinct from smooth attained equality. **§8.** For matched smooth sides, the cap costs \(c_i\) are half-sums and smooth closed capped energies tend to \(e_i+c_i\). A free first jet alone supplies no exact smooth energy identity. With both capped signs positive and \(e+c_1+c_2<2T\), one capped sphere is standard. If both individual sums are below \(T\), the ambient sphere is standard. If the ambient type is nonstandard and \(e\le I(M)+\delta\), removing the standard capped side \(i\) gives \(e_i\le c_j+\delta\), with the cap cost from the surviving side \(j\), and the corresponding chiral inequalities.
