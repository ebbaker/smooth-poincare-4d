For a reduced mixed \((1,1,2,1,1)\) presentation normalized by actual slides to winding row \((1,0)\), let \(Z_a\) be its winding-one cut with the other attaching data and closing from that same presentation. In a smooth entering conformal class it suffices that some closed hypersurface diffeomorphic to \(Z_a\) have \(\|B^\circ\|_3^2<c(g,F)\). Its induced conformal class is positive, the identity followed by the diffeomorphism gives PSC domination of the marked cut, and 05's mixed recognition theorem yields \(Z_a\cong S^3\), \(M\cong S^4\). Umbilicity is the zero case. A different embedded representative of that diffeomorphism type is allowed. The \(Z_b\) winding-zero cut and the two native GST zero-surgery cuts have different tests. The strict positive-double atlas excludes \(Y_k,\ k\ge2\), and the mixed winding-zero split with both halves \(b_1>0\) uses the two side bounds directly.
