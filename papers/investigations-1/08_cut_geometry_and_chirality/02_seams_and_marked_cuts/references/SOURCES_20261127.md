The incoming branch handoffs were live working files, some outside Git at the common HEAD. Their digests identify the inspected versions. Local source details, exact statements, editions, smoothness and normalization are given at the point of use below.

- The [uniform bending derivation](../notes/E05_UNIFORM_BENDING.md) records Brendle, Theorem 1, equation (2) for trace mean curvature, and Nash, Part C, Theorem 2. The former is a Euclidean submanifold inequality in arbitrary codimension at least two. The compact isometric embedding and the norm of its second fundamental form are for one ambient gauge. The lead also inspected the displayed general-codimension inequality. [Brendle, arXiv:1907.09446v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.09446) · [Nash, printed p. 59](https://mccuan.math.gatech.edu/courses/6455/nash55.pdf#page=40)
- The [atlas derivation](../notes/E03_DERIVATION.md) and [independent reading](../reviews/E08A_SELECTION_ATLAS.md) give the two Betti-number inequalities and the smooth equality cases with their actual receipts. Gursky's 1998 original full text was unavailable in this session. Sung's direct proof and the separately checked CGY implication for the first Betti number supply the dependencies used here. [Sung, Theorems 1.2 and 1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.03808v1)
- Chang, Gursky and Yang, positive-Ricci Corollary B and §8, and their sphere Theorem A and formula (0.4), are used only on smooth closed positive classes. The four-index square is four times the full operator square. Hillman, Theorem 5.2(1), assumes a whole embedded finite-group three-manifold in an integral homology four-sphere. The direct proof for the refined homology-sphere bound, source for corrections terms of a homology ball, and distinction from CMM's contractible filling are in [the cover review](../reviews/E08B_FINITE_COVERS.md). [CGY 2002](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0409583) · [CGY 2003](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1007/s10240-003-0017-z.pdf) · [Hillman, §5](https://www.maths.usyd.edu.au/u/jonh/embkDec24.pdf) · [CMM, Corollary 2.3 and Proposition 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574)
- The [matched cap](../notes/E02_CAP.md), [Robin and descent note](../notes/E04_ROBIN_DESCENT.md) and [analysis reading](../reviews/E07B_ANALYSIS_READBACK.md) give the AHS blocks, the closed Yamabe theorem, Cerf boundary marking and the flat-development argument. The Robin constants and the cap upper bound are derived there. Published closed pinching results are applied after controlled smoothing with strict margins.
- The independent [quaternionic and lens reading](../reviews/E08C_REALIZATION.md) gives the APS signature defect on the *extending* cover, the smooth product equality test and Zeeman's actual twist-spin charts. Their roles differ from the intrinsic quaternionic metric calculation delivered by the ledger branch. [Zeeman, pp. 486–493](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/zeemantwist.pdf)
