Fix a smooth entering gauge \(g\) on the compact oriented homotopy four-sphere and a smooth isometric embedding \(F:(M,g)\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^{3+m}\), \(m\ge2\). Let \(p=\min_{SM}(R-2\mathrm{Ric}(\nu,\nu))>0\) and \(K=\sup_{x,P^3\subset T_xM}|\mathrm{tr}_{P^3}\mathrm{II}_{F}|\). With the unit ball volumes \(\omega_j\), set
\[
 c(g,F)=\frac{9[(m+3)\omega_{m+3}/(m\omega_m)]^{2/3}}
                  {\max\{2,K^2/p\}} .
\]
For every closed two-sided cut \(Y\), \(Y(Y,[h])\ge c(g,F)-\|B^\circ\|_{L^3(Y)}^2\), using \(Y(\cdot,[h])\) for the intrinsic Yamabe constant here. Thus any smooth cut diffeomorphic to the actual winding-one \(Z_a\) of the reduced mixed presentation with winding row \((1,0)\) and strict \(\|B^\circ\|_3^2<c(g,F)\) gives \(Z_a\cong S^3\) and \(M\cong S^4\), by the 05 working theorem. The number may be evaluated once in a fixed representative/embedding, after which the invariant bending of a cut may be measured in any representative. On a hypothetical nonstandard mixed type with an entering class, the infimum of the squared bending over *all* smooth embeddings of this diffeomorphism type in the class is at least \(c(g,F)\). More generally this obstruction holds for every type lacking PSC domination. No existence of a nearly umbilic embedded cut, and no common numerical constant over varying classes, follows.
