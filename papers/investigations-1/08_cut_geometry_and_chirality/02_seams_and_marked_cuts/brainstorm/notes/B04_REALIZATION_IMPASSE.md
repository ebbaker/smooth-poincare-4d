**One remaining statement.** For each specified smooth marked splitting of a homotopy four-sphere along the PSC list which survives the necessary tests and is not an established control, determine whether
\(\inf_{[h]}(F_V(h)+F_{V'}(\phi_*h))<2T\).
The \(k=0\) row includes an individual quaternionic prime and the surviving reducible spherical sums; its halves are rational balls. For the pure integral homology-sphere subcase an embedded non-spherical PSC cut must be a balanced sum of oppositely oriented Poincaré spheres with the narrow E06 energy window. The \(k=1\) row includes \(S^1\times S^2\) with or without spherical factors and each actual marked pair of sides. Topological realization of one pair for the pure \(S^3\) and pure \(S^1\times S^2\) rows does not realize arbitrary fillings of those same boundaries. Free rank two or higher is already excluded. The two independent boundary-value infima in this statement have no interior compatibility condition. The equivalence and smoothing proof make the missing input precise, without solving its inequality.

**Considered but not used to claim a classification.** A Biquard or conformally compact Einstein solution with one boundary metric would still need the indicated compact filling, a matching umbilic jet, the Robin sign and a strict pair of energies. Capping reducible primes independently would require embedded compression disks, actual gluing marks and a controlled total energy. Instantly replacing a separating prime sphere by a smooth four-dimensional ball assumes precisely such extra data. Torsion correction terms and lattice obstructions can eliminate more *specified* embeddings but do not construct a metric on the known quaternionic disk pair. Turning the triaxial disk problem into a full variational shooting campaign belongs to the tube investigation; a request specifies the actual short axes. No classification of arbitrary smooth fillings is assumed.

**Reopening evidence.** One explicit compact filling on this marked projective-plane bundle with the verified Robin sign and half-sum below \(T\) would prove existence for the exhibited gluing. A lower bound for every competitor at that marking would close that example. For exclusion of a whole topological row the bound must hold for every matching \([h]\) and every filling in the stated row. **Reader's additional routes and their disposition, 27 November.** The [independent B04 review](../../reviews/E08C_REALIZATION.md) proposed three further tests. The extending degree-two quaternionic cover has \(\eta(\widetilde h)-2\eta(h)=1\) offset by its relative signature, so the lifted energy bound repeats the original one. Smooth equality in the \(2T/3\) bound for the projective disk is excluded by the fixed hypersurface in the round product equality model; this does not raise the infimum or exclude the full interval \([2T/3,T)\). The sharpened finite-order argument excludes order 60 for a finite perfect homology-ball group, giving the stronger \(119T/60\) bound in the integral case. The lens-pair alternative was tried as [B05](B05_LENS_PAIR_TEST.md) and [E09A](../../notes/E09A_LENS_METRIC.md), [E09B](../../notes/E09B_LENS_MARKING.md). It gives positive low-cost individual pieces. A product connector and flat parallel transport do not realize the kernel change; a nonflat connection on the two unit round half-disks costs more than the saved energy. A reparametrization by a time-dependent side diffeomorphism applied to the *full* product metric, including mixed terms, transports both ends and is still isometric to the original marked metric with transported boundary maps. A truly varying metric on the punctured-lens exterior or in the cap changes the curvature problem. The fixed common-boundary variational test (1) remains open beyond these classes.

**Disposition of the altered lens trial.** The [conformal marking computation](../../reviews/E08C_CONFORMAL_MARKING.md) excludes the exact untwisted boundary models even if the matching moves the geometric connector. The [flux comparison](../../reviews/E10_LENS_FLUX.md) and [independent proof](../../reviews/E11B_LENS_OBSTRUCTION.md) test unequal positive constant curvatures and allow general smooth \(SO(3)\) connections on the unit sphere fibres in the marked isometric connection model. Each closed chiral energy is at least \(501T/250>2T\). Thus the additional independently proposed construction and its curved-connection repair have both been tried. Removing the constant-curvature or round-fibre assumption changes the cost functional; positive examples would still have to satisfy the same marked criterion (1).

**Incoming short-axis certificate.** TUBE has now provided a smooth Robin-positive short-axis filling at \(h_{\sqrt3}\). Its chiral energies are enclosed in \([5.0412,5.0586]\pi^2\) and \([10.0406,10.0592]\pi^2\), and the half-sum in \([7.5423,7.5575]\pi^2\). The direct trial establishes finiteness and positivity but overspends. Its data sharpen the next step to reducing this smaller block on a different profile or proving a lower bound.
