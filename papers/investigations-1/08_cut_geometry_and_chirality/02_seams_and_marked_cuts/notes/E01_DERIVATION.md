## Gauss and the marked implication

Let $M$ be a closed connected smooth oriented homotopy four-sphere with a smooth Riemannian class. Set $E_\pm(U)=\int_U|W^\pm|_{\rm HS}^2\,dV$ using the real chiral two-form operators, $T=4\pi^2$, $e=E_+(M)=E_-(M)$, and $e_V=(E_+(V)+E_-(V))/2$. For an entering class $Y(M,[g])>0$ and $e<2T$. With $Z=\operatorname{Ric}-Rg/4$ and $A=(\operatorname{Ric}-Rg/6)/2$, Chern–Gauss–Bonnet yields $\int_M\sigma_2(A)=T-e/2>0$. Chang–Gursky–Yang use $2A$, so the integrated sign agrees. Their Theorem A and Corollary B give a smooth conformal representative with $P(\nu)=R-2\operatorname{Ric}(\nu,\nu)>0$ for every unit $\nu$. In parent conventions the pinching is $R>0, |Z|^2<R^2/12$, which gives the same inequality since $|Z(\nu,\nu)|\le(\sqrt3/2)|Z|$. [Chang–Gursky–Yang, equation (0.2), Theorem A, Corollary B and Lemma 1.1, pp. 710–715](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0409583#page=2)

On a smooth closed connected two-sided hypersurface $\Sigma$ use $B(X,Y)=g(\nabla_X\nu,Y)$, $H=\operatorname{tr}_h B$, $B^\circ=B-Hh/3$ and $\Delta=\operatorname{div}\nabla$. The contracted Gauss equation and $|B|^2=|B^\circ|^2+H^2/3$ give

$$
R_h+|B^\circ|^2=P(\nu)+\tfrac23H^2.
$$

For $\hat g=b^2g$ with $b>0$, one has $\hat B=b(B+\nu(\log b)h)$ and $\hat B^\circ=bB^\circ$. Writing $v=b^{1/2}|_\Sigma$, the induced change is $\hat h=v^4h$ and $|\hat B^\circ|_{\hat h}^2=v^{-4}|B^\circ|_h^2$. It follows that the quadratic forms for $P_{h,B}=-8\Delta_h+R_h+|B^\circ|^2$ are conjugate by $u\mapsto vu$, since $P_{\hat h,\hat B}u=v^{-5}P_{h,B}(vu)$. Compactness and the strictly positive potential in the entering gauge give $\lambda_1(P_{h,B})>0$ for the prescribed data. At $B^\circ=0$, this gives a PSC representative of the induced class. For $B^\circ\ne0$, the bending term is part of the necessary spectral test. The separate sufficient condition for intrinsic positivity in parent Theorem 4.1(3) bounds $\|B^\circ\|_{L^3}^2$ strictly below the Sobolev quotient formed with $P+2H^2/3$ in the entering gauge. Indeed Hölder bounds the negative term by $\|B^\circ\|_{L^3}^2\|u\|_{L^6}^2$.

The normalized row $(1,0)$ refers to signed intersections after actual framed slides and reorientations. $Z_a$ attaches the winding-one component first and $Z_b$ the winding-zero component first. The proof carries the actual framing and the closing maps.

### Side-by-side constraint on any entering direct trial

Before the two surgeries, the two hemispheres of the sphere factor give the two sides of the retained $\partial(F\times D^2)$. The enlarged tube at latitude $\pi/3$ and the one at $2\pi/3$ lie on opposite sides, since $5/64<\pi/6$. Each surgery deletes an interior $S^1\times D^3$ and adds $D^2\times S^2$, increasing $\chi$ by two. Deleting the core circle preserves $\pi_1$ by general position.
