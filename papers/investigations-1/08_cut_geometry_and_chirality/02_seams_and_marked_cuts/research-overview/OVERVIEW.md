## Objective

Which levels of a handle presentation can be seams of an entering class, and what does a seam, or a cut that is nearly one, recognize?

Turn the exact statements about umbilic cuts into selection rules that 05 and 07 can use as stated, and decide whether they have quantitative forms for cuts that are only nearly umbilic.

## Inputs that need a global extension

A generic class has no umbilic cut, so every exact statement applies only to assembled classes, invariant classes and round-neck limits. The atlas assumes that both doubled classes have positive Yamabe type. Proposition 8.1 assumes that both capped classes do.

## Place in the route to the conjecture

This child carries two alternatives of the [parent route](../../research-overview/OVERVIEW.md#the-proof-target): recognition from a single marked cut that is nearly umbilic, and descent at a minimizer with a quantitative capping cost. It also returns the selection rules to 07 and master B02. If the capping cost cannot be bounded, the umbilic statements stay exact statements about assembled and invariant classes and the quantitative lane is closed; the atlas and its extension are unaffected.
