## Exact family and claim

Each filling is a product of a punctured \(L(3,1)\) with an interval, with the product semicollar of the disk cap attached. Its boundary conformal structure consists of two copies of one round punctured lens model \(P=L(3,1)\setminus B(r_0)\), joined by the round product \(S^2\times[0,L]\). The angular coordinates at the two ends are those of the same puncture on the same round model, as supplied by the product marking of the filling. The attaching rotations for an exact product trial can differ from the displayed coordinates by one constant rotation of the sphere throughout the joining arc. This leaves the relative identification of the two end angular coordinates unchanged. Radius \(r_0<\pi/3\) and product length \(L>0\) may differ between fillings. Smooth radial adjustments retaining the indicated boundary conformal structure are included.

For any conformal diffeomorphism between two such boundaries, the induced automorphism on the two order-three free factors uses the same sign on both, after the allowed permutation and conjugation of factor groups. This does not require the diffeomorphism to preserve the geometric connector or either punctured lens face. Consequently gluing the two fillings by a conformal diffeomorphism leaves \(\mathbb Z/3\) as the closed fundamental group. The exact pair therefore cannot realize a homotopy four-sphere with common boundary conformal class.

## Developing holonomy of the two factors

Write the unit lens metric as the quotient of \(S^3\subset\mathbb C^2\) by the generator \(A(z)=e^{2\pi i/3}z\). Fix the same cyclic generator on both copies of \(P\), so they map to the same generator of the filling. A local round chart about the puncture center \(x_0\) uses stereographic coordinates from its antipode, with radius \(R=\tan(r/2)\) where \(r\) is the round distance to \(x_0\). Multiplication of the round metric by \(\csc^2r\) in the puncture annulus gives \(d(\log R)^2+g_{S^2}\). Across the connector the two model radii therefore satisfy \(R_A R_B=R_0^2e^{-L}\), with identical angular coordinates and \(R_0=\tan(r_0/2)\). This follows from \(R_A=R_0e^{-s}\), \(R_B=R_0e^{s-L}\) in the developed connector coordinate \(s\in[0,L]\), continuing each formula from its own collar. In a common development the holonomy of the two free factors is thus generated by \(A\) and \(MAM^{-1}\), using the same stereographic chart for each unreflected round lens model, where
\[
 M(y)=c\,\frac{y}{|y|^2},
 \qquad c=R_0^2e^{-L}\in(0,1).
\]
The relation uses the actual connector and works with different values of \(c\) on the two fillings. It may be recovered directly by analytic continuation from a connector chart into each lifted punctured round lens. Liouville uniqueness of development implies that a conformal diffeomorphism conjugates the holonomies by a Möbius transformation, where its induced group map enters on the target. This uses no global injectivity of a developing map. [Schoen–Yau, *Conformally flat manifolds, Kleinian groups and scalar curvature*, pp. 47, 49–50](https://math.jhu.edu/~js/Math646/schoen-yau.conformal.pdf)

Regard the Möbius transformations as isometries of \(\mathbb H^4\). In the ball model the isometry \(A\) is rotation by the indicated Clifford multiplication on \(\mathbb R^4\), fixing precisely the center since there is no nonzero fixed vector. In the upper half-space chart \((y,u)\in\mathbb R^3\times(0,\infty)\), the center is \(p_1=(0,1)\) and the extension of \(M\) is \((y,u)\mapsto c(y,u)/(|y|^2+u^2)\). It carries \(p_1\) to \(p_2=(0,c)\), the unique center of the second cyclic group. The axis joining the centers is vertical. Its oriented unit tangent \(e\), pointing from \(p_1\) to \(p_2\), defines a parallel vector field between the centers. For each factor define \(J_i\) by
\[
 dA_i|_{p_i}=\cos(2\pi/3)I+\sin(2\pi/3)J_i.
\]
Both \(J_i\) are orthogonal complex structures. The inverse generator changes \(J_i\) to \(-J_i\). Let \(v_i=J_i e|_{p_i}\). The two vectors lie in the transverse tangent spaces to the common axis. The differential of \(M\) in the orthonormal frame obtained by multiplying the Euclidean coordinate frame by \(u\) preserves the transverse frame and reverses the axial vector. The transverse frame is parallel along the axis for the metric \(u^{-2}(dy^2+du^2)\). Since \(J_2=dM\,J_1\,dM^{-1}\), this gives \(v_2=-v_1\) under parallel identification. The Möbius extension used here allows either orientation. One can verify directly that translations, rotations, dilations and the stated inversion preserve the upper half-space metric and induce the corresponding boundary maps. [Marden, *Hyperbolic Manifolds*, Chapter 1, pp. 5–6](https://assets.cambridge.org/97811071/16740/excerpt/9781107116740_excerpt.pdf)

## Group normalization and conjugacy

Let \(G=A_0*B_0\) with both factors cyclic of order three. Every element of order three is conjugate into a factor, since a cyclically reduced word of at least two syllables has powers of unbounded lengths. An automorphism must send the two factors to conjugates of distinct factors, as follows on abelianization. After a single inner conjugation and with target factors ordered as needed, the first image is \(A_0\) and the second has form \(wB_0w^{-1}\). Choose \(w\) shortest in its double coset \(A_0wB_0\), which is possible by using a further inner adjustment by an element of \(A_0\). If \(w\) is nonempty, it starts in \(B_0\) and ends in \(A_0\). Any reduced alternating word from the two image factors that uses the second factor expands without cancellations across the inserted \(w\) and \(w^{-1}\). Such an expanded word contains at least \(2|w|+1\) syllables. It cannot equal any nontrivial element of \(B_0\), of syllable length one. Words using only the first factor also miss those elements. Surjectivity forces \(w=1\). Thus up to overall inner conjugation the only choices are the permutation and signs \(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2\in\{-1,1\}\) on the product-marked generators. This argument only uses abstract free product normal form.

A conformal boundary diffeomorphism gives an automorphism of these free products. Adjust its holonomy conjugator by the target holonomy of the corresponding inner conjugation. The resulting hyperbolic isometry \(U\) takes \(p_i\) to \(p'_{\sigma(i)}\), where \(\sigma\) is the normalized permutation and primes refer to the other filling. Differentiating the intertwining relation gives \(dU\,J_i=\epsilon_i J'_{\sigma(i)}dU\) at each center. With \(e'\) oriented from \(p'_1\) to \(p'_2\), the axis derivative is \(dU(e)=\delta e'\) at both centers, where \(\delta=1\) for no permutation and \(-1\) for a swap. An isometry preserves parallel transport, so its transverse derivatives agree under the two source and target parallel identifications. In common transverse frames the two equations are
\[
 Dv_1=\delta\epsilon_1 v'_{\sigma(1)},\qquad
 Dv_2=\delta\epsilon_2 v'_{\sigma(2)} .
\]
The relations \(v_2=-v_1\) and \(v'_{\sigma(2)}=-v'_{\sigma(1)}\) force \(\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2\). This calculation does not require that the boundary map geometrically fix the decomposition into faces.

For the gluing pushout each boundary factor maps to the same chosen generator on its own interval filling. Conjugation of factors is immaterial in the cyclic targets. With the two signs equal, the two attaching relations both identify the same powers of the two side generators, giving \(\mathbb Z/3\). The marked two-twist-spin gluing has trivial ambient fundamental group and must require different signs. Therefore no conformal identification of these two exact product trial boundaries realizes it.

## Scope

The opposing parallel Hopf axes are part of the conformal structure produced by one fixed punctured round model and a single product transport on each filling. A genuine relative change of the two end geometries or transport could produce transverse vectors that are not oppositely aligned. The sign argument then needs new data and does not give an obstruction in general. Pulling an exact product metric back by varying coordinate rotations leaves its intrinsic conformal structure and this conclusion unchanged. If one only prescribes separate end metrics and a round connector on the abstract three-manifold, nonaligned vectors are possible in this holonomy calculation. A new four-dimensional filling estimate would be needed to realize that datum. The supplement therefore closes the exact product pair with arbitrary conformal gluing while retaining the wider common-class infimum in B04.
