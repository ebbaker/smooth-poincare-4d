# Smooth Poincaré investigations — series 1

1. [Chiral metric evolution](01_chiral_metric_evolution/README.md) studies curvature reconstruction and metric evolution from definite connections.
2. [Bach flow](02_bach_flow/README.md) explores metric evolution toward vanishing positive Weyl curvature.
3. [First-order twistor constructions](03_first_order_twistor/README.md) provides shared foundations and two separately planned sub-investigations: [sections and connections](03_first_order_twistor/01_sections_and_connections/README.md), and [Dirac operators and twistor spinors](03_first_order_twistor/02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/README.md).
4. [Hypersurface immersions](04_hypersurface_immersions/README.md) links [immersed handle constructions](04_hypersurface_immersions/01_immersed_handle_constructions/README.md) with [two-handle recognition](04_hypersurface_immersions/02_two_handle_recognition/README.md).
5. [Collar deformation and surgery](05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/README.md) pairs [deformation and adapted coordinates](05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/01_collar_deformation_and_adapted_coordinates/README.md) with [handle isolation and marked surgery](05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/README.md). The [revised plan](05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/research-plan/PLAN.md) prioritizes a reverse band and upper belt on an untreated irreducible-cut pair, or an actual unresolved rank-two closing.
6. [Bour's flow and fixed-type attainment](06_bour_flow_and_fixed_type_attainment/README.md) gives a working proof of Bach-flat attainment on each strictly entered smooth type. The [flow estimates](06_bour_flow_and_fixed_type_attainment/01_regularized_flow_and_energy/README.md) and [marked limits](06_bour_flow_and_fixed_type_attainment/02_marked_limits_and_attainment/README.md) have separate proof obligations.
7. [Positive subcritical entry](07_positive_subcritical_entry/README.md) seeks a first positive class below the strict Weyl threshold on a marked smooth type. Its [first assignment](07_positive_subcritical_entry/continuation-notes/CONTINUATION.md) is the balanced-presentation boundary and a non-product cap.
8. [Cut geometry and chirality](08_cut_geometry_and_chirality/README.md) studies the separating hypersurfaces of classes in the band: a ledger for the two chiral energies, positivity and selection rules for seams, the closing of one tube, and recognition by a sweepout. It is a parent with five high-level child branches and an initial [manuscript](08_cut_geometry_and_chirality/manuscript/README.md); set up by a Claude session at the author's request.

The earlier generalized-isothermal and double-copy studies are separately housed in [initial-investigations](../initial-investigations/README.md). They are not members of this direct-conjecture series.

Use each task family's sole live register; the twistor parent owns shared tasks and links its two child registers. This index does not duplicate task status. Future investigations follow [the setup workflow](../../INVESTIGATION_SETUP_WORKFLOW.md).
