- Atiyah–Hitchin–Singer, Theorem 4.1 and the horizontal/vertical
  description on pp. 435–436, supply the metric twistor integrability
  test after the chirality translation. The same source on p. 460
  records Lichnerowicz vanishing for harmonic spinors on a compact
  spin manifold with positive scalar curvature. Earlier uses of the
  holomorphic first-jet construction are recorded separately from the
  real metric setting. [Real twistor source record](../../../../references/SOURCE_NOTES_CME_REAL_TWISTOR_20261122.md)
- Gover–Hill–Nurowski, Remark 5.2, Proposition 5.3, and equations
  (14)–(20) in arXiv:0911.3364v3, supply the null sectional Weyl
  component and quartic. Friedrich, equation (1.1) and pp. 61–62,
  supplies the Riemannian twistor spinor equation and the curvature
  of its prolongation. [Canonical first-order source records](../../../../references/SOURCE_NOTES_BACH_FLOW_20261123.md#first-order-twistor-discussion--23-november-2026)
- Fine's definitions fix the reconstruction convention and the
  perfect-connection comparison. The companion manuscript supplies
  the compatibility algebra, chiral curvature block, and round
  transverse-traceless Hessian. [Fine source record](../../../../references/SOURCE_NOTES_CME_SPECIAL_CASES_20261122.md) ·
  [Companion review checkpoint](../../01_chiral_metric_evolution/reviews/FINAL_REVIEW_CHECKPOINT_DISPOSITION_20261123.md)
- The signature normalization and conformal developing argument
  give exact recognition at $W^+=0$. Chang–Gursky–Yang, Theorem A,
  identifies the smooth type under the positive Yamabe and strict
  Weyl-energy conditions. [Recognition sources](../../../../references/SOURCE_NOTES_BACH_FLOW_20261123.md#inherited-background-and-recognition)
