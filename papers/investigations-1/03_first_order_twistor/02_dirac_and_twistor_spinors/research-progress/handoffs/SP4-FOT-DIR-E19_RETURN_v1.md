and all seven frozen working-tree digests matched. On the supplied
smooth Riemannian homotopy four-sphere, a first eigenmap has nonzero
constant differential energy. A rank-one open set would locally factor
through an arclength-parametrized great circle. Scalar unique
continuation would put the whole image in that circle, and the global
angle lift would be harmonic and hence constant. For a great two-sphere,
the rank-two locus is therefore open dense. The Euler class of the
oriented plane splitting excludes rank two everywhere. Thus both
ranks one and two occur.

A submersion to a great three-sphere would give a nowhere-zero tangent
field from its oriented line kernel. A degree-zero map to the full
four-sphere must have a rank defect somewhere, and whenever rank four
occurs its signed Jacobian takes both signs. Surjectivity forces rank
four somewhere by an elementary coordinate-volume estimate. For the
two-sphere the pullback area form is closed, exact, decomposable and
nonzero on the rank-two locus. Its two Hodge components have equal
pointwise norm. Exactness together with this nonvanishing excludes
coclosedness and harmonicity.
