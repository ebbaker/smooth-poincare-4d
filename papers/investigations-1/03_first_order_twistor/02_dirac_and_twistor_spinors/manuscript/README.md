dimension four](dirac_twistor_and_yamabe_rigidity.tex), by Edward B. Baker
III, develops the analytical results of this investigation and the
remaining construction and compactness problems. Section 11 gives the marked-source seam correction, curvature of actual Green fields and physical-volume tests. Section 12 gives the open questions. The
[draft history](../DRAFT_HISTORY.md) gives its input and evidence scope.
