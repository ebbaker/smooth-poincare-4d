## One proof check

In a smooth closed oriented Riemannian spin four-manifold with
\(Y([g])\ge y_0>0\), suppose a single oriented embedded spherical collar
contains \([-2,L+2]\times S^3\) with \(L\ge6\). The cuts are at \(0,L\).
Supply a positive smooth conformal factor on the collar for which the
metric differs from \(dt^2+g_{S^3(1)}\) by a uniformly small
\(L^\infty\) tensor and a uniformly small first derivative in the
reference \(L^4\) norm on slabs of length one to two. Work in the same
fixed spin gauge on both sides of each cut. On actual cut pieces, test
existence, uniqueness and estimates for arbitrary pairs of
\(H^{1/2}\) jumps of \(H^1\) Dirac-harmonic spinors. Either chirality is
allowed.

Use two fixed one-sided trace lifts, an auxiliary smooth conformal
representative of bounded total volume agreeing with the collar gauge
on two fixed neighborhoods, and one closed Dirac inverse with
\(L^2\) gap from the Yamabe bound. Invert only the piecewise \(L^2\)
forcing of the lifts. Check a local \(H^1\) estimate by cutoff and
absorption at both seams and the transfer to the original metric by
the harmonic weights. Allow a supplied interior Dirac pole and
identify which correction and transversality estimates follow. Track
all parameters in the reference norms and specify the missing
information for global normalization or quantitative control in a
different metric gauge.

Completion is the stated two-jump inverse with estimates in the
proposed local norms, or the first obstruction in this class. A failed
conformal or trace premise reopens MATCH-B02. Assigned outputs are the
[derivation](../../notes/E07_GLOBAL_JUMP_SOLVER_20261125.md) and
[return](E07_RETURN_20261125.md). Shared registers and plans remain
with the lead.
