The completion test is met for the smooth unit round product of every
length \(L\ge2\). With \(A\) defined by left multiplication of \(D^+\)
by \(c(+\partial_t)^{-1}\), use \(P_+u(0),P_-u(L)\) as prescribed data and
\(\|g\|_{\mathcal T}=\||A|^{1/2}g\|_2\) as the trace norm. Within these
conventions, the inhomogeneous problem is bijective and
\[
 \|u\|_{H^1}^2+\|P_-u(0)\|_{\mathcal T}^2+\|P_+u(L)\|_{\mathcal T}^2
 \le \|L_0u\|_2^2+\|P_+u(0)\|_{\mathcal T}^2+\|P_-u(L)\|_{\mathcal T}^2.
\]
The \(H^1\) here is the product covariant norm. The proof uses mode
integration, the spherical gap \(3/2\) and the link scalar curvature
\(6\). It gives the actual full Hilbert adjoint
\(-\partial_t+A\) with \(P_-v(0)=P_+v(L)=0\); reflection gives the
same inverse constant and surjectivity. The explicit unit-strip lift
has \(H^1\) operator norm at most \(\sqrt{17}/3\) and \(L_0R\) has
norm at most \(\sqrt{2}/3\).
