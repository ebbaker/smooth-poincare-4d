## Result for lead assessment

The smooth gapped varying-slice criterion is sufficient. With
\(P_+(0)u(0)=P_-(L)u(L)=0\), the operator \(D=\partial_t+A_t\) has a
bijective realization from the homogeneous \(H^1\) domain onto \(L^2\).
The formal-adjoint identity changes both the endpoint sign and the
\(\dot A_t\) sign. The weak adjoint is \(H^1\) by the
Bär–Ballmann APS realization with inward adapted operator
\(A_0\oplus(-A_L)\). Its domain prescribes \(P_-(0)v(0)=P_+(L)v(L)=0\).
The two energy estimates then give closed range and zero cokernel.
The same closed-range argument for the adjoint uses \(\ker D=0\),
so the adjoint is onto and has the same \(L^2\to H^1\) bound.

If \(C_g\) is the uniform bound in \(\|w\|_{H^1}\le C_g\|A_tw\|_2\),
the inverse norm is at most \(C_*=\max(1,C_g/\sqrt{1-\theta})\).
For an \(H^1\to L^2\) remainder of norm \(b\) with \(bC_*<1\), the bound
is \(C_* /(1-bC_*)\) on the same domain. Fixed-width trace lifting gives
inhomogeneous incoming data bounds with an additional uniform upper
bound on the slice \(H^1\to L^2\) norm.

The shifted round Dirac crossing block has a kernel in the negative-to-positive
direction and a cokernel in the reverse direction. In contrast, the warped
metric with \(r(t)=2+\tfrac12\sin t\) has an actual unitary Dirac normal form
with \(B=0\), gap \(\kappa=3/5\) and \(\theta\le1/3\) uniformly in length.
The proof and tests used no E01 result.
