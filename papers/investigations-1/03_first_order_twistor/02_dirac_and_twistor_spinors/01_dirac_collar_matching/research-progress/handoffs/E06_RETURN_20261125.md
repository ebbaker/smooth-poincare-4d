**Conditional graph theorem.** For each supplied exact round product
germ of width at least two, assume its conformally round regular
filling gives a closed spin conformal class with \(Y\ge y_0>0\).
A conformal representative that preserves a fixed seam neighborhood
and has bounded volume has a uniform closed Dirac \(L^2\) gap by
\(\lambda^2\ge Y/(3\sqrt{\operatorname{Vol}})\). The core-side
negative-mode lift on the left has compactly supported forcing.
Correct it with this closed inverse. The correction has only
positive trace on that seam because it extends to the regular
filling. Local product elliptic and trace estimates then give the
bounded true left graph \(G_L:H_-\to H_+\) in \(H^{1/2}\). Extending
positive homogeneous trace over the regular cap and using closed
invertibility proves uniqueness. The right graph is
\(G_R:H_+\to H_-\) with the same convention.

**Matching consequence.** Across a source-free exact cylinder of
length \(L\), the two true graphs give a return operator of norm
at most \(\|G_L\|\|G_R\|e^{-3L}\). A uniform margin below one
gives matching and a cylinder \(H^1\) bound linear in the actual
affine shifts. The shift from a fixed pole is the outgoing
projection of a particular pole trace minus the graph applied
to its prescribed projection. A particular Green field from the
closed capped comparison already has zero prescribed projection.
The proof uses its actual weighted boundary trace.
