For one supplied coherent embedded physical annulus with a buffered
radial cutoff \(\eta\), the verified bound is
\[
 E_+(\mathrm{protected})\le C\left[
   a\int_{\mathrm{buffered}}|B|\,dV + (aK+d^2+d_T+a)^2\right].
\]
Here \(a=\|v\|_\infty\), \(K=\|\eta^2\mathrm{Rm}\|_2\),
\(d=\|\eta Dv\|_4\), and \(d_T\) is the same derivative norm
restricted to the two cutoff transitions. The constant is
independent of both radii and the length. The proof combines
the SEC §14 Euler variation with NECK-E07's constant principal
all-lowered curvature formula. It differentiates the moving
chiral projection and density and estimates their variation
with full curvature. It needs no coordinate Hessian bound.
The same argument applies on a fixed free oriented spherical
quotient with the stated upstairs and downstairs norms.

If the metric errors vanish, \(K\) is bounded and the source
Bach norm tends to zero, the protected energy tends to zero.
In fact \(a\int|B|\to0\) suffices. The ratio of the squared
projected derivative to the squared field norm integrates
on the nonzero set to at most half the protected energy,
uniformly over fields. No spinor floor is needed for this
ratio. The full residual needs a coherent chart covering it
or a cover whose estimates sum to zero. The E10 example has
Bach norm of order \(a^{-1}\); it therefore falls outside
these hypotheses. SEC-B36/E62 still owns extraction of the
required charts.
