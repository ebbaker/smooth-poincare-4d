## B01 decision and inputs

Test the gapped, slowly varying self-adjoint tangential family with
instantaneous endpoint projections and a small lower-order perturbation.
The fixed domain prescription, energy method, regularity question and pass
criterion make this an E packet. [B01](../../research-plan/PLAN.md#sp4-fot-match-b01)
· [E03](../../research-plan/PLAN.md#sp4-fot-match-e03) · [Live register](../REGISTER.md)

Use a supplied smooth oriented Riemannian spin collar \([0,L]\times S^3\),
\(L\ge 2\), with the product orientation and a smooth unitary identification of
the slice \(L^2\) spaces. Identify the chiral Dirac target with the source by
inverse normal Clifford multiplication by \(+\partial_t\) in normal form.
In the resulting fixed Hermitian bundle and measure, write \(D=\partial_t+A_t\)
and \(D+B\) for the operator with remainder. The family \(A_t\) is smooth,
formally and operator self-adjoint, first-order elliptic of Dirac type with
common domain \(H^1(S^3)\), uniform tangential graph and coefficient bounds,
and \(\|A_t w\|_2\ge\kappa\|w\|_2\) for \(\kappa>0\). Require
\[
 |\langle w,\dot A_t w\rangle|\le\theta\|A_t w\|_2^2,
 \qquad 0\le\theta<1 ,
\]
for every \(w\in H^1(S^3)\) and \(t\in[0,L]\). Here \(B\) is lower order and
small in \(H^1([0,L]\times S^3)\to L^2([0,L]\times S^3)\) in the fixed gauge.
Prescribe \(P_+(0)u(0)\) and \(P_-(L)u(L)\) with \(P_\pm(t)\) the strict
spectral projections of \(A_t\). Study the zero-data domain for the inverse.
