charts. The estimate uses the chart weights on the physical Bach and

\(O(L_j^{-3/2}d_{T,j}^2+a_j^2/L_j)\). A uniform unit-slab

\(O((\epsilon_j^2+a_j^2)/L_j)\). The bound also includes the
bulk sum \(\sum_j\|\eta_j Dv_j\|_4^4\) and the actual uncovered
chiral energy.

cutoff cost tending to zero the bulk sum can still stay positive.

summability in those coordinates.
