The completion test is met for the specified round-conformal standard
\(S^4\) with a regular flat core ball and a flat opposite cap ball
with one prescribed Green pole \(\eta\). Match in the common
cylindrical gauge with normal \(+\partial_t\) at both cuts and the
E01 trace norm \(\mathcal T\). The actual trace spaces are
\(P_-\mathcal T\) from the core and \(p_\eta+P_+\mathcal T\)
from the cap, where \(p_\eta\in\ker(A+3/2)\) is the pole trace.
The incoming quotient-coordinate matching map is the identity.
For arbitrary seam discrepancies the linear correction in the four
trace parameters has norm bounded by \(1+e^{-3L/2}\).
The trace spaces are realized by the regular half-cylinder mode
extensions transported back to the balls.

The unique matched field is \(u(t)=e^{-3(L-t)/2}p_\eta\).
With \(y=aSx/r^2\), the flat cap radius is
\(\rho_L=a e^{-L}\). In the cylindrical gauge,
\[
 |p_\eta|=\rho_L^{-3/2}|\eta|/\omega_3,\qquad
 |u(L/2)|=e^{-3L/4}|p_\eta|
          =a^{-3/2}e^{3L/4}|\eta|/\omega_3,\qquad \omega_3=2\pi^2 .
\]
The midpoint decays for a fixed incoming trace or for a
fixed flat cap radius with fixed \(|\eta|\). With a fixed
coordinate scale \(a\) and fixed \(|\eta|\) it grows in this
gauge as the flat cap radius decreases.

The field is a constant parallel spinor in the flat \(x\) gauge.
In the cylindrical harmonic gauge
\(|P_{h_0}u|=\sqrt3|u|\), so the fourth-power relative defect
integrates to \(9\omega_3 L\) for a nonzero pole. The distinct
twistor transport \(r^{-2}u\) is exact and extends in the flat cap
frame as \(-c_y(y)\eta/(a^2\omega_3)\), with a transverse zero.
This is the exact conformally flat benchmark. Its transversality
and field normalization formulas do not establish either property
for Cauchy spaces on nonspherical interiors.
