# Dirac matching on a supplied collar

Prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance,
25 November 2026 (America/New_York). The exact serving model and
reasoning effort are unavailable.

How little geometry does a supplied surgery collar need for Dirac
transmission estimates with constants independent of its length? The
first setting is a smooth Riemannian spin collar with spherical sections.
The initial test uses a round cylinder as a reference and controls only
the metric and its first derivatives in a normalized gauge. A further
direction uses a spectral condition on the cross-sections.

- [Brainstorm on relaxing the collar hypotheses](brainstorm/notes/RELAXING_COLLAR_HYPOTHESES_20261125.md)
  gives the model estimate, its proposed extensions, and the requirements
  for matching actual cap data and controlling Weyl energy.
- [Dirac manuscript](../manuscript/dirac_twistor_and_yamabe_rigidity.tex)
  supplies the first-order comparison in §3.3, the cap-pole construction
  in §4.1, and the curvature identity and its limitations in §§2.3 and 4.4.
- [Neck geometry](../../01_sections_and_connections/01_neck_geometry/README.md)
  supplies the collar extraction problem and the cut-and-cap setting.

This is an initial scoping branch of [Dirac operators and twistor
spinors](../README.md), following the [investigation setup
