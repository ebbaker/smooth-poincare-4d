## Research direction and evidence boundary

A metric with a nontrivial global chiral twistor spinor is an exact
recognizing target. A variable-metric construction and smooth attainment
remain open. Ordinary harmonic spinors are only one Dirac possibility;
zeros of the desired chiral spinor must be allowed.

Start with the shared conventions and round/fixed-metric benchmark,
then pursue the most informative existence question. The lead may compare
twistor and coupled Dirac systems, spectral or variational mechanisms,
boundary or gluing constructions, twisting connections, alternative
spinorial functionals, and geometric estimates or obstruction examples.
These possibilities are illustrative; promising new formulations need not
appear in the initial overview to be authorized.

Specify every new operator's bundles, reality, curvature terms and recognizing
implication. Separate ordinary harmonic, eigenvalue and twistor equations.
For a spectral mechanism, zero infimum and smooth nondegenerate attainment
are different obligations; track spinor mass, zeros and metric-dependent
bundle identifications. Cross-links to section or connection constructions
are legitimate research directions, with their hypotheses and ownership
made explicit.
