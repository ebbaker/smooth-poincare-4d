The example is a smooth one-parameter family on the standard oriented
$S^4$ with its unique spin structure. Fix one pole $p$, one oriented
Euclidean end frame, and one unit positive spinor $\Psi_\infty$ in
that frame. For all sufficiently small nonzero real $\epsilon$, the
E27 boundary solution is nowhere zero and is nonparallel. Its compact
twistor-weight field has exactly one simple zero with the prescribed
twistor jet. The relative cost has order $|\epsilon|$ and the
negative of the weighted scalar integral has order $\epsilon^2$.
Use $X\cdot X=-|X|^2$, the positive half-spin bundle on which
self-dual two-forms act, and the positive space-form curvature-operator
sign in [E12][e12].

## Metric, end, and curvature jet

Let $y$ be oriented stereographic coordinates on $N=S^4\setminus\{p\}$
and let $R=|y|$. Choose a fixed positive smooth $\rho(y)$ equal to $1$
on a ball around $y=0$ and to $R^{-2}$ for all sufficiently large $R$.
Set $\omega=\rho^{-1}$ and $g_0=\rho^2\delta_y$. In the second
oriented chart $x=S_a y/R^2$, with $S_a$ the reflection perpendicular
to a fixed unit vector $a$, $r=|x|=R^{-1}$ and $g_0=\delta_x$
near $p$. Inversion and the fixed reflection together preserve
orientation. There $\omega=r^{-2}$, and $h_0=\omega^2 g_0=\delta_y$
on all of $N$. If $\Omega(y)=2/(1+R^2)$ is the unit round
stereographic factor, then $\rho/\Omega=(1+r^2)/2$ near $p$.
Thus $g_0$ extends smoothly as a positive metric conformal to the
round metric. Round scalar curvature is $12$, so the integrated
Schrödinger–Lichnerowicz identity gives trivial round Dirac kernel.
Compact Dirac kernel dimension is preserved by the conformal
covariance stated in E27, so $D_{g_0}$ is invertible.
[Ammann–Humbert–Morel §2.1][ahm] · [E27, Dirac covariance][e27].

Choose a smooth real cutoff $\chi$ with support in the patch where
$\rho=1$, equal to one near $y=0$. Extend the symmetric tensor
\[
 k=\chi(y)(y^1)^2\big((dy^2)^2-(dy^3)^2\big)
\]
by zero to the compact manifold. Let $g_\epsilon=g_0+\epsilon k$
and $h_\epsilon=\omega^2g_\epsilon$. For small $|\epsilon|$ these
are positive definite. In the $y$ chart $h_\epsilon=\delta_y+\epsilon k$
everywhere because $\omega=1$ on the support of $k$. The marked
end remains exactly Euclidean and the complete metric has zero
geometric ADM mass by its exterior flux. The same compact flat
ball about $p$ is fixed for all $\epsilon$.

At $y=0$ the metric is Euclidean and its first derivatives vanish.
The only independent curvature entries there are
$R_{1212}=-\epsilon$ and $R_{1313}=\epsilon$, using
$R_{1j1j}=-\frac12\partial_1^2(g_\epsilon)_{jj}$ for these jets.
The scalar curvature is zero at the point. In the self-dual
orthonormal basis
$(e^{12}+e^{34},\,e^{13}-e^{24},\,e^{14}+e^{23})/\sqrt2$,
the positive diagonal block of $\mathcal R$ is therefore
\[
 W_{h_\epsilon}^+(0)=W_{g_\epsilon}^+(0)
                 =\operatorname{diag}(-\epsilon/2,\epsilon/2,0).
\]
The off-diagonal entries vanish because the full curvature
operator is diagonal in the elementary two-form basis at the
point. The equality with $W^+$ uses $s=0$ there and
$\mathcal R_{++}=W^++(s/12)I$.
[E11, curvature sign][e11] · [E12, curvature operator][e12].

## Fixed parametrix, resolvent, and nonvanishing

Identify $S_{g_0}$ unitarily with $S_{g_\epsilon}$ using $U_\epsilon$
from the oriented frame map
$A_\epsilon=(I+\epsilon g_0^{-1}k)^{-1/2}$ and its spin lift
continuous from the identity. This preserves chirality and is
the identity outside the support. Write
$\widehat D_\epsilon=U_\epsilon^{-1}D_{g_\epsilon}U_\epsilon$
on the fixed bundle. On every fixed Sobolev pair
$H^{m+1}\to H^m$ based on $g_0$, coefficients and their needed
derivatives depend smoothly on $\epsilon$. In particular
$\|\widehat D_\epsilon-D_{g_0}\|_{H^{m+1}\to H^m}=O(|\epsilon|)$.
Boundedness of $D_{g_0}^{-1}:H^m\to H^{m+1}$ follows from compact
elliptic regularity. The Neumann series for
$I+(\widehat D_\epsilon-D_{g_0})D_{g_0}^{-1}$ on $H^m$ gives
the inverse for sufficiently small $|\epsilon|$. For $m=0$ this
proves full invertibility. For $m=4$ it supplies smooth dependence
and uniform bounds for the inverse into $H^5$, which embeds
in $C^2$ in dimension four. No change of volume density is needed
to compare these bounded operators on the fixed Sobolev spaces.
[E11, metric comparison][e11] · [E26, compact invertibility conventions][e26].

Take $c=1/(2\pi^2)$ and the fixed negative pole spinor $\eta$
determined by $c\,a\cdot\eta=\Psi_\infty$. This fixes $c|\eta|=1$.
Choose a single cutoff $\chi_p$ supported in the fixed flat ball,
equal to one near $p$. The singular local term
$E=-c\,x\cdot\eta/r^4$ is fixed. The cutoff pole $\chi_pE$ and
its smooth residual $f=D_{g_0}(\chi_pE)-\delta_p\eta$ are supported
in the unchanged ball, where the spin identification is the
identity. In the fixed bundle
the Green solution is
$\widehat G_\epsilon=\chi_pE-\widehat D_\epsilon^{-1}f$.
The resolvent identity gives the smooth difference
\[
 \widehat G_\epsilon-G_0
   =\widehat D_\epsilon^{-1}(\widehat D_\epsilon-D_{g_0})D_{g_0}^{-1}f
   =O_{C^2}(|\epsilon|).
\]
Its regular term near $p$ is denoted $\gamma_\epsilon$ in
$G_\epsilon=E+\gamma_\epsilon$. The fixed-end boundary solution
is $\Psi_\epsilon=\omega^{-3/2}\beta_\epsilon G_\epsilon$,
where $\beta_\epsilon:S_{g_\epsilon}\to S_{h_\epsilon}$ is the
unitary conformal identification. It is positive, ordinary harmonic,
and has limit $\Psi_\infty$. At zero parameter, the everywhere
constant $\Psi_\infty$ is the E27 solution by its uniqueness in
this asymptotic class. Hence $\gamma_0=0$ near $p$.
[E27, Green formula, frame change and uniqueness][e27] ·
[Ammann–Humbert–Morel, Definition 2.1, Remark 2.2 and Proposition 2.3][ahm].

The unitary comparison $\widetilde U_\epsilon$ for
$h_0,h_\epsilon$ satisfies
$\beta_\epsilon U_\epsilon=\widetilde U_\epsilon\beta_0$.
The same $A_\epsilon$ is the frame map for the two rescaled
metrics, and lifting both maps from the identity gives the
stated compatibility. Thus the pointwise difference in the
fixed $h_0$ bundle is bounded by
$\omega^{-3/2}|\widehat G_\epsilon-G_0|_{g_0}$.
The factor $\omega^{-3/2}=\rho^{3/2}$ is bounded on $N$, so
$1/2\le|\Psi_\epsilon|_{h_\epsilon}\le 3/2$ everywhere for
sufficiently small $|\epsilon|$. More precisely in the same
parallel end frame E27 gives
\[
 \Psi_\epsilon-\Psi_\infty=O(|\epsilon|R^{-3}),\qquad
 \nabla^{h_\epsilon}\Psi_\epsilon=O(|\epsilon|R^{-4}).
\]
Indeed the difference is $R^{-3}(a\cdot(x/r)\cdot)\gamma_\epsilon(x)$,
and differentiation costs at least one power of $R^{-1}$.
On a fixed compact core $\nabla^{h_\epsilon}\Psi_\epsilon=O(|\epsilon|)$
by the $C^1$ spinor estimate, smooth dependence of the connection,
and parallelism of $\Psi_0$.

The compact twistor-weight spinor $\psi_\epsilon=\omega^{-2}G_\epsilon$
satisfies near $p$
\[
 \psi_\epsilon=-c\,x\cdot\eta+r^4\gamma_\epsilon(x),\qquad
 (\nabla_X^{g_\epsilon}\psi_\epsilon)(p)=-c\,X\cdot\eta,\qquad
 D_{g_\epsilon}\psi_\epsilon(p)=4c\,\eta.
\]
The linear term is a flat twistor spinor and the error vanishes
through order three, giving $P_{g_\epsilon}\psi_\epsilon=
O(|\epsilon|r^3)$ there. The derivative is a real isometry from
$T_pS^4$ to the positive half-spin fiber by Clifford norm and
$c|\eta|=1$. This proves a simple zero at $p$. The positive
conformal weight and the uniform bound on $\Psi_\epsilon$
exclude all other zeros. [E27, compact twistor transport][e27].

## Cost, sign, and scope

Use the relative cost $J_{g_\epsilon}$ of E07. The twistor weight
is $\Psi_\epsilon=\omega^{1/2}\beta_\epsilon\psi_\epsilon$. By
twistor conformal covariance in dimension four and
$D_{h_\epsilon}\Psi_\epsilon=0$,
\[
 J_{g_\epsilon}(\psi_\epsilon)^4
 =\int_N \frac{|\nabla^{h_\epsilon}\Psi_\epsilon|^4}{|\Psi_\epsilon|^4}
                           \,dV_{h_\epsilon}.
\]
The uniform lower norm bound, core estimate and integrable
$R^{-16}$ fourth-power tail give $J_{g_\epsilon}=O(|\epsilon|)$.
E28 applies because the end is exact with the same derivative
decay, so
\[
 \mathcal S_\epsilon
 :=\int_N s_{h_\epsilon}|\Psi_\epsilon|^2\,dV_{h_\epsilon}
 =-4\int_N|\nabla^{h_\epsilon}\Psi_\epsilon|^2\,dV_{h_\epsilon}
 =O(\epsilon^2).
\]
The upper bound uses the integrable $R^{-8}$ squared tail. Both
integrals are finite. If the derivative vanished identically,
the two quaternionic partners would trivialize the positive
spin connection by parallel sections. Its curvature block
would give $W^+=0$ by E28, contrary to the explicit curvature
jet for $\epsilon\ne0$. Hence $J_{g_\epsilon}>0$ and
$\mathcal S_\epsilon<0$ for all sufficiently small nonzero
$\epsilon$. The same nonzero $W^+$ excludes a nonzero parallel
positive half-spinor in any smooth positive conformal scale
on $N$, since the vanishing of $W^+$ is conformally invariant.
[E07, relative covariance][e07] · [Friedrich, twistor weight][friedrich] ·
[E28, weighted scalar identity][e28] · [Ammann–Humbert, boundary sign][ah].

There are matching lower orders. Write $T_\epsilon$ for
$\nabla^{h_\epsilon}\Psi_\epsilon$ pulled back in the spinor
factor to the fixed bundle, with the covector slots unchanged.
On a ball about $y=0$ this depends smoothly on $\epsilon$ in
$C^1$ by the resolvent estimate into $C^2$. Under the same
bundle identification the spin connection and curvature are
$\widehat\nabla_\epsilon$ and $\widehat R^S_\epsilon$, so
$d^{\widehat\nabla_\epsilon}T_\epsilon=
\widehat R^S_\epsilon\widetilde U_\epsilon^{-1}\Psi_\epsilon$.
Since $T_0=0$ and the background spin curvature vanishes, the
derivative is $d^{\nabla^{h_0}}\dot T=\dot R^{S}\Psi_\infty$.
At $y=0$ the right side on $(e_1,e_2)$ equals
$\frac12 e_1\cdot e_2\cdot\Psi_\infty$ in the E12 sign. This
is nonzero by the Clifford relations. Therefore $\dot T$
is nonzero somewhere in that ball. On a smaller fixed open
set it is bounded below in norm. Smooth parameter dependence
then gives $|T_\epsilon|\ge C|\epsilon|$ there, with
$C>0$ independent of small $\epsilon$. Uniform metric
equivalence gives the lower bounds in both integrals. Thus
$J_{g_\epsilon}\asymp|\epsilon|$ and
$-\mathcal S_\epsilon\asymp\epsilon^2$ for this family.
[E12, spin-curvature identity][e12].

The underlying manifold is standard $S^4$ by construction.
For $\epsilon\ne0$ the supplied compact metric has nonvanishing
$W^+$ and the boundary solution misses the parallelism criterion
despite having the required jet and no interior zeros. This
is consistent with the exact recognition implication of SH-E01,
which requires a supplied ASD metric. The round-conformal
background already supplies such a metric on this model.
No general homotopy-sphere construction or across-class bound is
inferred from this family. [SH-E01, supplied ASD metric][sh].
