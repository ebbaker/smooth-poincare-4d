## Fixed end and the rescaled fields

Let $M$ be a smooth closed connected oriented spin four-manifold,
$p\in M$, and $N=M\setminus\{p\}$. Use the shared Riemannian chirality
and $X\cdot X=-|X|^2$. Start with the supplied data of [E26][e26]–[E28][e28]:
a smooth compact metric $g$ with invertible full $D_g$, an oriented
flat chart $x$ about $p$, and a positive function $\omega$ on $N$ with
$\omega=r^{-2}$ near $p$, where $r=|x|$. Put $h=\omega^2g$. Fix
$y=S_a x/r^2$ with $S_a$ the reflection in the hyperplane perpendicular
to a unit $a$. This oriented chart makes $h$ Euclidean for $R=|y|>R_0$,
increasing $R_0$ so that $\omega=r^{-2}$ throughout that end. Fix the
parallel spin frame of E27 in $y$ and the compact parallel spin frame
in $x$. The harmonic positive spinor $\Psi$ has a specified unit limit
$\Psi_\infty$ in the end frame, with
$\Psi_y-\Psi_\infty=O(R^{-3})$ and $\partial_y\Psi_y=O(R^{-4})$.

Choose fixed radii $R_0<R_1<R_2$ and a smooth nondecreasing
$\chi:[0,\infty)\to[0,1]$ with $\chi=0$ on $[0,R_1]$ and
$\chi=1$ on $[R_2,\infty)$. Explicitly, for
$s=(R-R_1)/(R_2-R_1)$ take
$\chi(R)=b(s)/(b(s)+b(1-s))$, with $b(s)=e^{-1/s}$ for $s>0$
and $b(s)=0$ for $s\le0$. Both terms of the quotient derivative
are nonnegative on $0<s<1$. For $0<t\le1$ define $\Phi_t$ to be
the identity on the core and
\[
 \Phi_t(y)=A_t(R)y,\qquad
 A_t(R)=1+(t^{-1}-1)\chi(R)
\]
on the end. The radial function $R\mapsto A_t(R)R$ has derivative
$A_t+R(t^{-1}-1)\chi'\ge 1$ and maps $[R_0,\infty)$ onto itself.
The tangential factor $A_t$ is positive. This gives a smooth
orientation-preserving diffeomorphism, the identity for $R\le R_1$
and $y\mapsto y/t$ for $R\ge R_2$. Varying $t$ to $1$ gives an
isotopy to the identity, including after extension over $p$ below,
and hence a lift preserving the spin structure. Use
\[
 \widetilde h_t=\Phi_t^*h,\qquad
 h_t=t^2\widetilde h_t,\qquad
 \Psi_t=\beta_t\Phi_t^*\Psi,
\]
where $\beta_t:S_{\widetilde h_t}\to S_{h_t}$ is the unitary spin
identification for a constant metric length factor $t$. The
orientation-preserving lift preserves chirality. The Levi–Civita
connection is unchanged under constant scale. In the matched
orthonormal frames the spin covariant derivatives intertwine, and
tracing with Clifford multiplication gives
$D_{h_t}\beta_t=t^{-1}\beta_t D_{\widetilde h_t}$. Thus
$D_{h_t}\Psi_t=0$. The transport preserves pointwise amplitude
under pullback.

On $R\ge R_2$, the pulled-back orthonormal frame is
$t\,\partial_{y_i}$ and $\beta_t$ turns it into $\partial_{y_i}$.
There is no rotation in the frame transition. Choose the lift with
positive sign there, continued from $t=1$. In the original fixed
end coordinates and frame,
\[
 h_t=\delta_y,\qquad
 (\Psi_t)_y(y)=\Psi_y(y/t),\qquad
 (\Psi_t)_y-\Psi_\infty=O(t^3R^{-3}),\qquad
 \partial_y(\Psi_t)_y=O(t^3R^{-4}).
\]
The final derivative estimate includes the chain rule factor $t^{-1}$.
In particular the end limit is the original spinor, not just a spinor
of the same length. Completeness is preserved by pullback and
constant scaling. The ADM mass remains exactly zero in the fixed
Euclidean chart.

## Same compact pole, compact invertibility and Green normalization

In $x$, the exterior formula for $\Phi_t$ is $x\mapsto tx$, since
$x=S_a y/R^2$. It extends over $p$ as a smooth diffeomorphism
$\overline\Phi_t$ fixing $p$. Set
\[
 g_t=\omega^{-2}h_t=u_t^2\overline\Phi_t^*g,\qquad
 u_t=t\,\frac{\omega\circ\Phi_t}{\omega}.
\]
For $r<1/R_2$, $u_t=t^{-1}$ and $\overline\Phi_t^*g=t^2\delta_x$.
Thus $u_t$ extends positively and smoothly across $p$ and $g_t$
is exactly $\delta_x$ on the same ball for every $t$. Compact
invertibility follows from spin pullback and the conformal kernel
isomorphism. In the unitary identification $\beta_u$ for a length
factor $u$, the dimension-four formula is
$D_{u^2g}(u^{-3/2}\beta_u\zeta)=u^{-5/2}\beta_u D_g\zeta$.
Apply it on the compact manifold with $u=u_t$ and input metric
$\overline\Phi_t^*g$. Trivial full kernel gives invertibility for
each smooth compact $g_t$. [E27, eq. (2)][e27] · [Ammann–Humbert–Morel, §2.1][ahm].

Let $\beta_{\omega,t}:S_{g_t}\to S_{h_t}$ be the unitary spin
identification for $\omega$. The inverse Dirac-weight field
$G_t=\omega^{3/2}\beta_{\omega,t}^{-1}\Psi_t$ is $D_{g_t}$-harmonic
off $p$. Let $G_\eta$ denote the original compact Green spinor and
$c=1/|S^3|$. In E27's fixed frames the map from compact source
to end constant is $\eta\mapsto c\,a\cdot\eta$. The spin-frame
transition across the oriented inversion depends only on the
direction $x/r$, which the dilation preserves. Its lift has the
same sign fixed above. Thus in the fixed flat compact coordinates
and spin frame,
\[
 G_t(x)=t^3G_\eta(tx)
       =-c\,\frac{x\cdot\eta}{r^4}+t^3\gamma(tx)
 \quad(r<1/R_2).
\]
Indeed the original end field at $y/t$ has compact weight
$(tr)^3$ and the new inverse weight is $r^{-3}$. The compact
spin frame induced by the pullback of $\partial_{x_i}$ and subsequent
conformal identification is the original $\partial_{x_i}$ frame.
The smooth remainder $t^3\gamma(tx)$ is flat Dirac-harmonic.
The normalized Euclidean pole has distributional source
$\delta_p\eta$, so the extension of $G_t$ as a distribution has
that same source on $(M,g_t)$. Compact invertibility makes it the
normalized Green spinor. Equivalently E27's uniqueness at fixed
$\Psi_\infty$ applies to $\Psi_t$ and the transported new Green
spinor, since both have $O(R^{-3})$ error and the same end amplitude.
The $t^3$ in the compact formula ensures the same point source.
The end transport itself is unitary. The homothetic Dirac
conformal weight $t^{-3/2}$ would change the prescribed end
amplitude. [E27, eqs. (1)–(5)][e27] · [Ammann–Humbert–Morel,
Definition 2.1, Remark 2.2 and Proposition 2.3][ahm].

The compact twistor-weight field is $\psi_t=\omega^{-2}G_t$,
with smooth expansion
$\psi_t(x)=-c\,x\cdot\eta+r^4t^3\gamma(tx)$ at the fixed pole.
Its linear jet and transverse zero at $p$ are unchanged.
Interior zeros on $N$ are carried to $\Phi_t^{-1}(\{\Psi=0\})$.

## Bulk energies, critical cost and volume

Define
\[
 \mathcal D(h,\Psi)=\int_N|\nabla^h\Psi|_h^2\,dV_h,\qquad
 \mathcal S(h,\Psi)=\int_N s_h|\Psi|_h^2\,dV_h,\qquad
 \mathcal C_4(h,\Psi)=
 \int_{\{\Psi\ne0\}}\frac{|\nabla^h\Psi|_h^4}{|\Psi|_h^4}\,dV_h .
\]
The first two integrals are finite by the end bounds and compact support
of $s_h$. The critical integral takes values in $[0,\infty]$.
The transformation laws are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |\Psi_t|_{h_t}&=|\Psi|_h\circ\Phi_t,&
 |\nabla^{h_t}\Psi_t|_{h_t}&=t^{-1}|\nabla^h\Psi|_h\circ\Phi_t,\\
 s_{h_t}&=t^{-2}s_h\circ\Phi_t,&
 dV_{h_t}&=t^4\Phi_t^*dV_h.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Change of variables gives the exact identities
\[
 \mathcal D(h_t,\Psi_t)=t^2\mathcal D(h,\Psi),\qquad
 \mathcal S(h_t,\Psi_t)=t^2\mathcal S(h,\Psi),\qquad
 \mathcal C_4(h_t,\Psi_t)=\mathcal C_4(h,\Psi).
\]
The last equality holds as an extended nonnegative integral, since the
diffeomorphism identifies the nonzero loci. A zero-free input has
finite critical cost by compactness and the integrable end bound. If
there is an interior zero, the local logarithmic estimate of E29
gives infinite cost. Its needed density of the nonzero locus follows
by weak unique continuation for $D_h^2$, whose principal part is the
same scalar elliptic operator on each component. The fixed pole itself
has finite compact twistor relative cost by the displayed expansion:
$|\psi_t|_{g_t}\sim r$ and $|P_{g_t}\psi_t|_{g_t}=O(r^3)$ for
each $t$. On the punctured nonzero locus the compact relative twistor
density agrees with the end critical density by the exact conformal
identity in E29. [E29, local divergence and transport][e29] ·
[Aronszajn, §1 and Remark 3][aronszajn].

The complete metric has infinite volume and $\Psi_t$ is not in
$L^2(N,h_t)$ because its norm tends to $1$. For any fixed compact
$K$ in the identity core,
\[
 \operatorname{Vol}_{h_t}(K)=t^4\operatorname{Vol}_h(K),\qquad
 \int_K|\Psi_t|_{h_t}^2\,dV_{h_t}
      =t^4\int_K|\Psi|_h^2\,dV_h .
\]
Also $g_t=t^2 g$ there and the analogous compact $L^2$ mass of
$\psi_t$ scales by $t^4$ there. The compact volume is finite with
a fixed positive contribution from the flat ball. It is uniformly
bounded above: in the transition annulus the maps
$y\mapsto [t+(1-t)\chi(R)]y$ have bounded first derivatives and
$h_t$ is their Euclidean pullback, while the fixed exterior and
the contracting core each have bounded compactified volume.
Prescribing $\omega$ fixes the compactification formula and gives
no exact unit-volume condition in general.

If the compact conformal representative is free off a sufficiently
small fixed flat ball, compact volume one can be imposed without
changing the punctured data. Take a smaller closed flat ball of
volume less than one, and a smooth function $\zeta$ vanishing there
and strictly positive on its complement. The continuous increasing
function $b\mapsto\int_M e^{4b\zeta}\,dV_{g_t}$ tends to the ball
volume as $b\to-\infty$ and to infinity as $b\to+\infty$.
Choose $b$ attaining volume one and replace $(g_t,\omega)$ by
$(e^{2b\zeta}g_t,e^{-b\zeta}\omega)$. This preserves the flat germ,
compact Dirac invertibility, the exact $h_t$ and its spinor, with
the same point source when the compact Green spinor is expressed in
the new representative. This optional normalization uses the
freedom to change $\omega$ away from the fixed flat ball.

## Unit harmonic gauge and the minimizing consequence

If $\Psi$ is nowhere zero, let $k=|\Psi|_h^{4/3}h$ and take the
unit harmonic spinor $\xi=|\Psi|_h^{-1}\beta\Psi$ using the same
conformal Dirac law. Write $V=|\nabla^k\xi|_k^2$. Then $k_t=t^2\Phi_t^*k$,
the corresponding $\xi_t$ is the unitary pullback and scale transport,
and $V_t=t^{-2}V\circ\Phi_t$. For smooth $v>0$ equal to $1$ outside a
compact set, the metric $q=v^{-4}k$ has the harmonic spinor
$v^3\beta\xi$. Constant spinor norm outside the support gives
zero boundary flux in its integrated Lichnerowicz identity. Also
$s_k=-4V$ by the pointwise identity for a unit harmonic spinor.
Using $s_q=v^6(6\Delta_k v^{-2}+s_k v^{-2})$ with
$\Delta_k=d^*d$, the weighted scalar integral is
\[
 \int_N (6v^4\Delta_k v^{-2}+s_kv^2)\,dV_k
 =-48\int_N|dv|_k^2\,dV_k-4\int_N Vv^2\,dV_k.
\]
The integration by parts uses $d(v^{-2})=0$ outside a compact
set and $\langle d(v^4),d(v^{-2})\rangle=-8|dv|^2$.
The Dirichlet functional is therefore
\[
 I_k(v)=\int_N(12|dv|_k^2+Vv^2)\,dV_k .
\]
Both terms have length dimension two in dimension four, and
substitution gives $I_{k_t}(v\circ\Phi_t)=t^2 I_k(v)$.
Pullback bijects the compactly supported variations of the boundary
value $1$, so the infimum over those test functions scales by the
same factor. This gauge calculation requires a nowhere-zero field.
Its exterior uses the unit harmonic gauge of E31, which need only
be asymptotically Euclidean. [E31 scope][e31] ·
[Ammann–Humbert, corrected PDF, conformal scalar law and Theorem 2.2][ah].

The marked class in this packet admits an unrestricted infimum
$\inf\mathcal D=0$, equivalently $\inf(-\mathcal S)=0$ by E28,
on every homotopy sphere supplied by E26–E28. No sign hypothesis on
$s_h$ and no hypothesis on interior zeros enter that conclusion.
The critical cost is unchanged by this family, including when
infinite. Smooth attainment of $\mathcal D=0$ would give a parallel
unit positive spinor. By E28, the associated smooth compact spinor
is twistor for $g_t$, the compact positive Weyl block vanishes,
and on a homotopy four-sphere the shared signature and developing
argument recognizes standard $S^4$. [E28][e28] ·
[Friedrich, §1, eq. (1.1) and the conformal kernel paragraph][friedrich].
For any fixed input with
positive Dirichlet energy the scale family reaches zero only in its
degenerate limit, with the positive-definite core shrinking pointwise.

The operation changes the metric in the fixed marking through an
end-moving diffeomorphism and a homothety. In the flat transition
annulus write $\lambda_t(R)=A_t(R)R$. Its new radial length
factor is $t\lambda_t'(R)$ and its tangential factor relative to
$h$ is $t\lambda_t(R)/R$. For $t<1$ these differ where $\chi'>0$.
Thus the family is outside the pointwise compactly supported
conformal variations of the single fixed marked metric, even
though it agrees with the original tensor on the fixed exterior.
The compact metrics are conformal after an end-dilating
diffeomorphism that fixes the pole.
The free Dirichlet infimum supplies no compactness of the optimizing
data and no bound tending to zero for the critical relative cost.
