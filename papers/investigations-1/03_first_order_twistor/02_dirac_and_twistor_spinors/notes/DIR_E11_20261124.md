This is the single smooth spherical example in the [frozen request][request].

On the standard oriented smooth \(S^4\) with its spin structure there is a
sequence of smooth positive-definite metrics \(g_n\) and smooth positive
half-spinors \(\psi_n\) with one transverse zero at a fixed point such that
\(J_{g_n}(\psi_n)=O(n^{-1/2})\) and \(E_+(g_n)\ge c n\) for some \(c>0\)
and all sufficiently large integers \(n\). The metrics converge in
\(C^1\) to a fixed conformally round metric, so volume and bilipschitz
bounds hold. Use the [B06][b06] relative error

\[
J_g(\psi)=\left(\int_{S^4\setminus Z(\psi)}
\frac{|P_g\psi|_g^4}{|\psi|_g^4}\,dV_g\right)^{1/4},
\qquad P^g_X\psi=\nabla_X^g\psi+\tfrac14 X\cdot_gD_g\psi.
\]

As in the [shared conventions][common], vectors square to minus their
length squared, positive half-spin is the chirality acted on by self-dual
two-forms, and \(E_+(g)=\int |W_g^+|_{\mathrm{op,HS}}^2\,dV_g\).

## A one-zero solution and a flat patch

Fix a pole \(p\) and oriented stereographic coordinates \(x\) on
\(S^4\setminus\{p\}\). Write the unit round metric as
\(g_{\mathrm{rd}}=\Omega(x)^2\delta\) with
\(\Omega(x)=2/(1+|x|^2)\). A nonzero constant positive Euclidean half-spinor
\(\phi\) is parallel. In the normalized round stereographic spin frame
its conformal transport has components \(\Omega(x)^{1/2}\phi\), hence is
a twistor spinor on the chart. This uses the twistor operator and kernel
weight in [Friedrich, eq. (1.1) and pp. 59–60][friedrich]. The constant
spinor is also the constant part of his flat model, Example 2, pp. 62–63.

For extension at \(p\), choose oriented stereographic coordinates \(y\)
with \(p\) at \(y=0\), related by \(x=R_a y/|y|^2\) for the reflection \(R_a\)
in the hyperplane perpendicular to a fixed unit vector \(a\). The reflection
compensates the orientation change of inversion. The normalized round
coordinate frames on the overlap differ by \(R_aR_{\widehat y}\), where
\(\widehat y=y/|y|\). Indeed, the coordinate differential is
\(|y|^{-2}R_aR_{\widehat y}\) and \(\Omega(x)|y|^{-2}=\Omega(y)\).
A spin lift of this rotation is \(a\cdot\widehat y\cdot\), up to the common
choice of sign of the two local spin frames. Its inverse acts on the spinor
components, so in the \(y\)-frame the transported spinor is

\[
\pm\left(\frac{2}{1+|y|^2}\right)^{1/2} y\cdot a\cdot\phi.
\]

This extends smoothly and vanishes only at \(p\). Its real coordinate
derivative there is \(v\mapsto\pm\sqrt2\,v\cdot a\cdot\phi\), a real
isomorphism onto the positive half-spin fiber by the Clifford norm identity.
The twistor equation extends to \(p\) by smoothness. In particular its
covariant first jet satisfies the [admissible jet condition][e06].

On the \(x\)-chart fix a bounded open ball \(U\) and a smooth positive
function \(f\) equal to \(\Omega^{-1}\) on a neighborhood of \(\overline U\)
and equal to one near \(p\). For example multiply \(-\log\Omega\) by a
smooth compactly supported cutoff equal to one near \(\overline U\), then
exponentiate and extend. Set \(g_b=f^2g_{\mathrm{rd}}\) and let \(\psi_b\)
be the conformal transport of the round spinor, with weight \(f^{1/2}\).
The background pair is smooth and exact. On \(U\) the metric is \(\delta\)
and \(\psi_b\) has constant components \(\phi\) in the flat spin frame.

## Compactly supported metric perturbation and the spinor estimate

In \(U\) set \(H=(dx^2)^2-(dx^3)^2\), a real trace-free polarization
annihilating \(\partial_1\). Write \(T=(dx^1)^2\owedge H\), with the
Kulkarni–Nomizu convention \(T_{1a1b}=H_{ab}\) for \(a,b>1\). These are
its only independent possibly nonzero components. Its Ricci contraction
\(\sum_iT_{ijil}\) is \(H_{jl}\) and its scalar trace is zero. The constant
algebraic Weyl tensor \(C=2T-H\owedge\delta\) therefore satisfies
\(C_{i1j1}=H_{ij}\). For a real nonzero \(\chi\in C_c^\infty(U)\), set

\[
F_n(x)=\chi(x)\cos(nx^1),\qquad
(h_n)_{ij}=-n^{-7/2}\sum_{k,l} C_{ikjl}\,\partial_k\partial_l F_n,
\qquad g_n=g_b+h_n.
\]

Extend \(h_n\) by zero. The pair symmetry makes \(h_n\) symmetric, the
vanishing Weyl trace makes it trace-free, and antisymmetry in \(i,k\) makes
\(\partial^i(h_n)_{ij}=0\). Thus even the cutoff tensor is transverse and
trace-free for \(g_b\). When both derivatives hit the cosine its leading
part is \(n^{-3/2}F_n H\). For \(k=0,1,2\) the differentiated remainder
satisfies \(\|\partial^k(h_n-n^{-3/2}F_n H)\|_\infty=O(n^{k-5/2})\), since
each remaining term has at least one derivative on the fixed cutoff.
In particular \(\|h_n\|_{C^0}=O(n^{-3/2})\),
\(\|\partial h_n\|_\infty=O(n^{-1/2})\) and
\(\|\partial^2 h_n\|_\infty=O(n^{1/2})\). For large \(n\), the metrics are
positive and uniformly comparable to \(g_b\), and their volume densities
converge uniformly to that of \(g_b\).

Use the positive \(g_b\)-symmetric comparison
\(A_n=(I+g_b^{-1}h_n)^{-1/2}\) from the oriented \(g_b\)-orthonormal
frames to the oriented \(g_n\)-orthonormal frames. The path obtained by
replacing \(h_n\) by \(t h_n\), \(0\le t\le1\), determines the spin lift
\(\beta_n\) starting at the identity. Set \(\psi_n=\beta_n\psi_b\).
The maps \(\beta_n\) are fiberwise isometries and preserve chirality.
They are the identity outside the perturbation support. There \(P_{g_n}\psi_n=0\),
and the pair equals the background also near \(p\), preserving its unique
transverse zero and first jet.

On \(U\), derivatives of the paired frame \(A_n\partial_i\) are
\(O(n^{-1/2})\), by smoothness of the matrix inverse square root near the
identity. Christoffel symbols have the same bound since \(g_b=\delta\)
and \(\partial g_n=\partial h_n\) there. The orthonormal connection forms
and their spin lifts are thus \(O(n^{-1/2})\) in this frame. The components
of \(\psi_n\) in the paired spin frame are constant, giving
\(|\nabla^{g_n}\psi_n|_{g_n}\le C_1 n^{-1/2}|\psi_n|_{g_n}\). The twistor
operator is the orthogonal projection of the covariant derivative away
from its Clifford trace. The same bound holds for \(|P_{g_n}\psi_n|_{g_n}\).
The error vanishes off the fixed support, so the uniform volume bounds
give \(J_{g_n}(\psi_n)\le C_2 n^{-1/2}\). In the fixed reference spin bundle
the section \(\beta_n^{-1}\psi_n\) is exactly \(\psi_b\), and
\(\|\psi_n\|_{L^2(g_n)}^2\to\|\psi_b\|_{L^2(g_b)}^2>0\) by uniform
convergence of the volume densities. The sequence retains positive mass
on any fixed subset of \(U\) of positive volume.

## Weyl curvature and normalization

Use the curvature sign in which \(R_{ijij}\) is the sectional curvature
in an orthonormal frame. The linearized coordinate curvature at \(\delta\) is

\[
R'_\delta(h)_{ijkl}=\tfrac12\left(\partial_j\partial_k h_{il}
+\partial_i\partial_l h_{jk}-\partial_i\partial_k h_{jl}
-\partial_j\partial_l h_{ik}\right).
\]

The term of order \(n^{1/2}\) has both derivatives on the cosine, and is
\(\frac12 n^{1/2}F_n T\). For instance, the \(1a1b\) component is
\(-\tfrac12\partial_1^2(h_n)_{ab}\) to this order. The cutoff terms
and the second derivatives of the correction are \(O(n^{-1/2})\).
The nonlinear difference from linearization is bounded by a constant
times \(|h_n||\partial^2 h_n|+|\partial h_n|^2=O(n^{-1})\).
This follows from the Christoffel formula on the flat patch, with
uniformly bounded inverse-metric coefficients. Replacing the Euclidean
Weyl projection by the \(g_n\)-projection costs \(O(|h_n||R(g_n)|)=O(n^{-1})\)
there. Consequently, in fixed coordinates on \(U\),

\[
W(g_n)=\tfrac14 n^{1/2} F_n C+O_{L^\infty}(n^{-1/2}),\qquad
W(g_n)_{1212}=\tfrac14 n^{1/2} F_n+O_{L^\infty}(n^{-1/2}).
\]

Uniform metric equivalence gives a fixed lower comparison of the full
four-index Weyl norm in \(L^2(g_n)\) with the \(L^2(U,dx)\)-norm of
\(W(g_n)_{1212}\). Using \(\cos^2 t=(1+\cos 2t)/2\) and integrating
the oscillatory term by parts in \(x^1\) gives
\(\int_U |F_n|^2 dx\to \tfrac12\int_U\chi^2 dx>0\).
The coordinate component and the uniform error bound then imply
\(\int_{S^4}|W(g_n)|_{4\mathrm{ind}}^2\,dV_{g_n}\ge c_0 n\) for some
\(c_0>0\) and large \(n\). Since \(\tau(S^4)=0\), the signature identity
in the [shared recognition audit][recognition] gives
\(E_-=E_+\) and \(E_+=\frac18\int |W|_{4\mathrm{ind}}^2\,dV\).
This proves the claimed divergence in the positive block.

If volume one is required, set \(c_n=\operatorname{Vol}(g_n)^{-1/4}\)
and replace \(g_n\) by \(c_n^2g_n\), transporting the spinor with the
constant conformal weight \(c_n^{1/2}\). The constants tend to a positive
limit. The four-dimensional Weyl integral and the relative error are
unchanged, as are uniform bilipschitz bounds. The relative error is
unchanged by a further scalar spinor normalization, and the new
\(L^2\)-norm squared before that normalization is multiplied by \(c_n^5\).
The rescaled pair retains the fixed zero and the exact local twistor
equation there. [DIR-E07, conformal invariance][e07].
