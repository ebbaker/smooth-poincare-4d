## 1. Atiyah–Hitchin–Singer, Theorem 4.1 and p. 460

and reduces integrability to $W_-=0$ in the source orientation. Remark 1
uses the Levi–Civita horizontal splitting, the fiber complex structure,
and the corresponding orthogonal complex structure on the horizontal

negative-chiral bundle. Apply the theorem with the orientation reversed
relative to the branch orientation. The source's $P(V_-)$ then corresponds
to the branch's $S(\Lambda^+)$ (locally $P(S^+)$), and $W_-=0$ becomes
$W^+=0$. The projective bundle exists at the $SO(4)$ level. A global
half-spin bundle or an unprojectivized spinor requires the spin lift.
The theorem concerns the metric construction with its Levi–Civita splitting.
[AHS, journal article](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1978.0143)

The surrounding application uses a spin base and a self-dual connection
on the coefficient bundle and vanishes in the indicated $V_-$ chirality.
For the branch's untwisted Dirac operator $D_g$, use trivial coefficients

## 5. Friedrich, spinorial prolongation and conformal scale
