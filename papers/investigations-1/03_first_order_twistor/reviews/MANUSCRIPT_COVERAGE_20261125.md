## Checks and outstanding questions

The manuscript checks compared the Bach and spin-curvature signs in their stated conventions, the positive Yamabe and energy margins, the orientation and parametrization needed to transfer a capped smooth type, the Ricci and physical distance scales, and the order of the source and exhaustion limits. The examples support particular obstructions, including approximate-critical spikes and signed-current concentration. None supplies a general counterexample to weak extraction. The existing SEC-B36/E62 and DIR-B24/E51 retain their respective ready/reserved statuses. All eight manuscripts were compiled with the repository build command and their PDFs opened and checked. The final LaTeX passes resolved the citations and internal references. The three new papers have 33, 26, and 19 pages respectively. The reading copies are under the Git-ignored `output/pdf/` directory.

The remaining source question is whether the physical volume-bearing neighborhoods can be tied to the chiral curvature on both sides of a compatible weak cut or extended to a whole nonround base. A closed non-half-harmonic critical class can survive the available tests. Producing a positive subcritical starting class on every smooth homotopy four-sphere remains an additional global problem.
