## When the base determines the original smooth type

Suppose an oriented smooth closed homotopy-sphere base $N$ with finitely many smooth removable points has compatible smooth conformal comparison on whole compact truncations in the source, as in SEC §12.4. Assume approximate criticality. If $y<Y_{\rm rd}$ and **one fixed compared compact retains positive physical four-volume** along the subsequence, then
$$
 M\cong N,\qquad Y([h_N])=y,\qquad B_{h_N}=0,\qquad
 4\pi^2<E_+(N)\le e .
$$
If $E_i\to I^+(M)$, then $E_+(N)=e$ and the whole residual chiral energy tends to zero in the iterated limit. A fixed physical ball inside the compared compact is one sufficient witness, by the original Yamabe volume lower bound. The conclusion identifies the smooth type of the base with the source. It does not classify that type as standard. [Conformal volume test](../01_sections_and_connections/02_dirac_collar_extraction/notes/E09_CONFORMAL_BASE_VOLUME_DICHOTOMY_20261125.md) · [Marked smooth-type transfer](../01_sections_and_connections/01_neck_geometry/notes/E09_FINITE_PUNCTURE_TRANSFER_20261125.md)

To see why one compact suffices, express the physical pullbacks as $u_i^2 h_i$, with $h_i\to h$ smoothly on compact subsets of the punctured closed base. The weak factor $u$ satisfies $L_hu=yu^3$. Compactly supported tests give $Y([h])\ge y$. A nonzero $u$ extends over the punctures and obeys
$$
 Y([h])\left(\int_Nu^4\right)^{1/2}\le y\int_Nu^4,\qquad \int_Nu^4\le1.
$$
Consequently it carries all unit volume and is a positive Yamabe minimizer. Interior concentration of mass $m$ in these coordinates requires $m\ge(Y_{\rm rd}/y)^2$. In the subround case this exceeds the entire available volume. A physical-volume witness therefore rules out the zero factor. The remaining closed base is nonround by its Yamabe constant. Its exact gap leaves less than $4\pi^2$ outside it and the earlier cap argument applies to the actual smooth embedding. With ordinary smooth physical comparison, the constant test on the punctured compactification already yields $\operatorname{vol}(N)=1$ and $Y(N)=y$. A **physical** smooth removable base and a disjointly compared **nonflat complete smooth ALE** of SEC §11 cannot coexist in this band, even without minimizing $E_+$. If the base has positive energy, both it and the compactified bubble cost strictly more than $4\pi^2$. If the base is conformally round, physical volume saturation forces $y=Y_{\rm rd}$, whereas the nonflat ALE compactification would have $Y_{\rm rd}>Y(\widehat X)\ge y$. In the remaining round physical-base case, its smooth type is standard and all the limiting $e$ lies outside the exhausted base. The identity $D_i=4E_i+Y_i^2/12-32\pi^2$ gives trace-free Ricci deficit $4e$. A round *base* with this residual energy does not identify $M$. If only conformal comparison is supplied and physical volume disappears there, the argument identifying the limit of the Yamabe constants with the base value no longer applies.

**Where a round limit can lose the smooth type.** On a physical removable base the whole source converges in measured Gromov–Hausdorff distance to $(N,h)$. Each omitted piece has one boundary of diameter $O(b)$ and volume $O(b^4)$ in the sequence limsup. Uniform physical ball noncollapse bounds its intrinsic diameter by $O(b)$ as well. Along a subsequence the two chiral Weyl defect measures have the same weights $e_j$ at the marked points. The trace-free Ricci and full tensor curvature defects have weights $4e_j$ and $16e_j$, respectively. This identifies the part lost under *metric* convergence without imposing smooth convergence at the punctures. [Metric and curvature-measure proof](../01_sections_and_connections/02_dirac_collar_extraction/notes/E10_METRIC_LIMIT_AND_CURVATURE_ATOMS_20261125.md) · [Independent distance and measure check](../01_sections_and_connections/02_dirac_collar_extraction/reviews/E10_METRIC_COLLAPSE_CHECK_20261125.md)

If $M$ is nonstandard, $E_i\to I^+(M)>0$ and the physical base is round, one shrinking puncture exterior alone has capped smooth type $M$ on a marked subsequence. It carries the entire atom $e$; the other exteriors have zero limiting chiral energy. The marked cut and cap on this piece give another sequence on the original smooth type with energy tending to $e$, Yamabe constants tending to $Y_{\rm rd}$ and integrated Bach norm tending to zero. This is a restart at the same value. It gives no strict decrement to iterate. Such a branch also requires the smooth Yamabe invariant of $M$ to equal $Y_{\rm rd}$ as a supremum. At any fixed mass $0<\eta<e$, the least physical radius with some ball carrying $\eta$ tends to zero; rescaling there supplies a unit ball with that energy on the actual source. A smooth energy-preserving limit at that scale remains to be constructed. [Single-puncture restart](../01_sections_and_connections/01_neck_geometry/notes/E19_ROUND_BASE_SINGLE_PUNCTURE_20261125.md)

## Three ways to measure the residual Weyl energy

**A supplied coherent weak chart.** On a physical chart at constant rescaling $g=\delta+v$, set $a=\|v\|_\infty$, $d=\|\eta Dv\|_4$, $d_T=\|\eta Dv\|_{4,T}$ on the two transition shells, and $K=\|\eta^2\mathrm{Rm}\|_2$. The protected annulus $A$ can have arbitrary log-length. Testing the first variation with $\eta^4v$ yields
$$
 E_+(A)\le C\{a\|B\|_{L^1}+(aK+d^2+d_T+a)^2\}.
$$
In the all-lowered curvature expression the term linear in second derivatives has constant coefficients. This is what makes it possible to replace coordinate Hessian control by the intrinsic $K$. Constants are independent of both end radii and their ratio. On the nonzero locus of actual fields, the normalized squared projected curl of the twistor defect equals $|W^+|^2/2$. Thus the bound controls that quantity even if all amplitudes decay. It applies to a full residual when the chart covers it, or when the quantified errors over a cover and the uncovered chiral energy vanish. The selection of such a chart remains the geometric problem. [MATCH-E14](../02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/01_dirac_collar_matching/notes/E14_WEAK_CHART_BACH_DEFECT_20261125.md)

**Actual spinors on more general regions.** Commuting the harmonic Dirac equation gives a twisted Dirac equation for $\nabla\psi$ forced by $\operatorname{Ric}\cdot\psi/2$. Applying its integrated identity to a joint family divided by $(\sum|\psi|^2+\varepsilon)^{1/2}$ gives curvature control by the full Ricci-square integral, relative first gradients, and cutoff errors. The weights are measured in the metric of the equation. On an ordinary flat strip a quantitative higher-regularity comparison and actual incoming-trace control give nonvanishing on protected substrips. On standard smooth $S^4$ there are actual Green fields with small first-order error and a fixed positive chiral-energy defect. Their Bach integrals grow like $n^2$. The counterexample is consistent with the restricted Bach estimate above. [MATCH-E10](../02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/01_dirac_collar_matching/notes/E10_INTERIOR_SPINOR_DEFECT_20261125.md) · [MATCH-E11 and the summed bound](../02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/01_dirac_collar_matching/notes/E11_RELATIVE_BOCHNER_20261125.md)

**Two boundary frames with no intervening chart.** For a compact measured residual $U$ in the physical gauge, the signed chiral densities are
$$
 Q_\pm=\left(|W^\pm|^2+s^2/48-|\operatorname{Ric}_0|^2/4\right)dV .
$$
Their integrals are determined modulo $8\pi^2$ by the oriented sum of the boundary spin Chern–Simons terms. Small *absolute* total flux in specified frames and small physical volume fix the relative integer to zero in the source band. Small modular flux gives the same statement for the nearest representative. Consequently $E_\pm(U)-D(U)/4\to0$ on such a residual. Independent Cartesian frames on true physical AE and normal-coordinate ends have the requisite small terms after exhaustion. The equality of deficits alone allows a positive common deficit. [Relative integrality](../02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/01_dirac_collar_matching/notes/E12_RELATIVE_CHIRAL_CHARGE_20261125.md) · [Physical end estimates](../01_sections_and_connections/02_dirac_collar_extraction/notes/E06_PHYSICAL_END_FRAMES_20261125.md)

In the positive Schouten metric $h$ of the same conformal class the measure $Q_\pm(h)=|W^\pm|^2dV_h+2\sigma_2(A_h)dV_h$ is positive and its total is $8\pi^2$. The modular distance $d_\pm$ of the signed sum of the end terms satisfies
$$
 \min\{E_\pm(U),E_\pm(M\setminus U)\}\le d_\pm.
$$
The energies on the left can be measured on the original metric. If the exterior carries at least a fixed $\eta>0$, then $d_\pm\to0$ forces $E_\pm(U)\to0$, irrespective of its topology or its interior spinors. With $h=e^{2u}g$, the actual change is $\int_U Q_\pm(h)=\int_UQ_\pm(g)-2J(u,g;\partial U)$, where $J$ uses boundary jets of the measured conformal factor. If the physical charge tends to zero, control of the negative part of $J$ suffices. The two chirality integrals receive the same correction. Even two isometric round metrics related by a dilation can have a finite negative $J$ on shrinking physical annuli. The missing sign cannot be inferred from the global curvature numbers alone. ## The closed alternative and smooth recognition

If exact positive nonzero subcritical Bach-flat classes exist on a fixed smooth $M$, their least energy $a_M$ is attained and is isolated **among their distinct energy values**. In a fixed band every closed sublevel $b<a_M+\kappa_\delta$ is compact modulo diffeomorphisms in unit-volume Yamabe representatives and has finitely many energy values. Same-type descent excludes the smooth ALE alternative there. A real analytic finite-dimensional reduction in a Bach gauge makes the energy constant on nearby critical metrics, including at a degenerate Hessian. The first value may still be strictly above $I^+(M)$. [NECK-E15](../01_sections_and_connections/01_neck_geometry/notes/E15_FIXED_TYPE_CRITICAL_VALUES_20261125.md) · [Independent check](../01_sections_and_connections/01_neck_geometry/reviews/E15_CRITICAL_VALUES_CHECK_20261125.md)

If a positive closed class on a homotopy sphere has a representative with $\delta W^+=0$, then it is conformally round or
$$
 E_+>\frac{16\pi^2}{3}+\frac16\int_M |\operatorname{Ric}_0(g_Y)|^2\,dV_{g_Y}.
$$
Here $g_Y$ is a Yamabe minimizer of the same class. Equality in the harmonic-Weyl gap would yield a parallel nonzero two-form on a homotopy sphere. This excludes the positive Einstein case through $16\pi^2/3$ on **every** smooth homotopy sphere. For a nonround positive Einstein metric of scalar $s$ the TT Hessian has a negative direction exactly when $-\Delta_L$ in the cited convention has TT spectrum in $(s/3,s/2)$. The energy band supplies no eigenvalue in this window. A non-Einstein Bach-flat minimizer need not have a half-harmonic representative. [NECK-E16](../01_sections_and_connections/01_neck_geometry/notes/E16_HARMONIC_WEYL_SUBBAND_20261125.md) · [NECK-E13](../01_sections_and_connections/01_neck_geometry/notes/E13_CLOSED_EINSTEIN_STABILITY_20261125.md)

**Order of limits.** For the exact block first take its midpoint rescaling on a growing dyadic interval, then fix the limit annulus and its buffers, then transfer them for sufficiently large source index. For the perturbation first fix a source metric and its open set, then choose the tiny support and diagonalize in the source index. For all component and charge statements fix finitely many truncations and actual embedded faces; take the index limit or limsup before enlarging ALE radius and shrinking ordinary base radii. Smooth compact convergence for a closed critical sequence is on the original smooth type after diffeomorphisms. Constant rescaling preserves chiral energy, full curvature energy and integrated Bach. Variable conformal changes preserve chiral energy and integrated Bach but change physical volume, the Ricci-square term and the individual chiral connection charges.

On an arbitrary smooth homotopy sphere, construction of a positive subcritical class remains an entry problem. Even when one exists, the approximate sequence may have no extracted separating chart or physical-volume-bearing removable base; an exact nonround closed class can also survive. The same-type conditional identifications above do not decide these cases.
