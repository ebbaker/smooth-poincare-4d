## Research direction and evidence boundary

The mixed continuity and overlap-correction mechanisms remain proposals.
The global problem is to construct compatible recognizing geometry on every
smooth homotopy four-sphere. No round atlas, definite curvature connection,
positive Yamabe conformal class or completed global solver is supplied.

Start with the shared conventions and the proposed section/mixed
benchmarks, but use the evidence to choose the next direction. Relevant
extensions may include other first-order fields, local-to-global gluing,
boundary correction, deformation complexes, alternative continuity schemes,
quantitative defect control, or a different sufficient recognizing endpoint.
These are examples, not an exhaustive or compulsory list. Spinorial ideas
may be explored when they help this construction question.

Retain a common global conformal metric, genuine bundle transitions and
positive-definite Riemannian reality. A collection of separately flat patches
does not solve the overlap problem. Round TT positivity does not supply a
global range theorem. Distinguish exact integrability from positive-Yamabe
small-energy recognition, and account for the estimates and same-manifold
compactness that either proposed construction would require.
