## Why this is the next problem

The [two-sided section estimate](../reviews/SEC_SECTION_ENERGY_20261124.md) identifies the optimized differentiated section defect with the size of E₊, up to two universal constants. The hard step is to change the conformal geometry itself. The [mixed system](../notes/SEC_E03_20261124.md) admits an actual local lift under a full-domain adjoint condition and a [quantitative ball reaching zero](../notes/SEC_E04_20261124.md) under stronger bounds. Uncontrolled arbitrary metrics can have [arbitrarily small positive singular values](../notes/SEC_E11_20261124.md). Neither a uniform estimate nor failure in the specific Weyl direction follows for a suitably chosen reference on an arbitrary M.

A fixed two-ended torus collar has an [uncorrected nonlinear obstruction](../notes/SEC_E18_20261124.md) and [independent proof check](../notes/SEC_E19_20261124.md). [Corrected-source metrics](../notes/SEC_E15_20261124.md) exist in meaningful model classes, but [translation holonomy](../notes/SEC_E20_20261124.md) rules out retaining the flat product structure inside any global exact endpoint. The requirement that both end collars remain fixed prevents curvature or first derivatives from being transmitted to the exterior. A simply connected spherical interface avoids the specific translation obstruction and allows an exterior to respond.
