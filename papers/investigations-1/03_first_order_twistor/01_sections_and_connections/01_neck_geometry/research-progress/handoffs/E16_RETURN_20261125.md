The Gursky gap in the project endomorphism norm is
\(E_+\ge Y^2/24\) for nonzero harmonic \(W^+\) and positive Yamabe
constant. The proof uses the regularized \(w^{1/3}\) test through
zeros of \(w=|W^+|\). Equality forces constant nonzero \(w\) and
parallel \(W^+\) with a simple positive eigenline. This line has a
global parallel unit section on a homotopy four-sphere. Its nonzero
harmonic two-form contradicts \(b_2=0\).

Writing \(T_Y=\int|\operatorname{Ric}_0|^2\) in a Yamabe
minimizer, Gauss–Bonnet gives \(Y^2/24=16\pi^2-2E_++T_Y/2\).
Hence a nonzero half-harmonic class must satisfy
\(E_+>16\pi^2/3+T_Y/6\). This excludes the inclusive lower subband
for positive Einstein metrics, with \(T_Y=0\), and for any class
with a half-harmonic representative. The zero Weyl alternative
is conformally round.

The same computation gives
\(\lambda_1(-6\Delta+R-6\lambda_{\max}(W^+))<0\) in the nonzero
half-harmonic sector on this topology. The sign is conformally
invariant and yields \(36\int\lambda_{\max}(W^+)^2>Y^2\).
The exact identities and independent gap reproof were checked
in accessible primary articles. Gursky's 2000 article was reached
at publisher-metadata level. A general Bach-flat class still
requires a separate argument supplying the harmonic representative
or an alternative estimate.
