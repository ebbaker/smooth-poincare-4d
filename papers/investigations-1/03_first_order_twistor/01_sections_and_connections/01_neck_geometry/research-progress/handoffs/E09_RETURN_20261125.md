For one supplied smooth compactified component \(N\) with finitely
many smooth omissions, whole connected compact-truncation embeddings,
smooth oriented conformal convergence, source Yamabe lower bound
\(y_0>0\) and \(0\le e-e_C<4\pi^2\), the finite-puncture transfer is proved.
The \(H_0\) Mayer–Vietoris incidence sequence gives one complement
component per puncture. At fixed buffer length, then small cut radius,
then large \(i\), the cap estimates give each exterior a positive
Yamabe constant and the summed positive-chiral energy at most
\(e-e_C\) plus an arbitrarily small error. Each capped exterior is a
standard \(S^4\) by Chang–Gursky–Yang. Palais's Theorem C matches the
actual marked cap neighborhoods to the standard cap neighborhoods
in the recognized spheres. Replacing their exteriors by the original
coordinate disks gives an oriented smooth diffeomorphism \(M\cong N\).

Consequently \(I^+(M)\le e_C\le e\) and the entire residual is at
most the near-minimizing excess \(e-I^+(M)\). For a minimizing family
and such a smooth compactified base, \(e=e_C\) and the uncapped exterior
has vanishing chiral energy in the order of source limit followed by
cut exhaustion. A supplied nonflat ALE component with the strict SEC
residual and the upper bound is therefore excluded on a minimizing
family. Positive smooth conformal factors on the fixed comparison
domains suffice. Neither an orbifold cap nor puncture charts without
a simultaneous whole-core embedding are covered.
