using normal charts of the positive-time metric. At fixed normalized time

tensor by at most $C(t^{1/4}+\varepsilon_i+\sqrt t)$ outside a set of both
model and source rescaled volume at most
$Ct^{-1/2}(\varepsilon_i^2+t)$, where $\varepsilon_i$ is the vanishing block
$L^2$ full curvature norm. The new estimate follows by stopping each point
forward and backward in time before its accumulated Ricci norm gets large,

order is source-index limit and then $t\downarrow0$. The chart's physical
radius is of order $\rho_i\sqrt t$.
