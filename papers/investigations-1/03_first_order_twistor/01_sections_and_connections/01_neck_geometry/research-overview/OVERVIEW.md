## Question and role in smooth recognition

On an arbitrary smooth closed oriented homotopy four-sphere \(M\), can a sequence approaching Bach criticality hide Weyl energy between concentration scales? Can one instead find a separating spherical cut that puts a strictly better metric on the same smooth type? Either outcome could make a local deformation usable in the global argument. This branch owns scale selection and link topology for common use, the geometry needed for metric surgery, and energy accounting across the neck. Its geometric outputs may also supply a candidate region to the [Dirac extraction branch](../../02_dirac_collar_extraction/research-overview/OVERVIEW.md). [Parent manuscript §8.2, compactness on the same manifold](../../manuscript/twistor_sections_and_chiral_weyl_curvature.tex#L3010) · [§15, energy between scales](../../manuscript/twistor_sections_and_chiral_weyl_curvature.tex#L6503) · [Common route to SPC4](../../../brainstorm/notes/COLLAR_PROGRAM_AND_SPC4_20261125.md)

Work with smooth Riemannian unit-volume positive Yamabe minimizers \(g_i\). Fix \(\delta>0\), assume \(4\pi^2\le E_i=E_+(g_i)\le8\pi^2-\delta\) and \(\int|B_{g_i}|\,dV_{g_i}\to0\), and pass to a subsequence with \(E_i\to e\). The two chiral Hilbert–Schmidt energies agree on closed \(M\), where the full tensor Weyl integral is \(8E_i\). Use the actual norm on each subregion. The band supplies \(Y([g_i])\ge\sqrt{48\delta}\) and a total curvature budget. A marked round ball may shrink in this gauge. Producing such a sequence on each candidate smooth type, or reaching recognition directly, remains an open problem for the parent investigation. ## Two usable outcomes

**One cut.** Seek a fixed-width oriented embedded cylinder around a separating \(S^3\), with enough retained buffer on each side. In the normalized product gauge \(h_0=dt^2+\gamma_{S^3(1)}\), test a \(C^2\) error \(\eta_2\) giving total added chiral Weyl energy at most \(C\eta_2^2\). Audit the proposed lower bound on each capped Yamabe constant, \(\min\{Y_i,(1-C\eta_2)Y_{\rm rd}\}-C\ell^{-3/2}\), with retained length \(\ell\) on each side. If Bach control is needed afterward, a separate \(C^4\) transition error \(\eta_4\) should give an added \(L^1\) cost \(C\eta_4\). Smoothness of each metric by itself supplies no uniform scale bounds. Select \(\ell\) first and fit all errors inside the strict margins. [Cap construction and estimates](../brainstorm/notes/USING_A_SUPPLIED_COLLAR_20261125.md) · [§11.3, link topology](../../manuscript/twistor_sections_and_chiral_weyl_curvature.tex#L4047)

A separating spherical cut in a homotopy sphere gives two homotopy spheres after capping. Verify this by van Kampen and Mayer–Vietoris. Check the smooth boundary gluing using extension of diffeomorphisms of \(S^3\). This allows either capped summand to have unknown smooth type. If the sum of their energies is strictly below \(8\pi^2\) and both Yamabe signs are positive, at least one summand is standard. When the cut encloses almost all of a supplied nonflat smooth ALE core, test the stronger conclusion that the **exterior** is standard and the core compactification is diffeomorphic to \(M\). Then the residual lower bound gives a definite energy improvement on \(M\). [§§11.4–11.5, compactification and residual](../../manuscript/twistor_sections_and_chiral_weyl_curvature.tex#L4171) · [Topological gluing inputs](../brainstorm/notes/CHIRAL_DIRAC_AND_SURGERY_APPROACHES_20261125.md) · [Global descent test](../../../brainstorm/notes/COLLAR_PROGRAM_AND_SPC4_20261125.md)

**An entire neck.** For adjacent scales \(r_i/R_i\to0\) in identified annuli, seek

$$
\lim_{L\to\infty}\limsup_{i\to\infty}
 \int_{Lr_i<\rho<R_i/L}|W_{g_i}|_{\rm tens}^2\,dV_{g_i}=0.
$$

Section 14 gives separate estimates in a compatible chart with small deviation and bounded second-derivative energy, and using a transverse vector field with small weighted conformal Killing defect and controlled boundary geometry. Obtaining either from the sequence is the main geometric difficulty. With smooth component convergence and exhaustion of **all** complementary regions, the identity \(e=e_0+\sum_j e_j\) would exclude a nonflat smooth ALE bubble by the strict residual and the gap for a closed Bach-flat base. The residual contradiction only needs equality in \(E_+\). Summable chiral estimates from matched spinors could therefore replace the full Weyl estimate on particular strips. A positive closed critical value, a quotient link or a nonsmooth base each calls for its own analysis. ## The master’s separated-quantile shell

The master supplies a fixed-smooth-type minimizing sequence in the same positive Yamabe band. For each fixed fraction \(0<\theta<1/2\), the energy radius \(r_i(\theta)\) is the least physical radius at which some ball has \(\theta E_i\) of positive chiral Weyl energy. If \(r_i(\theta)/r_i(1/2)\to0\), choose an attaining center \(x_i\) for the smaller radius. Mark the full ball \(B_{g_i}(x_i,r_i(\theta))\) and the entire exterior \(M\setminus B_{g_i}(x_i,r_i(1/2))\), with masses \(\theta E_i\) and at least \(E_i/2\), respectively. The full curvature budget selects a physical distance block of any fixed logarithmic width with vanishing curvature integral. [Master energy-radius alternative](../../../../../master/manuscript/complete_limits.tex#L280) · [NECK-E11](../notes/E11_BALANCED_BLOCK_AND_NONCOLLAPSE_20261125.md)

The [master’s critical-annulus proposition](../../../../../master/manuscript/critical_annulus.tex#L10) supplies the splice once there is one oriented smooth embedded \(A(1/8,8)\) in the selected block, with both marks outside the closed trim \(1/4\le |x|\le4\) and on opposite sides of its middle sphere. The constant rescaling of the pulled-back physical metric must meet a fixed small Euclidean distortion threshold, be harmonic or have coordinate tension with derivative tending to zero in \(L^2(dx)\), and have full curvature tending to zero in \(L^2\) there. The Yamabe and chiral margins then exclude this configuration at the fixed-type infimum. The master’s statement uses near-minimality. ## Companion investigations

The round product has tangential chiral curvature forms, hence \(F_i\wedge F_j=0\). Curvature-only definite reconstruction there loses the metric scale and plane data. Independent compatible \((\Sigma,A)\) data still make sense. To restart chiral evolution on a capped component would require a suitable definite region or another reconstruction with its own hypotheses, a global bundle identification, compatibility estimates and an admissible next step. Meanwhile the unit round \(S^3\) has a tangential Dirac gap. A weaker collar may therefore be usable by extraction and matching even if the cap estimate fails.
