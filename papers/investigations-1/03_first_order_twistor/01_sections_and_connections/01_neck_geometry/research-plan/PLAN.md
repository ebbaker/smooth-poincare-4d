## Starting choices

The supplied-collar audit and geometric selection can begin independently. The first asks what follows if the cut already exists. The second asks what the almost-critical sequence itself forces. A third path can use the parent annular calculation as soon as an applicable result is available. The exit from each path is a statement about a particular region, followed by a separate check of what it implies on the original smooth manifold. [Overview](../research-overview/OVERVIEW.md) · [SPC4 argument](../../../brainstorm/notes/COLLAR_PROGRAM_AND_SPC4_20261125.md) · [Original eight-task proposal](../brainstorm/notes/PROPOSED_OVERVIEW_AND_PLAN_20261125.md)

| What the sequence supplies | Test first | If that premise fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full embedded smooth ALE core and differentiated end decay | Transfer a remote spherical collar and isolate the capped exterior. | Keep any exterior recognition. Examine the missing smooth identification, or switch to a budget-selected block. |
| Only separated scales and bounded curvature energy | Select a low-energy block. Determine its topology, chart and buffer quality. | Try a different block, integrated cap costs, or the sibling's first-order strip. A quotient link changes the filling question. |
| Usable estimates across a chain | Test the one-chart energy bound or the intrinsic transverse field with both ends retained. | Locate length growth, drift or an uncontrolled weighted term and switch formulations with the parent. |
| A smooth compact base, or a positive closed critical value | Check exhaustion of all other regions or the precise scope of a descent/critical-value contradiction. | Return a surviving closed critical case to the global selection problem. |

One candidate **for the parent to choose** is a linearized bound on a Euclidean annulus with dyadic shells \(A_j\),
\[
\sum_j\|W'(v)\|_{L^2(A_j)}
\ \le C\big(\|B'(v)\|_{L^1}+\mathcal T_{\rm ends}(v)\big).
\]
The end term must control the admitted modes in the two rescaled end shells. An endpoint whole-space bound does not itself sum the shell norms. A quasilinear test against the metric or the intrinsic transverse field offer other starting points. NECK-B02 uses an assessed parent estimate to control actual charts or fields. The parent owns the choice and execution of its first analytic test. [SEC-B36 handoff](../../continuation-notes/05_AFTER_ANNULAR_ENERGY_AND_CONSERVATION_20261124.md) · [§14.5, linearization](../../manuscript/twistor_sections_and_chiral_weyl_curvature.tex#L5946) · [§14.6, interaction of the estimates](../../manuscript/twistor_sections_and_chiral_weyl_curvature.tex#L6294)

The exact Bach-flat comparison of Chang–Qing–Yang, Theorems 2.4 and 3.1, is a benchmark. In positive Yamabe gauge the neck theorem uses, among other conditions, \(b_1=0\), scale separation, an inner area upper bound and small annular curvature. Its conclusion permits a finite spherical quotient and gives locally rescaled \(C^{1,\alpha}\) control. For an \(L^1\)-small Bach sequence, test the missing regularity and link identification explicitly before applying a cap model. ## Bounded research tasks

These are initial choices for an activated research session. Current status belongs to the [register](../research-progress/REGISTER.md). B decisions choose the mathematical route. Before an E check starts, freeze its source, hypothesis, method, finite scope, proof or counterexample criterion and disjoint output. The recommended model is given for each task. The selected lead retains responsibility for integration.

### SP4-FOT-NECK-E01

**Audit one metric cut.** E proof check. Use one pair of fixed transition profiles. Compute the combined chiral Weyl cost, localize the Yamabe form on both sides and isolate the fourth-derivative Bach cost. Give the order of choices of retained length and errors needed for both strict margins.

**Inputs** — Supplied spherical collar and the two fixed cap constructions in the supplied-collar note.

**Completion test** — A derivation with the dimensional scale of each term and separate conclusions for energy, sign and Bach error, or the first term the claimed bound misses.

**Revision** — B01 can change buffer placement or the regularity used for the affected cost.

**Recommended model** — gpt-6.2-sol/ultra. The cap profiles, norms and localization test are specified.

### SP4-FOT-NECK-E02

**Recognize the exterior of a supplied smooth bubble.** E proof check. Choose the retained length, then the fixed end radius, then large sequence index. Track the omitted tail, comparison and cap error. Identify the capped exterior by strict recognition, the capped core by a smooth identification with the compactification, and the boundary map reconstructing the connected sum.

**Inputs** — E01's Weyl and Yamabe bounds and the full embedded smooth ALE core of SEC §11, with differentiated end decay.

**Completion test** — A one-bubble implication with an explicit margin and smooth identifications, or the exact missing comparison, even if exterior recognition alone survives.

**Revision** — B03 reassesses the same-smooth-type gain if the inner identification or the sign fails.

**Recommended model** — gpt-6.2-sol/ultra. One specified residual-budget and gluing argument can be checked.

### SP4-FOT-NECK-B01

**Choose a cut and identify its link.** B decision. For a fixed number of buffered dyadic shells, use the total budget to select a low-energy block when the annular decomposition exists. List the missing volume, topology, coordinate and derivative bounds. Select one source of these bounds, or formulate an integrated cap estimate. Send any scale and link data to extraction.

**Inputs** — The band curvature budget, two separated scales and the exact Bach-flat neck theorem as a comparison.

**Completion test** — A stated sequence class, candidate block, intended topology, testable regularity bound and a reason to prefer it to the alternatives.

**Revision** — If curvature spikes or a quotient link defeat the cut, test a different block, a supplied smooth core or a first-order spin strip. Record the affected class.

**Recommended model** — Astra/ultra. Scale, topology and cap regularity can require different geometric mechanisms.

### SP4-FOT-NECK-E03

**Extract a fixed-width metric cut.** E proof check. On one block with buffers, build the chosen embedding and verify scale-independent bounds for the physical metric in the proposed gauge. Specify whether these support Weyl cost, Yamabe sign and Bach error separately.

**Inputs** — B01 selects a comparison class, method and one claim.

**Completion test** — An actual estimate with all input norms and a link identification, or a counterexample or missing hypothesis in that one construction. Send the chart to extraction when usable.

**Revision** — B01 reopens the failed implication or changes the cut.

**Recommended model** — gpt-6.2-sol/ultra. The selected chart or cap estimate will have a fixed method and norm.

### SP4-FOT-NECK-B02

**Turn an annular estimate into control of the geometry.** B decision. For one chain, choose how to control overlap drift and construct a single compatible chart, or build the transverse field of SEC §14.4. Specify admissible end data, scale-invariant norms and constants. Identify what still has to be extracted.

**Inputs** — Parent SEC-B36 and an applicable assessed estimate, including SEC-E62 if used. An independent quantified field or chart can be proposed under a recorded revision.

**Completion test** — A geometric implication with stated inputs, a construction on one chain and an E04 test whose constants can be tracked.

**Revision** — Return a missing analytic estimate to SEC-B36. Consider the intrinsic field or retain the independent cut route.

**Recommended model** — Astra/ultra. The analytic output can control different geometric objects.

### SP4-FOT-NECK-E04

**Account for one long neck.** E proof check. Apply one chosen §14 estimate with both end cutoffs. Track constants as log-length grows. At fixed truncation take the sequence limsup and then send the truncation parameter to infinity.

**Inputs** — B02 fixes the construction and its required geometric inputs on the whole chain.

**Completion test** — One long-neck conclusion in the stated class, or a quantified surviving mode, end flux or length-dependent remainder.

**Revision** — B02 changes the gauge, field or estimate if drift or a boundary term survives.

**Recommended model** — gpt-6.2-sol/ultra. The input class and limit order will be fixed.

### SP4-FOT-NECK-B03

**Choose the next global implication.** B decision. For a cut, identify the metric on the remaining smooth type, the admissible next class and a termination or minimizing-sequence argument. For accounting, cover the complement of a finite smooth comparison family. Test the location of any quotient, orbifold region, tail or closed critical value.

**Inputs** — E02 or E04 supplies the chosen route, or a scoped adverse result motivates a revision.

**Completion test** — A named global hypothesis and consequence with the first unsupported implication and a finite E05 request.

**Revision** — If a closed critical class survives, return to initialization or the parent critical-value problem. If an infinite tail survives, retain the covered-region estimate.

**Recommended model** — Astra/ultra. The choice of descent or exhaustion changes what is required globally.

### SP4-FOT-NECK-E05

**Verify one descent or accounting implication.** E proof check. For one finite smooth comparison family check exhaustion, equality and residual contradiction, or for one capped piece check strict decrease on the same smooth type and every premise of its proposed next use.

**Inputs** — B03 supplies one case and its component, margin and identification data.

**Completion test** — An implication listing each hypothesis and the exact recognition, exclusion or decrease obtained, or a named remainder with its size.

**Revision** — B03 revisits termination, restart or missing regions. Escalate any change of source class.

**Recommended model** — gpt-6.2-sol/ultra. The global statement and its allowed component class will be fixed.

## Revisiting a construction

When a cap norm or Yamabe margin fails, reopen B01 and record whether the underlying embedded strip remains useful to EXTRACT. A growing remainder on a long chain reopens B02 or the parent analytic decision. If an exterior is recognized but the inner identification fails, B03 keeps the exterior statement and examines exactly the missing smooth map. Preserve earlier task identities, counterexamples and issued requests. Date each changed premise, name the result and sibling that relied on it, and issue a fresh bounded request for the new case. Continue other ready paths.
