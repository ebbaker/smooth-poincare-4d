Work in smooth volume-one positive Yamabe minimizers \(g_i\) with \(e_i=E_+(g_i)\to I^+(M)\), where \(I^+(M)\) is the infimum on positive Yamabe classes on \(M\). Keep \(4\pi^2\le e_i\le8\pi^2-\delta\) and \(R_{g_i}=Y([g_i])\ge y_0=\sqrt{48\delta}\) for a fixed \(\delta>0\). $$

\qquad
\lambda_i(D_{i,-})=\theta e_i,\quad \lambda_i(D_{i,+})\ge e_i/2 .
$$

## Completion and failure

1. **Physical transition test.** In one admissible block, ask for \(D_i(s_i)=\operatorname{Vol}_{g_i} A_{g_i}(x_i,s_i,2s_i)\le Cs_i^4\) with \(C\) independent of \(i\). With \(c_0\) the uniform physical lower ball-volume coefficient for radii at most one and \(D=2/c_0\) the diameter bound, put \(c_Y=(1-2^{-1/2})y_0\sqrt{c_0}/6\). For \(C>0\), set \(\kappa=(C/c_Y)^{1/2}\). Choose the tested depth \(R_i>2s_i\) with \(R_i/s_i>\max\{2+\kappa,\kappa D\}\) and \(\kappa s_i<1\), keeping the relevant radial windows protected in the block. Alternatively, the direct E25 test at a chosen depth is \(D_i(s_i)<c_Ys_i^2\min\{1,R_i-2s_i\}^2\). If the two-arm exclusion applies, locate the full median exterior relative to the deep component. The component count leaves the embedding, source crossing test and critical estimates as separate obligations. For large \(i\) verify \(e_i+\gamma_{i,+}+\gamma_{i,-}<8\pi^2\) and \(\Delta_i+\max\{\gamma_{i,+},\gamma_{i,-}\}<\beta\).
