## Question and role in smooth recognition

Which collars can one **find on the degenerating metrics themselves** on an arbitrary smooth oriented homotopy four-sphere \(M\), with enough control to transmit a spinor? Seek an embedded strip, a choice of slices and a spin-compatible conformal gauge in which quantitative hypotheses of the [matching investigation](../../../02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/01_dirac_collar_matching/research-overview/OVERVIEW.md) hold. Its abstract estimates specify what the geometry must supply. The [neck investigation](../../01_neck_geometry/research-overview/OVERVIEW.md) provides candidate scales, topology and any available annular comparisons. Extraction may pick a different strip, and can begin with a supplied ALE end or one chart before the entire neck is understood. Give the fixed margin \(\delta>0\), the separated scales \(r_i\ll R_i\), and the region where the embedding is sought. Here \(E_+\) is the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of \(W^+\) integrated on the closed source. The oriented homotopy sphere has a unique spin structure. Record the induced spin structure and transition lifts on each comparison region, especially if a local link is \(S^3/\Gamma\). A quotient comparison by itself does not give a spherical cut. The parent proves a trivial link for its full smooth embedded ALE core under its stated hypotheses. ## What to deliver first

Seek orientation-preserving embeddings of \(I\times S^3\) into the identified region, with \(|I|\ge2\) fixed, specified inner and outer ends, physical radii and spin frame. Record the positive conformal factor from the pulled-back physical metric to the comparison metric \(h\). Set \(h_0=dt^2+\gamma_{S^3(1)}\) and \(k=h-h_0\). The first candidate sufficient condition for the sibling's fixed-projection problem is

$$
\varepsilon_D=\|k\|_{L^\infty(I\times S^3)}
 +\sup_{\substack{J\subset I\\1\le |J|\le2}}
       \|\nabla^{h_0} k\|_{L^4(J\times S^3,h_0)}
 \le\varepsilon_* .
$$

The threshold \(\varepsilon_*\) belongs to the operator estimate. Geometry returns a measured bound or a quantity tending to zero, along with the gauge and uniformity that made it small. If \(h=\Omega^2 g\) on the collar, the four-dimensional Dirac spinor and Dirac image carry factors \(\Omega^{-3/2}\) and \(\Omega^{-5/2}\) under the canonical spin identification. State the induced changes of measure and trace norms, particularly when \(\Omega\) varies between the two ends. [Proposed transmission criterion](../../../02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/01_dirac_collar_matching/brainstorm/notes/RELAXING_COLLAR_HYPOTHESES_20261125.md) · [Dirac §4.1, cap normalization](../../../02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/manuscript/dirac_twistor_and_yamabe_rigidity.tex#L516)

The calibration begins with a supplied buffered Euclidean annulus, \(g=\delta_{\rm eucl}+v\), \(a=\|v\|_\infty\), \(K=\|D^2v\|_2\). Section 14.3 bounds \(\|\eta Dv\|_4^2\le Ca(K+a)\) on an interior region. Test whether \(t=\log(\rho/\rho_0)\) and conformal rescaling by \(\rho^{-2}\) give \(\varepsilon_D\le C(a+\sqrt{a(K+a)})\), independent of physical scale. The harder result would extract one such chart or an intrinsically suitable slice family from the original sequence. A fixed-width low-energy block is a place to look. Its energy bound alone supplies neither an embedding nor a first-order gauge. ## How the question can change

If the metric fails to approximate one product, seek a varying slice family for which the identified operator has the form \(\partial_t+A_t+C_t\). Measure the tangential gap or a finite-dimensional low-mode space, slice elliptic constants, normal variation, and the lower-order remainder in the norms agreed with matching. If adjacent good strips admit incompatible spin gauges, a single strip can still support a cut test. A long-neck estimate requires compatible identifications and bounds independent of the number of strips. The flat germ for a Green pole belongs at the cap puncture. Exact conformal insertion through a flat overlap is a stronger use. If these strips cover the residual regions with summably vanishing \(E_+\) errors, the one-chirality identity in §12.4 can feed the smooth-bubble exclusion. Otherwise a global smooth limit or another recognition criterion must still connect the local control to the smooth type of \(M\). Metric cap costs or a definite chiral restart have their own stronger inputs.
