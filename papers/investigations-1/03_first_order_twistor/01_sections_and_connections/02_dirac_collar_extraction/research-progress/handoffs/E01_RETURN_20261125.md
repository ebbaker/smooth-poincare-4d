**Conditional estimate.** For SEC §14.3's supplied buffered chart and
a strip of length at least two in the cutoff plateau, the log-radius
cylinder satisfies
\[
 \|L_h-L_0\|_{H^1(S_0^+)\to L^2(S_0^+)}
 \le C\varepsilon_D \le C\bigl(a+\sqrt{a(K+a)}\bigr).
\]
Here the spaces use \(h_0=dt^2+\gamma_{S^3(1)}\) and the lifted
symmetric frame. The constant is independent of either physical
radius and the strip length. Frame derivatives contribute \(O(a)\)
on each unit-size slab, while \(\rho Dv\) is exactly scale critical
against \(dV_0=\rho^{-4}\Phi^*dx\). The slab Sobolev argument acts on
unrestricted \(H^1\) and hence on the matching branch's reference
spectral boundary domain.

The outward normals at the inner and outer ends have reference signs
\(-\partial_t\) and \(+\partial_t\). The conformal spinor and image
weights are \(\rho^{3/2}\) and \(\rho^{5/2}\). Both end traces use the
fixed reference \(H^{1/2}\) spaces with the factors
\(\rho_\pm^{3/2}\) after fiber identification. Intrinsic end \(L^2\)
is preserved for the conformally weighted spinor, since surface area
changes by \(\rho_\pm^{-3}\). The proof records the corresponding
physical weighted bulk and trace spaces.
