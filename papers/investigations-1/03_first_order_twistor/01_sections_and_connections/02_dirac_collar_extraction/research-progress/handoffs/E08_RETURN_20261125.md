**Exclusion with a physical base.** The smooth physically
compared removable base has volume one, scalar and Yamabe
value \(y=\lim Y_i\), and is exactly Bach-flat. A nonround
base has \(e_N>4\pi^2\) and a nonflat smooth SEC ALE
component has \(e_X>4\pi^2\), contradicting

base has \(y=Y_{\rm rd}\). The ALE compactification would
have \(Y_X\ge y\) by compact tests and the smooth-point
cutoff, but \(Y_X<Y_{\rm rd}\) by strict rigidity.
No energy identity is needed for either exclusion.

gives \(e=e_N=I^+(M)\) and the exact closed attaining

most \(e-I^+(M)\) once that type transfer is available.

volume-one round. The residual volume vanishes on
exhaustion, but the residual energy is \(e\ge4\pi^2\)

\(4e\). E09's strict transfer threshold is therefore
unavailable. This leaves a residual geometry problem
without a supplied nonflat smooth ALE component.
Fixed-radius round comparison traces do not identify
source core traces.
