## What an exterior must be allowed to change

An ASD Taub–NUT ball with the radius specified in E07 has E₋=11π²/2. It embeds in a coordinate chart of any M and extends smoothly as reference data. The signature identity forces at least 11π²/2 of self-dual Weyl energy **outside** the retained ball. Even strict approximate recognition via the CGY energy condition is then unavailable. This obstruction depends on retaining the conformal data on that finite ball. [Finite-core calculation](../notes/SEC_E07_20261124.md)

For a torus collar with both ends fixed flat, a seed can admit a flat endpoint while the chord between their Weyl fields is unrealizable at every intermediate parameter in the diagonal synchronous family. The scalar reduction is −y″+λ(y₀″/y₀)y=0 with Neumann conditions. Its endpoint quadratic forms have distinct null lines. For 0<λ<1 the interpolated form is positive. [Exact obstruction](../notes/SEC_E13_20261124.md)

The source can be corrected while preserving both flat ends. One even interior bump, tuned by the lowest Neumann eigenvalue, suffices for axial even seeds of any amplitude. For general small determinant-one symmetric torus-orbit metrics in synchronous coordinates, two moments in each of five matrix components give a codimension-ten local image. Ten fixed interior profiles correct the chord with size O(λ(1−λ)‖A₀‖²) and zero correction at the endpoints. These are genuine nonlinear collar solutions. For unrestricted tangentially varying sources the flat derivative with fixed ends has infinitely many independent adjoint pairings. A finite list of invariant profiles cannot handle arbitrary overlap discrepancies.
