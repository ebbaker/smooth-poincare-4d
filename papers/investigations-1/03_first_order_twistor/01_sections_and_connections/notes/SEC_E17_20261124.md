## Flat model and admissible fields

Use \(I=[-1,1]\), \(T^3=(\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z)^3\),
\(\Omega=\operatorname{int}(I)\times T^3\) and the real Riemannian
metric \(g_0=ds^2+\sum_{a=1}^3d\theta_a^2\). Put
\(e^0=ds\), \(e^a=d\theta_a\) and orient by \(e^0\wedge e^1\wedge
e^2\wedge e^3\), as in the [E13 collar][e13]. Use \(W^+=0\) for
ASD and the curvature-operator Hilbert–Schmidt target pairing of
[SH-B01][common] and [E02][e02]. Define
\[
 X=C_c^\infty(\Omega;S^2_0T^*),\qquad
 Y=C_c^\infty(\Omega;S^2_0\Lambda^+),\qquad
 D:X\longrightarrow Y,\quad D h=(W^+)'_{g_0}h .
\]
These are real spaces of smooth fields. In particular, \(X\) includes
all four-dimensional trace-free variations with compact support in the
open collar. Each is tangent to the fixed-volume metrics
\(g_\epsilon=g_0(e^{\epsilon h^\sharp}\cdot,\cdot)\), equal to \(g_0\)
near both ends. In differentiating \(W^+\) the derivative of the
bundle identification multiplies the zero background curvature.
[SEC-B01 fixed-volume chart][b01]

## Self-dual, null and trace-free modes

Write \(z=e^0+i e^1\), \(u=e^2+i e^3\) and \(\xi=z\wedge u\).
For the complex-bilinear extension of the Euclidean pairing,
\(z\cdot z=u\cdot u=z\cdot u=0\). With \(e^{ij}=e^i\wedge e^j\),
\[
 \xi=A+iB,\qquad A=e^{02}-e^{13},\qquad B=e^{03}+e^{12}.
\]
Since \(*e^{02}=-e^{13}\) and \(*e^{03}=e^{12}\), both \(A\) and
\(B\) are self-dual. They are orthogonal, each with squared norm \(2\),
so \(\langle\xi,\xi\rangle_{\mathbb C}=0\). For each integer \(k\ge1\)
put
\[
 Z_k=e^{ks+ik\theta_1}\xi\otimes\xi,\qquad
 T^k_{ij\,p q}=e^{ks+ik\theta_1}\xi_{ij}\xi_{p q}.
\]
The rank-one tensor denotes the endomorphism
\(\eta\mapsto \langle\xi,\eta\rangle_{\mathbb C}\xi\), times the
exponential. Its trace is zero by bilinear nullity. Extend by zero on
\(\Lambda^-\) to identify \(Z_k\) with the four-index tensor \(T^k\).
For this identification the endomorphism matrix in the orthonormal
basis \(e^{ij}\), \(i<j\), consists of the components with unordered
pairs counted once. [Chang–Gursky–Yang, p. 107, Remark 2][cgy]

The four-index tensor is skew in each pair and symmetric under
exchange of pairs. The first Bianchi identity follows from
\(\xi\wedge\xi=0\), since \(\xi\) is decomposable. Its Ricci
contraction vanishes because
\[
 \sum_i\xi_{ij}\xi_{i q}
 =(z\cdot z)u_j u_q-(z\cdot u)(u_j z_q+z_j u_q)
   +(u\cdot u)z_j z_q=0.
\]
The other contractions follow from the curvature symmetries. Also
\(\sum_i z_i\xi_{ij}=(z\cdot z)u_j-(z\cdot u)z_j=0\).
Integer \(k\) makes \(Z_k\) smooth and single-valued on the torus.

## Direct adjoint calculation

Use the sign \(R(X,Y)=[\nabla_X,\nabla_Y]-\nabla_{[X,Y]}\) and
\(R_{ij\,p q}=\langle R(e_i,e_j)e_q,e_p\rangle\) in the parallel
frame, giving \(R_{ij\,ij}\) as the sectional curvature. At the flat
metric the variation of the Christoffel symbol is
\(\dot\Gamma^p_{j q}=(\partial_j h_{qp}+\partial_q h_{jp}
-\partial_p h_{jq})/2\). It gives
\[
 \dot R_{ij\,p q}
 =\tfrac12(\partial_i\partial_q h_{jp}
          +\partial_j\partial_p h_{iq}
          -\partial_i\partial_p h_{jq}
          -\partial_j\partial_q h_{ip}).
\]
[E13 curvature sign][e13]

To pair \(Dh\) with \(Z_k\), extend to four indices. The self-dual
projection fixes \(Z_k\), and its vanishing contractions kill the
Ricci and scalar terms subtracted from \(\dot R\). Thus, with
summation over all ordered indices,
\[
 \langle Dh,Z_k\rangle_{\mathbb C,\mathrm{HS}}
 =\tfrac14\sum_{i,j,p,q}\dot R_{ij\,p q}T^k_{ij\,p q}
 =-\tfrac12\sum_{i,j,p,q}T^k_{ij\,p q}\partial_i\partial_p h_{jq}.
 \tag{1}
\]
The factor \(1/4\) counts the orderings of the two antisymmetric
pairs. The four curvature terms have the same contribution after
swapping dummy indices and using skew symmetry. Integrating twice
by parts gives the complex-linear extension of the real formal
adjoint on this field,
\[
 (D^*Z_k)_{jq}=-\tfrac12\sum_{i,p}\partial_i\partial_p T^k_{ij\,p q}.
 \tag{2}
\]
The expression is symmetric by pair exchange and trace-free by
vanishing contractions. Compact support of \(h\) removes the two
normal boundary terms, and all tangential integrations are periodic.
Equation (2) gives the flat double divergence computed in the
operator pairing, in comparison with the four-index formal-adjoint
formula of [Gover–Gursky, §1.1, p. 295][gg].

In fact the first divergence already vanishes:
\[
 \sum_i\partial_iT^k_{ij\,p q}
 =k e^{ks+ik\theta_1}\left(\sum_i z_i\xi_{ij}\right)\xi_{p q}=0.
\]
This proves \(D^*Z_k=0\). For the Hermitian extension, take the pairing
linear in its first slot, so the field paired against \(Dh\) in (1)
is \(\overline{Z_k}\). The real coefficients of \(D^*\) give
\(D^*\overline{Z_k}=\overline{D^*Z_k}=0\). In particular
\(\operatorname{Re}Z_k\) is a real formal-adjoint solution.

## Independent compactly supported tests and corrections

For real \(F\in Y\) define
\[
 \ell_k(F)=\int_\Omega
       \langle F,\operatorname{Re}Z_k\rangle_{\mathrm{HS}}\,dV_{g_0}.
\]
For every \(h\in X\), \(\ell_k(Dh)=0\) by (1)–(2). To see
independence choose any nonzero \(\chi\ge0\) in
\(C_c^\infty(\operatorname{int}I)\), and set
\[
 P=A\otimes A-B\otimes B,\quad Q=A\otimes B+B\otimes A,\qquad
 F_m=\chi(s)\cos(m\theta_1)P\quad(m\ge1).
\]
Both \(P\) and \(Q\) are real symmetric trace-free endomorphisms
of \(\Lambda^+\). The operator pairing gives
\(\langle P,Q\rangle_{\mathrm{HS}}=0\) and \(|P|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2=8\).
Then \(\operatorname{Re}Z_k=e^{ks}(\cos(k\theta_1)P-\sin(k\theta_1)Q)\).
Fourier orthogonality with coordinate period \(2\pi\) gives
\[
 \ell_k(F_m)
 = 8(2\pi)^2\pi\,\delta_{km}\int_I\chi(s)e^{ks}\,ds .
\]
The diagonal entries are positive. Each \(F_m\) has compact support
in the open collar and detects the \(m\)-th restriction independently.

For any fixed finite-dimensional correction space \(C\subset Y\) of
dimension \(d\), a nonzero linear combination of
\(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_{d+1}\) vanishes on \(C\).
The diagonal tests show it is nonzero on \(Y\). Hence
\(D X+C\) is a proper subspace of \(Y\) for every such \(C\), and
the algebraic quotient \(Y/D X\) is infinite-dimensional. If the
profiles are torus invariant, Fourier integration kills each
profile in every \(\ell_k\) separately. This applies in particular
to the ten prescribed profiles for the invariant model of
[SEC-E15][e15]. Thus their invariant moment role supplies no
correction for these tangential restrictions on the unrestricted
linear problem.
