## Statement

On the standard oriented smooth \(S^4\), fix once and for all a smooth
oriented extended coordinate chart \(\psi:B_{1+d}\to S^4\) with
\(d>0\) and \(K=\psi(\overline B_1)\). Fix the unit round
stereographic representative \(\psi^*g=\Omega^2\delta\) on
\(\overline B_1\), where \(\Omega(x)=2/(1+|x|^2)\). Let
\(\mathcal G_{K,0}\) be the smooth real positive-definite extensions
of this representative with conformal-class Yamabe constant zero.
We use \(W^+=0\) for ASD and
\[
 E_\pm(g)=\int_{S^4}|W^\pm_g|_{\mathrm{op,HS}}^2\,dV_g,\qquad
 \mathfrak B(g)=\int_{S^4}|B_g|_g\,dV_g .
\]
The first two norms are Hilbert–Schmidt norms of curvature
endomorphisms. \(B_g\) is the covariant Bach tensor in
Bahuaud–Guenther–Isenberg–Mazzeo's convention. With these norms,
\[
 \inf_{g\in\mathcal G_{K,0}}E_+(g)=8\pi^2
 \quad\text{and the infimum is unattained}.
 \tag{1}
\]
There is a sequence \(g_R\in\mathcal G_{K,0}\) for \(R\to\infty\)
with \(E_+(g_R)=E_-(g_R)=8\pi^2+O(R^{-1})\) and
\(\mathfrak B(g_R)=O(R^{-1})\). On the unit-volume scalar-flat
Yamabe representatives \(k_R\in[g_R]\) one has
\[
 \int_{S^4}|\operatorname{Ric}_{k_R}|_{k_R}^2\,dV_{k_R}
   =4(E_+(g_R)-8\pi^2)\longrightarrow0,\qquad
 \mathfrak B(k_R)=\mathfrak B(g_R)\longrightarrow0 .
 \tag{2}
\]

## Common smooth double and neck coordinates

Use the two \(\ell=1\) Taub–NUT balls \(r\le R\) of E29, with
opposite orientations and the identity boundary direction
marking. The balls are standard coordinate four-balls in the nut
coordinates and the cylinder is glued using the same \(S^3\)
coordinates at each end. This is the smooth double realized by
two latitude caps and their product complement on the standard
sphere. Use the E29 radial diffeomorphisms as \(R\) varies. They
are the identity in the common inner \(r\le1/2\) balls, and match
the seam coordinate \(u=r/R\) near the boundary. Take the common
cylinder \(N=[-T,T]\times S^3\), with the fixed \(T=L+2\) of the
negative construction in E35. One may choose this axial interval
on the \(R=1\) double from the outset. Its length is independent
of \(R\). In scaled metrics, both cores carry \(R^{-2}g_{\rm TN}\).
[E29 double and identifications][e29] · [E35 common neck][e35]

Put \(V_R(u)=1+(2Ru)^{-1}\), \(u_-(t)=1+t+T\) near the left
seam and \(u_+(t)=1+T-t\) near the right seam. The common scaled
Taub–NUT germ at each seam is
\[
 S_{R,\pm}=V_R(u_\pm)
       (dt^2+u_\pm^2\pi^*g_{S^2})
       +R^{-2}V_R(u_\pm)^{-1}\eta^2 .
 \tag{3}
\]
Here \(\eta\) is the common Hopf connection and the fibre period
is \(2\pi\). These are already the end germs of the scaled
negative metric \(\widehat g_R^-\) of E35.

Put the positive E34 metric on this neck by an increasing map
\(a_R:[-T,T]\to[-1,1]\). With
\(D_R(u)=\int_1^u\sqrt{V_R(v)}\,dv\), prescribe
\(a_R(t)=-1+D_R(u_-(t))\) near the left seam and
\(a_R(t)=1-D_R(u_+(t))\) near the right seam. In the E34
coordinate \(a_R\), the slice profile is exactly the outward
unit radial coordinate from the nearest seam in these collars.
Therefore \(a_R'^2=V_R(u_\pm)\), the pulled-back slice
coefficients correspond to \(u_\pm\), and the metric agrees
with (3) there. For a uniformly controlled extension of \(a_R\)
write \(d_\pm(t)=\sqrt{V_R(u_\pm(t))}\) in the end collars.
Use fixed disjoint end cutoffs \(b_\pm\) equal to one near
their respective seams. Choose the collar widths small enough
for these maps to stay within the exact E34 seam germs and
for \(\int(b_-d_-+b_+d_+)<1\) for all \(R\). Set
\[
 a_R'=b_-d_-+b_+d_++\gamma_R(1-b_--b_+),\qquad
 \gamma_R=\frac{2-\int(b_-d_-+b_+d_+)}
                 {\int(1-b_--b_+)}
 \tag{4}
\]
with integrals over \([-T,T]\). Integrating from \(-T\) with
value \(-1\) gives the two prescribed endpoint germs, including
the right endpoint value. The derivative is bounded above and
below by positive constants and has uniform bounds through
order four. The map and its inverse have uniform bounds through
order five in these coordinates. This accounts for the extra
derivative when pulling back a metric with four bounded
derivatives. Use the identity in \(u\) across the seams and on
the two core balls. Pullback gives a positive endpoint
\(\widehat g_R^+\) on the same double and preserves its Yamabe
sign. [E34 radial join and seam germs][e34] ·
[E35 reparametrization][e35]

## Convex path and full uniform estimates

For \(0\le b\le1\), on the common cylinder set
\(\widehat g_{R,b}=(1-b)\widehat g_R^++b\widehat g_R^-\).
The convex combination is of the full metric matrices on the
same neck coordinates. On the cores set
\(\widehat g_{R,b}=R^{-2}g_{\rm TN}\). The common germ (3) is
identical throughout two fixed seam neighborhoods, so this
gives a smooth path of smooth positive metrics with exact
matching to both cores.

Take finitely many fixed relatively compact base charts for
the Hopf bundle. Write \(\eta=d\phi+\alpha\) in a local
trivialization and work on the lifted coordinate
\(\zeta=\phi/R\). Then \(R^{-1}\eta=d\zeta+R^{-1}\alpha\).
The assessed lifted matrix bounds of E34 and E35, together
with (4), give uniform upper and positive lower metric
matrix bounds in \((t,x^1,x^2,\zeta)\), and uniform bounds
through four derivatives. The positive lower bound and the
derivative bounds persist along the matrix interpolation,
and the inverse and its derivatives are controlled by the
fixed lower bound. All are uniform in \(R\) and \(b\).
Curvature and its first two covariant derivatives are
uniformly bounded, since the local curvature formulas use
at most these four derivatives. The determinant bound and
the quotient period \(2\pi/R\) in the \(\zeta\) direction
give \(\operatorname{Vol}_{\widehat g_{R,b}}(N)=O(R^{-1})\).
These are local lifted coordinates on a neck with a
collapsing fibre period.

In the lifts both chiral Weyl endomorphisms and the Bach norm
are uniformly bounded. The latter follows from the
Schouten formula for \(B\), with two contracted second
derivatives of Schouten and the Schouten–Weyl product.
Thus the neck integrals of the squared chiral norms and
the unsquared Bach norm are each \(O(R^{-1})\).
In the core metrics \(B\equiv0\) by Ricci-flatness. One
core supplies the nonzero block in each chirality, with
norm energy
\[
 A_R=8\pi^2\left(1-\frac{4}{(1+2R)^3}
                  +\frac{3}{(1+2R)^4}\right)
     =8\pi^2+O(R^{-3})
 \tag{5}
\]
by the assessed E07/E29 calculation. Write
\(g^0_{R,b}=R^2\widehat g_{R,b}\) for the path with the original
unscaled cores. Both chiral energies and \(\mathfrak B\)
are invariant under this homothety. More generally, under
\(g\mapsto v^2g\) the Weyl operators scale by \(v^{-2}\)
and the volume by \(v^4\). The covariant Bach tensor scales
by \(v^{-2}\), so its norm scales by \(v^{-4}\), which
also cancels the volume weight. We obtain uniformly in \(b\)
\[
 E_\pm(g^0_{R,b})=8\pi^2+O(R^{-1}),\qquad
 \mathfrak B(g^0_{R,b})=O(R^{-1}).
 \tag{6}
\]
The signature formula gives equality of the chiral energies
for each \(R,b\). [E07 core integral][e07] ·
[E29 curvature and volume controls][e29] ·
[BGI+M, §1, equation (1.1) and conformal covariance][bgim]

## One marking radius for the whole path

The marking is performed in a fixed neighborhood in one unscaled
Taub–NUT inner core, disjoint from the entire neck. To use a given
chart \(\psi\), shrink its extended domain slightly to \(B_{1+d'}\)
with \(0<d'<d\). A single orientation-preserving diffeomorphism of
the standard sphere sends its compact closure into that inner
core. For example, the localized chart compression of E25 maps
the extended ball into an arbitrarily small coordinate
neighborhood of its center, and a fixed orientation-preserving
round rotation moves the center into the core. Choose the
compression sufficiently small for the composed image. Pull
the path back by this fixed map once. The whole marked chart
now lies in a fixed smooth unscaled core germ in the pulled-back
model. The neck and its tests can be referred to their
corresponding pulled-back coordinates. This placement is
independent of \(R\) and \(b\). [E25 chart compression][e25]

Before marking, apply the E30 replacement at the marked center,
in common oriented Taub–NUT normal coordinates. In the
ball of radius \(2\varepsilon\) replace \(g^0_{R,b}\) by
\(\delta+\chi(x/\varepsilon)(g^0_{R,b}-\delta)\), with the
fixed E30 cutoff. The metric is flat on radius \(\varepsilon\)
and unchanged outside radius \(2\varepsilon\). The normal
coordinates, germ, fixed initial radius and constants are
shared over all \(R,b\). E30 bounds the change of \(E_\pm\)
by \(C_E\varepsilon^4\) and the change of \(\mathfrak B\)
by \(C_B\varepsilon^2\). The opposite chirality estimate
also follows from signature. In the normal patch the
coefficient change is \(O(\varepsilon^2)\), scalar curvature
is bounded on the changed region and its volume is
\(O(\varepsilon^4)\).

At the positive endpoint use the E34 Yamabe quotient
comparison after replacement. For fixed \(R\), denote the
unscaled positive metric by \(h\) and write its dimension-four
numerator and denominator as \(Q_h(f)\) and \(D_h(f)\).
Here \(Y_h=Y(S^4,[h])>0\). Since \(h\) originally has
nonnegative scalar curvature, the E34 comparison applies to
the replacement \(h_\varepsilon\) with a constant \(C_R\):
\[
 |Q_{h_\varepsilon}(f)-Q_h(f)|
     \le C_R\varepsilon^2(Q_h(f)+D_h(f)),\qquad
 D_{h_\varepsilon}(f)\le(1+C_R\varepsilon^2)D_h(f).
\]
The numerator estimate follows from inverse metric and
volume comparison and an \(O(\varepsilon^2)\) local \(L^2\)
error in the scalar density. It implies
\(Q_{h_\varepsilon}(f)
\ge [Y_h-C_R\varepsilon^2(1+Y_h)]D_h(f)\)
when \(C_R\varepsilon^2<1\). At each \(R\) choose
\(\varepsilon(R)>0\) below the fixed admissible radius,
at most \(\varepsilon_*/R\) for some fixed \(\varepsilon_*>0\),
and small enough that \(C_R\varepsilon(R)^2(1+Y_h)\le Y_h/2\).
This keeps the positive endpoint positive. At the negative
endpoint the E35 separated test is supported in the central
neck. Its metric, numerator and denominator are unchanged by
the local replacement, so the sign stays strictly negative.
[E30 local cost][e30] · [E34 positive comparison][e34] ·
[E35 negative test][e35]

For this one radius at each \(R\), the E25/E30 marking
diffeomorphism and positive conformal multiplier can be chosen
identically for every \(b\). The normal chart, flat germ and
desired round coordinate expression are common. Both operations
are supported in the prescribed extended marked chart, away
from the negative test. The resulting smooth path \(g_{R,b}\)
equals \(\Omega^2\delta\) in the original marking on \(K\).
The operations preserve \(Y\), \(E_\pm\) and \(\mathfrak B\)
by diffeomorphism and conformal invariance. The replacement
costs are \(O(R^{-4})\) in each chiral energy and \(O(R^{-2})\)
in \(\mathfrak B\), which retain the uniform estimates (6) for
the whole marked path.

## Finding exact Yamabe zero

For each fixed \(R\), the path \(b\mapsto g_{R,b}\) is smooth.
To check continuity of the sign parameter with varying volume,
use the lowest eigenvalue of \(6\Delta_g+s_g\), with
\(\Delta_g=-\operatorname{div}_g\nabla\). Write
\(Q_b(v)=\int(6|dv|_{g_{R,b}}^2+s_{g_{R,b}}v^2)dV_{g_{R,b}}\)
and \(N_b(v)=\int v^2dV_{g_{R,b}}\) on the common \(H^1\)
domain. Fix one parameter \(b_0\) and choose \(K_0>\|s_{g_{R,b_0}}\|_\infty+1\).
The shifted form \(Q_{b_0}+K_0N_{b_0}\) controls the \(H^1\) norm
in the reference metric at \(b_0\). \(C^2\) convergence at
\(b_0\) makes the shifted forms relatively close, uniformly
over \(v\), with relative error \(o(1)\). The same comparison
holds for the positive denominators \(N_b\). Taking infima of
the shifted Rayleigh quotients gives continuity of
\(\lambda_1(b)+K_0\) and hence of \(\lambda_1(b)\).

A positive unit-volume Yamabe factor \(u\) satisfies
\((6\Delta_g+s_g)u=Y(S^4,[g])u^3\). Inserting \(v=u(v/u)\)
in the numerator and integrating by parts expresses it as
\(6\int u^2|d(v/u)|_g^2+Y\int u^2v^2\), with volume \(dV_g\).
For positive \(Y\) this bounds the lowest eigenvalue below
by \(Y\min u^2>0\). For \(Y=0\) the lowest eigenvalue is
zero by testing \(u\), and for negative \(Y\) the same test
gives a strictly negative eigenvalue. Thus the signs agree.
The endpoints of the marked path have opposite signs, so for
every \(R\) there is some \(b_R\in(0,1)\) with
\(Y(S^4,[g_{R,b_R}])=0\). Choosing one such value and setting
\(g_R=g_{R,b_R}\) produces the sequence with (6).
[E10 sign argument][e10] · [Schoen, §3, Theorem 3][schoen] ·
[Lee–Parker, §2, eqs. (2.6)–(2.7)][lp]

## Lower bound, equality and nonattainment

Let \(M\) be any smooth closed connected oriented homotopy
four-sphere and consider a smooth metric \(g\) with \(Y(M,[g])=0\).
Take a smooth unit-volume Yamabe representative \(k\in[g]\).
It has scalar curvature zero. In the operator-Hilbert–Schmidt
normalization, Chern–Gauss–Bonnet and the zero signature give
\[
 16\pi^2
 =2E_+(g)-\tfrac12\int_M|\operatorname{Ric}_k|_k^2\,dV_k,
 \qquad
 \int_M|\operatorname{Ric}_k|_k^2\,dV_k=4(E_+(g)-8\pi^2).
 \tag{7}
\]
Indeed the Weyl term of the Euler integrand is one quarter
of the full four-index squared norm, and the full integral
is \(4(E_++E_-)=8E_+\). The trace-free Ricci tensor is all
of Ricci in the scalar-flat representative. This gives
the lower bound \(E_+\ge8\pi^2\), with equality exactly when
the conformal class has a Ricci-flat representative. Conversely,
a Ricci-flat representative has \(Y=0\), because for its
Yamabe quotient the nonnegative Dirichlet numerator is zero
on constants. Equation (7) then gives equality.
[E10 Gauss–Bonnet identity][e10] · [Chang–Gursky–Yang,
p. 107, Remark 2 and eq. (0.4)][cgy] · [SH-E01 signature][sh]

If \(g\) also carries the fixed round cap and realizes equality,
the representative \(k\) is Ricci-flat and hence Bach-flat
by Schouten vanishing. Thus \(g\) is Bach-flat by positive
conformal covariance. Its marked cap has an open region of
vanishing Weyl curvature. E24 gives that the entire metric
is conformally round, contradicting \(E_+(g)=8\pi^2\).
In this use the cap condition is imposed on \(g\), and the
Ricci-flat conclusion of the equality case concerns \(k\).
In particular a representative fixed to the unit round
metric on the open cap has scalar curvature \(12\) there,
whereas the representative used in (7) is scalar-flat.
[E24 exterior stationarity and continuation][e24]

On the standard sphere, (6) at \(b_R\) and (7) prove (1) and
(2). The infimum is over extensions in a conformal class
of Yamabe constant zero, with the stated representative
condition on \(K\). More precisely it ranges over all such
classes and their marked representatives. The full four-index
Weyl energy tends to \(64\pi^2\) in this construction. The
scalar-flat representatives have the same Weyl energies and
the same \(\mathfrak B\) by conformal invariance. The unmarked
Yamabe-zero infimum on the standard sphere is also \(8\pi^2\):
it has the same bound and includes the exhibited sequence.
Unmarked attainment is equivalent to existence of a smooth
Ricci-flat metric there and is undecided by this proof.

On arbitrary \(M\), every Yamabe-zero class has the lower
bound (7) and the same Ricci-flat equality criterion. With a
fixed round cap, every admissible value is strictly above
\(8\pi^2\). If the marked class is nonempty and its infimum
equals \(8\pi^2\), that infimum is unattained. Existence of
these classes and sharpness of the bound on an arbitrary
smooth structure are separate questions.

## Consequence for same-manifold compactness

The marked \(g_R\) are minimizing only in the tested
Yamabe-zero marked problem. E31's small-Bach variant is
applicable without an unrestricted minimizing hypothesis.
Suppose some subsequence admitted smooth same-manifold gauges
with uniformly positive metric and strong \(C^4\) convergence,
and the pulled-back cap interiors contained a common nonempty
open set. Conformal and pullback invariance preserve
\(\mathfrak B\to0\) and \(E_\pm\to8\pi^2\); an orientation
reversal swaps the equal chiral integrals. E31 would then
force the limit to be Weyl-flat and the energies of that
subsequence to tend to zero, a contradiction. In particular
no such cap-surviving positive compactness gauge exists.
The same statement applies starting with \(k_R\), because
its scalar-flat normalization can be absorbed into the
conformal gauge and leaves the Weyl-zero open cap in place.
[E31 small-Bach variant][e31]
