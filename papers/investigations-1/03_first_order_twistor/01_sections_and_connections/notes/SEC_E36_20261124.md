## Setting and common normalization

Let \(M\) be a smooth closed connected oriented homotopy four-sphere
with a supplied smooth conformal class \([g_0]\) of real Riemannian
metrics. Assume \(Y=Y(M,[g_0])>0\) and
\(E=E_+(g_0)\le 8\pi^2-\delta\), with \(\delta>0\).
Here \(Y\) is the class Yamabe constant. The orientation gives the
self-dual block \(W^+\), with its curvature-operator Hilbert–Schmidt
norm and \(W^+=0\) for ASD. Full four-index norms will be denoted by
the subscript \(4{\rm ind}\); Ricci and scalar norms are the usual
ones. The signature and Euler characteristic are \(0\) and \(2\).
Conformal invariance and the operator-to-tensor conversion in
SH-E01 give

\[
 E_-=E,\qquad \int_M|W|_{4{\rm ind}}^2\,dV=8E
\]

in every representative of the supplied class. The common
Chern–Gauss–Bonnet identity, with \(T=\operatorname{Ric}-Rg/4\), is

\[
 16\pi^2=2E-\tfrac12\int_M |T|^2\,dV+\tfrac1{24}\int_M R^2\,dV .
 \tag{1}
\]

[SH-E01][recognition] · [SEC-E10, (2)][e10] ·
[Chang–Gursky–Yang, (0.4) and Remark 2, p. 107][cgy]

## Unit-volume Yamabe representative

Take the smooth minimizing representative \(g_Y\), rescaled to
volume one. The Yamabe theorem gives \(R_{g_Y}=Y\) in this scale.
The universal sphere upper bound is
\(Y\le Y(S^4,[g_{\rm rd}])=12\,\operatorname{Vol}(S^4,g_{\rm rd})^{1/2}
=8\sqrt6\,\pi\) for the round metric of sectional curvature one.
Substitution into (1) gives

\[
 Y^2=48(8\pi^2-E)+12\|T\|_{L^2(g_Y)}^2,\qquad
 \sqrt{48\delta}\le Y\le 8\sqrt6\,\pi .
 \tag{2}
\]

The lower bound uses the supplied positive sign. The dimension-four
Yamabe variational inequality in \(g_Y\), for every real
\(v\in H^1(M,g_Y)\), gives the inhomogeneous Sobolev estimate

\[
 \|v\|_{L^4(g_Y)}^2
 \le \frac6Y\|dv\|_{L^2(g_Y)}^2+\|v\|_{L^2(g_Y)}^2
 \le \frac6{\sqrt{48\delta}}\|dv\|_{L^2(g_Y)}^2
      +\|v\|_{L^2(g_Y)}^2 .
 \tag{3}
\]

The quotient defined over positive functions gives the same inequality
for real \(v\) by density and approximation of the absolute value.
[Schoen, §3, Theorem 3][schoen] · [Lee–Parker, §3, Theorems 3.2–3.3
and Aubin's Lemma 3.4][leeparker]

From \(Y^2\le384\pi^2\) and (2), \(\|T\|_2^2\le4E\) in \(g_Y\).
The orthogonal curvature decomposition in dimension four gives
\(|\operatorname{Rm}|_{4{\rm ind}}^2
=|W|_{4{\rm ind}}^2+2|T|^2+R^2/6\).
In operator norm the two trace-free Ricci off-diagonal blocks
contribute \(|T|^2/2\) together, and the scalar block contributes
\(6(R/12)^2\); both convert to the displayed terms after multiplication
by four. Equivalently, for diagonal \(T\) with eigenvalues \(t_i\),
the curvature operator of the \(T\)-part has eigenvalues
\((t_i+t_j)/2\) for \(i<j\), whose square sum is \(|T|^2/2\)
since \(\sum_i t_i=0\). This yields

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \|R\|_2^2&=Y^2\le384\pi^2, &
 \|\operatorname{Ric}\|_2^2&=\|T\|_2^2+Y^2/4\le4E+96\pi^2,\\
 \|W\|_{L^2,4{\rm ind}}^2&=8E, &
 \|\operatorname{Rm}\|_{L^2,4{\rm ind}}^2
     &\le16E+64\pi^2\le192\pi^2-16\delta .
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{4}
\]

These are the Yamabe representative's bounds. The Weyl energy
is itself conformally invariant. [Lee–Parker, §2, curvature
decomposition, pp. 42–43][leeparker]

## Unit-volume constant-\(\sigma_2\) representative

Write the Schouten tensor and its trace as

\[
 A=\tfrac12(\operatorname{Ric}-Rg/6)=\tfrac12 T+\tfrac{R}{24}g,
 \qquad \sigma_1(A)=R/6 .
\]

The eigenvalues are those of \(g^{-1}A\). The tensor in
Chang–Gursky–Yang (CGY), §1, p. 110, is
\(A_{\rm CGY}=\operatorname{Ric}-Rg/6=2A\).
Thus \(\sigma_2(A_{\rm CGY})=4\sigma_2(A)\). Their form (1.1)
of Chern–Gauss–Bonnet gives

\[
 \int_M\sigma_2(A_{\rm CGY})\,dV
 =16\pi^2-2E=2(8\pi^2-E)\ge2\delta .
 \tag{5}
\]

The hypotheses of CGY §1, Theorem 1.2, p. 112, are exactly a
smooth closed Riemannian four-manifold, positive class Yamabe
constant, and strictly positive integral (5). They give a
smooth conformal metric with positive constant
\(\sigma_2(A_{\rm CGY})\). Positive constant rescaling preserves
constancy and the integral of this degree-two eigenvalue
expression in dimension four. For the resulting unit-volume
representative \(g_\sigma\), the value in our convention is

\[
 q=\sigma_2(A_{g_\sigma})=\frac{8\pi^2-E}{2}\ge\frac{\delta}{2}>0.
 \tag{6}
\]

The positive scalar branch follows from the hypotheses.
Let \(S=\sigma_1(A)=R/6\). The identity
\(2q=S^2-|A|^2\) implies \(S\ne0\) and its sign is constant by
connectedness. If \(R<0\) everywhere, the constant function
in the Yamabe quotient in \(g_\sigma\) gives
\(Y\le\int_M R\,dV_{g_\sigma}<0\), contradicting the class sign.
Thus \(S>0\). The inequalities \(S>0\) and \(2q>0\) describe
the component \(\Gamma_2\) of \(\{\sigma_2>0\}\) containing
the positive orthant, since they reduce to the convex cone
\(S>|\lambda|\) for the eigenvalue vector \(\lambda\).
Moreover \(|A|^2\ge S^2/4\) gives
\(q\le3S^2/8\), hence \(R\ge\sqrt{96q}\ge\sqrt{48\delta}\).
[CGY, §1, (1.0)–(1.1) and Theorem 1.2][cgy]

## Sharp pointwise Ricci estimate and its limitation

At a point of \(g_\sigma\), let \(a\) be the least eigenvalue of
\(A\), and let \(b_1,b_2,b_3\) be the other eigenvalues. Their sum
is \(S-a>0\) since \(a\le S/4\). The least Ricci eigenvalue is
\(m=S+2a\). For fixed sum of the \(b_i\), their pair sum is
bounded by \((S-a)^2/3\) by Cauchy–Schwarz, with equality
precisely when all three are equal. Consequently

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 q&=a(S-a)+\sum_{i<j}b_i b_j
  \le\frac{(S-a)(S+2a)}3
   =\frac{m(3S-m)}6,\\
 m&\ge\frac{3S-\sqrt{9S^2-24q}}2
  \ge\frac{2q}{S}>0 .
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{7}
\]

Indeed \(q>0\) and \(S-a>0\) first give \(m>0\). The first
bound for \(m\) is the smaller root of the quadratic and
has equality in the three-equal case. The second also follows
directly by dropping \(m^2/6\) in the first line.

The coefficient \(2\) in \(m\ge 2q/S\) is sharp over the
admissible pointwise eigenvalues. Fix \(q>0\), choose \(S\)
arbitrarily large, and set

\[
 m_S=\frac{3S-\sqrt{9S^2-24q}}2,\qquad
 a=\frac{m_S-S}{2},\qquad b_i=\frac{3S-m_S}{6}\quad(i=1,2,3).
\]

These have \(\sigma_1=S\), \(\sigma_2=q\), and \(a\le b_i\).
Here \(m_S\sim2q/S\to0\) and \(R=6S\to\infty\).
Thus positive constant \(q\) permits arbitrarily large
scalar trace and arbitrarily small least Ricci eigenvalue in
pointwise algebra. The example is a family of algebraic spectra.
It supplies no sequence of metrics with those properties.

The constant scalar \(Y\) in the first gauge provides no scalar
upper estimate in the second by this argument. For example, if
\(g_\sigma=u^2g_Y\), the scalar change is
\(R_{g_\sigma}=u^{-3}(6\Delta_{g_Y}u+Yu)\) with
\(\Delta=-\operatorname{div}\nabla\). Equations (2)–(4) give
no pointwise control of the conformal factor and its second
derivatives for this comparison. [Lee–Parker, §2, (2.6)–(2.7)][leeparker]

## Metric ball control under an independent scalar bound

Suppose the selected \(g_\sigma\) also satisfies a uniform
scalar upper bound \(R\le\Lambda\). Then (6)–(7) give

\[
 \operatorname{Ric}_{g_\sigma}\ge\frac{6\delta}{\Lambda}\,g_\sigma,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{diam}(M,g_\sigma)
 \le D=\pi\sqrt{\frac{\Lambda}{2\delta}} .
 \tag{8}
\]

The diameter bound is Bonnet–Myers with \((n-1)k=6\delta/\Lambda\)
in dimension four. Compactness of the supplied smooth
manifold ensures completeness for the comparison. Bishop–Gromov
at nonnegative Ricci curvature gives, for any \(x\) and
\(0<\rho\le L\),

\[
 \frac{\operatorname{Vol}_{g_\sigma}B(x,\rho)}
      {\operatorname{Vol}_{g_\sigma}B(x,L)}
 \ge(\rho/L)^4.
\]

Choose \(L>D\) so the large ball has volume one, and let
\(L\downarrow D\). This yields
\(\operatorname{Vol}_{g_\sigma}B(x,\rho)\ge(\rho/D)^4\)
for \(0<\rho\le D\). Positive-curvature model comparison can
also be used, but this relative estimate already gives
the bound in terms of the independent \(\Lambda\) and the
prescribed energy gap. [Gromov, p. 82, Bishop relative inequality
and its positive-Ricci comparison][gromov]

If \(E<4\pi^2\), the same positive \(Y\) and the strict Weyl
threshold already give smooth sphere recognition by SH-E01.
The gauge results apply to the supplied conditional class and
give neither its construction on an arbitrary homotopy sphere
nor an estimate providing \(\Lambda\) in the second gauge.
[SH-E01, supplied strict approximate recognition][recognition]
