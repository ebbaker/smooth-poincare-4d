All B tasks recommend **Astra/ultra** because their stated conceptual
choice remains unresolved. All E tasks recommend **gpt-6.2-sol/ultra**
for a fixed calculation or proof method. Each task below states the
specific routing reason. Preserve the author-selected lead/effort and
record actual settings separately; a recommendation is not provenance.

Under an invoked broad continuation, delegate bounded analytical and
example-based E work to gpt-6.2-sol/ultra helpers with disjoint outputs. The lead alone edits shared
plans, indexes and registers. Freeze every request in handoffs with exact
input versions, equations, domains, norms, finite limits, deliverables and
failure rules. Reserved E slots are not executable until their B decision
fixes the method and the request is issued. A satisfied dependency means
an assessed result applicable to the requested claim, not merely a
completed packet with an adverse or conditional return.

A task completes at its stated finite boundary; that does not discharge
an entire global phase. Track remaining premises and create successive
bounded packets when needed. Source inspection and exact/symbolic/local,
conditional and global evidence remain distinct. No numerics are started
by this setup. An unfavorable result preserves its class and returns to
the named B gate; changing the mechanism is a visible conceptual decision.

The parent owns shared conventions (SH-B01) and endpoint audit (SH-E01).
Its optional cross-branch comparison is not a prerequisite for this
construction program. The broad grant includes necessary shared work and
relevant adjacent methods, including ideas from the sibling. It does not
automatically activate the sibling's separate backlog or resume paused CME
