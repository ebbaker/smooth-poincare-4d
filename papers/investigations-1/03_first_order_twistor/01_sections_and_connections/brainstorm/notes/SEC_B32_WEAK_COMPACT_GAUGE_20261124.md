The complementary case to an ALE blow-up is a uniformly elliptic metric family on the actual compact manifold, in a single atlas after smooth conformal/diffeomorphism changes. Choose a gauge with a uniform $W^{2,q}$ bound for some $q>2$. A subsequence converges in $C^0\cap W^{1,4}$ and weakly in $W^{2,q}$. A priori this leaves a possible defect in the $L^2$ Weyl energy. Test the unrestricted first variation against the metric difference itself. Its supremum norm vanishes. The local Weyl polynomial is linear in second derivatives. Its differential in the difference direction should equal the Weyl difference up to a term small in $L^2$, recovering strong curvature convergence. A nontrivial limiting smooth Bach-flat conformal class has an analytic Weyl tensor in suitable local gauges. Positive measure of the zero set suffices for continuation even if the round markings have no common open subset. The small Bach norm across all variations is essential. These tests address one specified compactness hypothesis, without declaring it follows from the band.
