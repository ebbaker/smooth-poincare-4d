A round marked cap costs arbitrarily little Weyl energy or Yamabe constant in approximation. At a stationary smooth extension that cap forces global conformal flatness. These two accepted results put the missing premise at same-manifold compactness of a marked minimizing sequence, even when the infimum is unknown. E31 tests this as a conditional recognition route.

Test a weaker compactness premise on the actual standard smooth sphere first. Use the dimensionless integral of the norm of the Bach tensor, dual to uniformly bounded metric variations. Double large finite Taub–NUT balls through a transition whose circle fiber collapses after rescaling by the outer radius. The core energies are known; the issue is whether every term in the Bach tensor stays controlled in the rescaled transition. This may produce positive-energy metrics with arbitrarily small total Bach norm. E29 owns the doubling and the geometry. E30 independently tests the local cost of installing the fixed marked round ball while preserving the small total Bach norm; the two results can be combined only following assessment. No assumption about the infimum, positivity of the Yamabe constant or subcritical energy enters the test.

E31 uses only E24, E25, E05 and SH-E01. The double is an independent test of which hypotheses may be removed. No numerical or flow experiment is required.
