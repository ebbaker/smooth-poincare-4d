Let \(g\) be a smooth Riemannian metric on a four-manifold, with the Weyl norm and the Bach tensor of E24 and the manuscript. (i) Write the first variation of the density \(|W|^2dV\) pointwise as \(-4\langle B,h\rangle dV\) plus the divergence of an explicit vector field depending bilinearly on \(W\) and \(h\). Check it at random metrics and directions. (ii) For an arbitrary vector field \(X\) derive the divergence-free vector field that expresses invariance under diffeomorphisms, and decide whether its flux through a compact two-sided hypersurface can be different from zero. (iii) Evaluate the identity for conformal Killing fields. State the consequence for Bach-flat cones over closed three-manifolds and for products with a line, and check it by hand on \(\mathbb R^2\times S^2\). (iv) On a neck with a function \(\tau\) and a vector field \(X\) with \(X(\tau)=1\), derive an integrated identity for \(\int\varphi(\tau)|W|^2dV\) and an estimate with no logarithm of the length. State the exact hypotheses and compare them with those of E59; say whether quotient necks are covered. Method: direct proof from the first variation and naturality; numerical checks by truncated Taylor arithmetic at random metrics and by quadrature on the unit three-sphere for cones and for a metric that is not a cone, with a vector field that is not conformal. Completion: identities with proofs and checks to stated precision, and the estimate, or the term that cannot be controlled. Escalate to B35 if the flux identity leaves a term at a far boundary. No claim about the existence of the vector field under the fixed-margin hypotheses.
