Work at the flat metric of \(\mathbb R^4\) with the linearized Weyl and Bach operators \(W'\), \(B'\) in the conventions of E24 and the manuscript. (i) For a constant symmetric trace-free matrix \(H\), compute \(B'(H\log|x|)\) in closed form, decide whether \(H\log|x|\) differs from the homogeneous field \(s_H=(x\otimes Hx+Hx\otimes x)/(2|x|^2)\) by a Lie derivative of the flat metric, and compute the Weyl energy and the \(L^1\) Bach norm per unit of \(\log|x|\). (ii) Let \(h\) be smooth on \(\mathbb R^4\setminus0\) and homogeneous of degree \(\mu\) up to a polynomial in \(\log|x|\). For \(-2<\operatorname{Re}\mu<2\), decide whether \(B'(h)=0\) on \(\mathbb R^4\setminus0\) forces \(W'(h)=0\). Identify the solutions with \(W'(h)\ne0\) at \(\mu=\pm2\). (iii) For compactly supported \(h\), bound the supremum of the divergence-free trace-free part of \(h\) and the Weyl energy \(\int|W'(h)|^2\) by \(\|B'(h)\|_{L^1}\) with explicit constants, and decide whether the bound transfers to linearized fields on the round four-sphere. (iv) State what the linear results imply for a long neck and name the term that prevents the same argument for the nonlinear Bach tensor. Method: Fourier transform and homogeneous distributions for (ii), an integral representation using that \(B'(h)\) is divergence free for (iii); exact rational algebra and quadrature only as checks, with finite ranges stated. Completion: proved statements with constants, or a counterexample to (ii). Escalate to B35 if a homogeneous solution of degree zero with nonzero Weyl tensor exists. No nonlinear claim.
