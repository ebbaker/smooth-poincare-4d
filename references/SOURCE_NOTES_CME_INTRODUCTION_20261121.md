is not stated as a prohibition on every possible Whitney move in dimension four.

As a current-status check, Ciprian Manolescu's

Poincaré problem as open. Its §7 also discusses the knot-sliceness approach
and remaining obstacles. This status check is not an additional theorem
used by the manuscript and was not added as a redundant bibliography entry.

## Existing references revisited

The existing local reading copies of AHS1978, Witten1978 and Fine2011 were
read again at the relevant passages. Their hashes agree with the identities
recorded in [the earlier source notes](SOURCE_NOTES_CME_BACKGROUND_20261121.md);
no fresh download or full-proof audit is claimed.

- **Atiyah–Hitchin–Singer:** Theorem 4.1 and its geometric remarks, printed
  pp. 435–436, identify twistor integrability for oriented Riemannian
  four-manifolds. The manuscript uses its stated opposite chirality naming:
  ASD means W⁺ = 0. The real rank-three Hodge bundles and complex
  three-dimensional integrable twistor space are distinguished.
- **Witten:** *An interpretation of classical Yang–Mills theory* (1978),
  printed pp. 394–395, is the intended paper. The nonsupersymmetric construction
  works locally in complexified flat spacetime; bundles are trivial on each
  complete direction quadric CP¹ × CP¹ and extend to third formal order off
  the null-line quadric. Arbitrary bundles or an unrestricted full extension
  are not substituted for these conditions.
- **Fine:** arXiv:1111.5005v2, 29 November 2011; Definitions 1.3–1.5,
  Conjecture 1.7, §1.6 and Theorem 4.11. The Introduction now names the exact
  version before developing the method. Conjectural global deformation and
  existence on an arbitrary homotopy sphere remain distinct from Fine's
  short-time theorem and stability near a perfect connection. The bibliography
  now links to the version-specific landing page. The manuscript's fixed
  curvature/volume/time normalizations are retained.

Penrose, Ward and Baker remain the citations used in the preceding background
revision; their mathematical roles and inspected passages are recorded in
the earlier source notes. Baker is still cited only through arXiv:2301.00029
and references within. No earlier repository manuscript is cited.
