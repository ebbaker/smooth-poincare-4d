# Primary inputs to the charged-disk, spectral and marked-boundary arguments

25 November 2026. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance. The [E59–E62 assessment](../papers/master/research-progress/handoffs/E59_E62_SPECTRAL_WINDOWS_20261125.md) identifies the source generations, derivations and independent readers. The entries below distinguish checks in this edition from earlier source records.

| Argument | Primary source, inspected passage and use |
| --- | --- |
| Full subcritical disk selection | Chang–Gursky–Yang's positive-Ricci representative is in the specified positive class. Saloff-Coste's Theorem 3.1 gives the local Sobolev inequality on comparison balls. Cheeger–Colding I, §§2, 5 and Appendix A.1, supplies noncollapsed volume convergence and regular ball embeddings with image inclusions. The new selection in [the descent draft](../papers/master/01_fixed_type_variational_descent/manuscript/spectral_windows.tex) constructs the scale and measures the energy of the original metric in these disks. [Earlier checked passages and editions](SOURCE_NOTES_THIRD_COLLAR_20261125.md) · [Disk transfer and review](../papers/master/01_fixed_type_variational_descent/reviews/E61_SPECTRAL_WINDOWS.md) |
| Modified Yamabe representative and finite equality | B. Chen and X. Zhu, *A conformally invariant classification theorem in four dimensions*, CAG 22 (2014), Lemma 2.1, Theorems 1.4, 1.6 and 1.7. On a smooth closed oriented four-manifold the modified scalar is ordinary scalar minus six times the larger of the two top Weyl eigenvalues. Nonstandard homotopy type excludes the strict and equality classifications on a positive class. The modified equation gives the volume-one representative, with its regularity as in Lemma 2.1. The E62 reader checked strict and equality applications to the positive-time Einstein limit. [Primary text](https://intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/cag/2014/0022/0005/CAG-2014-0022-0005-a002.pdf) · [Earlier normalization record](SOURCE_NOTES_MASTER_ENDPOINT_20261125.md) |
| Four-dimensional flow and rigidity | R. Hamilton, *Four-manifolds with positive curvature operator*, JDG 24 (1986), §2, pp. 157–158, supplies the moving-frame block evolution. The fixed-cutoff smoothing proof uses the entropy, derivative and compactness inputs already recorded in E57. M. Obata, Theorem A (1962), applies to the nonconstant Hessian solution on the positive Einstein limit. For the weaker curvature-tail premise, Chen–Tang–Zhu's Main Theorem (2012) classifies the closed positive-isotropic limit once the parallel-line case is excluded. [Flow and Obata passage checks](../papers/master/01_fixed_type_variational_descent/reviews/E62_ISOTROPIC_FLOW.md) · [E57 sources](SOURCE_NOTES_MASTER_RECOGNITION_GAP_20261125.md) |
| Closed Cotton-flat case | Lassas–Liimatainen, *Conformal harmonic coordinates*, Theorem 3.8, extends local conformal flatness from an open set of a connected smooth Bach-flat four-manifold. Kuiper's theorem then applies on the compact simply connected locally conformally flat type. The Codazzi eigenspace and Hessian reductions are derived in the [owning section](../papers/master/01_fixed_type_variational_descent/manuscript/cotton_variation.tex). [Earlier source record](SOURCE_NOTES_MASTER_VARIATIONAL_20261125.md) · [Closed variation review](../papers/master/01_fixed_type_variational_descent/reviews/E60_CLOSED_HESSIAN.md) |
| Radial defect in a protected annulus | C. Mantegazza, G. Mascellani and G. Uraltsev, *On the distributional Hessian of the distance function*, Pacific J. Math. 270 (2014), Corollaries 2.11–2.12. The distributional comparison includes the signed cut-locus contribution to the Laplacian of physical distance. This sign enters the conversion of an integrated radial estimate to total variation on the selected annulus. [Primary text](https://msp.org/pjm/2014/270-1/pjm-v270-n1-p07-p.pdf) · [Physical Ricci and inner transition hypotheses](../papers/investigations-1/03_first_order_twistor/01_sections_and_connections/01_neck_geometry/reviews/E60_LOG_SHELLS.md) |
| Signature of a product-germ cut | M. Atiyah, V. Patodi and I. Singer, *Spectral asymmetry and Riemannian geometry*, I (1975), Theorem 4.14, and II (1975), Proposition 2.12. The smooth product-germ signature formula and the round lens eta with outward complex-link orientation fix the normalization. Smooth excision of the three quotient points and transgression on a product-ended cylinder give the adiabatic and finite terms separately. [APS I](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/aps001.pdf) · [APS II](https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/aps002.pdf) · [Independent computation with the weights and orientations](../papers/investigations-1/04_hypersurface_immersions/01_immersed_handle_constructions/reviews/E60_HOMOGENEOUS_ETA.md) |
| Seifert conventions and marked smooth type | O. Biquard, M. Herzlich and M. Rumin (2007), §5, equations (75)–(77) and the round lens check (93). The reader re-summed the signed periodic terms using the direct local weights. The original full text of Ouyang was unavailable. The independent APS computation determines the sign for the metric used here. Auckly–Kim–Melvin–Ruberman, *Equivariant corks* (2017), §1 and Figures 1–2, identifies the specific cork and doubles used for the smooth-type comparison. An isometric gluing requires the actual retained face metric. [Spectral series](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_2007_4_40_4_589_0.pdf) · [Cork presentation](https://msp.org/agt/2017/17-3/agt-v17-n3-p11-s.pdf) · [Retained-half analysis](../papers/investigations-1/04_hypersurface_immersions/01_immersed_handle_constructions/notes/E59_SHORT_SEAM.md) |
