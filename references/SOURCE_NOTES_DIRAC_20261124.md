[Author PDF](https://home.mathematik.uni-freiburg.de/mlisting/scalar_symmetric.pdf) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.1832) | Theorem 3 and (6), (7)–(8), (11)–(15), (21)–(22), §§2–3, especially pp. 4–5, 11–13.
