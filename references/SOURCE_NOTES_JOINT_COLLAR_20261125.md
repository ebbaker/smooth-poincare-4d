**R122. Allen E. Hatcher.** “A proof of the Smale conjecture, $\mathrm{Diff}(S^3)\simeq O(4)$”, *Ann. of Math.* 117 (1983), 553–607. The opening theorem and discussion identify the components (Cerf's theorem). An orientation-compatible smooth attaching diffeomorphism is isotopic to an orthogonal representative and extends over the standard four-ball using a collar. Applied after the capped exterior is recognized in NECK-E02/E05/E10. [Author's text](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/Papers/SmaleConjecture.pdf)

**R123. Christian Bär and Werner Ballmann.** “Boundary value problems for elliptic differential operators of first order”, arXiv:1101.1196v2, 21 November 2011. Standard Setup 1.5, Theorems 1.7 and 1.12, Addendum 1.13, Example 1.16(a) and §7.2 cover the maximal domain, elliptic spectral boundary conditions and the Hilbert adjoint for a smooth boundary-symmetric elliptic operator. MATCH-E03 applies these on a compact cylinder at each fixed length. Length-independent constants come from the separate signed energy estimates. [Inspected version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1101.1196v2)

**R124. Christian Bär, Paul Gauduchon and Andrei Moroianu.** “Generalized cylinders in semi-Riemannian and spin geometry”, *Math. Z.* 249 (2005), 545–580. Equations (3.6), (4.1) and Theorem 5.1 of the author text dated 30 January 2004. In the Riemannian unit-normal splitting the extrinsic mean-curvature term is removed by the varying volume-density isometry. EXTRACT-E03 and MATCH-E03 track the regularity and sign convention in the resulting fixed slice Hilbert space. [Author text](https://moroianu.perso.math.cnrs.fr/tex/2005mz.pdf)

**R125. Oussama Hijazi.** “A conformal lower bound for the smallest eigenvalue of the Dirac operator and Killing spinors”, *Comm. Math. Phys.* 104 (1986), 151–162, DOI 10.1007/BF01210797. Theorem A, (2.1)–(2.2), of preprint MPI/SFB 85-29 applies on a compact boundaryless Riemannian spin manifold, $n\ge3$. In dimension four $\lambda^2\ge\mu_1(6\Delta+s)/3$. MATCH-E06/E07 use the closed inequality in a smooth conformal representative retaining the measurement strips and bounding total volume. E06 also derives this case from conformal covariance and the integrated twistor identity. [Original preprint](https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/3484/1/preprint_1985_29.pdf)

**R126. Richard S. Palais.** “Extending diffeomorphisms”, *Proc. Amer. Math. Soc.* 11 (1960), 274–277, DOI 10.1090/S0002-9939-1960-0117741-0. Theorem C extends a neighborhood diffeomorphism of a smooth cell to an ambient diffeomorphism, agreeing on a neighborhood of the cell, with the orientation condition in the orientable case. NECK-E09 uses this on a standard sphere after smooth recognition of an individually capped exterior. [Article text](https://paperzz.com/doc/8888202/extending-diffeomorphisms) · [Publication](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1960-0117741-0)

**R127. Bernd Ammann.** “The smallest Dirac eigenvalue in a spin-conformal class and cmc-immersions”, author version dated September 2005. Equations (1.1)–(1.2) and §2.2 give the scale-invariant eigenvalue and conformal spinor setting for MATCH-E08. Its dual estimate with the displayed constant is derived in the note. [Author text](https://ammann.app.uni-regensburg.de/preprints/comin/comin.pdf)
