## Marked exteriors, surgery boundaries and closed limits

25 November 2026, checked for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance. The published 2010 Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson article already indexed above was rechecked at Propositions 2.2, 3.1 and 3.2, pp. 2310–2314. Proposition 3.1 identifies the two-component tunnel-number-one link with surgery to \(\#_2(S^1\times S^2)\). The proof of 3.2 gives the isotopy in \(S^1\times S^2\) that puts the remaining framed knot in a ball with zero framing. Laudenbach–Poénaru's Theorem A and its extension argument, pp. 341–342, handle the transported boundary maps. They are used for the one-sided diagram and, in the mixed case, after specifying the actual intermediate boundary. [Published GST text](https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-4/gt-v14-n4-p13-s.pdf) · [Published gluing theorem](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/bsmf.1741.pdf) · [Mixed surgery audit](../papers/master/reviews/E43_SURGERY_AUDIT.md)

Giovanni Catino and Davide Dameno, *Conformal Kähler rigidity of Einstein four-manifolds*, arXiv:2607.21538 (2026). Inspected the author PDF, Theorem 1.4 and Corollary 1.5, p. 4, and their treatment of Weyl zeros. A compact oriented Einstein four-manifold with a non-identically-zero positive Weyl block satisfying \(\lambda_2\le s\lambda_3\) everywhere for some fixed \(s<1\) is conformally Kähler with positive scalar curvature after at most a double cover. The integral version in the master passes to a smooth compact Einstein limit before invoking the theorem and uses reversed orientation for the other block. Publication beyond the inspected preprint is not assumed.
