Exact curvature and sign assumptions for the closed Hessian, the round cap and the warped models are recorded in their [assessed returns](../papers/master/research-progress/handoffs/RICCI_AND_IMMERSION_RETURNS_20261125.md). These results are analytic. An indexed curvature inequality or a Euclidean comparison on a shell supplies neither a global immersion bound nor a physical shell marking.

[Palmas, *Complete rotation hypersurfaces with \(H_k\) constant in space forms*, §1.2.1, equation (7), Theorem 2](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/EMIS/journals/em/docs/boletim/vol302/v30-2-a2-1999.pdf) supplies the classical rotational \(H_3=0\) model identified in E32. The two-face Gauss mass, cutoff to exactly planar annuli and full-side sign estimate are computed in that packet.
