## R143 · Local mollification of metrics with small curvature concentration

Man-Chun Lee and Tang-Kai Lee, *Local mollification of metrics with small curvature concentration*, Compositio Mathematica (2026). Theorems 1.1 and 1.3. Initial upper ball-volume ratios, Sobolev control and small local critical curvature give positive-time smoothing; the distance conclusion alone is bi-Hölder. Read the journal theorem and arXiv v2, including its limiting statements. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12673v2) · [Publication record](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0010437X26103595).

## R144 · Convergence of Riemannian manifolds with integral bounds on curvature. II

Deane Yang, *Convergence of Riemannian manifolds with integral bounds on curvature. II*, Annales scientifiques de l’École Normale Supérieure (1992). Theorems 3.1 and 7.1. The supercritical Ricci and coordinate hypotheses used for harmonic-coordinate control distinguish this route from the physical critical bound in NECK-E23. Inspected the named passages. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.1647.pdf) · [Publication record](https://doi.org/10.24033/asens.1647).

## R145 · On the structure of spaces with Ricci curvature bounded below. I

Jeff Cheeger and Tobias H. Colding, *On the structure of spaces with Ricci curvature bounded below. I*, Journal of Differential Geometry (1997). Theorems 2.1 and 5.9 give regularity almost everywhere and volume convergence for the noncollapsed limit. Appendix A, Theorems A.1.8–9 give an embedded ball on an almost-maximal-volume ball, smooth on each smooth source. Metric regularity and the finite smooth ball markings are used in EXTRACT-E17. No first-derivative bound is imported. Named passages checked. [Primary text](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214459974) · [Publication record](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214459974).

## R146 · Uniformly elliptic operators on Riemannian manifolds

Laurent Saloff-Coste, *Uniformly elliptic operators on Riemannian manifolds*, Journal of Differential Geometry (1992). Theorem 3.1, p. 423. Relative local Sobolev inequality on the complete lower-Ricci-bounded manifold, used with bounded-window noncollapse in EXTRACT-E17. Named theorem inspected. [Primary text](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~lsc/papers/uniformly-jdg.pdf).

## R147 · Local Sobolev constant estimate for integral Ricci curvature bounds

Xianzhe Dai and Guofang Wei and Zhenlei Zhang, *Local Sobolev constant estimate for integral Ricci curvature bounds*, Advances in Mathematics (2018). Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 4.6, (4.14). With vanishing negative Ricci part, use sufficiently small balls and convert normalized averages by noncollapse; gives the critical local Sobolev exponent. Named statements inspected. [Primary text](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~wei/paper/Dai-Wei-Zhang.pdf).

## R148 · Four-dimensional conformal C-spaces

A. Rod Gover and Paul-Andi Nagy, *Four-dimensional conformal C-spaces*, The Quarterly Journal of Mathematics (2007). Theorem 4.1 and Proposition 4.1 in the checked preprint furnish the Hermitian Weyl structure and rank calculation. NECK-E22 recomputes the chiral divergence specialization before applying the Hermitian identity. [Primary text](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/Research/Reports/Series/555.pdf) · [Publication record](https://doi.org/10.1093/qmath/ham030).

## R149 · Explicit self-dual metrics on $\mathbb{CP}^{2}\#\cdots\#\mathbb{CP}^{2}$

Claude LeBrun, *Explicit self-dual metrics on $\mathbb{CP}^{2}\#\cdots\#\mathbb{CP}^{2}$*, Journal of Differential Geometry (1991). Section 5, especially the positive-scalar calculation and corollary. On four positive complex projective summands the self-dual class supplies the closed countertest outside the homotopy-sphere band. Named passage inspected. [Primary text](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~claude/jdg1991.pdf) · [Publication record](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214446999).

## R150 · Einstein manifolds and extremal Kähler metrics

Claude LeBrun, *Einstein manifolds and extremal Kähler metrics*, Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik (2013). Section 3, p. 78, chiral expression for the Bach tensor, checked for the covariant-index computation in NECK-E22. [Primary text](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~claude/crelle13.pdf) · [Publication record](https://doi.org/10.1515/crelle.2012.006).

## R151 · On the existence of certain singular integrals

A. P. Calderón and A. Zygmund, *On the existence of certain singular integrals*, Acta Mathematica (1952). Chapter I, Theorem 1, and pp. 88–89. Fixed Euclidean second-derivative multipliers are bounded on finite Lp, used for localized elliptic absorption in EXTRACT-E15. Named passages inspected in the lead review. [Primary text](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02392130) · [Publication record](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02392130).
