## Initial manuscripts, 26 November 2026

Mikhael Gromov and H. Blaine Lawson, Jr., *The classification of simply connected manifolds of positive scalar curvature*, Ann. of Math. 111 (1980), 423–434. Theorem A and its connected-sum consequence on p. 423 were checked in the author-hosted text. In dimension at least three a connected sum of compact positive-scalar-curvature manifolds admits positive scalar curvature. This supplies the metric on the sum of spherical factors remaining after compression and the converse for the two recognizing boundaries. The surgery acts here on the three-dimensional boundary types. [Author-hosted text](https://www.ihes.fr/~gromov/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/827.pdf) · [Publication](https://doi.org/10.2307/1971103)

## Penultimate cuts, the Mazur metric and a curved fill

Passages checked 26 November 2026 for the [surgery manuscript](../papers/investigations-1/05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex) and [deformation manuscript](../papers/investigations-1/05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/01_collar_deformation_and_adapted_coordinates/manuscript/deformation_of_marked_spherical_collars.tex).

- Yi Ni, *Null-homotopic knots have Property R*, Math. Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 175 (2023), 217–223, Theorem 1.1, p. 217. A nontrivial null-homotopic knot in a connected closed oriented three-manifold whose knot exterior has no product summand cannot have an $S^1\times S^2$ summand in its zero surgery. The trace group argument establishes null-homotopy before removing common prime factors. The rank-one case also follows from the proof of Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson, Proposition 3.2, pp. 2313–2314. [Published author copy](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~yini/Published/NullPropR.pdf) · [Rank-one proof](https://msp.org/gt/2010/14-4/gt-v14-n4-p13-s.pdf)
- Ronald Brown and Graham J. Ellis, *Hopf formulae for the higher homology of a group*, Bull. London Math. Soc. 20 (1988), 124–128. Opening formula, p. 124, identifies integral second group homology for a free presentation. Only the classical second-homology formula enters the knot proof. [Author text](https://groupoids.org.uk/pdffiles/brown-ellis.pdf)
- Mark Walsh, *Metrics of positive scalar curvature and generalised Morse functions, part 1*, arXiv:0811.1245v1. Theorem 1.2 and Definition 4.3 give a positive extension with product ends for an admissible compact Morse cobordism. Here its sole index-one handle attaches between two 3-dimensional boundary components in codimension three. [Text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.1245)
- Richard H. Bamler and Bruce Kleiner, *Ricci flow and contractibility of spaces of metrics*, arXiv:1909.08710v1, Theorem 1.1. The space of smooth PSC metrics on a connected, orientable, closed three-manifold is empty or contractible. Applied to two metrics pulled to the actual common boundary by the original gluing. [Author text](https://math.nyu.edu/~bkleiner/psc.pdf)
- An-Min Li, Udo Simon, Guosong Zhao and Zejun Hu, *Global Affine Differential Geometry of Hypersurfaces*, second edition (2015), Theorem 1.3(ii), p. 24. A simply connected oriented manifold with positive first form and Gauss–Codazzi compatible second form admits a Euclidean hypersurface immersion. The sign of the normal is fixed here by positive outward shape. Convex embedding, the marked smooth ball and the sign of the joined quadratic form are checked in the deformation argument. [Publisher excerpt](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783110268898_A22687562/preview-9783110268898_A22687562.pdf)
- John Milnor, *A Unique Decomposition Theorem for 3-Manifolds*, Amer. J. Math. 84 (1962), 1–7, Theorem 1. Uniqueness of the prime decomposition in the two closed orientable fillings identifies their residual factors after removing common copies of $S^1\times S^2$ from the knot exterior. [Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.2307/2372800)
