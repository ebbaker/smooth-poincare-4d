All links below name the versions inspected or a publication identifier. No third-party reading copy was added to the repository.

| Source | Passage inspected and dependence |
| --- | --- |
| Otis Chodosh, Davi Maximo and Anubhav Mukherjee, *Complete Riemannian 4-manifolds with uniformly positive scalar curvature*, [arXiv:2407.05574v1, 8 July 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574v1) | Proposition 2.2 and Corollary 2.3, pp. 4–5, give the orientable three-manifold decomposition under nonzero-degree PSC domination and the integral homology sphere case. Proposition 4.2, p. 10, excludes every nonempty connected sum of signed Poincaré spheres as the boundary of a compact smooth contractible four-manifold. Only its smooth boundary conclusion enters here. Its proof uses both the correction term and the periodic end obstruction. The new reverse-order filling argument supplies this contractible manifold after compressing the free factors. |
| Daniel Räde, *Scalar and mean curvature comparison via μ-bubbles*, [arXiv:2104.10120v4, 23 May 2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.10120v4) | Remark 1.4, Theorems 2.7–2.8 and Corollaries 2.9 and 2.25. A compact product over a closed connected oriented three-manifold admitting no PSC metric has strict width bound at scalar curvature at least 12. The two-ended comparison with specified mean curvature has a non-strict model width bound. The boundary convention gives $+3$ on the unit sphere bounding the Euclidean ball. Comparisons use the distance inside the actual band. |
| Kyle Hayden, Thomas E. Mark and Lisa Piccirillo, *Exotic Mazur manifolds and knot trace invariants*, Adv. Math. 391 (2021), 107994, [doi:10.1016/j.aim.2021.107994](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2021.107994), [arXiv text, 1908.05269](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.05269) | Proposition 3.2 and proof, p. 12 of the preprint. An integral homology sphere which bounds a Mazur manifold and is a Heegaard Floer $L$-space is $S^3$. The proof identifies the attaching knot with the product core. Used only as an alternative certificate on the chosen winding-one component, including a probe framing. |
| Michel A. Kervaire, *Smooth homology spheres and their fundamental groups*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 144 (1969), 67–72, [Theorem 2](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/kervsmoo.pdf) | The finite fundamental group of a smooth homology three-sphere is trivial or the binary icosahedral group. The reader checked Theorem 2 together with the spherical-space-form identification used in the proof of the Chodosh–Maximo–Mukherjee corollary. |
| Martin Scharlemann, *Sutured manifolds and generalized Thurston norms*, J. Differential Geom. 29 (1989), 557–614, [author PDF](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~mgscharl/papers/suturedman.pdf) | Theorem 10.1, p. 612, in the independent reader's check of the reverse Property R statement. A surgery from a knot in $S^1\times S^2$ to $S^3$ identifies the product core up to isotopy. Applies to a specified surgery, with the remaining framed tubes transported in the actual upper marking. |
