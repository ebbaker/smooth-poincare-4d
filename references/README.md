# Shared reference catalog

The initial program was motivated by Edward B. Baker III's three unfinished draft papers, preserved in [unfinished_notes](unfinished_notes/README.md). The [reusable bibliography](references.bib) includes all three and selected sources from the current sphere manuscript. Existing manuscripts retain their self-contained bibliographies. This setup does not rewrite their scientific text or silently convert draft references into published works.

The catalog below is inherited from the supplied [20 November overview](../papers/research-overview/PROJECT_SUMMARY_2026-11-20.md). Titles, dates, passage notes, and earlier inspection claims are inherited metadata, not a new full-paper check in this setup. File hashes and sizes are in [IMPORT_MANIFEST.json](../IMPORT_MANIFEST.json). Follow [REFERENCE_WORKFLOW.md](../REFERENCE_WORKFLOW.md) before newly relying on a theorem.

## Ownership and availability

record the Fine–Panov sphere-bundle construction, the AHS integrability
theorem and the earlier first-jet/whole-fiber reconstruction hypotheses
used in the revised CME research plan. General off-integrable chart
theorems and general-data temporal bounds remain open; the
[assessed round-basin real comparison](SOURCE_NOTES_CME_REAL_TWISTOR_20261122.md#along-flow-real-comparison-consumers--e137e139e141)
now supplies specified along-flow estimates. The shared bibliography adds the
Fine–Panov 2009 entry while retaining its inspected 2008 preprint version.

The [22 November summary and special-case source notes](SOURCE_NOTES_CME_SPECIAL_CASES_20261122.md)
record the primary passages used to compare the chiral program with existing
sphere theorems and positive definite connection conjectures. They include the
Chang–Gursky–Yang norm conversion, Fine's local stability hypotheses,
the circle-invariant classification, and the limits of the targeted novelty
search. These checks support an explanation and candidate-target assessment,
not a new convergence or classification theorem.

The [Introduction progression source notes](SOURCE_NOTES_CME_INTRODUCTION_20261121.md)
record the additional uniformization, Hamilton–Perelman, Smale, Milnor,
Freedman, handle/knot-theoretic and Donaldson references checked on 21 November 2026.
They distinguish full-text passage checks, indexed passages and publisher
abstracts, and preserve the distinction between the published Donaldson
surface monograph and the accessible earlier draft. The shared bibliography
contains the ten new entries. Existing Fine, Atiyah–Hitchin–Singer and
Witten passages were revisited for the new opening narrative.

All branches reuse this catalog and record branch-specific use in their own references folder. Published reading PDFs are copied locally and ignored by Git; their copyright remains with their owners. Original author-draft PDFs are tracked. A fresh Git clone therefore includes the author drafts but not the third-party reading collection. Missing reading copies should be retrieved from primary sources with recorded provenance; no download was performed during setup.

Additional sources already cited in the [sphere manuscript](../papers/investigations-1/01_chiral_metric_evolution/manuscript/chiral_curvature_and_metric_evolution.tex) include Fine–Krasnov–Panov (2014), Gursky–Viaclovsky (2015), and Kuiper (1949). Their stable identifiers are retained in references.bib; no local copies or fresh inspection are implied.
