**R128. Jeffrey Streets.** *Asymptotic curvature decay and removal of singularities of Bach-flat metrics*. 0708.0869v1, Theorem 1.2 and §5. The scalar-flat constant-scalar Bach-flat limit must meet the Sobolev, curvature, volume and topological hypotheses. E12 uses smooth removal after the one-cone conclusion. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.0869)

**R129. Paul M. N. Feehan.** *Resolution of singularities and geometric proofs of the Łojasiewicz inequalities*. 1708.09775v13, Theorem 1. Analytic real functions on a finite-dimensional Euclidean neighborhood satisfy a local gradient inequality at a critical point. E15 applies this only to a finite-dimensional analytic graph. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.09775v13)

**R130. Sun-Yung A. Chang, Matthew J. Gursky, Paul C. Yang.** *An equation of Monge-Ampère type in conformal geometry, and four-manifolds of positive Ricci curvature*. Annals 155 (2002), Theorem A, Corollary B, arXiv:math/0409583v1. Positive Yamabe sign and positive total second Schouten symmetric function give a smooth admissible representative. The source doubles the Schouten convention used in E14/E18. [Source](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2002/155-3/p02)

**R131. Matthew Gursky, Jeffrey Streets.** *A formal Riemannian structure on conformal classes and uniqueness for the 2-Yamabe problem*. Geometry & Topology 22 (2018), §2.1 (2-1)–(2-5), Lemma 2.1 and Theorem 1.5. EXTRACT-E07 uses the divergence-free first Newton tensor and conformal derivative of the Schouten tensor. Fixed-class uniqueness of the admissible constant-curvature solution has a round exception. [Source](https://msp.org/gt/2018/22-6/gt-v22-n6-p08-p.pdf)

**R132. Matthew J. Gursky, Jeff A. Viaclovsky.** *Volume comparison and the sigma-k-Yamabe problem*. math/0210302v2, Theorems 1.1 and 1.4. E14 checks the assumptions of the conformal volume comparison, including the distinction between the borderline exponent and a strictly stronger cone. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0210302v2)

**R133. Shiing-Shen Chern, James Simons.** *Characteristic forms and geometric invariants*. Annals 99, 48–69, §3, Proposition 3.2. Transgression for an invariant polynomial on a connection. MATCH-E12 computes the curvature block factors, relative extension and trace normalization separately. [Source](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/1974/99-1/p03)

**R134. Hubert Bray, Felix Finster.** *Curvature estimates and the positive mass theorem*. math/9906047v3, §3 and Corollary 3.4. Comparison for MATCH-E11. The mass-weighted Witten-spinor estimate is for a complete asymptotically flat three-manifold and does not directly give the four-dimensional local estimate. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9906047v3)

**R135. Felix Finster.** *A level set analysis of the Witten spinor with applications to curvature estimates*. math/0701864v3, Theorem 3.3. Higher-dimensional level-set estimates for an asymptotically flat Witten-spinor family are antecedents of the normalization issue in MATCH-E11. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0701864v3)

**R136. Christian Bär.** *On nodal sets for Dirac and Laplace operators*. Main theorem for a nontrivial smooth solution of a generalized Dirac equation on a connected Riemannian manifold. The zero set has measure zero in the situation of MATCH-E11. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/dg-ga/9707008)

**R137. Hai-Ping Fu.** *Four-manifolds with positive Yamabe constant*. Pacific J. Math. 296 (2018), Proposition E, (2-1)–(2-9), Theorems B/B*. E16 converts the four-index norm and verifies the strict half-harmonic gap, including its equality case on a homotopy sphere. [Source](https://doi.org/10.2140/pjm.2018.296.79)

**R138. Matthew Gursky, Stephen McKeown, Aaron Tyrrell.** *Self-dual and even Poincaré–Einstein metrics in dimension four*. JIMJ 25 (2026), §3, (3.3), (3.7) and Corollary 3.4. The half-harmonic formulas used in E16 precede their application to a filling. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748025101060)

**R139. Andrzej Derdziński.** *Self-dual Kähler manifolds and Einstein manifolds of dimension four*. Compositio Math. 49 (1983), §2 Remark 1 and Proposition 5. The repeated-eigenvalue case when half-Weyl is harmonic has a local conformal Kähler interpretation. E16 also checks the equality obstruction directly. [Source](https://www.numdam.org/article/CM_1983__49_3_405_0.pdf)

**R140. Haïm Brezis, Elliott Lieb.** *A relation between pointwise convergence of functions and convergence of functionals*. Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 88 (1983), Theorem 1. Splitting a bounded fourth-power mass for a.e.-convergent functions is used in the fixed-source normalization step of E17. [Source](https://www.mat.ufpb.br/jmbo/papers/Brezis/Brezis-Lieb.pdf)

**R141. Giovanni Catino, Paolo Mastrolia, Dario Daniele Monticelli, Marco Rigoli.** *Conformal Ricci solitons and related integrability conditions*. Inspected author manuscript, §2 (2.12). The four-dimensional Bach formula and conformal weight enter the bounded-energy oscillatory example in MATCH-E10. [Source](https://cvgmt.sns.it/document/2850/)
