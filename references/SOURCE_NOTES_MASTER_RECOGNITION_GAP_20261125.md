25 November 2026. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance. Checked passages are identified below. | Primary text and inspected version | Passage and use |

| G. Perelman, *The entropy formula for the Ricci flow and its geometric applications*, arXiv:math/0211159, §3. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0211159) | On an existing smooth closed Ricci flow, transfer the initial uniform lower bound for $\mu(g(0),t+\tau)$ to $\mu(g(t),\tau)$. The initial inhomogeneous Sobolev bound and scalar lower bound furnish the initial estimate for bounded positive parameters. |
| R. Ye, *The logarithmic Sobolev inequality along the Ricci flow*, arXiv:0707.2424v4, Theorem 5.5(1), equations (5.17)–(5.18). [Version inspected](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0707.2424) · [Journal version](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40304-015-0046-1) | Use the fixed-time implication from the bounded-parameter logarithmic inequality to the inhomogeneous one with potential $R/4$. Its negative part is uniformly bounded by scalar minimum propagation. Ye's initial normalized Sobolev constant in §2 has a prescribed lower-order coefficient. The proof here uses the general initial inequality directly in the entropy step. |
| R. S. Hamilton, *The Formation of Singularities in the Ricci Flow*, Surveys in Differential Geometry 2, Theorems 7.1, 8.1 and 16.1. [Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.4310/SDG.1993.v2.n1.a2) · [Compactness article](https://doi.org/10.2307/2375080) | The first derivative bound is used on an already bounded parabolic slab. On a closed flow a finite maximal time forces curvature blow-up. Marked compactness at a positive time requires bounds on all curvature derivatives on fixed balls and a positive basepoint injectivity radius. The present proof obtains a uniform diameter bound to get diffeomorphisms of closed manifolds. |
| W.-X. Shi, *Deforming the metric on complete Riemannian manifolds*, J. Differential Geom. 30 (1989), 223–301. [Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214443292) · [Independent verification](../papers/master/01_fixed_type_variational_descent/reviews/E57_RIGIDITY.md) | On a complete already curvature-bounded positive-time slab use derivative bounds on a smaller time interval. Standard higher-derivative input, distinguished here from the separately derived common lifespan. |
| J. Cheeger, M. Gromov and M. Taylor, *Finite propagation speed, kernel estimates for functions of the Laplace operator, and the geometry of complete Riemannian manifolds*, J. Differential Geom. 17 (1982), Theorem 4.7(i), p. 47. [Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214436699) | Apply on each positive-time source at a fixed radius below the positive curvature comparison scale. Bounded sectional curvature and the measured volume lower bound there give uniform injectivity. |
| S.-Y. A. Chang, M. J. Gursky and P. C. Yang, *A conformally invariant sphere theorem in four dimensions*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 98 (2003), Theorem B and Remarks 1–2. [Published version](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1007/s10240-003-0017-z.pdf) | Apply the equality classification on the actual smooth positive-Yamabe limit with full four-index Weyl integral $16\pi^2\chi$. The limit is diffeomorphic to the late source manifolds. The other classified topologies are incompatible with the homotopy sphere. |
| T. Liimatainen and M. Salo, *Local Gauge Conditions for Ellipticity in Conformal Geometry*, IMRN 2016, Theorem 1.1. [Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnv255) | The determinant-normalized conformal metric and four-harmonic coordinate condition motivate the [marked disk gauge](../papers/master/01_fixed_type_variational_descent/notes/E56_DISK_COLLARS.md).
