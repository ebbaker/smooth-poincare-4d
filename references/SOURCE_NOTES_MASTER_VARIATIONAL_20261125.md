| Gang Tian and Jeff Viaclovsky, *Volume growth, curvature decay, and critical metrics*, Comment. Math. Helv. 83 (2008), 889–911. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.4171/CMH/147) · [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0612491) | Published (1.5) and Thm 1.2 give interior curvature estimates on the limit solving the critical Ricci system with local volume lower bound and small full curvature energy. Thm 1.1 requires a homogeneous Sobolev inequality, little-o quadratic decay and finite negative Ricci energy. It gives finitely many order-zero ALE ends without a Betti bound. Checked both theorem statements. Their narrower Thm 1.3 is unnecessary. |
| Tian–Viaclovsky, *Moduli spaces of critical Riemannian metrics in dimension four*, arXiv:math/0312318v4. [Text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0312318) | §2.1 gives the Bach variation and constant-scalar Ricci system. Conditions following (1.5)–(1.6) specify differentiated end coordinates. Used on the limit after local minimizing variations. |
| Antonio Ache and Jeff Viaclovsky, *Obstruction-flat asymptotically locally Euclidean metrics*, arXiv:1106.1249v2. [Text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1106.1249) | Definitions 1.1 and 1.6, Theorems 1.2 and 1.8, Remark 7.2. In dimension four the scalar-flat Bach equation improves the differentiated order-zero ends to order two. The removable-point result needs constant scalar on the punctured neighborhood and its coordinate bounds. Checked in the paper. In the trivial group case the new chart extends smoothly. |
| Michael T. Anderson and Jeff Cheeger, *Cα-compactness for manifolds with Ricci curvature and injectivity radius bounded below*, JDG 35 (1992), 265–281. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1214448075) · [Inspected text](https://electronicsandbooks.com/edt/manual/Magazine/J/Journal%20of%20Differential%20Geometry/1992/35-2-265.pdf) | Thm 0.3 and (0.5)–(0.7), local harmonic radii under a Ricci lower bound and local injectivity control. The source bounds here additionally control all Ricci components in rescaled windows. |
| Igor Belegradek and Vitali Kapovitch, *Finiteness theorems for nonnegatively curved vector bundles*, arXiv:math/0002028v1. [Text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0002028) | Thm 3.1 and proof, relative compactness and buffered compact domains. The proof uses finite harmonic atlases to give whole-domain smooth identifications. Stronger coefficient regularity here comes from the bounded rescaled curvature and harmonic Ricci equation. |
| Rodrigo Avalos, Albachiara Cogo and Andoni Royo Abrego, *Bach-flat metrics and global regularity of conformal structures*, arXiv:2610.35257v1. [Text](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.35257v1) | Thm 2.2(ii) and its local form, Lemma 5.1, (4.1) and (5.6). Inspected the local tensor Laplacian regularity and weak Ricci identity used on the rough harmonic limit with bounded curvature and constant scalar. No global compact-manifold theorem applied to the open limit. |
| Massimiliano Iori and Riccardo Piergallini, *4-manifolds as covers of the 4-sphere branched over non-singular surfaces*, Geom. Topol. 6 (2002), 393–401. [Primary text](https://mat.unicam.it/piergallini/home/papers/fourmanifbis.pdf) | p. 395, simple five-fold covering of the PL sphere and locally flat PL branch surface. | Pertti Mattila, *Geometry of Sets and Measures in Euclidean Spaces*, Cambridge 1995, §2, Thm 2.1. The [joint collar sources](SOURCE_NOTES_JOINT_COLLAR_20261125.md) and [second collar sources](SOURCE_NOTES_SECOND_COLLAR_20261125.md) own the marked topology, end and characteristic-form inputs. E11 and E13 checked the points at which those arguments use the limit equation or a whole embedding. Schoen's four-dimensional strict comparison in Theorem 2 is used on the smooth conformal compactification; the point has zero Sobolev capacity. [Schoen](https://math.jhu.edu/~js/Math646/schoen.yamabe.pdf) · [Independent end assessment](../papers/master/reviews/E11_COMPLETE_LIMIT_CHECK.md) · [Independent manuscript assessment](../papers/master/reviews/E13_MANUSCRIPT_AUDIT.md).

The [Einstein packet](../papers/master/notes/E05_CLOSED_STABILITY.md) identifies its inspected Gursky-type sharp chiral bounds, equality passages and Akutagawa–Endo–Seshadri comparison on the standard sphere. Its extension over varying homotopy-sphere types uses the owning marked end obstruction. The [flow packet](../papers/master/notes/E07_BACH_SELECTION.md) identifies the derivative, volume, lifespan and oscillatory norms verified against Bahuaud–Helliwell, López and Bahuaud–Guenther–Isenberg–Mazzeo. Neither secondary route provides a general source of compactness.
