| Publication and inspected passage | Use and hypotheses |

| Bing-Long Chen and Xi-Ping Zhu, *A conformally invariant classification theorem in four dimensions*, Communications in Analysis and Geometry 22 (2014), 811–831, Theorems 1.4, 1.6, 1.7, Lemma 2.1 and (1.9). [Published paper](https://www.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/cag/2014/0022/0005/CAG-2014-0022-0005-a002.pdf) | Maximum of the two largest real Weyl operator eigenvalues; finite equality has its own classification. Their modified quotient and a positive conformal factor in the enlarged Hölder class give constant modified scalar curvature. The spectral deficit, equality exceptions on a homotopy sphere, and auxiliary volume argument were each checked. |
| Hai-Ping Fu, *Four-manifolds with positive Yamabe constant*, Pacific J. Math. 296 (2018), Theorem 1.3 and p. 79. [Published paper](https://msp.org/pjm/2018/296-1/pjm-v296-n1-p05-s.pdf) | Independent full-tensor Weyl pinching test for an actual positive class; the Ricci-window proof also follows directly from the Chen–Zhu deficit. |
| Jeffrey Meier, Trent Schirmer and Alexander Zupan, *Classification of trisections and the generalized Property R conjecture*, Corollary 1.3(2), specialized to two components, and Jeffrey Meier and Alexander Zupan, *Generalized square knots and homotopy 4-spheres*, JDG 122 (2022), Theorems 1.1–1.2. [Tunnel theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.06561) · [Square knots](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.08527) | The input is the actual integrally framed link with the specified surgery boundary. At tunnel number two the extra split zero-framed unknot belongs to a cancelling pair. The knot theorem covers a specified component of a two-handle presentation with no 1-handles. |

J. 26 (1979), §1 and Theorem 2; Tsukasa Isoshima, *Trisections of the doubles of some Mazur type 4-manifolds*, arXiv:2403.17356v2, Fig. 37. No immersed-height realization is imported. |
