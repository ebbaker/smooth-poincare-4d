# Sources for the Hopf, degree and punctured Dirac tests

24 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III by the selected OpenAI lead. Specific passages were inspected by the assigned Sol 6.2/ultra helpers or the lead on this date. Realized model metadata unavailable. No new reading copies retained. Source records distinguish the original hypotheses from the derivations in the [branch notes](../papers/investigations-1/03_first_order_twistor/02_dirac_and_twistor_spinors/notes/README.md).

**R60 · Vector Bundles and K-Theory.** Allen Hatcher. Author text v2.2, November 2017. §3.1 Prop. 3.10, §3.2 Prop. 3.13(a,c), §3.3 Props. 3.19 and 3.22. Classification of line bundles on CW spaces, Euler class and the primary obstruction to a sphere-bundle section over a four-complex. E18/E22 specialize these to pullbacks over a homotopy sphere. [Inspected text](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/VBKT/VB.pdf)

**R61 · Lecture notes for 18.155: distributions, elliptic regularity, and applications to PDEs.** Semyon Dyatlov. Author lecture notes dated 10 December 2022, §17.3.4, Thms. 17.15 and 17.17, pp. 250–252. Compact Hodge decomposition and harmonic representatives used in E18; the connection constants are derived there. [Inspected text](https://math.mit.edu/~dyatlov/18.155/155-notes.pdf)

**R62 · A unique continuation theorem for solutions of elliptic partial differential equations or inequalities of second order.** Nachman Aronszajn. Journal scan, §1 (1.1) and restrictions on pp. 235–236, Remark 3 p. 248 for systems with the same scalar principal part. Smooth compact metrics give the local regularity required for E19 scalar eigenfunctions and E29 Dirac-square components. [Inspected text](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~blwilson/Nodal/Aronszajn.pdf)

**R63 · The smallest Laplace eigenvalue of homogeneous 3-spheres.** Emilio A. Lauret. arXiv:1801.04259v3, 17 March 2025, (1.2), Thm. 1.1, (2.5), Prop. 2.3 (2.8), (3.8), Prop. 3.9. Fixed homogeneous Berger family only. E20 checks the homothety convention against the actual metric formula. [Inspected text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.04259)

**R64 · On bifurcation of solutions of the Yamabe problem in product manifolds.** L. L. de Lima, P. Piccione, M. Zedda. Published Cor. 4.8 and §4.2 through Prop. 4.9. The two equal-dimensional round factors reach the first bifurcation ratio at squared-radius ratio 1/2; the open uniqueness result does not assert global minimality there. E20. [Inspected text](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1016/j.anihpc.2011.10.005.pdf)

**R65 · Sharp interpolation inequalities on the sphere: New methods and consequences.** Jean Dolbeault, Maria J. Esteban, Michal Kowalczyk, Michael Loss. Published p. 99, (1.1), unit round sphere and normalized measure. E20 uses this inequality with its own cutoff and fiber estimate to handle a sufficiently short circle. [Inspected text](https://repositorio.uchile.cl/bitstream/handle/2250/126455/Sharp%20Interpolation.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)

**R66 · Lectures on Kähler Manifolds.** Werner Ballmann. Oxford-hosted text, Appendix A (A.33)–(A.35), Remark A.37, Prop. A.38(1), pp. 130–131. Chern–Weil determinant and naturality. E22 computes the quaternionic Hopf sign in its own orientation. [Inspected text](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/~joyce/Nairobi2019/BallmannKahlerManifolds.pdf)

**R67 · Connecting rational homotopy type singularities.** Robert Hardt, Tristan Rivière. Journal PDF, pp. 27–28, 53–59. Prop. 3.4 (3.14)–(3.16) and slicing in (3.22)–(3.25). On the sphere domain weak critical Sobolev convergence suffices for the integer signed defect in the degree case. E25/E30 give the direct fixed-manifold proof for smooth limit and strong W¹,², and the coefficient for the Hilbert–Schmidt energy. [Inspected text](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/117/6699-11511_2008_Article_23.pdf)

**R68 · Harmonic spinors and local deformations of the metric.** Bernd Ammann, Mattias Dahl, Emmanuel Humbert. Published §1 (1), Thm. 1.1 and Cor. 1.1, pp. 927–928, §2.1 p. 929, §3.2 p. 932. Compared arXiv:0903.4544v3, where the corollary is 1.2. A compact connected spin manifold admits a D-minimal metric equal to the original outside a prescribed ball; the dimension-four argument is explicit. E26 takes the ball disjoint from the flat germ. [Inspected text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.4544)

**R69 · The index of elliptic operators on compact manifolds.** M. F. Atiyah, I. M. Singer. Published §3.3 Thm. 4, p. 428, spin Dirac index on a closed spin manifold. E26 combines the complex index with the previously recorded Hirzebruch signature formula. [Inspected text](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/atiyahsing.pdf)

**R70 · Harmonic spinors.** Nigel Hitchin. Published §4.2, remark after Prop. 4.2 p. 41 on the real index normalization, and §1.4 Prop. 1.3 pp. 10–11 on conformal Dirac weights. E26/E31 distinguish the quaternionic index from the complex one and the harmonic conformal weight from the twistor weight. [Inspected text](https://users.math.msu.edu/users/parker/GT/Harmonic%20Spinors.pdf)

**R71 · Mass endomorphism and spinorial Yamabe type problems on conformally flat manifolds.** Bernd Ammann, Emmanuel Humbert, Bertrand Morel. arXiv:math/0503299v2, 27 September 2006. §2.1, Def. 2.1, Remark 2.2 and Prop. 2.3 pp. 3–4. Invertible compact Dirac and flat germ give a Euclidean pole plus smooth harmonic remainder; E27 checks the sign by a cutoff parametrix. Prop. 2.8 and Remark 2.13 give adjoint symmetry and quaternionic equivariance. The global conformal-flat assumption of other applications is unnecessary for the local expansion. [Inspected text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0503299)

**R72 · Positive mass theorem for the Yamabe problem on spin manifolds.** Bernd Ammann, Emmanuel Humbert. Corrected author PDF, 15 December 2005, proof of Thm. 2.2 through (2.3), pp. 3–4. Compare harmonic Green spinor transport and integrated boundary formula. Their scalar-flat Yamabe Green blow-up uses positive Yamabe class. E27/E28 use a prescribed exactly flat end and derive their own signed scalar identity without this hypothesis. [Inspected text](https://ammann.app.uni-regensburg.de/preprints/masse/masse_corr.pdf)

The existing R54–R59 records continue to own Listing, Bourguignon–Gauduchon, Lee–Parker and Talenti. E31 additionally checked Lee–Parker (1.1) p. 38, Def. 8.2 and (8.10) p. 73 and the Appendix pp. 88, 90 for the boundary expression and their ADM convention. Friedrich §1 pp. 59–60 is the existing source for twistor covariance. [Earlier passages](SOURCE_NOTES_DIRAC_20261124.md)

**R73 · $W^{2,p}$-Solvability of the Dirichlet Problem for Nondivergence Elliptic Equations with VMO Coefficients.** Filippo Chiarenza, Michele Frasca, Placido Longo. Published Thm. 4.1, (4.4), p. 850. E32 applies the local elliptic estimate after annular rescaling of smooth uniformly elliptic coefficients. [Inspected text](https://www.karlin.mff.cuni.cz/~kaplicky/pages/pages/2010z/ChiaFraLon1993.pdf)

**R74 · The mass of an asymptotically flat manifold.** Robert Bartnik. Published §4 (4.4), (4.6), Thm. 4.2 pp. 680–681. E32 checks the decay, weighted regularity and scalar integrability for chart independence on the single end. Its raw flux and normalized ADM mass differ by a factor $6|S^3|$. [Inspected text](https://math.jhu.edu/~js/Math646/bartnik.mass.pdf)

**R75 · Spinors and mass on weighted manifolds.** Julius Baldauf, Tristan Ozuch. Publisher version, 7 July 2022, §1.2 after (0.5). E32 checks the flux normalization in the unweighted case. [Inspected text](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-022-04420-y)

**R76 · Slowly converging Yamabe flows.** Alessandro Carlotto, Otis Chodosh, Yanir A. Rubinstein. Published §2 Prop. 7 and (4), Prop. 13, Prop. 25 and App. A, pp. 1530–1534, 1562–1564; compared arXiv:1401.3738v3. The reduced cubic is proportional to the triple product on the Jacobi kernel. E39 checks the dimension of the full sine–cosine kernel in the circle product example. The authors’ 2 June 2016 correction to Prop. 13 concerns the nonlinear exponent in higher nullity. [Erratum](https://web.stanford.edu/~ochodosh/Errata-Slow-Flow.pdf). [Inspected text](https://msp.org/gt/2015/19-3/gt-v19-n3-p11-p.pdf)

**R77 · Certain conditions for a Riemannian manifold to be isometric with a sphere.** Morio Obata. Published Thm. A p. 334 and Thms. 2, 4–5 p. 336. Recognizes a connected complete manifold from a nonconstant function with prescribed full Hessian. E39 separates this hypothesis from the sharp fourth-moment step at a Yamabe minimizer. [Inspected text](https://doi.org/10.2969/jmsj/01430333)

**R78 · On the volume elements on a manifold.** Jürgen Moser. Published §1 pp. 286–287, oriented compact manifold and two smooth positive equal-volume forms. E40 checks the direction of the volume-form transport after its exponential tilt. DOI 10.1090/S0002-9947-1965-0182927-5. [Inspected text](https://abel.math.harvard.edu/~dafr/M392C-2018-MorseTheory/Readings/Moser.pdf)

**R79 · Pincement sur le spectre et le volume en courbure de Ricci positive.** Erwann Aubry. Published Thm. 1 p. 388, indexing p. 387, §6 Prop. 19 pp. 401–402. On a complete manifold with normalized Ricci lower bound $n-1$, pinching the $n$th positive eigenvalue toward $n$ gives smooth sphere recognition and a Gromov–Hausdorff bound. E45 rescales the additional pointwise Ricci hypothesis; the Yamabe quartic argument has no such hypothesis. [Inspected text](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_2005_4_38_3_387_0.pdf)
