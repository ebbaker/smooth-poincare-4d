# Sources for the reflective-seam and calibration additions to the master

26 November 2026, America/New_York. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III by Claude (Anthropic, model `claude-fable-5-2`, Cowork session, reasoning effort max as the session's configuration records it). Repository baseline `de2125d`. This receipt records the sources on which the three inputs in the master's [claude folder](../../papers/master/manuscript/claude/umbilic_doubling.tex) depend, and the level at which each was inspected on that date. Every reading was made online, through a fetched text extraction or a search excerpt. No third-party file was added to the repository. Bibliographic records are in the shared [references.bib](../references.bib). The four new keys are `Kobayashi1985Conformal`, `LeBrun2013DelPezzo`, `LeBrun2023Balance` and `Labbi1997Surgery`. The derivations that use these sources are in the [brainstorm note](../../papers/master/brainstorm/notes/claude/PATHS_FORWARD_AFTER_ATTAINMENT_AND_ENTRY_TRIALS_20261126.md) and its check scripts.

The norms are those of the master: Hilbert–Schmidt for each block \(W^\pm\), so that \(12\pi^2\tau=\int(|W^+|^2-|W^-|^2)\). Fu's four-index square is four times this for one block. LeBrun's two papers use the master's norm.

Two citations point to companion manuscripts. The Weyl eigenvalues \((K+1)(1/3,-1/6,-1/6)\) of a product \(q+g_{S^2}\) are taken from the subsection "The cost of a product circle cap" of the [fixed-type descent paper](../../papers/master/01_fixed_type_variational_descent/manuscript/fixed_type_chiral_energy_descent.tex) and were recomputed from the Riemann tensor in `check_01`. The strict Einstein bound is the companion theorem named above. I read its statement and proof once and did not audit it.

The additions also use standard facts without citation, and I inspected no source for them: homotopic embedded circles in a four-manifold are isotopic; surgery on the circle factor of \(S^1\times S^3\) gives \(S^4\) for either framing; the boundary term of Chern–Gauss–Bonnet vanishes on a totally geodesic boundary; an umbilic hypersurface of a conformally flat space lies in a round sphere, and conformal maps between such domains are Möbius in dimension at least three; a smooth closed disc is conformal to the closed unit disc by a map smooth up to the boundary; a Fuchsian group of a compact surface with geodesic boundary is convex cocompact, and its quotient of the domain of discontinuity on \(S^4\) is the boundary of the surface times \(D^3\).

Not imported: A. Malchiodi and F. Malizia, *Weyl energy and connected sums of four-manifolds*, arXiv:2505.17752v1, 23 May 2025, abstract only. The note mentions its strict subadditivity statement. The master does not cite it, and it has no entry in the bibliography.
