## 3. Sources of the frameworks note

| Key, source and inspection | Statement relied on |
| --- | --- |
| `McDuffSalamon2012JHolomorphic`, Theorem 9.4.2, and `Gromov1985PseudoHolomorphic`. Book and paper not opened. The first excerpt omitted hypothesis (1); the second states it. | A connected symplectic four-manifold with no symplectically embedded sphere of square $-1$, symplectomorphic outside a compact set to the complement of a compact star-shaped set in standard $\mathbb R^4$, is symplectomorphic to $\mathbb R^4$, and the symplectomorphism can be chosen equal to the given one outside any neighbourhood of the compact set. Abstracts and introductory excerpts. | Near-symplectic Gromov invariants, defined when all zero circles are untwisted, equal the Seiberg–Witten invariants modulo two.
