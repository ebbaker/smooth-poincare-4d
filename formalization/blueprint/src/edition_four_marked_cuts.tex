\section{Edition 04: geometric inputs for smooth recognition}
\label{sec:ed04}
The seven program targets below are followed by checked results on fixed sources and supplied marked presentations.
The smooth category, the markings, and the quantifiers are part of each
target. Write $T=4\pi^2$. For an oriented metric, $E_\sigma(g;U)$
is the integral of the squared Hilbert--Schmidt norm of the signed Weyl
block over $U$. Write $I^+(X)$ for the infimum of $E_+$ over the
positive Yamabe classes on the indicated smooth type.

\begin{openproblem}[Round product at a fixed physical face]
\label{ed04:exact-face}
\uses{prop:ed04-physical-face}
On a closed oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere in a positive class
of energy $e<2T$, fix one smoothly marked separating three-sphere with
conformally round induced metric and umbilic second fundamental form.
Formalize its conformal Gaussian normalization and cutoff on two
independently chosen positive subwidths $w_L,w_R$. For each tolerance,
construct a smooth path fixed on the outer germs with product endpoint,
peak joined energy below $2T$, and signed drift on each complete side
smaller in absolute value than the tolerance. All choices are made on
this fixed source.
\end{openproblem}

\begin{openproblem}[Positivity on the two actual caps]
\label{ed04:caps}
\uses{ed04:exact-face,lem:master-wall,prop:ed03-inclusive-caps,prop:ed04-positive-class,prop:ed04-round-face-control}
From the joined path, insert a round product cylinder of arbitrary
finite length. Transplant each compactly supported test to the joined
metric, retain a uniform positive Yamabe bound, and pass through the
added center on each cap by density. Identify each capped smooth type
in its prescribed angular marking and recover the original oriented
manifold by its actual boundary map. Each cap energy in either sign is
the corresponding endpoint side energy.
\end{openproblem}

\begin{openproblem}[Limiting raw masses on fixed capped types]
\label{ed04:side-masses}
\uses{ed04:caps,prop:ed03-inclusive-caps,prop:ed04-fixed-cap-masses}
Under the exact-face hypotheses, prove that on each original side the
two chiral masses agree at a value $m_j$ and $I^+(\widehat P_j)\le m_j$.
For a nonstandard source precisely one capped side has its smooth type
and the other has raw mass at most $e-I^+(M)$. First construct actual
positive caps for each tolerance on this fixed source, then take the
infimum on each fixed capped type. For a parametrized disk the exterior
cap carries the original smooth type. Its excess bound is the disk
specialization recorded in Proposition~\ref{prop:ed03-net-saving}.
\end{openproblem}

\begin{openproblem}[Recognition of the intermediate three-manifold]
\label{ed04:prefix}
\uses{prop:ed04-integral-prefix,prop:ed04-product-price}
For a closed smooth homotopy sphere in a reduced mixed presentation
with one lower, two middle and one upper handle of indices one, two and
three respectively, realize the winding row $(1,0)$ by marked slides.
For the winding-one first boundary, a map of nonzero degree from a closed
oriented three-manifold of positive scalar curvature implies that the
boundary is diffeomorphic to $S^3$. For the winding-zero first boundary,
the corresponding conclusion is the connected sum of two copies of
$S^1\times S^2$. In the latter case the smooth filling and its
compressions are required to exclude residual homology-sphere summands.
In either case, use the original framed trace and top attachment to
identify the closed four-manifold.
\end{openproblem}

\begin{openproblem}[Reduction followed by a transported second test]
\label{ed04:successor}
\uses{prop:ed04-native-prefix}
For a reduced presentation with handle counts $(1,1,3,2,1)$ in index
order and normalized winding row $(1,0,0)$, a positive-scalar-curvature
domination certificate on the first winding-one boundary gives a
presentation on the same closed smooth type with no lower one-handle
and two middle handles. Carry both surviving attaching maps and the
upper identification through the reduction. Domination on the second
actual prefix then recognizes the closed type. For counts $(1,2,3,1,1)$
use the reversed presentation with its own cuts and markings.
\end{openproblem}

\begin{openproblem}[Positive entry on a marked double]
\label{ed04:entry}
\uses{prop:ed04-round-mode}
Fix a smooth compact oriented contractible half $W$, a boundary metric
$\gamma$ in its marking and an orientation-preserving $\gamma$-isometry
$f$. Seek a positive-scalar-curvature extension of the exact tensor
$\gamma$ with strictly positive Robin trace form on every nonzero
$H^{1/2}$ trace. The sign criterion supplies a totally geodesic positive
filling in the marked conformal class. For an actual such filling $h$,
write $B_h=E_+(h;W)+E_-(h;W)$. Construct positive metrics on the fixed
double $W\cup_f(-W)$ with both chiral energies tending to $B_h$.
Writing $\Lambda$ for the greater of the two top Weyl eigenvalues and
$G_h=\int_W\Lambda_h^2\,dV_h$, the closed spectral quantity
$4E_++3\int\Lambda^2\,dV$ should tend to $4B_h+6G_h$.
If $B_h<2T$, one finite member enters the subcritical band. A witness
on a candidate $M$ requires its marked smooth identification with this
double and the budget of the resulting filling.
\end{openproblem}

\begin{openproblem}[Selection on every untreated smooth type]
\label{ed04:coverage}
For each hypothetical nonstandard smooth homotopy four-sphere, take a
finite handle presentation minimizing the number of middle handles.
Produce a marked strict net reduction of this minimum, direct smooth
recognition, or positive entry together with a finite positive
competitor of energy strictly below $I^+(M)$ on this very type.
The alternative used may depend on $M$. A single entry metric, an
excluded shape of a charged disk, and a boundary test on a known
presentation each address only part of this selection problem.
\end{openproblem}

\subsection{Fixed-source and marked-prefix results}
\label{sec:ed04-fixed-source-results}

\begin{proposition}[Normalization of the entire ordinary surgery matrix]
\label{prop:ed04-integral-prefix}
\lean{SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.normalizedBlockCongr, SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.windingZeroBlock_eq_zero_of_rank, SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.mixedSurgeryMatrix_det, SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.windingZeroBlock_eq_zero_of_integralCokernel, SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.unitWinding_relation, SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.boundaryInclusionEquiv, SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.compressionBoundary_bijective}
\leanok
In the normalized integral order (dot, winding-one component, remaining
components), write $L$ for the full matrix and $C$ for its last $k$ rows
and columns restricted to those components. An integral change of generators
gives $A\oplus C$, where $A=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
An identification $\operatorname{coker}_{\mathbb Z}L\cong\mathbb Z^k$
forces every entry of $C$ to vanish. For $k=1$, $\det L=-C_{00}$.
The unit winding relation kills the cyclic generator. For the other order,
if the integral pair sequence of the ordinary comparison filling has its
two flanking maps zero, boundary inclusion is an isomorphism on $H_2$.
If its factor spheres form a basis, attaching the relative three-handles
along those spheres gives a bijective boundary map in degree three.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The leading block is unimodular and the Schur correction is zero.
Rank-nullity gives integral rank two. A nonzero $C_{ij}$ would give a
three-by-three minor of determinant $-C_{ij}$ in the transformed matrix.
Exactness gives the inclusion isomorphism. On the specified handle basis
the relative boundary agrees with that isomorphism composed with the
change to the sphere basis.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Transport past a supplied cancelling pair]
\label{prop:ed04-native-prefix}
\lean{SmoothPoincare.exists_nativeThreeToTwo, SmoothPoincare.prefixResidualTubes_disjoint, SmoothPoincare.prefixTraceHomeomorph_commutes, SmoothPoincare.prefixClosingMark_marked, SmoothPoincare.orientedDegree_prefixTransport_ne_zero_iff, SmoothPoincare.ClosedMarkedControl.splitAbsorptionFace_product, SmoothPoincare.smoothMarkedAssembly_marked}
\leanok
A native geometric index-one/two cancelling pair in the specified regular
Morse strip of a three-middle presentation removes precisely its two
critical points. Above that strip the cuts retain their original points.
Given the boundary comparison $e$ from the successor to the original cut,
both framed tubes pull back by $e^{-1}$, including their disk parameters.
The quotient trace comparison commutes with first attaching just one of the
two handles. Its upper boundary comparison $e_{\rm top}$, when supplied,
gives closing map $\varphi e_{\rm top}$ and inverse
$e_{\rm top}^{-1}\varphi^{-1}$. An incoming certificate keeps nonzero
integral degree under the inverse marking of its own cut.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Remove the two points in the pinned smooth cancellation theorem and count
the remaining indexed critical points. Apply the actual lower comparison
on the body and the identity on each handle. These maps and their inverses
respect the attaching relations. Degree under a diffeomorphism changes by
a unit. Smooth closed assembly requires local smoothness in the two
interiors and across the whole marked seam in both directions.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Energy of the physical product band]
\label{prop:ed04-product-price}
\lean{SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.cylinderMetric_chiralWeylDensities, SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.riemannianProductBand_integral_eq, SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.riemannianProductBand_completeSideLedger, SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.exists_productBand_width_and_budget_iff, SmoothPoincare.PositiveScalarCuts.exists_shortProductBand_twoMean_and_budget_iff}
\leanok
On the marked product cylinder in the three-dimensional Ricci frame write
$J=\int|\mathrm{Ric}^{\circ}_h|^2$ and $A_\sigma$ for both retained
sides plus the entire fixed transition in sign $\sigma$. Each half of
the band contributes $LJ/8$ in each chirality. Suppose $\rho>0$ and
$J\ge0$. There is a length with $\rho L^2\ge3\pi^2$ and joined
endpoint energy strictly below $2T$ in both signs if and only if
$A_\sigma+\pi\sqrt{3/\rho}\,J/4<2T$ in both signs. With zero end
means, an arbitrarily short positive length passes the strict end-mean
test and this energy budget exactly when both $A_\sigma<2T$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The complementary spatial sectional curvatures are $R/2-r_i$ and the
axial curvatures vanish. Both trace-free chiral compressions therefore
have entries $-(r_i-R/3)/2$ in the adapted frame. Product integration
gives the two half prices. The full energy is nondecreasing in $L$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A fixed positive cap family tests the original masses]
\label{prop:ed04-fixed-cap-masses}
\lean{SmoothPoincare.FixedMarkedCutCore.rawMasses_of_positiveCaps, SmoothPoincare.fixedSourceRawSideMasses, SmoothPoincare.eventually_no_smallDriftCaps_of_chargedOriginalDisk}
\leanok
On two fixed marked closures assume actual positive cap metrics approximate
the corresponding full raw energies in both signs at every tolerance.
Each original side then has equal signed masses $m_j$ and
$I^+(\widehat P_j)\le m_j$. With complete a.e.-disjoint coverage,
$e<2T$, and the two furnished recognition and reassembly comparisons,
a nonstandard source is the surviving capped type and the discarded
side has mass at most $e-I^+(M)$ in each sign.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The cap has equal signed totals at every tolerance. Test each actual
cap in the infimum of its fixed marked type, then let its error tend
to zero on that source. One raw mass is strictly below $T$. Choose a
positive tolerance leaving its actual cap within the inclusive recognition
bound and apply the marked comparison in that direction. The charged-disk
sequence exclusion uses this finite-source implication at each index.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Attained energy of a round end mode]
\label{prop:ed04-round-mode}
\lean{SmoothPoincare.IntrinsicEntry.roundCylinderMode_isLeast, SmoothPoincare.IntrinsicEntry.roundCylinderMode_inf_eq}
\leanok
For a unit round degree-$l$ mode of trace $f$, in the finite-energy
classical half-cylinder domain with decay, the least energy is
$6(l+1)f^2$ and the profile $w(t)=f\exp(-(l+1)t)$ attains it.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Integrate the cross term in the square of $w'+(l+1)w$. Its endpoint
contribution uses the prescribed trace and decay. The remaining square
vanishes for the displayed admissible exponential.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Physical interpolation on a supplied normalized bicollar]
\label{prop:ed04-physical-face}
\lean{SmoothPoincare.exactFaceNormalizingExponent_face_jet, SmoothPoincare.exactFaceNormalizedMetric_first_tangential_jet, SmoothPoincare.exists_exactFacePhysicalPath, SmoothPoincare.exactFacePhysicalPath_joint_coefficient, SmoothPoincare.exactFaceNormalizedPath_pullback_sub_round, SmoothPoincare.exactFaceDefect_taylor_bounds, SmoothPoincare.exactFacePhysicalPath_joined_bound, SmoothPoincare.exists_exactFace_subwidth_budgets}
\leanok
An original signed Gaussian chart supplies the conformal normalizing
exponent with prescribed value and derivative $-H/3$ on the face.
Given the normalized marked bicollar and its two available physical
widths, each positive subwidth pair supports a smooth metric path from
the incoming metric to a metric equal to the round product near the
face. It preserves the outer germs. Suppose on that path the original
and deformed strips of side $j$ each have signed charge at most
$C_jw_j$, the oriented frames agree off the strips, and the complete
measurable sides cover with null common seam. Then each absolute
full-side drift is at most $C_jw_j$, and the peak of the joined energy
is at most $e+C_Lw_L+C_Rw_R$. For $e<2T$ the widths can be chosen
independently small enough to preserve the strict wall and meet both
drift tolerances.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Extend the product tensor from a fixed compact slab, interpolate with
the normalized metric, and apply the smooth conformal factor that
restores the physical metric away from the face. The two cutoff
derivatives scale with the actual width on their respective halves.
Locality cancels the unchanged portion of each complete signed measure.
Both old and new strip masses lie between zero and $C_jw_j$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Positive closed classes and transplanted cap tests]
\label{prop:ed04-positive-class}
\lean{SmoothPoincare.capFactorScalar_eq_dirichletForm, SmoothPoincare.classYamabeFour_pos_of_scalar_pos, SmoothPoincare.standardRoundFour_yamabe_pos, SmoothPoincare.exists_capLogTest_of_nonnegativeFactor, SmoothPoincare.hasJoinedCapTests_of_transplantedFactors}
\leanok
A positive scalar curvature metric on a nonempty compact smooth
Riemannian four-manifold without boundary has positive ordinary
class Yamabe constant. In the cap comparison, a smooth nonnegative
transplant with the specified scalar and volume errors and positive
volume can be used through smooth logarithmic conformal tests.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The minimum of the scalar curvature is positive. Green's identity
bounds the conformal numerator from below by a positive multiple of
the sum of the squared $L^2$ norms of the factor and its gradient.
The compact critical Sobolev inequality bounds the squared $L^4$
norm from above by a fixed multiple of that same sum. For a
nonnegative tested factor use its positive constant regularizations
and continuity of the two integrals.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A round equatorial cut with two physical widths]
\label{prop:ed04-round-face-control}
\uses{prop:ed04-positive-class,prop:ed04-fixed-cap-masses}
\lean{SmoothPoincare.RoundEquatorialCaps.logFactor_jointly_smooth, SmoothPoincare.RoundEquatorialCaps.marked_product_endpoint, SmoothPoincare.RoundEquatorialCaps.cap_restrictions, SmoothPoincare.RoundEquatorialCaps.flip_fixes_equator, SmoothPoincare.RoundEquatorialCaps.roundInclusion, SmoothPoincare.RoundEquatorialCaps.fixedSource_at_physical_widths}
\leanok
Fix the round oriented unit four-sphere and its signed last-coordinate
equator. Any two geodesic latitude widths in $(0,\pi/2)$ admit a
smooth path with the incoming metric on the two outer collars and
a round product germ at the endpoint. Both entire marked hemispheres
admit smooth positive-class caps agreeing with their retained endpoint
metrics. The joined charge at every time and each signed charge on every
measurable subset are zero. The fixed capped-type infimum test holds on
both original closed hemispheres.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Set $a=\sin w_L$, $b=\sin w_R$ and write $z=\cos r$ for the signed
height. With the two-sided cutoff $\chi$, use the global log factor
$-s\log(1-\chi z^2)/2$. At the product endpoint the cylinder coordinate
is $t=-\operatorname{artanh}(\cos r)$. The symmetric choices $(a,a)$
and $(b,b)$ extend the two complete retained halves, and reflection in
the equator fixes its entire angular parametrization. Conformal
invariance preserves the positive round class and both vanishing
Weyl measures. The cap comparison therefore has zero drift at every
positive tolerance on this fixed source.
\end{proof}
