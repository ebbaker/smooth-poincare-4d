## Source and comparison measures

Work in one fixed chirality on smooth, oriented Riemannian four-manifolds. In SEC `msale:source` and `msale:source-euler`, the closed source is a homotopy sphere and $g_i$ is a volume-one positive Yamabe minimizer. Write $E_i=E_+(g_i)=E_-(g_i)$ by signature, $Y_i=s_{g_i}>0$, and $D_i=\int|\operatorname{Ric}_{0,i}|^2\,dV_i$. Then

$$
Y_i^2=48(8\pi^2-E_i)+12D_i,\qquad
\sqrt{48\delta}\le Y_i\le 8\sqrt6\pi,\qquad D_i\le4E_i .
$$

The Weyl tensor satisfies $|W|_{\rm tens}^2=4(|W^+|_{\rm HS}^2+|W^-|_{\rm HS}^2)$ pointwise. The factor eight for the **integrated** full tensor on the closed source uses signature and cannot be applied to an individual strip. The full Riemann integral on the source is $64\pi^2+4D_i$, which gives the intrinsic curvature budget in the physical gauge and under constant scaling. For variable conformal scaling the chiral energy density and $L^1$ Bach density are invariant, whereas the volume and the trace-free Ricci budget depend on the gauge. The conversion of $\int|B|/E_i\to0$ to $\int|B|\to0$ uses the upper energy bound. [SEC §§11.1 and 14][S11] · [Chang–Gursky–Yang, (0.4)][CGY]

For each fixed $L$, use a smooth compact truncation $K^j_L$ in every named component. Use one common source subsequence for all components. Each comparison is an orientation-preserving embedding of a neighborhood of the **whole** compact. Convergence is smooth on fixed compacts, after positive conformal rescaling on the base and after constant metric rescaling $\lambda_i^{-2}$ on bubbles, with $\lambda_i\to0$. $C^2$ comparison suffices for the curvature density on the fixed truncation. Passage of the Bach equation uses the stated smooth comparison, or a separately justified fourth-jet or weak convergence argument. Scalar and Ricci limits must use the constant rescaling on the bubble. Compact interiors have eventually disjoint images for each fixed $L$ and smooth boundary images are null for the volume and the chiral Weyl measure. On a component the increasing domains exhaust up to null deleted smooth points. The base and bubble measures are finite by smooth compactification, conformal invariance or the supplied energy budget. [SEC `msale:budgets`, `msgap:accounting`][ACCOUNT]

Set $\mu_i=|W^+_{g_i}|_{\rm HS}^2dV_i$, $c_{Li}=\sum_j\mu_i(\iota_{jLi}K^j_L)$ and $a_L=\sum_j\mu^j(K^j_L)$. Fixed-compact comparison gives $c_{Li}\to a_L$. Exhaustion gives $a_L\to\sum_j\mu^j(\mathrm{univ})$ for the finite family. Absolute continuity and the null boundaries supply a.e. disjointness. The exact finite source split identifies $E_i-c_{Li}$ with the mass of the actual complementary set. For a strip family, bound that mass by the **sum** of all strip bounds plus the mass outside their union. Intersecting strips with the complementary set improves the bound and is optional. The useful order is $\forall\varepsilon>0\;\exists L_0\;\forall L\ge L_0\;\exists i_0(L,\varepsilon)\;\forall i\ge i_0$. A single low-energy strip or a bound on each member of a growing family gives no summability by itself. Fixed truncation convergence followed by exhaustion supplies the one-sided inequality without the strip hypotheses. [SEC §12.4 and `msneck:no-neck-target`][ACCOUNT] · [SEC §15][S15]

## Independent check of E01

Put $p=4\pi^2$ and suppose $x_j>0$ for an index in the finite family. Its gap gives $x_j>p$, so $e-x_j<p-\delta<p$. The assumed identity makes this remainder $b+\sum_{k\in J\setminus\{j\}}x_k$. Nonnegativity bounds each summand above by this remainder, and every other piece and the base must vanish by their gaps. Then $e-x_j=0$, contradicting the strict residual. Every bubble vanishes and the identity gives $e=b$. This includes $J=\varnothing$ and additional zero pieces. The lower endpoint $p\le e$ is part of the frozen type and is unused by this implication. Dropping strict residual fails with $J$ a singleton, $b=0$, $\delta=\pi^2$, and $e=x_j=6\pi^2$. All other assumptions hold. Likewise an inequality alone allows strictly positive residual with a nonzero bubble. Geometric flatness from zero chiral bubble energy uses the scalar-flat AE identities in `msale:ae-budgets`. All energy on a smooth compactified base is consistent with a positive closed critical value. [Route 02 opening type][PLAN] · [SEC `msgap:strict`, `msgap:accounting`, §15][ACCOUNT]
