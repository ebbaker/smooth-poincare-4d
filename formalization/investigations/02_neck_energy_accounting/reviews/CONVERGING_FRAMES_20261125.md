## Pinned API and route

Mathlib's `InnerProductSpace.gramSchmidt_def''` gives the recursive projection formula, `gramSchmidt_ne_zero` makes each denominator positive for a basis, and `gramSchmidtOrthonormalBasis_apply` identifies the basis vectors with positive norm normalization of the unnormalized vectors. `gramSchmidtOrthonormalBasis_det` expresses the relative determinant as the product of diagonal pairings. The inspected Gram–Schmidt and LDL modules contain the algebraic triangular constructions. The needed convergence follows by induction in the Gram–Schmidt formula. [Pinned Gram–Schmidt source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/c55e6e786f49471c72fbddbec5415808896aec1e/Mathlib/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/GramSchmidtOrtho.lean#L52) · [Pinned LDL source](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/c55e6e786f49471c72fbddbec5415808896aec1e/Mathlib/Analysis/Matrix/LDL.lean#L49)

The pinned geometry API provides `MetricFiberData.toNormedAddCommGroupOfTopology` and `MetricFiberData.toInnerProductSpaceOfTopology`, which equip the tangent fiber with each metric's inner product while keeping its original topology. `tangentMetricData` packages the actual pairing of each `SmoothRiemannianMetric` at the chosen point. `TangentMetricData.inner_eq` identifies the packaged pairing with `g.inner x`. [Pinned metric fiber topology](https://github.com/qinz1yang/differential-geometry/blob/788efe97894474c032de6dfb1289d515f613d15a/DifferentialGeometry/Geometry/Metric/MetricFiberData/Topology.lean#L68) · [Pinned tangent metric data](https://github.com/qinz1yang/differential-geometry/blob/788efe97894474c032de6dfb1289d515f613d15a/DifferentialGeometry/Geometry/Metric/TensorInner/Fiber/MetricData.lean#L77)

Fix a prescribed positively oriented orthonormal basis $b$ for the limiting metric. Write $D_i$ for the source metric pairing and assume entrywise convergence of $D_i(b_a,b_b)$. For two vectors varying in the fixed tangent topology, expand their pairing as a finite double sum of their basis coordinates times the Gram entries. Coordinate linear maps are continuous in the finite-dimensional tangent topology. Consequently, pairing values converge on converging vectors. This permits well-founded induction on the basis index in the unnormalized Gram–Schmidt formula. At each stage the denominator is the metric square of a nonzero pivot. Positive normalization then preserves convergence. [Checked proof](../../../FirstOrderTwistor/ConvergingFrames.lean)

The Gram–Schmidt diagonal pairings of a normalized pivot with its reference vector equal the pivot's positive norm. The determinant therefore has positive sign, giving exactly the reference orientation for every metric. Orthogonality of the limiting reference basis removes every projection term and its unit lengths make the normalizing factors one. This recovers the prescribed limiting frame. ## Application boundary

The frame construction supplies the moving-frame premise pointwise on a fixed comparison tangent space. Applying it to a comparison chart requires the pulled-back metric's Gram convergence at that point. Curvature-slot convergence still requires convergence of the actual curvature tensors in a fixed tangent topological vector space. Integration over a truncation also requires the measurable and dominated coefficient data and the Jacobian hypotheses in the density theorem.
