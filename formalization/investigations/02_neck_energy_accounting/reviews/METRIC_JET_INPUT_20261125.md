## Actual metric jets to full Riemann

The direct pinned API is `DifferentialGeometry.CheegerGromovCompactness.MetricCPConvergenceOn.tendsto_metricRm04StandardAt` in `DifferentialGeometry/Geometry/Metric/Convergence/Curvature/TensorConvergence.lean:22`. Supply smooth Riemannian $G_n,g,R$ on the same smooth Hausdorff manifold with a boundaryless real finite-dimensional model, compact $K$, `MetricCPConvergenceOn K 2 G g R`, and $x\in K$. For every four **fixed** vectors in $T_xM$ it gives convergence of the actual all-lowered Levi–Civita Riemann evaluations to those of $g$. The order-two convergence in `Geometry/Metric/Convergence/Defs.lean:50` is uniform on $K$ in all orders through two, using the $R$ covariant derivatives of the metric difference and the $R$ fiber norms. The theorem changes the reference to $g$ internally. It also works pointwise by choosing $K=\{x\}$ if just those three metric jets at $x$ converge.

The same file has `MetricCPConvergenceOn.tendsto_normSq_metricRm04At` for the full four-slot squared norm in the respective metrics, `tendsto_curvDerivNorm_zero` for its square root, and `eventually_curvDerivNorm_zero_le` for a uniform bound $4(C+1)$ on $K$ given a bound $C\ge0$ for the limit curvature norm there. The latter uses `BoundarylessManifold I M`. Squared norms in the respective metrics and the norm of the **difference** are distinct quantities.
