# FOT-E15a · Integral estimate from the supplied flux identity

25 November 2026. Prepared for Edward B. Baker III with OpenAI Codex assistance. The packet specified GPT-6.2 Sol at ultra. Actual serving model and reasoning effort were unavailable.

`FirstOrderTwistor.weightedEnergy_le_of_intrinsicFlux` proves the integral inequality of SEC `msneck:neck` on a fixed measure, from the scalar form of `msneck:integrated`. It obtains integrability of both signed contractions by domination, uses integral monotonicity on the negative Bach contraction and the positive Weyl-defect contraction, and applies Hölder with exponents two and two to each of the two defect products. The resulting constants are $8$ for the Bach norm, $4$ for the Weyl product, and $2$ on the differentiated defect norm. Square roots of integrals of squares give the real $L^2$ norms. ## Supplied hypotheses

- Use the Riemannian volume restricted to the protected support $U_\varphi\subset U$ as the Lean measure. The cutoff is $\varphi(\tau)$ with $0\le\varphi\le1$ and compact support there. The transverse identity $X(\tau)=1$ gives $X(F)=\varphi(\tau)$ for $F=\Phi\circ\tau$, $\Phi'=\varphi$. The geometric integrated flux identity with $dF$ compactly supported and the tensor identifications remain inputs. [SEC conserved current and integrated flux](../../../../../papers/investigations-1/03_first_order_twistor/01_sections_and_connections/manuscript/twistor_sections_and_chiral_weyl_curvature.tex#L5874)
- The signed Bach integrand is $B(\nabla F,X)$. Its absolute value is at most $\Lambda|B|$ with $\Lambda=\sup_{U_\varphi}|X||\nabla\tau|$. The Weyl integrand is the signed contraction with the three terms in `msneck:integrated`. Supply its absolute bound $|W|(d_0+2d_1)$ where $d_0=(|\varphi'(\tau)||\nabla\tau|^2+\varphi(\tau)|\nabla^2\tau|)|k|$, $d_1=\varphi(\tau)|\nabla\tau||\nabla k|$, and $k=L_Xg-fg$. The factor $2$ accounts for the two first-derivative contractions. The geometric chain rule, tensor norm bounds and matching of supports remain inputs. [SEC nearly conical neck](../../../../../papers/investigations-1/03_first_order_twistor/01_sections_and_connections/manuscript/twistor_sections_and_chiral_weyl_curvature.tex#L5896)
- The theorem requires integrability of the weighted Weyl square and the Bach norm, a.e. strongly measurable signed contractions, and a.e. nonnegative $L^2$ functions for the Weyl norm and both defects. The Weyl norm is the full four-index norm in this section. Passing to a chiral Hilbert–Schmidt bound or choosing such a field on the actual neck uses separate geometric data.
