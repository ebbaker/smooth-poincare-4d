The public `FirstOrderTwistor` library now contains constructors for Route 02's existing `RemainderEstimates F κ` for the same `TruncationFamily F` and the same finite physical energy measures $\mu_i$. Each constructor takes its finite strip indices at the actual pair $(L,i)$. The public import and the new blueprint input are integrated in the shared package. Route 04 owns the local modules and their [register](../../../04_dirac_collar_transmission/research-progress/REGISTER.md).

Bring the sum and the uncovered mass below each tolerance in the required order. [Floor constructor](../../../../FirstOrderTwistor/SpinCoverAccounting.lean) |

It uses finite $L^2$ norms and permits bounds measured on a larger region in the same gauge. [Normalized constructor](../../../../FirstOrderTwistor/SpinRatioAccounting.lean) · [Energy identity](../../../../FirstOrderTwistor/SpinResidualEstimate.lean) |

Each weighted physical integral has a measured upper bound. | Sum the weighted local costs and charge the complementary energy once. Under the summed Bochner majorant, `energyIdentityOfSpinBochnerBudgets` derives energy equality from bounded total curvature-square cost, vanishing Ricci-square, relative fourth-power and cutoff costs, and small uncovered mass. For the first two rows the physical and compared energy measures agree on $R_{L,i}\cap U_j$. On a four-dimensional conformal strip the existing [restricted conformal measure identity](../../../../FirstOrderTwistor/ConformalEnergy.lean) yields that comparison when matched chiral coefficients transform by $e^{-2u}$. Each spinor floor, numerator and denominator uses the local metric. The existing physical truncated-component comparisons, total limit and separate strict bubble gap/residual inputs retain their scope.

Do not replace summed smallness on a growing family by smallness on each strip. A checked example has $n+1$ strip costs of $1/(n+1)$; another has one strip with a vanishing raw defect and a compensating shrinking floor.
