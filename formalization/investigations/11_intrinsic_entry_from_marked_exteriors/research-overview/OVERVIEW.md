Fix a closed connected oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere $M$ with no initial positive-class witness assumed. For any smooth Riemannian metric $g$, use $E_\pm(g;U)=\int_U |W_g^\pm|_{\rm HS}^2\,dV_g$, the Hilbert–Schmidt norm of the endomorphism on real $\Lambda^\pm$. Put $T=4\pi^2$, $L_g=\max(\lambda_{\max}W_g^+,\lambda_{\max}W_g^-)$, and $P([g])=4E_+(g;M)+3\int_M L_g^2\,dV_g$. Zero signature gives equality of the two closed energies. Orientation reversal swaps the blocks on each piece and preserves $L_g$. The first entry target is one actual conformal class with $Y([g])>0$ and $E_+(g;M)<2T$. A finite positive class with $E_+\le T$ feeds direct recognition. The infimum $I^+(M)$ over positive classes is $+\infty$ for an empty class set. The parent owns the recognition results and the non-effective inclusive infimum gap on paper, including the case of unattained equality. ## Round-ended retained exterior

Take an actual compact complement $P_0\subset M$ of finitely many disjoint parametrized standard four-balls. Let $h$ be smooth on $P_0$ with the exact unit round product $dt^2+\gamma_{S^3}$ in those markings. Cap by conformally flat ends using the marked radial coordinates so that each closed chirality equals its complete raw exterior charge. For $Q_h(v)=\int_{P_0}(6|dv|^2+R_hv^2)dV_h$ and traces $f_j$, the scalar sign on the actual closed extension is equivalent to positivity on every nonzero $v\in H^1(P_0)$ of
\[
 Q_h(v)+6\sum_j\langle f_j,\sqrt{1-\Delta_{\gamma_{S^3}}}\,f_j\rangle .
\]
Use $-\Delta\ge0$, the $H^{1/2}$–$H^{-1/2}$ pairing, and the outward normal of the retained exterior. The sufficient scalar Robin test is positive first eigenvalue for $-6\Delta_h+R_h$ with $\partial_\nu v+v=0$ on the marked components. A single round mode of degree $l$ has cylinder energy $6(l+1)|f_l|^2$. Extending this to the full sign equivalence requires trace spaces, zero-trace remainders, localization near the capped points, conformal covariance and compact closed spectral theory. The paper also states the separate modified potential $R_h-6L_h$ criterion. [Master Proposition `prop:round-boundary-form`](../../../../papers/master/manuscript/marked_cut.tex)

## A specified nonround seam

For a compact oriented smooth contractible half $W$ with connected boundary $Y$, fix the marking of the actual gluing and a smooth metric $\gamma$ on $Y$. A permitted parameter is an orientation-preserving $\gamma$-isometry $f:Y\to Y$ in the orientation inherited from $W$. The two halves in $M_f=W\cup_f(-W)$ have opposite induced seam orientations. Contractibility of the halves and the boundary homology sphere give a simply connected integral homology sphere by van Kampen and Mayer–Vietoris, hence a homotopy sphere by Hurewicz and Whitehead. When the original carrier is supplied separately, use its actual comparison to this marked assembly.

The prescribed-class test starts from a smooth PSC extension $g$ of the exact boundary representative $\gamma$. Let $H_g$ be the trace of the second fundamental form for the outward normal, and $\mathcal N_g f=\partial_\nu u_f$ for the $(-6\Delta_g+R_g)$-harmonic extension of $f$. It requires
\[
 6\langle f,\mathcal N_g f\rangle+2\int_Y H_g f^2dA_\gamma>0
 \quad\text{for every nonzero }f\in H^{1/2}(Y).
\]
The Robin boundary condition is $\partial_\nu u+(H_g/3)u=0$. The paper's existence equivalence produces a PSC totally geodesic filling $h$ inducing the marked conformal class $[\gamma]$. The energy to test belongs to the resulting $h$. The boundary deformation and the relative sign test alone supply no estimate for that energy. Write $B_h=E_+(h;W)+E_-(h;W)$ and $G_h=\int_W L_h^2dV_h$. Conformally normalize the boundary metric to the exact $\gamma$ with zero normal derivative, straighten the seam on a shrinking actual collar, and keep the free-trace scalar form coercive. Round punctures, if used, are flattened and reclosed in their original marks. For each fixed allowed $f$, the product seams and any short strip must give oriented diffeomorphisms onto the same $M_f$. Then the paper's positive classes satisfy $E_\pm\to B_h$ and $P\to4B_h+6G_h$. A strict $B_h<2T$ gives one finite entry witness for each such $f$. Inclusive $B_h\le T+\varepsilon_*$ or $4B_h+6G_h\le8T+\eta_{\rm gap}$ uses the parent's infimum theorem. Equality in the approximation limits places no requirement that the limiting numerical value be attained by a smooth class. Closed classes are positive at each tested width. ## Control and outstanding existence

For the actual $W^-(0,3)$ half of the Mazur double, fix $Y=\Sigma(2,5,7)$ and its specified Milnor framing. In that frame $\gamma(v)=(e^1)^2+(e^2)^2+v^2(e^3)^2$, with $v_*^2=\sqrt{159}/11-1$. In the paper's fixed spin and orientation conventions the signature and spin eta values vanish at $\gamma(v_*)$, while the transverse ordinary scalar potential is negative. These boundary evaluations remove specific obstructions. The known exact-metric PSC extension and the known free-class totally geodesic fill give different existence results. The first still needs a strictly positive Robin form for some extension of the exact $\gamma(v_*)$. The second has no certificate that it realizes $[\gamma(v_*)]$. A qualifying half must also meet the full two-chirality budget.
