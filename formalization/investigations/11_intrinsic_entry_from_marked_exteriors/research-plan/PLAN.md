## First bounded proof

Following B01, fix $l\in\mathbb N$, $k=l+1$, and a real cylinder coefficient $w$ whose endpoint trace is $f$, with a specified integrable energy and limit at infinity. Prove the energy identity by expanding $|w'+kw|^2$ and applying the boundary evaluation of $ww'$. Check that $w(t)=f e^{-kt}$ attains the modal minimum and that the coefficient is $6k$. Freeze the exact segment of the argument using the available integration API before assigning it. The spectral theorem and closed extension equivalence are later packets. Compare ${l=0}$ and ${l=1}$ under the same normalization.

For every issued E packet, use the pinned Lake targets and repeat `#check` and `#print axioms` from an importing file. Verify the applied type on a marked example with all supplied hypotheses visible. Compare against the source and the umbrella blueprint before promotion. A checked conditional statement retains its hypotheses on the return record. A different smooth carrier, an unquantified remainder or an unavailable Sobolev or geometric premise returns control to the relevant B decision.
