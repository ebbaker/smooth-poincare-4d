## Physical distance and curvature

`SmoothPoincare.physicalMetricSpace` obtains the metric space from the pinned `SmoothRiemannianMetric.toPseudoMetricSpace` on the connected compact source. Its real distance is `(riemannianEDistOf g x y).toReal` and its topology is definitionally the original topology. `geometricChiralMeasure` weights the volume of `g` by the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of the positive chiral block in pointwise oriented orthonormal frames. `metricFullCurvatureMeasure` uses that same volume and the sum over all four ordered slots of the squared actual curvature tensor components. With the specified integrability, the chiral total is `chiralFrameEnergy` and the full mass on each measurable region is its curvature integral. ## Selection and its premises

- `exists_isMetricChiralQuantile` uses nullity of the actual metric's distance spheres for its physical volume. Absolute continuity transfers nullity to the chiral measure, and compactness and continuity give the least attaining radius for each fraction strictly between zero and one when total chiral energy is positive. The distance-sphere nullity theorem is owned by SCALE-E01.
- `exists_lowMass_metricQuantileAnnulus` uses an inner and median quantile witness, a full-energy budget and room for a positive number of annuli at the specified ratio. It gives the actual open annulus, the attaining inner ball and the entire complement of the open median ball at the same center. Both full chiral sets are disjoint from the annulus, and the annulus has full mass at most the budget divided by the number of annuli.
- `metricChiralQuantileAlternatives` instantiates the sequence result with each index's own physical metric space, both measures of that metric, positive total chiral energy, a uniform full curvature integral budget and a physical quantile witness at each index and fraction. The separated branch chooses one subsequence and attaining centers, then fits low full-energy annuli at each fixed logarithmic width and multiplicative guard. The other branch gives the diffuse estimate in this same physical distance. The selected shell is measurable. A marked smooth shell, separation by an embedded sphere and coefficient control remain geometric inputs.
