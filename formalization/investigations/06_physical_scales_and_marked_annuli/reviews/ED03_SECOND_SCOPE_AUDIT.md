## One chart on the physical source

The low-mass guarded quantile shell fixes a physical distance set and two positive chiral charges about the same center. The anchored placement theorem requires an actual embedded chart with the lower metric comparison on its larger closed band and a strict full-curvature bound on that band. A small full integral on a physical distance shell applies to this band if its image is contained there, by measure monotonicity. The existence of a small distance shell and a bounded-distance anchor alone does not establish that inclusion or that curvature bound. Upper chart travel and both physical volume comparisons also enter the finite placement theorem. For a simply connected source the radial halves and connected component labels are proved for the supplied chart. No initial assignment of a charged set to a side is needed. The conclusion concerns the entire median exterior, including its disconnected parts. ## Inclusive threshold and strict cap signs

The current finite criterion allows the two actual energies to sum to \(2T\). At \(E_1=E_2=T\), both inclusive recognition tests apply if both caps satisfy the geometric premises. Both strict energy tests fail at that boundary. `eitherCappedEnergy_le_threshold` and `geometricDiscardedMass_le_excess_inclusive` use the inclusive endpoint, taking separate oriented recognition hypotheses at \(E_s\le T\). The latter can start from the actual cap-energy total or the sufficient source-energy-plus-surcharges total. In either case the individual cost bound for the retained cap is required to control the discarded mass. `MarkedSideReplacement.recognizeOriginalOfBothCaps_le_threshold` composes the actual reassembly map with the other cap's recognition, retaining the full raw-side image data.

Each `GeometricMarkedCutSide` still requires strictly positive class Yamabe constant. The inclusive energy bound supplies no sign test at the wall. In the signed path theorem, the connected sign detector is known positive initially and cannot vanish while the entire path has one strict subcritical margin. Allowing energy to touch the zero wall at a putative sign crossing removes that argument. The manuscript's stretching and compactification transfer the strict uniform margin to both cap signs. The inclusive two-side comparison takes those signs, actual capped metrics, both positive recognition implications, and the marked collar realization as inputs. ## Common chart and density

`chartSpliceRiemannLower_sub` computes the all-lowered curvature commutator of an actual interpolated smooth metric using genuine first and second Fréchet derivatives in one chart. Its second derivative part is the cutoff product-rule error with constant tensor coefficients. The difference of the first-jet curvature terms remains explicit. The manuscript's uniform \(L^2\) estimate still needs that lower-order difference controlled using common ellipticity, inverse coefficients, \(L^4\) first derivatives and fixed cutoff bounds. An identity in separate gauges does not give an error bound for the same tensor difference. [Jet module](../../../SmoothPoincare/Variational/ChartSpliceJets.lean) · [First-order splice](../../../../papers/master/manuscript/first_order_splice.tex)

`tendstoUniformly_geometricSpliceEnergy` transports the actual moving metric's squared positive Weyl density to one fixed reference volume. It takes the \(L^2\) curvature residual, the uniform moving projector bound, the norm of their difference, and the weighted equality to the actual oriented orthonormal-frame Weyl block. The paper obtains these ingredients from the common oriented chart, the positive inverse square roots of its metric matrices, and their determinant weights. Separate oriented orthonormal frames can preserve each Hilbert–Schmidt norm while their pointwise rotations spoil a numerical block difference or a uniform operator comparison. For example, conjugation by the oriented chiral basis change $\operatorname{diag}(-1,-1,1)$ negates a nonzero $(1,3)$ entry without changing the norm. The geometric density proof therefore needs compatibility on the same tensor representation and parameter. It also needs a separate application on each splice region to derive both individual cap upper costs. It does not construct the chart, the positive path or either closed cap.
