## What can be used today

The current Lean library supplies an admissible energy on a specified smooth type, oriented pullback of a metric and its chosen curvature frame, and an infimum comparison given an actual positive competitor. It also supplies conditional one-core descent, smooth-component accounting and signed spinor estimates. The principal new tasks are to construct the two-sided and local variational arguments from these ingredients, and to identify the precise geometric inputs a minimizing sequence must supply.
