Fix one smooth oriented Riemannian homotopy four-sphere and a given smooth two-sided totally umbilic cut whose boundary is conformally round. Use a conformal ball cap and the boundary identification with opposite outward normals. Equality of the tangential metrics and opposite second fundamental forms give first-jet equality in a common signed normal coordinate. The signature-zero identities of the smooth capped homotopy spheres are geometry inputs. Each energy is the integral of the Hilbert–Schmidt square of the corresponding real Weyl block against that metric's volume. [Signed cap implementation](../../../SmoothPoincare/Variational/MarkedCapSmoothing.lean)

With fixed smooth extensions from both sides, let \(d\) be the difference of their metric coefficients and let \(c(t)\) be a cutoff changing on \(|t|\leq\delta\). `firstJet_normalCutoff_bounds` uses two mean-value estimates to show that equal first jets and bounded second derivatives give \(|d|\leq B\delta^2\) and \(|d'|\leq B\delta\). With \(|c|\leq1\), \(|c'|\leq A/\delta\), and \(|c''|\leq A/\delta^2\), `firstJet_cutoff_errors` bounds the zeroth error by \(B\delta^2\), the first by \((A+1)B\delta\), and the second by \((3A+1)B\). In coordinates tangential to the seam the normal cutoff has no tangential derivatives. Differentiating the smooth extensions' seam identities tangentially gives the bounds for the mixed terms. A finite atlas and a common positive metric lower bound give the local-to-global comparison on the fixed smooth carrier. The uniformly bounded second derivative error is supported in a strip of vanishing volume, so the piecewise coefficient comparison gives strong \(W^{2,2}\) convergence along with the uniform first-order limit. This geometric construction and its assembly in a finite marked atlas supply the coordinate estimates to Lean.

`markedCap_energyLimit_of_firstJet` uses the actual signed densities and measures of the source and each smooth cap. The complement of the strip has the original interior charge by the marking and the zero Weyl charge of the conformally flat cap interior. The theorem integrates a common density bound on the two shrinking strips, obtaining one signed limit on each complete side for each chirality. It also passes the capped signature identity to each complete raw side. The finite marked atlas, exact agreement on the unmodified regions, and physical strip-volume and density estimates remain inputs of `MarkedCapSmoothing`.

`MarkedCapSmoothing.eventually_positive` transfers strict coercivity by a bound on the error of the actual smooth scalar numerator in one reference squared \(H^1\) norm. Scalar curvature convergence in \(L^2\) pairs with the squared \(L^4\) norm of the conformal factor. Uniform control of the gradient and volume coefficients controls the remaining form difference. With the supplied uniform Sobolev bound on the smooth test denominator, Lean proves a positive floor for the conformal quotient and hence for the full Yamabe infimum.
