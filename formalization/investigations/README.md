## Overall research program

Version 02 follows the master manuscript from entry into the positive Yamabe band to a comparison on the original smooth type. Route 05 studies minimizing sequences and physical scales. Route 03 studies a single marked cut with two full sides. The earlier full-energy and Dirac routes contribute when their geometric hypotheses are available.
