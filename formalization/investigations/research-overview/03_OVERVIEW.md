Fix a closed connected oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere $M$. Write $E_+(g)=\int_M|W^+_g|_{\rm HS}^2\,dV_g$ for the energy of its self-dual Weyl endomorphism. The updated master plan asks for one successful argument on **each** candidate smooth type. Different candidates can be covered by different arguments. Its three implications have distinct entry hypotheses. [Master research overview](../../../papers/master/research-overview/OVERVIEW.md) · [Master plan and coverage test](../../../papers/master/research-plan/PLAN.md)

| Implication | Witness that would establish it |
| --- | --- |
| Direct recognition | One positive-Yamabe class with $E_+\le4\pi^2$, or one smooth Euclidean hypersurface immersion whose induced conformal class has $E_+\le64\pi^2/9$. The immersed energy test applies independently of Yamabe sign. |
| Recognition from an immersed height | One immersion with $E_+<8\pi^2$ selects directions with two middle critical points on a hypothetical nonstandard type. One must also obtain and recognize the actual framed diagram of a selected height. |
| Intrinsic descent | One positive class with $E_+<8\pi^2$ gives entry to fixed-type minimization. It suffices to find one admissible minimizing sequence with a marked cut or replacement producing a contradiction, or another independently established recognition result on that type. |

The first two tests concern the induced conformal class of one immersion. Its quantitative construction remains open. A positive immersed class can also provide intrinsic entry, after which the minimizing classes may be arbitrary positive classes on $M$. The positive-class infimum $I^+(M)$ uses $+\infty$ for the empty set. The Lean comparison with a real infimum carries an actual positive-class witness.
