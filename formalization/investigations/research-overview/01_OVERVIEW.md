## The result this plan should deliver

For a given smooth homotopy four-sphere, what would a controlled collar let us conclude? Version 01 aims to give a Lean proof for each established step leading to a collar and each established consequence of its stated geometry. Its principal target is a conditional energy-accounting theorem on a fixed smooth manifold. Two further targets concern a single spherical cut and spinor transmission. The sources below use FOT for the parent manuscript, SEC for Sections and Connections, DIR for Dirac and Twistor Spinors, and CME for Chiral Metric Evolution. ## Recognition on the original manifold

Fix a smooth, closed, connected, oriented $M$ homotopy equivalent to $S^4$, with a positive-definite Riemannian metric $g$. Thus $\chi=2$, $\tau=0$, and the spin structure is unique. With the given orientation, $\Lambda^+$ is the self-dual bundle, $W^+=0$ is the ASD condition, and real self-dual forms act on $S^+$. The chiral energies use operator Hilbert–Schmidt norms. On any domain $U$,

$$
\int_U |W|_{\mathrm{tens}}^2\,dV=4\big(E_+(U)+E_-(U)\big).
$$

On the **closed** $M$, signature gives $E_+=E_-$, hence the total integral is $8E_+$. The local factor four and the global factor eight have different uses in neck accounting. [SEC §1.1, `eq:energies`, `eq:sign`][SEC] · [DIR §§2.1–2.2][DIR]

There are two recognition theorems to formalize. If $W^+=0$, signature and smoothness give $W=0$. The developing map of the locally conformally flat structure is a covering of $S^4$ with one sheet. This identifies the *conformal class*. If $Y([g])>0$ and $E_+(g)<4\pi^2$, the full tensor energy is strictly below $16\pi^2\chi(M)$. Chang–Gursky–Yang then identify the *smooth type*, with simple connectivity eliminating $\mathbb{RP}^4$. The latter conclusion applies to the actual closed metric tested. [SEC `prop:recognition`][SEC] · [Chang–Gursky–Yang, Theorem A and Remarks 1–2](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1007/s10240-003-0017-z.pdf#page=3)

The parent manuscript supplies three ways to reach exact recognition. Three integrable twistor directions, distinct modulo sign, can be chosen locally for **one** conformal metric precisely in the ASD case. The projective Frobenius equation uses local spin lifts. A nontrivial smooth positive twistor spinor implies $W^+=0$ across its zeros. Independently, a positive triple with $\Sigma_i\wedge\Sigma_j=2\delta_{ij}dV$, transforming in its oriented frame bundle, determines the metric. Compatibility $d_A\Sigma=0$ identifies $A=\Gamma^+$. The trace-free part of $C_{ij}dV=-F_A^i\wedge\Sigma_j/2$ is then $W^+$. In particular the global construction never requires a global almost complex structure or a nowhere-zero positive spinor. [FOT §§2–5][FOT] · [CME §§4–5][CME]

## The energy-accounting theorem

Start with a **supplied sequence** of smooth volume-one positive Yamabe minimizers $g_i$ on $M$, a fixed $\delta>0$, $4\pi^2\le E_i=E_+(g_i)\le 8\pi^2-\delta$, and $\int_M|B_{g_i}|\,dV_{g_i}\to0$. Pass to a common subsequence with $E_i\to e$. Gauss–Bonnet and the round Yamabe upper bound give $Y([g_i])\ge\sqrt{48\delta}$ and a total $L^2$ curvature budget. A round mark in one representative can have vanishing volume in Yamabe gauge. The existence of such a sequence on an arbitrary $M$ is a separate global obligation. [SEC §§8–13, `msale:source-euler`, `msgauge:reverse`][SEC]

| Stage | Supplied data | Target consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Components | A smooth compactifiable base and smooth ALE (asymptotically locally Euclidean) limits with the oriented comparison embeddings of SEC §§11–12, differentiated end decay, and full embedded cores where topology uses them | For disjoint truncated images, $e_0+\sum_j e_j\le e$. Each nonflat component of the stated class costs $e_X>4\pi^2$ and leaves $0<e-e_X<4\pi^2-\delta$. |
| One interface | Separated scales $r_i/R_i\to0$ and control of the enlarged middle annulus, in a common chart or by the intrinsic transverse-field estimate | The energy of $Lr_i<\rho<R_i/L$ tends to zero with $\lim_{L\to\infty}\limsup_{i\to\infty}$. The constants must survive increasing logarithmic length. |
| Whole complement | All truncated component images and a cover of their complement, including transition regions, with vanishing **total** $E_+$ error in that order of limits | $e=e_0+\sum_j e_j$ in one chirality. If $X$ were nonflat, the closed Bach-flat gap forces the base and all other components to have zero energy. Equality would force $e=e_X$, contradicting the strict residual. |

For a finite smooth comparison family this gives a precise endpoint. All energy lies on the closed base and its class realizes the limiting critical value. Below the first positive subcritical closed critical value, when that value exists, this excludes the sequence. A positive closed critical class can otherwise persist. Countably many comparison pieces require uniform control of the remaining tail for equality. Quotient links or singular intermediate bases require their own comparison theorems. [SEC §§11–15, `msgap:accounting`, `msgap:minimum`, `msneck:no-neck-target`][SEC] · [Energy-accounting note][account]

Two calibrations explain the different regularity demands. On the exact unit round product $dt^2+g_{S^3}$, the chiral curvature forms are tangential and $F_i\wedge F_j=0$. Its ordinary chiral connection is smooth, while curvature-only *definite* reconstruction would have to divide by a vanishing volume. The model cap uses rescaled $C^2$ data for the Weyl cost and Yamabe sign, with $C^4$ data for a Bach cost. The model Dirac perturbation asks for small $L^\infty$ metric error and uniformly small slab $L^4$ first derivatives. Its collar may have Weyl curvature. The exact Green pole instead requires a flat germ **at the puncture** and an invertible compact Dirac operator. The brainstorms specify the inputs for the cap and transmission estimates. The subsequent derivations below supply conditional statements for Lean to verify. [Supplied-collar calculations][cut] · [Chiral, Dirac and surgery comparison][comp] · [DIR §§4.1, 10.1][DIR]

The joint collar work has supplied narrower conditional targets since those brainstorms. On a supplied nonflat smooth ALE limit with full-core convergence and differentiated ends, the cut can be taken in the smooth compactification chart. The two-cap estimate then gives a positive-Yamabe Bach-flat competitor on the **original** $M$ with $I^+(M)\le e_X\le e-\kappa_\delta$, where $I^+(M)$ is the infimum of $E_+$ over positive Yamabe classes on $M$. This would exclude that bubble for a minimizing sequence without first accounting for the whole neck. The spinor work identifies the actual Cauchy graphs for cores with exact product germs and positive capped Yamabe bounds. A second argument solves prescribed jumps on the original closed source using the Yamabe bound and measured seams. Its critical-norm estimate bounds how large a joint spinor floor the corrections can sustain. Curvature control still requires independently measured differentiated errors. ## What counts as isolating the neck

The final review must expand the hypotheses of each composed Lean theorem. Every non-collar implication on its chosen route must resolve to a checked declaration on the geometric objects in the manuscript. A cited theorem still awaiting a Lean proof, a missing spin or regularity bridge, or a positive closed critical value receives an explicit entry with its effect on the conclusion. A route is conditional on precisely the input data listed in its statement. For SPC4 itself one would additionally need entry from every smooth type and an argument reaching recognition on that same type, for instance a surviving mark in a compact gauge or a justified continuation or descent to the strict threshold.
