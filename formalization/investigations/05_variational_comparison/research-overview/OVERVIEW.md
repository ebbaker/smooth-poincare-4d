## From one repair to compactly supported variation

Smooth oriented embeddings supply $\Phi_i^*g_i=u_i^2m_i$ near a fixed compact. Work in the comparison $m_i\to h$ locally uniformly and strongly in $W^{1,4}$ with $\mathrm{Rm}_{m_i}$ locally bounded in $L^2$ and $h$ smooth. For a fixed cutoff $\chi$, transplant $h+(1-s\chi)(m_i-h)$ using the original conformal factor and the same smooth marking. The highest derivatives cancel in the difference of all-lowered curvature tensors. Weak convergence controls the cross term against the fixed limiting chiral density. The square of the defect loses the weight $2s\chi-s^2\chi^2$. The original-type path stays below $8\pi^2$, so its endpoint gives an admissible competitor. At $s=1$ this forces strong $L^2$ convergence on every compact where $\chi=1$, for both chiralities. A second, fixed variation on the installed smooth metric gives stationarity.
