## Scaled chart and boundary geometry

On the unit annulus use \(d_r(y)=ry\) and \(h_r=r^{-2}d_r^*h\). The components of \(h_r\) are \(h_{ab}(ry)\), and each derivative in \(y\) introduces \(r\), so on \(3/4\le |y|\le 5/4\) one has \(\|h_r-\delta\|_{C^2}\le Cr^{-\tau}\).
