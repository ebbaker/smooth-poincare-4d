## First bounded proof on activation

For the first packet use $M$ as both carriers, a `SignedCollarMetric o` called `q`, a whole diffeomorphism `Φ : M ≃ₘ⟮I, I⟯ M`, and `hΦ : Φ.preservesOrientation o o`. Let `pull := q.pullback Φ hΦ` and `I₀ := sInf (geometricPositiveEnergies o)`. For every such datum assume `0 < classYamabeFour o.dimension_eq q.metric` and `q.energy .plus ≤ I₀`. Prove positivity on `pull.metric` and `∀ sign, pull.energy sign = I₀` by the actual Yamabe and signed-curvature pullbacks, admission of the pulled-back metric and `csInf_le`. If supplied a Bach-flat predicate, its truth at `q.metric` and its transport across this exact pullback, append its truth at `pull.metric`. The proof owner must keep geometric Bach flatness explicit when this leaf is applied. This packet uses an already marked candidate and leaves existence, cap construction and compactness to their own packets.
