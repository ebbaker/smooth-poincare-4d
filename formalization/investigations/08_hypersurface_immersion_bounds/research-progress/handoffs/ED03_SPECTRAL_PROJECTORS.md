For a smooth real $4\times4$ matrix field on an open set with $A^2=a^2\operatorname{Id}$ and $a>0$, `shapeSpectralMagnitude_eq` and `shapeSpectralMagnitude_contDiffOn` identify $a$ with the smooth function $\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(A^2)/4}$. `balancedShape_square_and_trace` proves the square equation and trace zero from the pointwise orthogonally diagonalized spectrum $(-a,-a,a,a)$.

The smooth maps $P_\sigma=(\operatorname{Id}+(\sigma/a)A)/2$ satisfy $P_\sigma^2=P_\sigma$, $P_\sigma+P_{-\sigma}=\operatorname{Id}$, $P_\sigma P_{-\sigma}=P_{-\sigma}P_\sigma=0$, and $AP_\sigma=P_\sigma A=(\sigma a)P_\sigma$. For symmetric $A$ the projectors are symmetric. A vector is fixed by $P_\sigma$ exactly when it satisfies the corresponding eigenvalue equation. With trace zero the dimension of its range is two. The rank proof uses the trace of the idempotent linear endomorphism on its range and kernel. [Lean proof](../../../../SmoothPoincare/Immersions/SpectralProjectors.lean)

`shapeSpectralProjector_fderiv` differentiates the actual matrix product $AP_\sigma=(\sigma a)P_\sigma$ on the open set and projects the result at the base point. It proves $P_\sigma DA(v)P_\sigma=\sigma Da(v)P_\sigma$. `shapeSpectralProjector_covariantDerivative` proves the same formula with $DA(v)+[\Gamma(v),A]$ for any connection matrix, since the commutator term vanishes in that block. `shapeCovariantDerivative_sameSign_entry` gives the corresponding components in a frame diagonalizing $A$ at the base point. The importing check applies these components with row $k$, column $j$ to `saturatedCodazziJets_zero`. That test derives the projected premise from the local matrix data and retains Codazzi and self-adjointness as hypotheses. An application to an actual immersion uses a chart realization of its shape field and connection.
