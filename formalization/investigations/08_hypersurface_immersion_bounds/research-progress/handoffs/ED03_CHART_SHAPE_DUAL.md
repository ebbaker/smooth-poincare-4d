`exists_chartShapeDual` takes a smooth Riemannian metric $g$, the specified chart center $p$, a subset $U$ of that chart target, and a $C^2$ continuous bilinear form $b$. It constructs a $C^2$ endomorphism field $S$ on $U$ and proves $b_z(u,v)=K_z(S_z u,v)$ there for the actual pinned chart metric $K_z=\operatorname{chartMetricBilin}(g,p,z)$. The coordinate Gram coefficients give smoothness of $K$. Positivity of $g$ and invertibility of the tangent trivialization give positivity of $K$ on the chart target. The metric map to the continuous dual is injective, and finite-dimensional duality makes it surjective. The proof then uses smooth inversion of invertible bounded maps and composes the resulting inverse with $b$. [Lean proof](../../../../SmoothPoincare/Immersions/ChartShapeDual.lean)

`chartNormalHessianBilin` packages the normal contraction of the written chart Hessian as a continuous bilinear map. A $C^4$ map and a $C^2$ normal yield $C^2$ coefficients on an open set. When the normal is perpendicular to the chart differential, the connection correction has zero normal component, so this bilinear field agrees pointwise with `chartNormalForm`. `exists_immersionChartShapeDual` applies these results to the same `IsometricHypersurface`, written chart, chart Levi–Civita connection, and supplied perpendicular normal field. Its equality can be restricted to a neighborhood germ for the chart Codazzi proof. [Shape derivative](../../../../SmoothPoincare/Immersions/ShapeDerivative.lean)
