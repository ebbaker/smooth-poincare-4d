The argument uses those basis vectors as constants in the chart. Differentiating $S_y(S_y b_j)=a_y^2b_j$, pairing with $b_k$ and using self-adjointness divides out $2\sigma_j a$ for an equal-sign pair. The connection terms $\Gamma_X S-S\Gamma_X$ have zero pairing for the same reason.
