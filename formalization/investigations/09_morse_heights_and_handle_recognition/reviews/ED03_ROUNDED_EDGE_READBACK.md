## Attachment to the actual incoming body

For the actual isolated index-one point $p$, let $B_-=\{f\le f(p)-\varepsilon\}$, $B_+=\{f\le f(p)+\varepsilon\}$, and $L_-=\{f=f(p)-\varepsilon\}$. Each of the two full disk feet $D^3\to L_-\hookrightarrow B_-$ lies in the connected component of its center. Let $c_-,c_+\in\pi_0(B_-)$ be those two component labels and let $j:B_-\to B_+$ be the lower inclusion of the adjunction followed by the native trace map. Since the handle is connected and its feet meet $B_-$, the map $j_*$ is surjective and its fibers are given by

\[
 j_*(x)=j_*(y)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 x=y\quad\lor\quad x,y\in\{c_-,c_+\}.
\]

The merged piece is connected because the handle meets the image of each incident component. Every nonincident component stays separate. Indeed, any such component is open and closed in the compact manifold $B_-$. Its image in the adjunction is open and closed because its entire preimage under the quotient map is its copy on the lower summand. The same saturation argument applies to the union of the two incident components with the handle. Regular transport identifies the two local sublevels with the corresponding filtration cuts. The deep-lower identities of the native trace specify where $j$ agrees with literal inclusion of points. ## Local rim and signed germs

In normal coordinates write $f=c+(-x^2+\|y\|^2)/2$. The positive-scale substitution $x=\sqrt{2\varepsilon+r^2\|z\|^2}\,a$, $y=rz$ sends $a=\pm1$ to precisely the two native feet, including their unit rims. Its value throughout the product block is

\[
 f=c-\varepsilon a^2+\tfrac12r^2(1-a^2)\|z\|^2.
\]

Thus the block side \(\|z\|=1\) contains the rims at $c-\varepsilon$ and does not in general represent the actual outgoing level $c+\varepsilon$. A chart of that level and its nearby signed levels instead uses $y=\sqrt{x^2+2(\varepsilon+t)}\,\omega$ for \(\omega\in S^2\), on the part inside the native Morse chart and with positive radicand. This recovers $t=f-(c+\varepsilon)$ there. These two model calculations leave the comparison to the same rounded trace and the complete outgoing marking as geometric requirements.
