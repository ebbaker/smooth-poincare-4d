## Foot charts

Write $s=b(0)\in\{-1,1\}$ for an original attaching label $b:S^0$. The extended graph on the actual incoming level is
$$
z\longmapsto\chi\bigl(s\sqrt{2\varepsilon+r^2\|z\|^2},\,rz\bigr).
$$
Its open source is the preimage of the doubled Morse ball under the graph map. Its target lies in the corresponding open sign sector in the native level. The graph norm squared is $2\varepsilon+2r^2\|z\|^2$, so the strict bound at $\|z\|=2$ places the entire closed radius-two ball inside the smooth source. The inverse divides the positive coordinate of $\chi^{-1}$ by $r$. On the inverse domain the level equation determines the magnitude of the negative coordinate and the sector inequality selects its sign. `footPartialDiffeomorph` records both smooth directions, disjoint targets for the two labels, agreement with the full native `cocoreAttachingEmbedding` on the closed disk and the same ambient point after `morseAttachingEmbedding` includes it in the incoming sublevel. ## Relative signed collar

For a supported smooth level isotopy $J_s$, the signed product lift and its inverse are
$$
(x,t)\longmapsto(J_{\rho(t)}x,t),\qquad
(y,t)\longmapsto(J_{\rho(t)}^{-1}y,t).
$$
The smooth even cutoff $\rho$ is one on $|t|\le a$ and zero on $|t|\ge b$ for $0<a<b$. Both maps fix every signed parameter over the protected set. In supplied source and target collar charts $\chi,\psi$ on the same strip the conjugate is $\psi\circ P\circ\chi^{-1}$, with inverse $\chi\circ P^{-1}\circ\psi^{-1}$. On the whole chart range its pullback normal is exactly the original signed coordinate. For a protected parametrized tube $\tau$ in the common strip the forward map sends $\chi\circ\tau$ to $\psi\circ\tau$ pointwise and the inverse recovers $\chi\circ\tau$. With the same ambient chart this fixes the entire tube as a subset with its given parametrization. On the central plateau a foot mark obeys the same two equations with $\tau(z,t)=(u(z),t)$ on the actual side and $(u'(z),t)$ on the normalized side whenever $J_1u=u'$. [Signed product and ambient-chart adapters](../../../SmoothPoincare/Heights/RelativeFootCollar.lean)

On the compact source, `exists_nativeSignedHeightCollar` constructs such a coordinate at a nonempty regular cut $f=c$. The image of the closed critical set is compact and avoids $c$, giving a positive-width interval of regular values. A smooth normalized gradient-like flow, with no critical charts in the strip, supplies the forward map and the projection back to $f=c$. The chart maps exactly onto the open height band, is the ambient inclusion at $t=0$, and satisfies $f(\Phi(x,t))-c=t$ and $(\Phi^{-1}y)_2=f(y)-c$. At the common upper level $h=b$ the same chart therefore uses $\smash{h-b}$ on the forward height and its negative $\smash{-h+b}$ on the reversed height. The unsigned level comparison is the identity on the ambient points. Choosing this chart and the opposite sign on reversal calibrates the two sides on the actual band. At the outer parts of a strip where the isotopy is zero, its ambient comparison agrees with $\psi\circ\chi^{-1}$ in both directions. Extending across those parts requires agreement of the exterior maps with that transition.
