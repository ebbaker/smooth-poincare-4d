## Relative signed collar

For a supported smooth level isotopy $J_s$, the signed product lift and its inverse are
$$
(x,t)\longmapsto(J_{\rho(t)}x,t),\qquad
(y,t)\longmapsto(J_{\rho(t)}^{-1}y,t).
$$
The smooth even cutoff $\rho$ is one on $|t|\le a$ and zero on $|t|\ge b$ for $0<a<b$. Both maps fix every signed parameter over the protected set. In supplied source and target collar charts $\chi,\psi$ on the same strip the conjugate is $\psi\circ P\circ\chi^{-1}$, with inverse $\chi\circ P^{-1}\circ\psi^{-1}$. On the whole chart range its pullback normal is exactly the original signed coordinate. For a protected parametrized tube $\tau$ in the common strip the forward map sends $\chi\circ\tau$ to $\psi\circ\tau$ pointwise and the inverse recovers $\chi\circ\tau$. With the same ambient chart this fixes the entire tube as a subset with its given parametrization. On the central plateau a foot mark obeys the same two equations with $\tau(z,t)=(u(z),t)$ on the actual side and $(u'(z),t)$ on the normalized side whenever $J_1u=u'$.
