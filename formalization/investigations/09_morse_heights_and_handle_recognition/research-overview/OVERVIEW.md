For one smooth hypersurface immersion $F:M\to\mathbb R^5$ of a closed oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere, give the direction sphere $S^4$ its normalized round probability. For almost every direction $u$ the height $h_u=\langle F,u\rangle$ is Morse. Let $m_2(u)$ count its index-two critical points and extend the count measurably across exceptional directions. For $U$ the points with two positive and two negative principal curvatures and $\omega=|S^4|=8\pi^2/3$, the source gives
$$
\mathbb E[m_2]=\frac{2}{\omega}\int_U\det A\,dV_g .
$$
Both $u$ and $-u$ enter the indexed area formula. On a nonstandard candidate the smooth handle argument gives $m_2\ge2$ at every Morse height. Route 08's lower Weyl bound together with $E_+(g)<8\pi^2$ gives $\mathbb E[m_2]<9/4$. Integer-valuedness then gives $\Pr(m_2=2)>3/4$. The probability step has an independent Lean target. The topological lower count and the geometric mean bound each require a separate proof. [Immersion manuscript](../../../../papers/master/manuscript/immersion_entry.tex) · [Middle-handle audit](../../../../papers/master/reviews/E33_INDEX_TWO_HANDLES.md)

To use a selected height, transport its actual attaching maps through the reductions of the extremal handlebodies. With two middle handles the reduced counts $(m_1,m_3)$ can be $(1,1)$, $(0,2)$ or $(2,0)$. In the mixed case, a specified framed isotopy or allowed slides could make a 2-handle attaching circle meet the belt sphere of the 1-handle transversely once. Smooth cancellation would then reduce to the one-middle-handle argument. Geometric single intersection remains an additional hypothesis even with unit algebraic incidence. The other two cases lead to a two-component surgery diagram or its dual. The count bound gives no probability estimate for any particular diagram class.
