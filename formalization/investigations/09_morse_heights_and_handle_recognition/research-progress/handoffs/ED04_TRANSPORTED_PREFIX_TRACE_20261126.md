## Exact interface

- `SmoothPoincare.ClosedMarkedControl.splitAbsorptionFace` is an open partial diffeomorphism on the product meridian face with forward map $((z,s),w)\mapsto((1+s)z,w)$ and inverse $((Z,w))\mapsto((Z/\lvert Z\rvert,\lvert Z\rvert-1),w)$. Its signed coordinate is $s=1-t$ on the original lower product, hence its forward formula there is $((2-t)z,w)$. Both product parameters and the local inverse survive on the entire modeled face. The comparison leaves the rounded rim and radial-to-round ball extension as separate inputs. `smoothMarkedAssembly` promotes the closed marked homeomorphism when the actual signed collars and the two open interior patches satisfy the stated equations. Its inverse is proved smooth on the same open cover and has the inverse product germ. Realizing those premises over both entire rounded control traces remains a geometric step. - `SmoothPoincare.exists_nativePrefixCancellation` consumes the pinned `isCancellingPair` on an actual substrip of the original height. The index-$j$ critical set of the returned height is the original index-$j$ critical set minus the two specified points, for every $j$. Both actual boundary levels and closed lower bodies are the same subsets. `exists_nativeThreeToTwo` applies with $p$ of index one and original cardinalities one and three, returning cardinalities zero and two and the same critical sets in the other indices. No stabilization is used. `nativePrefixCancellation_sameUpperCut` gives equality of both regions at every higher cut. Strictly above the strip the two heights agree on an open ambient neighborhood, so any physical cost on those identical regions in the given metric is retained. The existence of the geometric pair on an arbitrary selected height is still an input. This is an application of the pinned first cancellation theorem, whose hypotheses include an isolated adjacent pair and a unique transverse trajectory expressed in its Sard coordinates.
