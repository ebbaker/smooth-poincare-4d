## Excising the new component in the height variable

Let $D$ be the selected native index-zero handle at $p$ of the smooth height $f$ on the compact Hausdorff boundaryless four-manifold. Assume $p$ is the only critical point in the full isolating band. Write $a=f(p)-\varepsilon$, $b=f(p)+\varepsilon$, and let $E$ be the native birth chart on its open source. With $U=\operatorname{target}(E)$ and $B=E(\overline B^4_1)$, the exact normal form throughout the chart gives

\[
 B=U\cap f^{-1}((-\infty,b]),\qquad f|_U\ge f(p).
\]

The pinned smooth bump theorem gives $0\le\chi\le1$, equal to one on a neighborhood of the compact $B$, with topological support inside $U$. Set $g=f+2\varepsilon\chi$. On $B$ one has $g\ge f(p)+2\varepsilon>b$. On $U\setminus B$ one has $g\ge f>b$. Outside $U$, $f$ and $g$ agree on a neighborhood of every point. The whole $g$ band $[a,b]$ therefore lies outside $U$, where the original critical-point hypothesis excludes any critical point of $g$. This argument requires no derivative bound on the cutoff inside $U$. ## Open comparison at the actual incoming feet

For a genuine merger, the selected-birth edge construction places the complete feet on opposite carrier spheres. A carrier is an ambient partial diffeomorphism $P$ defined on a neighborhood of its entire ball. Its target contains the whole corresponding actual foot. Let $A$ be the native signed foot collar or the actual four-dimensional edge block. In either case the compact closed foot on its center section lies in the open source of $A$ and maps into $P$'s target. The compact tube lemma gives a positive-width open product domain $W$ where both properties hold. The transverse radius can be chosen strictly between one and two and hence includes the entire rim and its outside. [Selected two-birth edge](../../../../SmoothPoincare/Heights/SelectedBirthEdge.lean) · [Compatible birth collars](../../../../SmoothPoincare/Heights/CompatibleBirthCollars.lean)

On $W$ take the model chart $B=P^{-1}\circ A$ and let $T$ be the restriction of $P$ to $B(W)$. Its exact source and target are $B(W)$ and $A(W)$. Both $T$ and its inverse are the literal restrictions of the chosen body chart and its inverse. For every $q\in W$ the verified equations are $T(B(q))=A(q)$, $T^{-1}(A(q))=B(q)$, and $B^{-1}(T^{-1}(A(q)))=q$. The signed version has $W=B^3_\sigma\times(-r,r)$ and the same actual value $f(p)-\varepsilon+t$ on both sides. The direct block version uses $W=B(b,r)\times B^3_\sigma$ in the original E41 block coordinates and retains the entire native normal-form expression for the actual height. The two transformations for a given carrier agree on their common domains because both are restrictions of that very carrier. The model collar here is chosen by pullback.
