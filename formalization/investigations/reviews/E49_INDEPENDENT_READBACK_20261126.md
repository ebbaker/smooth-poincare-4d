## Closed spatial adjoints and normalization

Write \(K=\dot g\) for the covariant variation and \(L=\operatorname{tr}_g\nabla^2\) for the signed rough Laplacian. The differentiated Christoffel tensor is \(\dot\Gamma^k{}_{ij}=\frac12(\nabla_i K^k{}_j+\nabla_j K^k{}_i-\nabla^kK_{ij})\). The Ricci variation uses \(\nabla_k\dot\Gamma^k{}_{ij}-\nabla_j\dot\Gamma^k{}_{ki}\), and \(\dot\Gamma^k{}_{ki}=\frac12\nabla_i\operatorname{tr}K\). In particular, the crossed derivative with outer slot \(k\) and inner slot \(i\) has the following adjoints against a smooth test tensor \(A\) on the closed slice:

\[
\int A^{ij}\nabla_k\nabla_i K^k{}_j\,dV
=-\int (\nabla_k A^{ij})\nabla_i K^k{}_j\,dV
=\int K^k{}_j\nabla_i\nabla_k A^{ij}\,dV.
\]

The order reverses when both derivatives move. The other crossed term uses the other tensor slot and gives the same pairing for symmetric \(A,K\). The rough term has coefficient \(-1/2\) in the Ricci variation and has adjoint \(-\frac12 L\) for this sign of \(L\). Metric compatibility fixes which slots are raised and allows the first integration by parts with the physical measure. The trace variation contributes \(-\frac12\nabla_j\nabla_i\operatorname{tr}K\), which vanishes for a trace-free speed. [Bour, §3 and Appendix §9.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1012.0342v1#S3) · [Gursky–Viaclovsky, (2.2)–(2.5)](https://arxiv.org/html/1105.4648v2#S2)
