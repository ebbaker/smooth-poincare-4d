**The two programs are working on the same problem and the same editions.** Lean has particularly close counterparts for energy accounting on the original smooth type, division of a marked disk, and the geometry of heights of an actual immersion. Three manuscript arguments still have substantial gaps in formal coverage: selection of the initial charged disk, uniform recognition from an infimum, and construction and analysis of the limiting modified Ricci flow. Universal positive subcritical entry and a strict same-type repair remain research questions in the manuscript itself.

## Priority for the intrinsic proof

The most useful next formal target is the implication in Theorem 6.4 from an arbitrary eligible minimizing sequence to a marked core with a fixed positive mass of the same chirality on each side. That output is precisely what the existing four-measure construction can take as input. Completing it would link the scale-selection argument to the disk and cap theorems. For the smoothing route, the corresponding target is the spatial Euler identity on the supplied modified flow, followed by passage to the weak initial trace. The handle work directly supports the independent immersed proof. The original Weyl pilot, neck accounting and Dirac work remain available for the branches that use their hypotheses.
