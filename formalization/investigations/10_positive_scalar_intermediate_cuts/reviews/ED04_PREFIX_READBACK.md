## Actual product band

In the specified complementary wedge order the three normal-containing curvature rows vanish, and a tangential Ricci eigenframe produces three sectional entries $R/2-r_i$. In `metricCurvatureFrameTensor` the wedge diagonal evaluates $Rm(u,v,v,u)$. The last-two-slot swap is also present in the cylinder-to-tangential formula. The normalized chiral projection divides by two, and taking the trace-free part leaves $-(r_i-R/3)/2$ for each chirality. Thus the density per chirality is $\frac14\lvert\operatorname{Ric}^\circ_h\rvert^2$. This gives zero on a round three-sphere and density $1/6$ on the unit $S^1\times S^2$ product, whose Ricci eigenvalues are $(0,1,1)$. Each complete side has band cost $LJ/8$, where $J=\int_N\lvert\operatorname{Ric}^\circ_h\rvert^2dV_h$. The fixed transition belongs to the complete upper side and enters the joined charge exactly once. For the two-mean product, the expression reduces to $\sqrt{\rho/3}L$ and strict positivity is equivalent to $L>0$ under $\rho>0$. One signed base cost at the wall defeats this budget even for arbitrarily short bands and $J=0$. These are tests on the chosen endpoint and fixed transition costs.
