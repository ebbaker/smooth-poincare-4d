## Opening bounded proof

Fix $k\ge0$, $d\in\mathbb Z$, $v:\operatorname{Fin} k\to\mathbb Z$ and $C:\mathrm{Matrix}(\operatorname{Fin} k,\operatorname{Fin} k;\mathbb Z)$. Define the matrices, with the full index type $\operatorname{Fin}2\oplus\operatorname{Fin}k$, by
$$
 A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&d\end{pmatrix},\quad
 A_i=\begin{pmatrix}-d&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
 B=\binom{0}{v^{\mathsf T}},\quad
 L=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\B^{\mathsf T}&C\end{pmatrix},\quad
 P=\begin{pmatrix}1&-A_i B\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Return $AA_i=A_iA=1$, the two-sided integral inverse of $P$ obtained by changing the upper-right sign, $B^{\mathsf T}A_iB=0$, and $P^{\mathsf T}LP=A\oplus C$. Then prove that rank two of the rational image of $L$ forces $C=0$ and recover $\det(L)=-C_{00}$ for $k=1$. The rank premise is separate from the output. The surgery application needs the identification of the integral cokernel with $H_1$ of the **full** actual outgoing boundary and its known free rank. At minimum, check $k=0$, a mixed example with nonzero off-diagonal $v$, and a nonzero $C$ lacking the rank premise. The fixed deliverable consists of these matrix facts.
