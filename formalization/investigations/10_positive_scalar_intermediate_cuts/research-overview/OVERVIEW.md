## The supplied cut

Take a reduced marked presentation of a closed oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere. The actual lower and upper bodies, full framed solid tori, regular levels, and closing map are part of the input. With one lower 1-handle, the winding row is primitive because its cokernel is the integral first homology of the closed manifold. Use the actual framed slides, core orientations and permutations supplied by Route 09 to normalize that row to $(1,0,\ldots,0)$. A chosen order defines actual outgoing boundaries after each selected initial set of attachments. Reversal gives its own normalized dual presentation and marked cuts. All surgery and fillings here use smooth manifolds and integer framings. [Surgery manuscript, `sec:mark` and `sec:psc`](../../../../papers/investigations-1/05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex)

For a closed connected oriented smooth three-manifold $Z$, PSC domination means a nonzero integral-degree map to $Z$ from a closed oriented smooth three-manifold carrying a smooth metric whose scalar curvature is positive everywhere. A disconnected source is reduced to a connected component of nonzero degree. A positive metric on $Z$ gives the identity witness. Chodosh–Maximo–Mukherjee identifies the diffeomorphism type under domination as a connected sum of spherical space forms and $S^1\times S^2$ factors, with the empty sum $S^3$. If $Z$ is an integral homology sphere this allows every finite connected sum of both signs of the Poincaré sphere. A compact **smooth** contractible filling excludes every nonempty such sum, including collections with both signs and vanishing net signed count. Use the boundary diffeomorphism conclusion here. Route 09 supplies the marked smooth ball where the reduction needs it. [Chodosh–Maximo–Mukherjee, Proposition 2.2, Corollary 2.3 and Proposition 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.05574v1)

## Two prefixes in the mixed case

Write $Y_j=\#_j(S^1\times S^2)$. In a reduced $(1,1,2,1,1)$ presentation, let $Z_a$ be the winding-one first cut and $Z_b$ the winding-zero first cut after the same normalization.

| Actual cut | Geometric and algebraic inputs | Boundary certificate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $Z_a$ | The native one-handle and winding-one 2-handle trace has fundamental group killed by the winding relation and unit integral cellular differential. Its smooth compact filling is contractible and its boundary is an integral homology sphere. | PSC domination is equivalent to $Z_a\cong S^3$. |
| $Z_b$ | In the ordinary boundary-surgery description, replace the dot by zero. In the order dot, winding one, winding zero, the off-diagonal winding entries are $1,0$. The full boundary has $H_1=\mathbb Z$ by the actual upper marking to $Y_1$. The full determinant is minus the winding-zero framing, which therefore vanishes. The ordinary two-component prefix is zero-matrix surgery. | PSC domination is equivalent to $Z_b\cong Y_2$. |

For the zero-matrix assertion more generally, the ordinary trace of a zero-matrix $j$-component link is a simply connected compact smooth 0/2 body with $H_2=\mathbb Z^j$ and zero pairing. The integral pair sequence and duality identify $H_2(\partial V)\to H_2(V)$ by using $H_3(V,\partial V)=0$ and the zero intersection map. The decomposition under domination and $H_1(\partial V)=\mathbb Z^j$ give exactly $j$ product factors and an integral homology sphere sum of spherical factors. Pull back the $j$ disjoint product spheres, including their normal intervals, and compress by attaching auxiliary 3-handles. They kill an integral basis of $H_2(V)$ with no kernel and preserve simple connectivity. The resulting compact smooth filling is contractible. The residual spherical sum is PSC by spherical metrics and connected sum, so the signed Poincaré sum obstruction removes it. The comparison filling classifies the original cut. Route 09 receives this classification to use the original upper gluing and attaching maps. [Zero-matrix lemma and mixed theorem](../../../../papers/investigations-1/05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex) · [Marked mixed recognition, `thm:mixed`](../../../../papers/investigations-1/04_hypersurface_immersions/02_two_handle_recognition/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_morse_heights.tex)

For one lower 1-handle and $n$ middle handles, the normalized full ordinary matrix has blocks $A=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, $B=\binom{0}{v^{\mathsf T}}$ and the winding-zero block $C$. The inverse of $A$ is integral and has bottom-right entry zero, so $B^{\mathsf T}A^{-1}B=0$. An explicit triangular integral basis change gives $A\oplus C$. The outgoing first homology $\mathbb Z^{n-1}$ gives rational rank two and hence $C=0$. Only the actual homology identification with the full outgoing cut supplies this rank. A net 2/3 cancellation in the larger diagram additionally requires matching upper geometry beyond the winding-zero first-cut classification. ## Physical certificate on the same level

On the supplied compact smooth marked Riemannian product $Z\times[t_-,t_+]$, let $\rho>0$ be a scalar lower bound and let $d$ be the distance between the ends measured **inside that band**. With orientation induced from the lower side, a separating family homologous to the lower slice has total projection degree one. Reversing the orientation changes this sign. A PSC separating family therefore gives PSC domination by one component. Räde's strict four-dimensional comparison on $(\rho/12)g$ gives $\sqrt{\rho/12}\,d<\pi/2$ when the target admits no PSC metric. Consequently $\rho d^2\ge3\pi^2$ is a certificate, including equality.

The alternate two-mean test uses $a_\pm=\inf H_{\mathrm{out},\pm}$ with outward divergence convention, so the outward unit sphere has mean $+3$. The non-PSC implication is
$$
\arctan\!\frac{2a_-}{\sqrt{3\rho}}+\arctan\!\frac{2a_+}{\sqrt{3\rho}}
   +\sqrt{\rho/3}\,d\le0.
$$
Strict positivity certifies the cut. Equality in this alternate bound occurs on the flat $T^3$ model in the manuscript. The lapse and stable-separator tests are separate extensions. In the stable test retain full stability, positive scalar curvature along the component and its nonzero degree onto this cut. [Surgery manuscript, `prop:band` and `prop:slice`](../../../../papers/investigations-1/05_collar_deformation_and_surgery/02_handle_isolation_and_marked_surgery/manuscript/recognition_from_marked_handle_cuts.tex) · [Räde, Corollary 2.25 and Theorem 2.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.10120v4)

## Strict reduction and the second cut

For a reduced $(1,1,3,2,1)$ presentation, or the reversed $(1,2,3,1,1)$ presentation when read from its single-1-handle end, take row $(1,0,0)$ and actual ordered cuts $Z_a,Z_{ab}$. A PSC-dominated $Z_a$ is $S^3$. Route 09 must return the relative geometric cancellation and its marked ball map $\psi$, giving a strict $3\to2$ decrease on the same smooth manifold with no lower 1-handle. Keep both tubes $v_j=(\partial\psi)^{-1}u_j$, $j=b,c$, and the two induced trace comparisons $\Psi_b,\Psi_{bc}$. These send the first successor boundary back to exactly $Z_{ab}$ and carry the closing map forward by composition. Postcompose an actual nonzero-degree witness for $Z_{ab}$ with $(\partial\Psi_b)^{-1}$, preserving nonzero degree including a possible change of sign. The full successor boundary is $Y_2$ in its actual upper marking, so its two-by-two matrix is zero. The rank-one zero-matrix result gives $Y_1$ for a dominated first successor cut. Property R then gives an unknot component in the actual residual two-component link. Gompf–Scharlemann–Thompson Proposition 3.2 and Laudenbach–Poénaru Theorem A become applicable once Route 09 supplies the framed trace comparison and its boundary map. The first cut is already a strict reduction. Recognition by the second cut depends on the transported certificate and the two-component hypothesis.
