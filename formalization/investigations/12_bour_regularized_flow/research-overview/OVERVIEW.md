## Fixed type, fixed parameter, and physical energy

Fix a closed connected oriented smooth homotopy four-sphere $M$ with an actual positive Yamabe class of energy below $2T$, $T=4\pi^2$. Use smooth real Riemannian metrics. Write $E_\pm(g)=\int_M\lVert W_g^\pm\rVert_{\rm HS}^2dV_g$ for real curvature endomorphism norms, $D(g)=\int_M\lVert\mathrm{Ric}_0\rVert^2dV_g$ in the ordinary two-tensor norm, and $S(g)=\int_M R^2 dV_g$. Use $I=I^+(M)$ for the witnessed same-type positive-class infimum. The closed signature gives $E_+=E_-=e$. Bour uses double-form norms, so $F_W=E_++E_-$ and $\lVert\mathrm{Rm}\rVert_{\rm tens}^2=4\lVert\mathrm{Rm}\rVert_{\rm op}^2$. In the master convention the Yamabe quotient integrates $6\lVert du\rVert^2+Ru^2$ over $\lVert u\rVert_4^2$, giving $Y=6Y_{\rm Bour}$. Bour's Laplacian is $\Delta_B=-\operatorname{tr}\nabla^2$. Keep the sign convention explicit when comparing the trace or principal symbol. [Master conventions](../../../../papers/master/manuscript/chiral_weyl_energy_and_smooth_poincare.tex) · [Bour v1, §§1 and 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/1012.0342v1)

On the original closed source the dictionary is
$$
16\pi^2=2e+S/24-D/2,\qquad
\mathcal F^\alpha=(1-\alpha)2e+\alpha D/2
                  =2e+\alpha S/24-16\alpha\pi^2,
\qquad \int_M \lVert\mathrm{Rm}\rVert_{\rm op}^2dV_g=16\pi^2+D .
$$
The last identity uses the closed Gauss–Bonnet formula and the orthogonal curvature decomposition. On a positive conformal class the constant test and Cauchy–Schwarz give $S\ge Y^2$. The Yamabe representative attains $S=Y^2$, so the minimum of $\mathcal F^\alpha$ within that class is $2e+\alpha Y^2/24-16\alpha\pi^2$ for $\alpha>0$. On a pointed domain retain the separate chiral integrals and local decomposition. [Working manuscript, Proposition `prop:budget`](../../../../papers/investigations-1/06_bour_flow_and_fixed_type_attainment/manuscript/fixed_type_attainment.tex) · [Original budgets](../../../../papers/investigations-1/06_bour_flow_and_fixed_type_attainment/01_regularized_flow_and_energy/notes/ORIGINAL_FLOW_20261126.md)

For fixed $0<\alpha<1$, start at an actual volume-one Yamabe metric $g_0$ with $Y_0>0$, $e_0<2T$, $S_0=Y_0^2\le384\pi^2$ and $\epsilon=e_0-I\ge0$. Put $F_0=\mathcal F^\alpha(g_0)$ and $m_\alpha=(1-\alpha)16\pi^2-F_0=2(2T-e_0)-\alpha Y_0^2/24$. The strict initial condition is $\alpha Y_0^2<48(2T-e_0)$. The sufficient condition $\alpha<1-e_0/(2T)$ uses the round Yamabe bound. On the original smooth solution of $\partial_tg=-2G(g)$, $G=\operatorname{grad}_{L^2_g}\mathcal F^\alpha$ as a covariant tensor, the flow identities are
$$
\operatorname{Vol}(g_t)=1,\qquad
\frac{d}{dt}\mathcal F^\alpha(g_t)=-2\lVert G(g_t)\rVert_{2,g_t}^2,\qquad
\int_0^t \lVert G(g_s)\rVert_{2,g_s}^2 ds
=\frac12\bigl(F_0-\mathcal F^\alpha(g_t)\bigr).
$$
Use constant-scale invariance and the trace of the physical gradient for volume preservation. The strict margin gives $e(t)\le F_0/[2(1-\alpha)]=2T-m_\alpha/[2(1-\alpha)]<2T$. The Yamabe representative of each class yields $Y([g_t])^2=48(2T-e(t))+12D_{\rm Yam}(t)$. Continuity of the lowest conformal-Laplacian eigenvalue, its agreement in sign with $Y$, and positivity at time zero give $Y([g_t])\ge\sqrt{24m_\alpha/(1-\alpha)}$ on the connected time interval. The weaker $\sqrt{24m_\alpha}$ also follows in the master units of Bour's Lemma 4.5. The bound $e_0/(1-\alpha)$ is below $2T$ under the sufficient parameter condition and eventually on the vanishing-parameter family. [Bour v1, Lemma 4.5 and §8](https://arxiv.org/html/1012.0342v1) · [Original strict margin](../../../../papers/investigations-1/06_bour_flow_and_fixed_type_attainment/01_regularized_flow_and_energy/notes/ORIGINAL_FLOW_20261126.md#strict-pinching-and-positivity-at-all-smooth-times)

After positivity gives $I\le e(t)$, the two sublevel formulas give
$$
D(t)\le D_0+\frac{4(1-\alpha)}{\alpha}\epsilon
       =4I+\frac{4\epsilon}{\alpha}+\frac{Y_0^2-384\pi^2}{12}
       \le4I+\frac{4\epsilon}{\alpha},
\qquad S(t)\le Y_0^2+\frac{48\epsilon}{\alpha}.
$$
The total dissipation satisfies $2\int_0^{t_*}\lVert G\rVert_2^2\le F_0-2(1-\alpha)I\le 2\epsilon+2\alpha I$. If $\alpha_j\downarrow0$ and $0\le\epsilon_j\le\alpha_j^2$, let $\delta=2T-I>0$. Eventually $e(t)\le2T-\delta/2$, $Y([g_t])\ge\sqrt{24\delta}$, $S(t)\le384\pi^2+48\alpha_j$, and $D(t)\le4I+4\alpha_j\le32\pi^2-2\delta$, uniformly over each original smooth time interval. The chiral totals tend to $I$ uniformly over those original times. Short-time existence and smoothing each use fixed-parameter inputs. [Working manuscript, `prop:budget`](../../../../papers/investigations-1/06_bour_flow_and_fixed_type_attainment/manuscript/fixed_type_attainment.tex) · [Dissipation and physical energy](../../../../papers/investigations-1/06_bour_flow_and_fixed_type_attainment/01_regularized_flow_and_energy/notes/ORIGINAL_FLOW_20261126.md#physical-energy-bounds-and-a-uniform-margin)
