Use a smooth oriented Riemannian spin four-manifold. At a point choose a spin lift of a positively oriented orthonormal frame. Both half-spin fibers have underlying real space ℍ, with its ordinary squared norm and a commuting right complex structure. In the positive-to-negative direction the four Clifford vectors act by left multiplication by (−1,i,j,k). In the reverse direction they act by (1,i,j,k). The ordered self-dual two-forms are (01+23,02+31,03+12)/√2, as in the [metric block](../../../../FirstOrderTwistor/GeometricChiralBlock.lean). They act on the positive fiber by √2 times left i,j,k. In the curvature-operator convention ⟨Rop(X∧Y),Z∧U⟩=g(R(X,Y)U,Z), the spin curvature has coefficient −1/2. The curvature density is the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of the positive three-dimensional block.

For a conformal change h=a²g, compare oriented metric frames and use the isometric spin identification β. Harmonic spinors transform by a^(−3/2)β and twistor solutions by a^(1/2)β. The differential defect, spinor norm, joint floor and volume integral on a strip all use that strip's metric. To add its contribution, identify the local chiral energy *measure* on the physical region. The existing [conformal metric theorem](../../../../FirstOrderTwistor/ConformalEnergy.lean) proves measure equality from the actual volume transformation and the matched Weyl coefficient transformation. Prescribed poles require their own chirality, scale and normalization.

Fix the increasing normal along [0,L] and write the product equation as (∂t+A)u=f. On the unit round link the prescribed traces are P₊u(0) and P₋u(L). The complementary adjoint conditions are P₋v(0)=0 and P₊v(L)=0. Use the |A|^(1/2) trace norm (equivalent to the inhomogeneous half-order norm at a fixed gap) and length-uniform H¹ estimates for the full two-end problem on lengths bounded below. A perturbation must keep the reference projectors and their domains identified. On a general measured slice both the spectral gap and projector variation require input. Use product-core graphs only when the actual two core problems and collar have compatible product germs. On the original positive-Yamabe closed source one may instead lift the two measured jumps locally, then solve away their smooth or weak Dirac error globally. This construction needs collars on both sides of both seams; a prescribed Green singularity is supplied separately. Neither a trace norm nor an H¹ norm gives a pointwise four-dimensional floor. The other independent input is a bound on the projected *differentiated* defect. In a fixed-width flat comparison annulus an ordinary harmonic spinor has Pψ=∇ψ. Commuting the twisted Dirac operator through ∇ gives Ricci times ψ, and a nested cutoff estimate controls ∇(Pψ). The Ricci norm is on the larger buffer. Alternatively use a strong W²ᵖ comparison (p>4), compatible incoming traces near the specified nonzero flat field, interior elliptic estimates and a verified floor. The constant and the volume in either argument belong to the same annular gauge. A conformal product cylinder has different reference Ricci and spinor derivatives.
