## From spinors to residual energy

Let $(M^4,g)$ be smooth, oriented, Riemannian and spin. Use positive half-spinors $S^+$, with self-dual two-forms acting on this chirality. For each smooth field $\psi_a$ put $T_a=P_g\psi_a$ for the twistor operator and set $H=\sum_a|\Pi(d^\nabla T_a)^+|^2$. DIR §2.3 gives a pointwise algebraic identity that can be tested before introducing any Dirac boundary condition,

$$
2H=|W^+|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\sum_a|\psi_a|^2 .
$$

On a measured strip $U_j$ where $\sum_a|\psi_{j,a}|^2\ge c_j^2>0$, this controls its chiral energy by $2c_j^{-2}\int_{U_j}H_j$. Local normalizations can vary. On overlapping strips, add the energy of the uncovered set once and sum the nonnegative strip bounds. If the weighted sum and uncovered energy vanish in the required iterated limit, Route 02 can use the result to control its complement. On the reference cylinder use $L_0=\partial_t+A$, a common increasing normal, incoming $P_+u(0)$ and $P_-u(L)$, and gap $3/2$ on the **unit** round sphere. The harmonic spinor conformal weight in four dimensions is $-3/2$, while the twistor weight is $+1/2$. A prescribed Green pole has its own flat germ and normalization. Fixed-width first-order control allows curved collars. An inverse for $D$ and small first-order twistor error alone leave the differentiated term undetermined. There is also a global recognition endpoint. On the **same** smooth, closed homotopy sphere, unit $L^2$ fields with vanishing twistor defect in a supplied nondegenerate compact gauge can limit to a nonzero twistor spinor. Exact recognition then uses prolongation across the zero set and the parent's ASD recognition theorem. The separate five-mode Yamabe equality in E27 tests scalar spectral rigidity. It does not require spinor transmission.
