## Decisions on the surviving candidate

* **Positive least \(D\).** Put \(Z=\mathrm{Ric}_0\) and \(W=((Z^\sharp)^2)_0^\flat\), where the trace-free projection and lowering use \(g_*\). Check independence modulo diffeomorphism and conformal directions. Symmetry of the actual Hessian \(H\) gives a negative direction if \(H(Z,Z)H(W,W)-H(Z,W)^2<0\). When \(H(Z,Z)=0\), a nonzero mixed term suffices. Verify the path stays positive for both signs and apply the checked attained variation criterion. A degenerate plane or a nonnegative restriction leaves that metric for further tests. \(W\) needs a divergence check for any TT-only formula.
* **Zero least \(D\).** On the selected positive Einstein metric compute the Weyl second variation with the signed convention \(\Delta_L\) leading with \(\nabla^i\nabla_i\). For a nonzero transverse-traceless eigentensor of \(-\Delta_L\), the sign test is strict precisely in \((s/3,s/2)\). Record separately a proof of factorization and the existence of a mode on the actual candidate. A gap estimate that excludes the candidate directly also closes that case.
* **Conditional scientific 06 extension.** After X, set \(J_p=\inf_{Y>0} F^p\). At small fixed \(0<p<1\), \(pY_*^2<48(8\pi^2-I)\) and \(F^p\le F^p(g_*)+\epsilon\) give a sublevel on which to test \(D\le D_*+2\epsilon/p<4I\) with a fixed margin. Prove positive original trajectories and rule out each of the two marked bubbles using \(4I\le 4e_X=D_X\). First let the near-\(J_p\) error go to zero at fixed \(p\), then take \(p\downarrow0\) on actual global minimizers. They would supply finite-\(p\) stability. On a non-Einstein Weyl minimizer a direction can decrease \(F^p\) without decreasing \(e\). To return this extension to R, derive an independent signed Weyl variation or a recognition statement.
