## First bounded proof

**SP4-FORM-FOT-E01 · Finite energy accounting.** Let $J$ be finite, $b\ge0$ the base energy, $x_j\ge0$ component energies, and $\delta>0$. The algebraic inputs are

$$
\begin{gathered}
4\pi^2\le e\le 8\pi^2-\delta,\qquad e=b+\sum_{j\in J}x_j,\\
b=0\ \lor\ b>4\pi^2,\qquad
\forall j\in J,\quad (x_j=0\ \lor\ x_j>4\pi^2),\\
\forall j\in J,\quad x_j>0\ \Longrightarrow\ e-x_j>0 .
\end{gathered}
$$

Prove $(\forall j\in J,\ x_j=0)\land e=b$. A positive $x_j$ leaves $e-x_j<4\pi^2-\delta$. Nonnegativity and the gap force $b$ and every other $x_k$ to vanish, contradicting the strict residual. Cover $J=\varnothing$ as well. Use `Finset` and real energies. The proposed module is `formalization/SectionsAndConnections/FiniteAccounting.lean` and the name is `SectionsAndConnections.noPositiveBubbleOfFiniteAccounting`. Import it through the current library. Sources are SEC `msgap:strict`, `msgap:accounting`, and §15. Reserve the new blueprint label `lem:finite-bubble-accounting`. E17 must obtain the equality, gap dichotomies and strict residual from geometry. E01 is a finite algebra lemma even after Lean verifies it. [SEC §§12.1, 12.4, 15][SEC]
