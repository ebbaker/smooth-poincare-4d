## First deliverables

1. **Start with one independently repaired ball, then sum.** Route 05 takes the infimum of the existing set `geometricPositiveEnergies` on a source with a positive competitor. For each marked ball produce membership of its repaired metric and compare its total with the raw exterior plus its net cost. The first bounded target accepts those witnesses and proves the finite inequality, including the empty family. A geometric specialization must expose the actual metric, sign, measure and integrability. [VAR-E01 and its Lean proposition](../05_variational_comparison/research-plan/PLAN.md#first-bounded-proof)
2. **In parallel, make the cut argument independent of a chosen bubble.** Route 03 starts with two full raw sides, two cap metrics and the original gluing. Test both choices of surviving cap. Prove $m_{3-s}\le e-I^+(M)+\gamma_s$ without any compactified ALE core. Then derive the tensor error of the cutoff interpolation, uniformly along the path. Route 05 supplies the positive-path result on the joined type. Route 03 stretches at each fixed index, transfers compact tests and uses zero point capacity on both closures. The endpoint is `prop:short-cut`. [Route 03 packets](../03_single_cut_descent/research-plan/PLAN.md#the-short-marked-cut) · [Master proof](../../../papers/master/manuscript/variational_comparison.tex)
3. **Prove strong first-order comparison at the infimum.** On a fixed compact use a Hilbert-space calculation to identify the weighted defect removed by a cutoff. Then derive that calculation's premises from the actual metric interpolation and a smooth compactly supported variation. This is the first major analytic milestone. The earlier theorem deriving convergence from metric two-jets and an integrable majorant is available for that stronger setting. [Route 05 plan](../05_variational_comparison/research-plan/PLAN.md) · [Existing fixed-domain theorem](../../FirstOrderTwistor/FixedMetricEnergy.lean)
4. **Select physical scales alongside the cut and replacement work.** The bounded quantile packet needs finite measures, an attaining center and a full-energy budget. Its outputs are the two opposite sets and the low-energy annulus or the diffuse alternative. Chart realization is a further question. A separate packet addresses diffuse complements with fixed tolerance before the source limit. [Physical quantiles](../../../papers/master/manuscript/complete_limits.tex) · [Joint selection](../../../papers/master/manuscript/physical_volume.tex)

## When the geometry changes the priority

- If a marked low-curvature shell is supplied, route its actual sign, first derivatives and domain to Route 03. A short flat-looking distance annulus alone admits no splice calculation. A measured spin strip can instead supply a Route 04 estimate with its own endpoint and cover assumptions.
- If definite mass is diffuse, try independent repairs. In the calculated smooth normal-jet model, flattening a radial core of relative volume $f$ can cost at least $1/(1-f)$ times the incoming nonzero-block mass to leading order. A repair estimate must therefore track the change in energy relative to the raw exterior. At fixed volume tolerance require bounded family size, aggregate cost and a bound on all uncovered chiral mass. A comparison of the whole intervening region is another option. [Boundary-matched repair calculation](../../../papers/master/notes/E17_DIFFUSE_REPAIR.md)
- If physical curvature stays bounded, follow closed attainment. If it diverges, a fixed maximum-curvature-scale ball has vanishing integrated energy. A round restart requires a decreasing quantity or a termination proof. A nonround closed minimizer requires rigidity or a competitor of strictly smaller energy on the same smooth type.
- A positive metric at or below $T$ invokes direct recognition. A general entry construction would put every hypothetical counterexample in the band but still requires one of the subsequent contradictions. The mathematical [master register](../../../papers/master/research-progress/REGISTER.md) owns these existence questions.
